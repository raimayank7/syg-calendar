Substitute holidays

When a public holiday falls on a weekend, each of the three places has its own rule.

Singapore

In Singapore a public holiday that falls on a Sunday is followed by a holiday on the Monday for people whose rest day is that Sunday, as MOM states for 2026 and 2027 (for example, 8 February 2027 follows Chinese New Year on Sunday 7 February). A holiday on a Saturday is not moved.

Hong Kong

In Hong Kong every Sunday is a general holiday. When a holiday falls on a Sunday another day is named in its place: in 2027 the second day of Lunar New Year falls on a Sunday and the Government has made the fourth day, 9 February, a general holiday instead. Saturdays are working days for some employers and rest days for others.

Japan

In Japan, when a national holiday falls on a Sunday the next day that is not a holiday becomes a holiday (a furikae kyujitsu, shown as 休日 in the official file), and a day between two holidays also becomes a holiday. In 2027 the Vernal Equinox Day falls on Sunday 21 March, so Monday 22 March is a holiday. The rule is set by the Act on National Holidays; we describe it from general knowledge and the example in the official file, not from the text of the Act.

Why check the official list

Dates of Islamic festivals (Hari Raya Puasa, Hari Raya Haji) and of Buddhist and Hindu festivals depend on the moon and on announcements, so a government can set the day close to the date. Always check the official page before you book leave or travel.
