Japan in 2027

Japan has 17 public holidays on the official list for 2027, of which 1 fall on a Saturday or Sunday: Vernal Equinox Day on Sunday, 21 March 2027. The list is published by the Cabinet Office of Japan, file of national holidays 1955-2027.

Long weekends

There are 7 runs of three or more days off in a row in 2027 for people with a Saturday and Sunday weekend: 1 Jan to 3 Jan (3 days); 9 Jan to 11 Jan (3 days); 20 Mar to 22 Mar (3 days); 1 May to 5 May (5 days); 17 Jul to 19 Jul (3 days); 18 Sep to 20 Sep (3 days); 9 Oct to 11 Oct (3 days). Taking one or two days of leave next to a holiday in the middle of the week can turn it into a long break.

Making longer breaks with leave

Taking one or two days of leave next to a holiday gives these breaks in 2027, for people with a Saturday and Sunday weekend: 1 Jan to 4 Jan, 4 days off for 1 day of leave; 8 Jan to 11 Jan, 4 days off for 1 day of leave; 11 Feb to 14 Feb, 4 days off for 1 day of leave; 29 Apr to 5 May, 7 days off for 1 day of leave; 18 Sep to 23 Sep, 6 days off for 2 days of leave.

How days are moved

In Japan, when a national holiday falls on a Sunday the next day that is not a holiday becomes a holiday (a furikae kyujitsu, shown as 休日 in the official file), and a day between two holidays also becomes a holiday. In 2027 the Vernal Equinox Day falls on Sunday 21 March, so Monday 22 March is a holiday.
