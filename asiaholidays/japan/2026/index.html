Japan in 2026

Japan has 18 national holidays in 2026. Golden Week, the run of holidays from 29 April to 5 May, is the longest break of the year in 2026. The Vernal Equinox Day is on Friday, 20 March 2026 and the Autumnal Equinox Day on Wednesday, 23 September 2026, dates that are fixed each year from the position of the sun and published by the Cabinet Office in advance. The longest run of days off is 2 May to 6 May, 5 days.

Other years: 2020 2021 2022 2023 2024 2025 2027.

Breaks with leave in 2026

With one or two days of leave next to a holiday you can make these breaks in 2026: 1 Jan to 4 Jan, 4 days off for 1 day of leave; 9 Jan to 12 Jan, 4 days off for 1 day of leave; 20 Feb to 23 Feb, 4 days off for 1 day of leave; 2 May to 10 May, 9 days off for 2 days of leave; 19 Sep to 27 Sep, 9 days off for 2 days of leave. This assumes a Saturday and Sunday weekend.

Weekend holidays

In 2026 the following holidays fall on a Saturday or Sunday: Constitution Memorial Day (Sunday, 3 May 2026). Under the Japanese rule a holiday that falls on a Sunday is followed by a substitute holiday.
