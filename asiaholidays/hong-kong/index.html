Hong Kong in 2027

Hong Kong has 17 public holidays on the official list for 2027, of which 4 fall on a Saturday or Sunday: Lunar New Year's Day on Saturday, 6 February 2027, The day following Good Friday on Saturday, 27 March 2027, Labour Day on Saturday, 1 May 2027, Christmas Day on Saturday, 25 December 2027. The list is published by the Hong Kong Government (GovHK), last revised May 2026.

Long weekends

There are 7 runs of three or more days off in a row in 2027 for people with a Saturday and Sunday weekend: 1 Jan to 3 Jan (3 days); 6 Feb to 9 Feb (4 days); 26 Mar to 29 Mar (4 days); 3 Apr to 5 Apr (3 days); 1 Oct to 3 Oct (3 days); 8 Oct to 10 Oct (3 days); 25 Dec to 27 Dec (3 days). Taking one or two days of leave next to a holiday in the middle of the week can turn it into a long break.

Making longer breaks with leave

Taking one or two days of leave next to a holiday gives these breaks in 2027, for people with a Saturday and Sunday weekend: 1 Jan to 4 Jan, 4 days off for 1 day of leave; 5 Feb to 9 Feb, 5 days off for 1 day of leave; 25 Mar to 29 Mar, 5 days off for 1 day of leave; 2 Apr to 5 Apr, 4 days off for 1 day of leave; 13 May to 16 May, 4 days off for 1 day of leave.

How days are moved

In Hong Kong every Sunday is a general holiday. When a holiday falls on a Sunday another day is named in its place: in 2027 the second day of Lunar New Year falls on a Sunday and the Government has made the fourth day, 9 February, a general holiday instead. Saturdays are working days for some employers and rest days for others.
