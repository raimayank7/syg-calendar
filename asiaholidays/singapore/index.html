Singapore in 2027

Singapore has 11 public holidays on the official list for 2027, of which 4 fall on a Saturday or Sunday: Chinese New Year (first day) on Saturday, 6 February 2027, Chinese New Year (second day) on Sunday, 7 February 2027, Labour Day on Saturday, 1 May 2027, Christmas Day on Saturday, 25 December 2027. The list is published by the Ministry of Manpower (MOM), last updated 19 June 2026.

Long weekends

There are 4 runs of three or more days off in a row in 2027 for people with a Saturday and Sunday weekend: 1 Jan to 3 Jan (3 days); 26 Mar to 28 Mar (3 days); 15 May to 17 May (3 days); 7 Aug to 9 Aug (3 days). Taking one or two days of leave next to a holiday in the middle of the week can turn it into a long break.

Making longer breaks with leave

Taking one or two days of leave next to a holiday gives these breaks in 2027, for people with a Saturday and Sunday weekend: 1 Jan to 4 Jan, 4 days off for 1 day of leave; 25 Mar to 28 Mar, 4 days off for 1 day of leave; 15 May to 20 May, 6 days off for 2 days of leave; 6 Aug to 9 Aug, 4 days off for 1 day of leave; 28 Oct to 31 Oct, 4 days off for 1 day of leave.

How days are moved

In Singapore a public holiday that falls on a Sunday is followed by a holiday on the Monday for people whose rest day is that Sunday, as MOM states for 2026 and 2027 (for example, 8 February 2027 follows Chinese New Year on Sunday 7 February). A holiday on a Saturday is not moved.
