April 2027 at a glance

April 2027 has 30 days. It begins on a Thursday and ends on a Friday. There are 22 weekdays (Monday to Friday) and 8 Saturdays and Sundays. After the 2 gazetted holidays that fall on a weekday, a five-day office works 20 days. Thursdays and Fridays occur five times; the other weekdays occur four times.

The month covers ISO week 13 to week 17. Its first day is day 91 of the year and its last is day 120, leaving 245 days of 2027 after it. July 2027 starts on the same weekday, so the two months line up on the page.

In the Indian national calendar, 1 April is 11 Chaitra 1949 and 30 April is 10 Vaisakha 1949; the month of Vaisakha begins on 21 April.

About April

The origin of April's name is uncertain; one old explanation links it to the Latin word for opening, as buds open. It has 30 days.

The new financial year and, in many states, the new school session begin on the 1st. Summer sets in over the plains and the wheat harvest is celebrated at Vaisakhi in mid-April, when Vishu, Puthandu, Poila Boishakh and Bohag Bihu also bring regional new years. Fixed days: World Health Day on the 7th, Ambedkar Jayanti on the 14th, Civil Services Day on the 21st, Earth Day on the 22nd and National Panchayati Raj Day on the 24th.

Festivals that follow the Moon change their date every year. For 2027 this page lists only the dates in the official lists above.

Questions

How many days are in April 2027?

April 2027 has 30 days: 22 weekdays and 8 Saturdays and Sundays.

What day does April 2027 start on?

1 April 2027 is a Thursday. The month ends on Friday, 30 April 2027.

Which holidays fall in April 2027?

The Central Government gazetted holidays are: Ram Navami on 15 April and Mahavir Jayanti on 19 April.
