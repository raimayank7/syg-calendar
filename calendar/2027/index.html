2027 at a glance

2027 begins on a Friday and ends on a Friday. It is not a leap year, so it has 365 days: 261 weekdays from Monday to Friday and 104 Saturdays and Sundays. By the international week count it has 52 numbered weeks, and week 1 starts on Monday, 4 January 2027, so the first days of January belong to the last week of 2026.

The 13th falls on a Friday once: in August. January and October each have five Fridays, five Saturdays and five Sundays. Every date in 2027 shares its weekday with the same date in 2010, 2021, 2038 and 2049, so a calendar from one of those years can be reused; see years with the same calendar.

A quick way to place any date: in 2027 the dates 4 April, 6 June, 8 August, 10 October and 12 December, and the last day of February, all fall on a Sunday. Count from the nearest of them to find the weekday of any other date. The method is explained on the day of the week page.

In the Indian national calendar the Saka year 1949 begins on Monday, 22 March 2027. In the financial calendar, 1 April 2027 opens FY 2027-28.

Questions about 2027

Is 2027 a leap year?

No. 2027 has 365 days. The next leap year is 2028.

What day does 2027 start on?

1 January 2027 is a Friday, and 31 December 2027 is a Friday.

How many weeks are in 2027?

2027 has 365 days, which is 52 weeks and 1 day. In the international (ISO) week count it has 52 numbered weeks.
