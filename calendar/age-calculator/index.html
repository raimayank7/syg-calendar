Age calculator: exact age from date of birth

Choose the date of birth. The exact age today, or on any date you pick, appears in years, months and days.

How to use it

  1. Choose the date of birth.
  2. Age on this date is today. Change it to find the age on another day: the cut-off date in an exam notice, the date of joining, a date in the past.
  3. Read the age, the same span in months, weeks and days, and the date of the next birthday.

Nothing you enter is stored or sent anywhere. The sum is done on your own device.

Age on a cut-off date

Application forms for jobs, exams and school admissions rarely ask for your age today. They ask for your age as on a fixed date printed in the notice, and they give a lowest and highest age. To check yourself against such a rule:

  1. Put your date of birth in the first box, exactly as on your certificate.
  2. Put the notice's "as on" date in the second box.
  3. Compare the completed years, months and days with the limits.

Notices often state the limit the other way round: "born not earlier than one date and not later than another". Then you need no calculator; your birth date must simply lie between the two. Relaxations for categories, and the exact way a body counts age, are stated in each notice. Always follow the notice itself.

How age is counted

You complete a year of age on your birthday. Before the birthday in any year you are still the younger age. So the page counts whole years first, then the whole months since the last birthday, then the days since the last monthly date.

Example: born 15 August 1990, age on 20 March 2027. The last birthday was 15 August 2026: 36 years. From 15 August to 15 March is 7 months. From 15 March to 20 March is 5 days.

Authorities can differ on details such as whether the cut-off day itself is counted. Where an age limit matters to the day, follow the wording of the notice or ask the authority concerned.

Born on 29 February

A person born on 29 February has a true birthday only in leap years. In other years this page counts the birthday on 28 February, the last day of that month. Some authorities and some countries count it on 1 March instead. If you were born on that date and a rule depends on the exact day, check which convention the authority uses.

Age in other units

The page also shows the age as total months, weeks and days. These are useful for a baby's age (weeks, then months), for "10,000 days old" celebrations, and for forms that ask for age in months. The day count is exact, with every leap day included.

How do I calculate my age without a calculator?

Subtract the birth year from the present year, then take away 1 if this year's birthday has not come yet.

Why does the age in months and days differ from another calculator by a day?

Months have different lengths, and tools differ in how they handle the end of a month. The total days line is the same in every correct calculator.

Can I find someone's age on a past date?

Yes. Set "Age on this date" to any date on or after the date of birth.
