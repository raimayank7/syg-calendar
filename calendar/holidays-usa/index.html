US federal holidays 2026 to 2030

The eleven federal holidays of each year, with the day the holiday is observed when it falls on a weekend.

Source and date. Federal Holidays schedule of the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM), opm.gov, fetched on 12 November 2026. Holiday names are given as OPM gives them, which are the names in the law.

What a federal holiday is

The United States has eleven federal holidays, named in section 6103 of title 5 of the United States Code. On these days federal government offices are closed and federal employees are paid for the day. The tables below give the dates for 2026 to 2030 as OPM publishes them, including the day a holiday is observed when its true date falls on a weekend.

Federal holidays are not "national holidays" that bind everyone. Each state decides the holidays of its own offices and schools, and private employers decide for themselves. In practice most employers close on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day, and far fewer on the others.

The weekend rule

OPM states the rule this way for most federal employees:

  • If a holiday falls on a Saturday, the Friday before is treated as the holiday.
  • If a holiday falls on a Sunday, the Monday after is treated as the holiday.

That is why Independence Day is shown on Friday 3 July in 2026 and Monday 5 July in 2027, and why New Year's Day 2028, a Saturday, is observed on Friday 31 December 2027. Rows marked "observed" in the tables are such moved dates. The festival itself, with its fireworks or family meal, is still kept on the true date.

Fixed dates and floating Mondays

Five holidays have fixed dates: New Year's Day (1 January), Juneteenth National Independence Day (19 June), Independence Day (4 July), Veterans Day (11 November) and Christmas Day (25 December). The others are tied to a weekday, most of them to a Monday, which gives a three-day weekend:

About the names

OPM notes that the February holiday is designated "Washington's Birthday" in the law, and that it always uses the names in the law, although states, local governments and businesses may use other names. In everyday speech the day is widely called Presidents' Day. In the same way, a number of states and cities mark the second Monday of October as Indigenous Peoples' Day.

Inauguration Day

Every fourth year, 20 January, the day a President is sworn in, is an additional holiday for federal employees who work in the Washington, DC area. It was in OPM's schedule for 2025 and the next one falls in January 2029.

Working with people in the United States

  • Dates are written month first. 7/4/2027 is the Fourth of July. See date formats.
  • Expect offices to be quiet from Thanksgiving Thursday to the following Monday, and between Christmas and New Year.
  • Stock exchanges and banks keep their own calendars, which are close to the federal list but not identical. Check the exchange's or the bank's own notice.
  • To count US working days roughly, use the working days calculator with the Indian holiday box unticked, and subtract the federal holidays in the period by hand.
How many federal holidays are there in the United States?

Eleven each year, plus Inauguration Day every fourth year for federal employees in the Washington, DC area.

Is the day after Thanksgiving a federal holiday?

No. Many employers give it as a day off, but it is not in the federal list.

Are federal holidays the same in every state?

The federal list applies to federal employees everywhere. State and local governments set their own holidays, which mostly but not entirely match.
