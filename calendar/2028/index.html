2028 at a glance

2028 begins on a Saturday and ends on a Sunday. It is a leap year, so it has 366 days: 260 weekdays from Monday to Friday and 106 Saturdays and Sundays. By the international week count it has 52 numbered weeks, and week 1 starts on Monday, 3 January 2028, so the first days of January belong to the last week of 2027.

The 13th falls on a Friday once: in October. December has five Fridays, five Saturdays and five Sundays. Every date in 2028 shares its weekday with the same date in 1972, 2000, 2056 and 2084, so a calendar from one of those years can be reused; see years with the same calendar.

A quick way to place any date: in 2028 the dates 4 April, 6 June, 8 August, 10 October and 12 December, and the last day of February, all fall on a Tuesday. Count from the nearest of them to find the weekday of any other date. The method is explained on the day of the week page.

In the Indian national calendar the Saka year 1950 begins on Tuesday, 21 March 2028. In the financial calendar, 1 April 2028 opens FY 2028-29.

Questions about 2028

Is 2028 a leap year?

Yes. 2028 has 366 days and February has 29.

What day does 2028 start on?

1 January 2028 is a Saturday, and 31 December 2028 is a Sunday.

How many weeks are in 2028?

2028 has 366 days, which is 52 weeks and 2 days. In the international (ISO) week count it has 52 numbered weeks.
