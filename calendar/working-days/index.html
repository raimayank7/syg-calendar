Working days between two dates

Count the working days between two dates, for a five-day or a six-day week, with or without public holidays.

How to use it

  1. Choose the first and last date of the period. Both dates are counted if they are working days.
  2. Tick Six-day week if only Sunday is a weekly off, as in many shops, factories, schools and private offices. Leave it unticked for a Monday to Friday week.
  3. Leave the holiday box ticked to remove the gazetted holidays, or untick it to count every weekday.

What counts as a working day here

The page starts with every date in the period and removes:

  • Sundays, always;
  • Saturdays, unless you tick the six-day week;
  • the 17 gazetted holidays of 2027 for Central Government offices in Delhi and New Delhi, when the box is ticked and the holiday falls on a day that would otherwise be worked.

A holiday that falls on a weekly off is not removed twice. In 2027 several do: Independence Day and Id-e-Milad on Sunday 15 August, Guru Nanak's Birthday on Sunday 14 November, and Gandhi Jayanti, Dussehra and Christmas Day on Saturdays. The official order says no substitute holiday is given in such cases.

The limits of the holiday list

Please read this before relying on the number.

  • The list is the Central Government's list for its offices in Delhi and New Delhi, from the official memorandum dated 16 July 2026. It is on our 2027 holiday page in full.
  • Central Government offices in other states observe 14 of these holidays compulsorily and choose 3 more locally, so their list can differ.
  • State governments, banks, courts, schools and private employers each have their own lists. Banks follow the holidays their state notifies and are closed on the second and fourth Saturdays.
  • Holidays that depend on sighting the moon (Id-ul-Fitr, Id-ul-Zuha, Muharram, Id-e-Milad) may be moved by a day at short notice.
  • We hold the official list for 2027 only. For other years the page removes weekends only, and says so under the result.

For your own office, count with the box unticked and then subtract your employer's holidays by hand.

Where the count is used

  • Salary and wages: days to be paid for someone who joined or left in the middle of a month.
  • Leave: working days of leave that a stretch of dates will use up. Rules differ on whether weekends inside a leave period count; check your leave rules.
  • Delivery and service promises: "within 7 working days".
  • Project plans: effort available between two milestones.
  • Notice periods and replies that are fixed in working days.

Banks and alternate Saturdays

Banks in India are closed on Sundays and on the second and fourth Saturdays of each month, and open on the other Saturdays. This page has no setting for that pattern. For a rough bank count, use the five-day result and add the first, third and fifth Saturdays of the period by hand.

To move forward by working days

This page counts between two known dates. To find the date that lies a number of working days after a start date, use add days to a date and choose "Working days".

How many working days are there in a month?

With a five-day week, 20 to 23 before holidays. With a six-day week, 24 to 27.

How many working days are there in 2027?

There are 261 weekdays (Monday to Friday) in 2027. Taking out the gazetted holidays that fall on weekdays gives the figure this page shows for 1 January to 31 December 2027.

Are Saturdays working days?

It depends on the employer. Central Government offices work five days, many private offices five or five and a half, and most shops and factories six.
