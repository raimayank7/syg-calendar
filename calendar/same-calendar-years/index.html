Which years have the same calendar?

Type a year to see which earlier and later years share its calendar, weekday for weekday.

Why calendars repeat

A calendar for a year is fixed by just two facts: the weekday of 1 January and whether the year is a leap year. There are seven possible weekdays and two kinds of year, so only 14 different calendars exist. Every year uses one of the 14, and any two years that agree on both facts have identical calendars from the first page to the last.

The pattern

An ordinary year is 52 weeks and one day long, so the next year starts one weekday later. A leap year is 52 weeks and two days, so the year after it starts two weekdays later. Within any stretch that has a leap year every four years, the whole arrangement repeats every 28 years (7 weekdays × the 4-year leap cycle). Inside those 28 years:

  • an ordinary year's calendar comes round again after 6, then 11, then 11 years (in some order, depending on where the year sits between leap years);
  • a leap year's calendar repeats only once, 28 years later.

For example, 2027 starts on a Friday and is not a leap year. The same calendar served in 2010 and 2021, and will serve again in 2038 and 2049. The leap year 2028 matches 2000 and will next match 2056.

The 28-year pattern is broken by century years that are not leap years, such as 1900 and 2100. Around those years the gaps change; a calendar from 2096 will not be right again in 2124. The tool handles this correctly because it tests each year separately.

What matches and what does not

Matches: every date's weekday, the number of days in each month, and the ISO week numbers.

Does not match:

  • Festivals fixed by the Moon: Diwali, Holi, Eid, Easter, Guru Nanak's Birthday and many others fall on different dates each year.
  • Public holiday lists, which governments announce afresh for each year.
  • The printed year, of course.
  • Notes in an old calendar about eclipses, tides, sunrise times and moon phases.

So an old calendar is good for dates and weekdays only. For the holidays of the year in front of you, see our holiday pages.

Practical uses

  • Reusing a favourite calendar or diary: an unused 2021 planner or a picture calendar from 2010 works date for date in 2027.
  • Checking a document: if a paper dated in a certain year names a weekday for a date, the two facts must agree with that year's calendar.
  • Teaching: the 14 calendars are a pleasing piece of arithmetic for a classroom, and a perpetual calendar can be built from them.
  • Planning far ahead: once you know 2038 repeats 2027, the weekdays of every date in 2038 are known.

When can I reuse a 2027 calendar?

In 2038, 2049 and 2055. Earlier years with the same calendar were 2010 and 2021.

How often does a calendar repeat?

Ordinary years repeat after 6 or 11 years. Leap years repeat after 28 years. Century years that are not leap years disturb the pattern.

Do the festivals match too?

No. Only the weekdays of the dates match. Festival dates and holiday lists are different every year.
