Date formats: DD/MM/YYYY, MM/DD/YYYY and ISO 8601

The same string of digits can mean two different days, depending on where the reader lives.

The problem

Write 03/04/2027 and an Indian reader sees 3 April 2027. An American reader sees March 4, 2027. Both are sure, and they are a month apart. Any date whose day is 12 or less can be misread this way, which covers 132 days of every year. Missed meetings, wrong expiry dates and spoiled spreadsheets all come from this one habit.

The three orders

OrderExample for 3 April 2027Used in
Day, month, year (DMY)03/04/2027, 3-4-2027, 3 April 2027India, the United Kingdom, most of Europe, Africa, Latin America, Australia
Month, day, year (MDY)04/03/2027, April 3, 2027The United States, and alongside other forms in Canada and the Philippines
Year, month, day (YMD)2027-04-03China, Japan, Korea, Hungary, Sweden; the international standard

India uses day, month, year in everyday writing, on forms and in law. The usual separators are the slash, the hyphen and the full stop: 03/04/2027, 03-04-2027, 03.04.2027.

The international standard: ISO 8601

The standard ISO 8601 writes dates as YYYY-MM-DD: four digits of year, two of month, two of day, with hyphens. 3 April 2027 is 2027-04-03.

It has three strengths.

  1. No one can misread it. No country writes year-day-month, so a year at the front settles the order.
  2. It sorts correctly. File names and lists in this form fall into date order when sorted as plain text. 2027-04-03 report comes before 2027-11-20 report. With 03-04-2027 they do not.
  3. Computers agree on it. It is the form used in databases, programming and data exchange, and the form the date boxes on this site use behind the scenes.

The same standard defines week numbers (2027-W13) and the 24-hour time format (2027-04-03T14:30).

How to write a date nobody can misread

  • Spell the month: 3 April 2027 or 3 Apr 2027. This is the safest choice for letters, notices, invitations and anything read by people in more than one country.
  • Or use ISO: 2027-04-03, best for file names, spreadsheets and technical work.
  • Always write four digits for the year. "03/04/27" is three two-digit numbers in an unknown order.
  • On forms with boxes, follow the labels printed under them (DD MM YYYY).
  • On cheques in India, banks expect the date in the DDMMYYYY boxes, as digits.

Dates in spreadsheets

Spreadsheet programs read typed dates according to the computer's region setting. A file made on a computer set to the United States will read 03/04/2027 as March 4. Two signs of trouble: dates that line up on the left like text, and dates where only days above 12 look "wrong".

  • Type dates as 2027-04-03 or 3-Apr-2027. Both are read correctly under any region setting.
  • When opening a CSV file from another country, import it and state the date order, instead of double-clicking it.
  • Check the region setting if dates swap day and month: on Windows it is under Settings, Time & language, Language & region, Regional format.

Reading dates on products

Packaged goods and medicines carry dates in several styles: "EXP 04/2027" (month and year), "Best before 03 APR 27", or a string such as "270403". Month-and-year labels mean the end of that month unless the label says otherwise. When a date on imported goods could be read two ways, look for a spelled month elsewhere on the pack, or check whether one of the numbers is above 12.

Speaking dates

In Indian and British English people say "the third of April" or "third April". In American English it is "April third". Both are understood everywhere; it is only the all-digit written form that causes trouble.

What date format does India use?

Day, month, year: DD/MM/YYYY. 15/08/2027 is 15 August 2027.

What is the ISO date format?

YYYY-MM-DD, for example 2027-08-15. It is defined by the international standard ISO 8601.

Why does America write the month first?

It follows the spoken form "April 3, 2027", which was also common in Britain in the past. Britain later moved to day-first; the United States kept the older habit.
