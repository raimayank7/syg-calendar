Add or subtract days, weeks, months or years

Choose a starting date, then add or subtract days, weeks, months, years or working days.

How to use it

  1. The start date is today unless you change it.
  2. Choose Add or Subtract, type a number and pick the unit.
  3. The answer shows the date, its weekday, the week number and the day of the year. Tap Copy the date to paste it elsewhere.

Common questions this answers

  • What date is 30, 45, 60, 90 or 180 days from today?
  • When does a 15-day return window, a 30-day notice or a 90-day visa end?
  • What was the date 100 days ago, or 6 months before a given day?
  • What date is 10 working days after an order was placed?
  • When is a baby 100 days old, or a couple married 1,000 days?

Days and months do not behave alike

Adding days or weeks is exact. Ninety days is ninety sunrises, whatever months lie between.

Adding months or years follows the calendar, and months are of unequal length. The page keeps the same day of the month where it can. Where the target month is too short, it stops at that month's last day:

StartAddResult
15 January1 month15 February
31 January1 month28 February (29 in a leap year)
31 March1 month30 April
29 February 20281 year28 February 2029

Because of this, "3 months" and "90 days" are different lengths: three months from 1 February is 1 May (89 days in a common year), while 90 days from 1 February is 2 May. Contracts and rules usually say which they mean. If yours says "months", use months.

Working days

Choosing Working days steps forward or back one day at a time and skips every Saturday and Sunday. The start date itself is not counted: one working day after a Friday is the following Monday. Public holidays are not skipped here, because they differ from state to state and from office to office. For a count between two dates with the Central Government holidays taken out, use the working days calculator.

Does the first day count?

The page does not count the start date. Adding 1 day to Monday gives Tuesday. Many rules for limitation periods, notice and delivery work the same way: the day of the event is left out and the period begins the next day. Some rules do count the first day; then add one day fewer. When money or a legal right depends on the date, read the wording of the rule or ask the office concerned.

Tips

  • For "the same weekday so many weeks later", choose weeks. Seven-day steps always land on the same weekday.
  • To find a date in the past, choose Subtract, or use the days between dates page if you know both dates.
  • The result's week number follows the international (ISO) rule explained on the week number page.
What is 90 days from today?

Leave the start date as today, type 90 and choose Days. The answer appears at once and changes every day.

Is 6 months the same as 180 days?

No. Six calendar months are between 181 and 184 days, depending on which months they are.

Why does adding 1 month to 31 January give 28 February?

February has no 31st, so the result stays on February's last day. The page never rolls over into March.
