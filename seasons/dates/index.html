Reading the table

All times are UTC. For a local date, add your time zone offset: 5 hours 30 minutes for India. Across 41 years the March equinox falls between 19 March and 20 March, the June solstice between 20 June and 21 June, the September equinox between 22 September and 23 September and the December solstice between 21 December and 22 December. The moments slide a few hours later each year and jump back in a leap year.
