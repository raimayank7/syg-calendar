What this site shows

The equinoxes and solstices are the four moments in the year when the Sun reaches the points that astronomers use to start the seasons. Choose a year to see the moments in your own time zone, or open a year page for a table of eight time zones and the length of each season. The moments are calculated and checked against the U.S. Naval Observatory.

Using the dates

The dates are useful for school projects, festivals that follow the seasons, gardening and for working out how many daylight hours there will be. Keep in mind that the date in your place can differ by a day from the date in UTC.
