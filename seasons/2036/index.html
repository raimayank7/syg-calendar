The year 2036 in seasons

In 2036 the Sun crosses the equator northward at the March equinox, on 20 Mar 2036, 01:03 UTC. Summer in the northern hemisphere starts at the June solstice on 20 Jun 2036, 18:32 UTC, autumn at the September equinox on 22 Sep 2036, 10:23 UTC and winter at the December solstice on 21 Dec 2036, 07:12 UTC. In the southern hemisphere the names of the seasons swap.

Length of the seasons

The astronomical seasons are not equal in length. Northern spring 2036 lasts 92 days 17 hours, summer 93 days 15 hours and autumn 89 days 20 hours, and winter that began in December lasts 88 days 23 hours. Summer is the longest because the Earth is near its farthest point from the Sun in July and moves more slowly along its orbit.

Dates in local time

The date of an equinox or solstice depends on your time zone. The March equinox of 2036 falls on 20 March in UTC and on 20 March in Indian time; the same moment is 19 March in the US Pacific zone. That is why almanacs of different countries print different dates for the same event.
