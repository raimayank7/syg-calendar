January 9: what happened on this day

What happened on 9 January: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 12 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2015: Contaminated beer served at a funeral in Tete Province, Mozambique, killed 75 people and made at least 230 others ill. Mozambique funeral beer poisoning
  • 2015: A hostage situation, related to the Charlie Hebdo shooting, occurred at a Jewish market in Vincennes. Hypercacher kosher supermarket siege
  • 2011: In poor weather conditions, Iran Air Flight 277 (aircraft pictured) crashed near Urmia Airport, Iran, killing 78 of the 105 people on board. Iran Air Flight 277
  • 1992: Radio astronomers Aleksander Wolszczan and Dale Frail announced the discovery of two pulsar planets orbiting PSR B1257+12 (depicted), the first confirmed detection of exoplanets. Aleksander Wolszczan
  • 1991: Representatives from the United States and Iraq met at the Geneva Peace Conference to find a peaceful resolution to the Iraqi invasion of Kuwait. Geneva Peace Conference (1991)
  • 1981: U.S. representative Raymond Lederer was convicted of bribery and conspiracy for his role in the Abscam scandal, but continued to serve his term for three more months. Raymond Lederer
  • 1975: The Great Storm spawned the first of 45 tornadoes over a three-day period in the Southeastern United States. Great Storm of 1975
  • 1972: The Los Angeles Lakers of the National Basketball Association lost to the Milwaukee Bucks, ending a 33-game winning streak, the longest in major American professional team sports. Los Angeles Lakers
  • 1972: Seawise University, formerly RMS Queen Elizabeth (pictured), an ocean liner that sailed the Atlantic for Cunard Line, caught fire in Victoria Harbour, Hong Kong. RMS Queen Elizabeth
  • 1917: First World War: Troops of the British Empire defeated Ottoman forces at the Battle of Rafa on the Sinai–Palestine border. World War I
  • 1917: World War I: A meeting of the German Crown Council decided upon the resumption of unrestricted submarine warfare. World War I
  • 1857: An earthquake registering 7.9 Mw ruptured part of the San Andreas Fault in central and southern California. 1857 Fort Tejon earthquake

More events

  • 2021: Sriwijaya Air Flight 182 crashes north of Jakarta, Indonesia, killing all 62 people on board. Sriwijaya Air Flight 182
  • 2017: Mont-Libre Agile Learning Centre, the province of Quebec's first alternative schooling democratic learning centre to support homeschooled youth, opens in the city of Montreal. Mont-Libre Agile Learning Centre
  • 2015: A mass poisoning at a funeral in Mozambique involving beer that was contaminated with Burkholderia gladioli leaves 75 dead and over 230 people ill. Mozambique funeral beer poisoning
  • 2011: Iran Air Flight 277 crashes near Urmia in the northwest of the country, in icy conditions, killing 78 people. Iran Air Flight 277
  • 2005: Mahmoud Abbas wins the election to succeed Yasser Arafat as President of the Palestinian National Authority, replacing interim president Rawhi Fattouh. Mahmoud Abbas
  • 2004: An inflatable boat carrying illegal Albanian emigrants stalls near the Karaburun Peninsula en route to Brindisi, Italy; exposure to the elements kills 28. This is the second deadliest marine disaster in Albanian history. Inflatable boat
  • 1997: Comair Flight 3272 crashes in Raisinville Township in Monroe County, Michigan, killing 29 people. Comair Flight 3272
  • 1992: The Assembly of the Serb People in Bosnia and Herzegovina proclaims the creation of Republika Srpska, a new state within Yugoslavia. National Assembly (Republika Srpska)
  • 1991: Representatives from the United States and Iraq meet at the Geneva Peace Conference to try to find a peaceful resolution to the Iraqi invasion of Kuwait. Iraq
  • 1962: Apollo program: NASA announces plans to build the C-5 rocket launch vehicle, then known as the "Advanced Saturn", to carry human beings to the Moon. Apollo program
  • 1960: President of Egypt Gamal Abdel Nasser opens construction on the Aswan Dam by detonating ten tons of dynamite to demolish twenty tons of granite on the east bank of the Nile. President of Egypt
  • 1957: British Prime Minister Sir Anthony Eden resigns from office following his failure to retake the Suez Canal from Egyptian sovereignty. Prime Minister of the United Kingdom
  • 1941: World War II: First flight of the Avro Lancaster. World War II
  • 1927: A fire at the Laurier Palace movie theatre in Montreal, Quebec, Canada, kills 78 children. Laurier Palace Theatre fire
  • 1923: Lithuanian residents of the Memel Territory rebel against the League of Nations' decision to leave the area as a mandated region under French control. Lithuanians
  • 1920: Ukrainian War of Independence: The All-Ukrainian Central Executive Committee outlaws the Makhnovshchina by decree, igniting the Bolshevik–Makhnovist conflict. Ukrainian War of Independence
  • 1917: World War I: The Battle of Rafa is fought near the Egyptian border with Palestine. Battle of Rafa
  • 1914: The Phi Beta Sigma fraternity is founded by African-American students at Howard University in Washington D.C., United States. Phi Beta Sigma
  • 1903: Hallam Tennyson, 2nd Baron Tennyson, son of the poet Alfred Tennyson, becomes the second Governor-General of Australia. Hallam Tennyson, 2nd Baron Tennyson
  • 1861: American Civil War: "Star of the West" incident occurs near Charleston, South Carolina. American Civil War
  • 1858: British forces finally defeat Rajab Ali Khan of Chittagong. Revolt of Rajab Ali
  • 1839: The French Academy of Sciences announces the Daguerreotype photography process. French Academy of Sciences
  • 1816: Humphry Davy tests his safety lamp for miners at Hebburn Colliery. Humphry Davy
  • 1799: British Prime Minister William Pitt the Younger introduces an income tax of two shillings to the pound to raise funds for Great Britain's war effort in the Napoleonic Wars. Prime Minister of the United Kingdom
  • 1792: Treaty of Jassy between Russian and Ottoman Empire is signed, ending the Russo-Turkish War of 1787–92. Treaty of Jassy
  • 1787: The nationally known image of the Black Nazarene in the Philippines is transferred from what is now Rizal Park to its present shrine in the minor basilica of Quiapo Church. This is annually commemorated through its Traslación (solemn transfer) in the streets of Manila and is attended by millions of devotees. Black Nazarene
  • 1693: Sicily earthquake: The first of two earthquakes destroys parts of Sicily and Malta. After the second quake on 11 January, the death toll is estimated at between 60,000 and 100,000 people. 1693 Sicily earthquake
  • 1349: The Jewish population of Basel, believed by the residents to be the cause of the ongoing Black Death, is rounded up and incinerated. Basel
  • 1038: An earthquake in Dingxiang, China kills an estimated 32,300. 1038 Dingxiang earthquake
  • 475: Verina, the Eastern Roman dowager Empress, instigates a riot in Constantinople and persuades emperor Zeno, her son-in-law, to flee. The Byzantine senate, however, acclaims Basiliscus as emperor and not her lover Patricius. Verina

About the date itself

January 9 is the 9th day of an ordinary year, with 356 days left after it (357 in a leap year, where it is the 9th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Saturday, 2028: Sunday, 2029: Tuesday, 2030: Wednesday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 9 January 2027 in 2027 is 1 Sha'ban 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 1.
