February 16: what happened on this day

What happened on 16 February: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 9 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2013: At least 91 people were killed and 190 others injured after a bomb hidden in a water tank exploded at a market in Hazara Town, Pakistan. February 2013 Quetta bombing
  • 1998: While on approach to Chiang Kai-shek International Airport, China Airlines Flight 676 crashed due to an over-reliance of the autopilot, which resulted in the deaths of 202 people. Taoyuan International Airport
  • 1996: Eleven people died in a train collision in Silver Spring, Maryland, leading to the creation of comprehensive U.S. federal rules for the design of passenger cars. 1996 Maryland train collision
  • 1983: The Ash Wednesday bushfires burned over half a million acres (over 2,000 km2) each in both South Australia and Victoria, killing 75 people and injuring 2,676 others. Ash Wednesday bushfires
  • 1961: The DuSable Museum, the first museum dedicated to the study and conservation of African American history, culture, and art, was chartered. DuSable Black History Museum
  • 1959: Fidel Castro was sworn in as Prime Minister of Cuba, beginning his decades-long rule over the country. Fidel Castro
  • 1922: A landslide in Byblos revealed a sarcophagus in an underground tomb that was later discovered to be part of a large Bronze Age necropolis. Byblos
  • 1918: The Council of Lithuania signed the Act of Independence (pictured), proclaiming the restoration of an independent Lithuania. Council of Lithuania
  • 1900: The Southern Cross Expedition led by Carsten Borchgrevink (pictured) achieved a new Farthest South of 78° 50'S, making the first landing at the Great Ice Barrier. Southern Cross Expedition
  • 1859: The French government passed a law setting the musical note A4 to a frequency of 435 hertz, in the first attempt to standardize concert pitch. Musical note
  • 1804: First Barbary War: Lieutenant Stephen Decatur led a U.S. Navy raid to destroy the captured USS Philadelphia in the harbor of Tripoli (depicted), denying her use to the Barbary States. First Barbary War
  • 1270: Livonian Crusade: In the Battle of Karuse, the Grand Duchy of Lithuania achieved a decisive victory over the Livonian Order on the frozen surface of the Baltic Sea. Livonian Crusade

About the date itself

February 16 is the 47th day of an ordinary year, with 318 days left after it (319 in a leap year, where it is the 47th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Tuesday, 2028: Wednesday, 2029: Friday, 2030: Saturday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 16 February 2027 in 2027 is 9 Ramadan 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 7.
