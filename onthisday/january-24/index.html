January 24: what happened on this day

What happened on 24 January: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 11 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 1990: Japan launched the Hiten spacecraft (pictured), the first lunar probe launched by a country other than the Soviet Union or the United States. Hiten (spacecraft)
  • 1987: About 20,000 protestors marched in a civil rights demonstration in Forsyth County, Georgia, United States. 1987 Forsyth County protests
  • 1978: The Soviet nuclear-powered satellite Kosmos 954 burned up during atmospheric reentry, scattering radioactive debris across Canada's Northwest Territories. Nuclear power
  • 1977: Spanish transition to democracy: Neo-fascists attacked an office in Madrid, killing five people and injuring four others. Spanish transition to democracy
  • 1972: Japanese soldier Shoichi Yokoi was found in the jungles of Guam, where he had been hiding since the end of World War II. Shoichi Yokoi
  • 1968: Vietnam War: The 1st Australian Task Force launched Operation Coburg against the North Vietnamese army and the Viet Cong. Vietnam War
  • 1913: Greek military aviators Michael Moutoussis and Aristeidis Moraitinis performed the first naval air mission in history, with a Farman MF.7 hydroplane. Michael Moutoussis
  • 1857: The University of Calcutta was established as the first modern university in the Indian subcontinent. University of Calcutta
  • 1848: James W. Marshall discovered gold at Sutter's Mill (reconstruction pictured) in Coloma, California, leading to the California gold rush. James W. Marshall
  • 1458: The Estates unanimously proclaimed 14-year-old Matthias Corvinus King of Hungary after being persuaded to do so by his uncle Michael Szilágyi. Estates of the realm
  • 914: The Fatimid Caliphate began their first invasion of Egypt, against the Abbasids, which eventually ended in failure. Fatimid Caliphate
  • 771: Kʼakʼ Tiliw Chan Yopaat dedicated a 65-ton stele, the largest stone known to be quarried by the Maya civilization, at his city of Quiriguá. Kʼakʼ Tiliw Chan Yopaat

About the date itself

January 24 is the 24th day of an ordinary year, with 341 days left after it (342 in a leap year, where it is the 24th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Sunday, 2028: Monday, 2029: Wednesday, 2030: Thursday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 24 January 2027 in 2027 is 16 Sha'ban 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 3.
