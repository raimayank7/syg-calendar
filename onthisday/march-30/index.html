March 30: what happened on this day

What happened on 30 March: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 12 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2023: Donald Trump became the first former U.S. president to be indicted by a grand jury. Donald Trump
  • 2009: The Manawan Police Academy in Lahore, Pakistan, was attacked and held for several hours by 12 gunmen, resulting in 16 deaths and 95 injuries. Lahore
  • 2002: Queen Elizabeth The Queen Mother, widow of King George VI and mother of Queen Elizabeth II, died at Royal Lodge aged 101. Queen Elizabeth the Queen Mother
  • 1972: Vietnam War: North Vietnamese forces began the Easter Offensive in an attempt to gain as much territory and destroy as many South Vietnamese units as possible. Vietnam War
  • 1964: Jeopardy!, the popular American game show created by Merv Griffin, made its debut on the NBC television network. Jeopardy!
  • 1921: The Australian Air Corps was disbanded, to be replaced the following day by the Australian Air Force. Australian Air Corps
  • 1918: Four days of inter-ethnic clashes broke out in Baku, Azerbaijan, resulting in about 12,000 deaths. March Days
  • 1912: Sultan Abd al-Hafid signed the Treaty of Fes, making Morocco a French protectorate. Abd al-Hafid of Morocco
  • 1863: George I (pictured) became King of the Hellenes. George I of Greece
  • 1861: British chemist William Crookes published his discovery of thallium using flame spectroscopy. William Crookes
  • 1815: Joachim Murat, King of Naples, issued the Rimini Proclamation, among the earliest calls for Italian unification. Joachim Murat
  • 1730: The Author's Farce, by Henry Fielding, premiered at the Little Theatre in London. The Author's Farce

  • 1856: The Treaty of Paris is signed, ending the Crimean War. Treaty of Paris (1856)

  • 1841: The National Bank of Greece is founded in Athens. National Bank of Greece
  • 1818: Physicist Augustin Fresnel reads a memoir on optical rotation to the French Academy of Sciences, reporting that when polarized light is "depolarized" by a Fresnel rhomb, its properties are preserved in any subsequent passage through an optically-rotating crystal or liquid. Augustin-Jean Fresnel

About the date itself

March 30 is the 89th day of an ordinary year, with 276 days left after it (277 in a leap year, where it is the 90th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Tuesday, 2028: Thursday, 2029: Friday, 2030: Saturday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 30 March 2027 in 2027 is 22 Shawwal 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 13.
