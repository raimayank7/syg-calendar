January 12: what happened on this day

What happened on 12 January: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 11 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2010: Iranian physicist Massoud Ali-Mohammadi was assassinated while leaving his home for the University of Tehran, where he was a professor. Massoud Ali-Mohammadi
  • 2010: An earthquake registering 7.0 Mw struck Haiti, killing more than 100,000 people. 2010 Haiti earthquake
  • 2007: Comet McNaught (pictured) reached perihelion, becoming the brightest comet in over 40 years, with an apparent magnitude of −5.5. Comet McNaught
  • 1964: Sultan Jamshid bin Abdullah was overthrown by rebels led by John Okello, ending 200 years of Arab dominance in Zanzibar. Jamshid bin Abdullah
  • 1918: An underground explosion at a coal mine in Staffordshire, England, killed 155 men and boys. Minnie Pit Disaster
  • 1916: Oswald Boelcke and Max Immelmann became the first German aviators to be awarded the Pour le Mérite, Germany's highest military honour. Oswald Boelcke
  • 1895: The National Trust, a conservation organisation in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, was founded. National Trust
  • 1879: Anglo-Zulu War: Natal Native Contingent and British troops defeated Zulu forces in the Action at Sihayo's Kraal. Anglo-Zulu War
  • 1865: The foundation stone of Saint Fin Barre's Cathedral in Cork was laid. Saint Fin Barre's Cathedral
  • 1777: Mission Santa Clara de Asís (pictured), a Spanish mission in California that formed the basis of both the city of Santa Clara and Santa Clara University, was established by the Franciscans. Mission Santa Clara de Asís
  • 1659: The fort at Allahabad was surrendered to the forces of Mughal emperor Aurangzeb. Allahabad Fort
  • 1554: Bayinnaung, who later assembled what was probably the largest empire in the history of mainland Southeast Asia, was crowned as the king of the Burmese Toungoo dynasty. Bayinnaung

More events

  • 2020: Taal Volcano in the Philippines erupts and kills 39 people. Taal Volcano
  • 2016: Ten people are killed and 15 wounded in a bombing near the Blue Mosque in Istanbul. January 2016 Istanbul bombing
  • 2015: Government raids kill 143 Boko Haram fighters in Kolofata, Cameroon. Battle of Kolofata (2015)
  • 2010: An earthquake in Haiti occurs, killing between 220,000 and 300,000 people and destroying much of the capital Port-au-Prince. 2010 Haiti earthquake
  • 2007: Comet C/2006 P1 (McNaught), one of the brightest comets ever observed is at its zenith visible during the day. Comet McNaught
  • 2005: Deep Impact launches from Cape Canaveral on a Delta II rocket. Deep Impact (spacecraft)
  • 2004: The world's largest ocean liner, RMS Queen Mary 2, makes its maiden voyage. Ocean liner
  • 1998: Nineteen European nations agree to forbid human cloning. Human cloning
  • 1997: Space Shuttle program: Atlantis launches from the Kennedy Space Center on mission STS-81 to the Russian space station Mir, carrying astronaut Jerry M. Linenger for a four-month stay on board the station, replacing astronaut John E. Blaha. Space Shuttle Atlantis
  • 1991: Persian Gulf War: An act of the U.S. Congress authorizes the use of American military force to drive Iraq out of Kuwait. Gulf War
  • 1986: Space Shuttle program: Congressman and future NASA Administrator Bill Nelson lifts off from Kennedy Space Center aboard Columbia on mission STS-61-C as a payload specialist. Space Shuttle program
  • 1976: The United Nations Security Council votes 11–1 to allow the Palestine Liberation Organization to participate in a Security Council debate (without voting rights). United Nations Security Council
  • 1970: Biafra capitulates, ending the Nigerian Civil War. Biafra
  • 1969: The New York Jets of the American Football League defeat the Baltimore Colts of the National Football League to win Super Bowl III in what is considered to be one of the greatest upsets in sports history. New York Jets
  • 1966: Lyndon B. Johnson states that the United States should stay in South Vietnam until Communist aggression there is ended. Lyndon B. Johnson
  • 1964: Rebels in Zanzibar begin a revolt known as the Zanzibar Revolution and proclaim a republic. Zanzibar
  • 1962: Vietnam War: Operation Chopper, the first American combat mission and first American helicopter assault in the war, takes place. Vietnam War
  • 1945: World War II: The Red Army begins the Vistula–Oder Offensive. Red Army
  • 1942: World War II: United States President Franklin D. Roosevelt creates the National War Labor Board. World War II
  • 1932: Hattie Caraway becomes the first woman elected to the United States Senate. Hattie Caraway
  • 1918: The Minnie Pit Disaster coal mining accident occurs in Halmer End, Staffordshire, in which 155 men and boys die. Minnie Pit Disaster
  • 1915: The United States House of Representatives rejects a proposed constitutional amendment to require states to give women the right to vote. United States House of Representatives
  • 1911: The University of the Philippines College of Law is established. University of the Philippines College of Law
  • 1895: The National Trust is founded in the United Kingdom. National Trust
  • 1866: The Royal Aeronautical Society is formed in London. Royal Aeronautical Society
  • 1848: The Palermo rising takes place in Sicily against the Bourbon Kingdom of the Two Sicilies. Sicilian revolution of 1848
  • 1808: The organizational meeting leading to the creation of the Wernerian Natural History Society, a former Scottish learned society, is held in Edinburgh. Wernerian Natural History Society
  • 1792: Federalist Thomas Pinckney appointed first U.S. minister to Britain. Thomas Pinckney
  • 1554: Bayinnaung, who would go on to assemble the largest empire in the history of Southeast Asia, is crowned King of Burma. Bayinnaung
  • 1528: Gustav I of Sweden is crowned King of Sweden, having already reigned since his election in June 1523. Gustav Vasa

About the date itself

January 12 is the 12th day of an ordinary year, with 353 days left after it (354 in a leap year, where it is the 12th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Tuesday, 2028: Wednesday, 2029: Friday, 2030: Saturday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 12 January 2027 in 2027 is 4 Sha'ban 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 2.
