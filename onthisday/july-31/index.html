July 31: what happened on this day

What happened on 31 July: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2020: A mid-air collision in Soldotna, Alaska, United States, killed seven people, including state representative Gary Knopp. 2020 Alaska mid-air collision
  • 2012: The largest power outage in history occurred across 22 Indian states, affecting more than 620 million people, or about 9 percent of the world's population. 2012 India blackouts
  • 2006: Following intestinal surgery, Fidel Castro provisionally transferred the duties of the Cuban presidency to his brother Raúl. Fidel Castro
  • 2002: Hamas attackers detonated a bomb at the Mount Scopus campus of the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, killing nine students and injuring about 100 more. Hamas
  • 1991: Soviet Special Purpose Police Unit troops killed seven Lithuanian customs officials in Medininkai in the most serious attack of their campaign against Lithuanian border posts. OMON
  • 1975: The Troubles: In a botched paramilitary attack, three members of the popular Miami Showband and two Ulster Volunteer Force gunmen were killed in County Down, Northern Ireland. The Troubles
  • 1966: The pleasure cruiser MV Darlwyne disappeared off the coast of Cornwall with the loss of all 31 people aboard. Loss of MV Darlwyne
  • 1954: A team of Italian climbers became the first to reach the summit of K2, the world's second-highest mountain. 1954 Italian expedition to K2
  • 1924: A private senator's bill by Herbert Payne to introduce compulsory voting in Australia became law. Private member's bill
  • 1917: First World War: The Battle of Passchendaele began near Ypres, Belgium, with the Allies aiming to force German troops to withdraw from the Channel Ports. World War I
  • 1777: The Second Continental Congress passed a resolution commissioning the Marquis de Lafayette (pictured) as a major general in the American revolutionary forces. Second Continental Congress
  • 1667: The Second Anglo-Dutch War ended with the signing of the Treaty of Breda. Second Anglo-Dutch War

About the date itself

July 31 is the 212th day of an ordinary year, with 153 days left after it (154 in a leap year, where it is the 213th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Saturday, 2028: Monday, 2029: Tuesday, 2030: Wednesday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 31 July 2027 in 2027 is 27 Safar 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 30.
