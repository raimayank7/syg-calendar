April 13: what happened on this day

What happened on 13 April: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 9 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2017: War in Afghanistan: In an airstrike in Nangarhar Province, the U.S. military dropped the most powerful conventional bomb used in combat. War in Afghanistan (2001–2021)
  • 2011: The board of Rosendale, New York, voted 3–2 to approve the purchase of Joppenbergh Mountain. Rosendale (CDP), New York
  • 2009: Andrew Hussie's webcomic Homestuck debuted, and later concluded on the same day in 2016. Andrew Hussie
  • 1996: An Israel Defense Forces helicopter fired rockets at an ambulance, killing two women and four children in Mansouri, Lebanon. Israel Defense Forces
  • 1976: Forty people died in an explosion at an ammunition factory in Lapua, Finland. Lapua Cartridge Factory explosion
  • 1958: In the midst of the Cold War, American pianist Van Cliburn won the inaugural International Tchaikovsky Competition in Moscow. Cold War
  • 1953: Ian Fleming's novel Casino Royale was published as the first work to feature the British spy character James Bond. Ian Fleming
  • 1953: The CIA began Project MKUltra, an illegal, covert human research program into mind control. Central Intelligence Agency
  • 1943: The Neoclassical Jefferson Memorial (pictured) in Washington, D.C., was formally dedicated on the 200th anniversary of Thomas Jefferson's birth. Neoclassical architecture
  • 1942: Austrian soldier Anton Schmid was executed for rescuing Jews from the Ponary massacre in Vilnius. Anton Schmid
  • 1930: C. Rajagopalachari led the non-violent Vedaranyam March from Trichinopoly towards Vedaranyam. C. Rajagopalachari
  • 1777: American Revolutionary War: British and Hessian forces conducted a surprise attack against a Continental Army outpost at Bound Brook, New Jersey. American Revolutionary War

More events

  • 2025: Rory McIlroy wins the Masters Tournament, becoming just the sixth person to complete the Grand Slam in golf. Rory McIlroy
  • 2024: Six people and the perpetrator are killed and twelve others injured in a mass stabbing at Westfield Bondi Junction shopping centre in Sydney, Australia. Bondi Junction stabbings
  • 2014: Three people are killed in a shooting in Overland Park, Kansas. 2014 Overland Park shootings
  • 2013: Salam Fayyad resigns as Prime Minister of the Palestinian National Authority following an ongoing dispute with President Mahmoud Abbas. Salam Fayyad
  • 2009: A fire destroys a homeless hostel and kills at least 22 people in Kamień Pomorski, Poland. Kamień Pomorski homeless hostel fire
  • 2006: The United Front for Democratic Change's attack on the Chadian capital of N'Djamena is repelled by the Chadian army United Front for Democratic Change
  • 1996: Two women and four children are killed after an Israeli helicopter fires rockets at an ambulance in Mansouri, Lebanon. Mansouri attack
  • 1976: The United States Treasury Department reintroduces the two-dollar bill as a Federal Reserve Note on Thomas Jefferson's 233rd birthday as part of the United States Bicentennial celebration. United States Department of the Treasury
  • 1975: Beirut bus massacre: A confrontation between Phalangist paramilitaries and PLO militia marks the start of the 15-year Lebanese Civil War. 1975 Beirut bus massacre
  • 1972: The Universal Postal Union decides to recognize the People's Republic of China as the only legitimate Chinese representative, effectively expelling the Republic of China administering Taiwan. Universal Postal Union
  • 1970: At 10:08 PM EST an oxygen tank aboard the Apollo 13 Service Module explodes, putting the crew in great danger and causing major damage to the Apollo command and service module (codenamed "Odyssey") while en route to the Moon. Oxygen
  • 1964: At the Academy Awards, Sidney Poitier becomes the first African-American man to win the Best Actor award for the 1963 film Lilies of the Field. 36th Academy Awards
  • 1953: CIA director Allen Dulles launches the mind-control program Project MKUltra. Central Intelligence Agency
  • 1948: In an ambush, 78 Jewish doctors, nurses and medical students from Hadassah Hospital, and a British soldier, are massacred by Arabs in Sheikh Jarrah. This event came to be known as the Hadassah medical convoy massacre. Hadassah Medical Center
  • 1945: World War II: Soviet and Bulgarian forces capture Vienna. Soviet Union
  • 1943: World War II: The discovery of mass graves of Polish prisoners of war killed by Soviet forces in the Katyń Forest Massacre is announced, causing a diplomatic rift between the Polish government-in-exile in London and the Soviet Union, which denies responsibility. World War II
  • 1941: A pact of neutrality between the USSR and Japan is signed. Soviet–Japanese Neutrality Pact
  • 1924: A.E.K., a major Greek multi-sport club, is established in Athens by Greek refugees from Constantinople. A.E.K. (sports club)
  • 1909: The 31 March Incident leads to the overthrow of Sultan Abdul Hamid II. 31 March incident
  • 1873: The Colfax massacre: More than 60 to 150 black men are murdered in Colfax, Louisiana, while surrendering to a mob of former Confederate soldiers and members of the Ku Klux Klan. Colfax massacre
  • 1865: American Civil War: Raleigh, North Carolina is occupied by Union forces. Raleigh, North Carolina
  • 1861: American Civil War: Union forces surrender Fort Sumter to Confederate forces. American Civil War
  • 1829: The Roman Catholic Relief Act 1829 gives Roman Catholics in the United Kingdom the right to vote and to sit in Parliament. Roman Catholic Relief Act 1829
  • 1777: American Revolutionary War: American forces are ambushed and defeated in the Battle of Bound Brook, New Jersey. American Revolutionary War
  • 1699: The Sikh religion is formalised as the Khalsa – the brotherhood of Warrior-Saints – by Guru Gobind Singh in northern India, in accordance with the Nanakshahi calendar. Sikhs
  • 1613: Samuel Argall, having captured Pocahontas in Passapatanzy, Virginia, sets off with her to Jamestown with the intention of exchanging her for English prisoners held by her father. Samuel Argall
  • 1455: Thirteen Years' War: the beginning of the Battle for Kneiphof. Thirteen Years' War (1454–1466)
  • 1204: Constantinople falls to the Crusaders of the Fourth Crusade, temporarily ending the Byzantine Empire. Constantinople
  • 1111: Henry V, King of Germany, is crowned Holy Roman Emperor by pope Paschal II. Henry V, Holy Roman Emperor
  • 1055: Election of Pope Victor II following the death of Pope Leo IX in the previous year. Pope Victor II

About the date itself

April 13 is the 103rd day of an ordinary year, with 262 days left after it (263 in a leap year, where it is the 104th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Tuesday, 2028: Thursday, 2029: Friday, 2030: Saturday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 13 April 2027 in 2027 is 6 Dhu al-Qa'dah 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 15.
