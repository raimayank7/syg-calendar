January 11: what happened on this day

What happened on 11 January: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2024: Several thousand North Korean migrant workers in Helong engaged in civil unrest, including a factory occupation and the taking of managers as hostages, due to unpaid wages. North Korean migrant workers
  • 2013: French special forces failed in an attempted rescue of a DGSE agent, who had been taken hostage in 2009 by al-Shabaab, in Bulo Marer, Somalia. Bulo Marer hostage rescue attempt
  • 1986: The Gateway Bridge (pictured) in Brisbane, Australia, opened as the largest prestressed-concrete, single-box bridge in the world. Sir Leo Hielscher Bridges
  • 1964: In a landmark report (cover pictured), U.S. surgeon general Luther Terry issued a warning that tobacco smoking may be hazardous to health, concluding that it has a causative role in lung cancer, chronic bronchitis, and other illnesses. Smoking and Health
  • 1946: The People's Republic of Albania was proclaimed, with Enver Hoxha as the country's de facto head of state. People's Socialist Republic of Albania
  • 1943: Italian-American journalist and trade-union activist Carlo Tresca, a leading public opponent of the Mafia infiltration of unions, was assassinated in New York City. Trade union
  • 1923: Troops from France and Belgium invaded the Ruhr to force the Weimar Republic to pay reparations in the aftermath of World War I. Occupation of the Ruhr
  • 1914: The Karluk, the flagship of the Canadian Arctic Expedition, sank after being crushed by ice. Karluk (ship)
  • 1863: American Civil War: The Battle of Arkansas Post concluded with the Union Army capturing a fort from Confederate forces near the mouth of the Arkansas River. American Civil War
  • 1820: A conflagration in Savannah, Georgia, destroyed more than 400 buildings and caused millions of dollars in damages. Great Savannah Fire of 1820
  • 1693: The most powerful earthquake recorded in Italy struck the island of Sicily, causing 60,000 deaths and prompting a period of architectural revival. 1693 Sicily earthquake
  • 1654: Arauco War: The Mapuche-Huilliche of southern Chile defeated a slave-hunting Spanish army at the Battle of Río Bueno. Arauco War

  • 1805: The Michigan Territory is created. Michigan Territory

  • 1654: Arauco War: A Spanish army is defeated by local Mapuche-Huilliches as it tries to cross Bueno River in Southern Chile. Arauco War
  • 1158: Vladislaus II, Duke of Bohemia becomes King of Bohemia. Vladislaus II, Duke and King of Bohemia

  • 630: Conquest of Mecca: Muhammad and his followers conquer the city, and the Quraysh association of clans surrenders. Conquest of Mecca

About the date itself

January 11 is the 11th day of an ordinary year, with 354 days left after it (355 in a leap year, where it is the 11th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Monday, 2028: Tuesday, 2029: Thursday, 2030: Friday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 11 January 2027 in 2027 is 3 Sha'ban 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 2.
