October 11: what happened on this day

What happened on 11 October: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2002: A bomb exploded in the Myyrmanni shopping center in Helsinki, Finland (aftermath pictured), resulting in seven deaths and 159 injuries. Myyrmanni bombing
  • 1987: Sri Lankan Civil War: The Indian Peace Keeping Force began Operation Pawan to take control of Jaffna from the Tamil Tigers and enforce their disarmament as a part of the Indo-Sri Lanka Accord. Sri Lankan civil war
  • 1984: Aboard the Space Shuttle Challenger, astronaut Kathryn D. Sullivan (pictured) became the first American woman to perform a space walk. Space Shuttle Challenger
  • 1973: Typhoon Nora, the fourth-most intense tropical cyclone on record, dissipated after killing 40 people and leaving more than a million homeless across Taiwan and the Philippines. Typhoon Nora (1973)
  • 1950: A field-sequential color system developed by Hungarian-American engineer Peter Goldmark became the first color television system to be adopted for commercial use, only for it to be abandoned a year later. Field-sequential color system
  • 1950: CBS's field-sequential color system for television was the first to be licensed for broadcast by the U.S. Federal Communications Commission. CBS
  • 1937: Former British king Edward and his wife arrived at Berlin Friedrichstraße station to begin a tour of Nazi Germany, where they were greeted with Nazi salutes and dined with high-ranking members of the state apparatus. Edward VIII
  • 1890: In Washington, D.C., the Daughters of the American Revolution was founded. Washington, D.C.
  • 1865: The Morant Bay rebellion, led by Paul Bogle and George William Gordon, began in Jamaica; after protestors burned down the courthouse, it was brutally suppressed by Governor Edward John Eyre. Morant Bay rebellion
  • 1840: Bashir Shihab II (pictured) surrendered to the Ottoman Empire and was removed as Emir of Mount Lebanon after an imperial decree by Sultan Abdülmecid I. Bashir Shihab II
  • 1797: French Revolutionary Wars: The Royal Navy captured eleven Dutch Navy ships without any losses at the Battle of Camperdown. French Revolutionary Wars
  • 1311: The peerage and clergy of the Kingdom of England published the Ordinances of 1311 to restrict King Edward II's powers. Peerage of England

  • 1649: Cromwell's New Model Army sacks Wexford, killing over 2,000 Irish Confederate troops and 1,500 civilians. Sack of Wexford
  • 1643: A Parliamentarian cavalry force defeats a Royalist cavalry force in the battle of Winceby. Roundhead

  • 1531: The five Catholic cantons defeat the Protestant canton of Zurich in the battle of Kappel. Canton of Zurich

  • 1138: A massive earthquake strikes Aleppo; it is one of the most destructive earthquakes ever. 1138 Aleppo earthquake

About the date itself

October 11 is the 284th day of an ordinary year, with 81 days left after it (82 in a leap year, where it is the 285th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Monday, 2028: Wednesday, 2029: Thursday, 2030: Friday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 11 October 2027 in 2027 is 11 Jumada al-awwal 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 41.
