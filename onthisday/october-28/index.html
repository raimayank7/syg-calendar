October 28: what happened on this day

What happened on 28 October: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2013: Members of the Turkistan Islamic Party drove a vehicle into a crowd in Tiananmen Square, Beijing, China, killing five people and injuring thirty-eight others. Turkistan Islamic Party
  • 2007: In the Argentine general election, Cristina Fernández de Kirchner became the first woman to be elected president of Argentina. 2007 Argentine general election
  • 1992: Hans-Adam II threatened to dismiss the Landtag of Liechtenstein over disagreements on the date of a referendum for the country's accession to the EEA. Hans-Adam II of Liechtenstein
  • 1965: In St. Louis, Missouri, U.S., the 630-foot (190 m)-tall steel catenary Gateway Arch was completed. St. Louis
  • 1940: World War II: Italy invaded Greece after Greek prime minister Ioannis Metaxas rejected Benito Mussolini's ultimatum demanding the cession of Greek territory. World War II
  • 1925: The funerary mask of Tutankhamun, possibly originally made for Queen Neferneferuaten, was uncovered for the first time in approximately 3,250 years. Mask of Tutankhamun
  • 1919: The U.S. Congress passed the Volstead Act over president Woodrow Wilson's veto, reinforcing Prohibition in the United States. United States Congress
  • 1886: In New York Harbor, U.S. president Grover Cleveland dedicated the Statue of Liberty (pictured), a gift from France commemorating the Declaration of Independence. New York Harbor
  • 1776: American Revolutionary War: As George Washington's Continental Army retreated northward from New York City, the British Army captured the village of White Plains. American Revolutionary War
  • 1707: The Hōei earthquake ruptured all segments of the Nankai megathrust simultaneously – the only earthquake recorded to have done so. 1707 Hōei earthquake
  • 1640: The Treaty of Ripon was agreed, ending the Second Bishops' War and forcing Charles I to summon the Long Parliament, ultimately leading to the First English Civil War. Treaty of Ripon
  • 1453: Ladislaus the Posthumous was crowned King of Bohemia, although George of Poděbrady remained in control of the government. Ladislaus the Posthumous

More events

  • 2023: The 2023 Rugby World Cup final is held at the Stade de France in Saint-Denis, France. It saw South Africa defeat New Zealand 12 to 11, claiming their fourth Webb Ellis Cup, becoming the first nation to do so. 2023 Rugby World Cup final
  • 2014: A rocket carrying NASA's Cygnus CRS Orb-3 resupply mission to the International Space Station explodes seconds after taking off from the Mid-Atlantic Regional Spaceport in Wallops Island, Virginia. Cygnus Orb-3
  • 2009: The 28 October 2009 Peshawar bombing kills 117 and wounds 213. 28 October 2009 Peshawar bombing
  • 2009: US President Barack Obama signs the Matthew Shepard and James Byrd Jr. Hate Crimes Prevention Act. Barack Obama
  • 2006: A funeral service takes place at the Bykivnia graves for Ukrainians who were killed by the Soviet secret police. Bykivnia graves
  • 1990: Georgia holds its only free election under Soviet rule. 1990 Georgian Supreme Soviet election
  • 1982: The Spanish general election begins fourteen years of rule by the Spanish Socialist Workers' Party. 1982 Spanish general election
  • 1965: Pope Paul VI promulgates Nostra aetate, by which the Roman Catholic Church officially recognizes the legitimacy of non-Christian faiths. Pope Paul VI
  • 1958: John XXIII is elected Pope. Pope John XXIII
  • 1954: Aeroflot Flight 136 crashes near Krasnoyarsk, killing 19. Krasnoyarsk
  • 1948: Paul Hermann Müller is awarded the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for his discovery of the insecticidal properties of DDT. Paul Hermann Müller
  • 1942: The Alaska Highway first connects Alaska to the North American railway network at Dawson Creek in Canada. Alaska Highway
  • 1928: Indonesia Raya, now the national anthem of Indonesia, is first played during the Second Indonesian Youth Congress. Indonesia Raya
  • 1919: The U.S. Congress passes the Volstead Act over President Woodrow Wilson's veto, paving the way for Prohibition to begin the following January. Volstead Act
  • 1918: World War I: Czech politicians peacefully take over the city of Prague, thus establishing the First Czechoslovak Republic. First Czechoslovak Republic
  • 1891: The Mino–Owari earthquake, the largest inland earthquake in Japan's history, occurs. 1891 Mino–Owari earthquake
  • 1864: American Civil War: In East Tennessee, Union forces under General Alvan C. Gillem rout Confederate troops under General John C. Vaughn during the Battle of Morristown. American Civil War
  • 1834: The Pinjarra massacre occurs in the Swan River Colony. An estimated 30 Noongar people are killed by British colonists. Pinjarra massacre
  • 1746: The 1746 Lima–Callao earthquake causes up to 6,000 deaths in Peru, the deadliest in its history up to that point. 1746 Lima–Callao earthquake
  • 1707: The 1707 Hōei earthquake causes more than 5,000 deaths in Japan. 1707 Hōei earthquake
  • 1640: The Treaty of Ripon is signed, ending the hostilities of the Second Bishops' War. Treaty of Ripon
  • 1628: French Wars of Religion: The Siege of La Rochelle ends with the surrender of the Huguenots after fourteen months. French Wars of Religion
  • 1538: The Universidad Santo Tomás de Aquino is founded in what is now the Dominican Republic. Universidad Santo Tomás de Aquino
  • 1520: Ferdinand Magellan reaches the Pacific Ocean. Ferdinand Magellan
  • 1492: Christopher Columbus lands in Cuba on his first voyage to the New World, surmising that it is Japan. Christopher Columbus
  • 1449: Christian I is crowned king of Denmark. Christian I of Denmark
  • 1344: The lower town of Smyrna is captured by Latin Christians in response to Aydınid piracy during the Smyrniote crusades. Aydinids
  • 969: The Byzantine Empire recovers Antioch from Arab rule. Byzantine Empire
  • 306: Maxentius is proclaimed Roman emperor. Maxentius

About the date itself

October 28 is the 301st day of an ordinary year, with 64 days left after it (65 in a leap year, where it is the 302nd day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Thursday, 2028: Saturday, 2029: Sunday, 2030: Monday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 28 October 2027 in 2027 is 28 Jumada al-awwal 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 43.
