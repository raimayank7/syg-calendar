October 27: what happened on this day

What happened on 27 October: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2019: The Mocking of Christ (pictured) by Cimabue sold at auction in France for €19.5 million, a record for a pre-1500 artwork. The Mocking of Christ (Cimabue)
  • 2004: The Boston Red Sox completed a sweep of the St. Louis Cardinals to win the 2004 World Series, breaking the so-called "Curse of the Bambino". Boston Red Sox
  • 1999: Armed men led by Nairi Hunanyan carried out a mass shooting at the Armenian parliament, killing Prime Minister Vazgen Sargsyan, National Assembly speaker Karen Demirchyan, and six others. Nairi Hunanyan
  • 1992: U.S. Navy Petty Officer Allen R. Schindler Jr. was killed in Sasebo, Japan, for being gay, which led to the U.S. Armed Forces' "don't ask, don't tell" policy. Petty officer third class
  • 1980: The Chun Doo-hwan government arrested 153 people related to the Jogye Order. Chun Doo-hwan
  • 1964: Actor Ronald Reagan delivered a speech on behalf of Republican presidential candidate Barry Goldwater, launching his own political career, which culminated in him serving two full terms as U.S. president. Ronald Reagan
  • 1946: Inter-religious riots in which Hindu mobs targeted Muslim families began in the Indian state of Bihar, resulting in thousands of deaths. 1946 Bihar riots
  • 1942: The Empire of Japan achieved a pyrrhic victory against the United States at the Battle of the Santa Cruz Islands. Empire of Japan
  • 1913: A rail workers' strike in Chile escalates into a general strike, organized by the Regional Workers' Federation of Chile. General strike
  • 1904: The first underground segment of the New York City Subway opened, connecting New York City Hall (station pictured) with Harlem. Early history of the IRT subway
  • 1726: J. S. Bach led the first performance of Ich will den Kreuzstab gerne tragen, BWV 56, one of few works he called a cantata. Johann Sebastian Bach
  • 1726: In Leipzig, J. S. Bach led the first performance of Ich will den Kreuzstab gerne tragen, one of the few works that he called a cantata. Leipzig

About the date itself

October 27 is the 300th day of an ordinary year, with 65 days left after it (66 in a leap year, where it is the 301st day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Wednesday, 2028: Friday, 2029: Saturday, 2030: Sunday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 27 October 2027 in 2027 is 27 Jumada al-awwal 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 43.
