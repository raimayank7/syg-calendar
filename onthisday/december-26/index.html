December 26: what happened on this day

What happened on 26 December: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2025: Israel recognises Somaliland as an independent state, becoming the first country to do so. Israel–Somaliland relations
  • 2015: A violent tornado moves through several suburbs of Dallas, Texas, killing ten and injuring almost 500 others. It was the deadliest tornado to ever hit Texas during the month of December. 2015 Garland–Rowlett tornado
  • 2006: Two earthquakes off the southwest coast of Taiwan damaged submarine communications cables, disrupting Internet services in Asia and affecting financial transactions. 2006 Hengchun earthquakes
  • 1996: Six-year-old American beauty queen JonBenét Ramsey was found beaten and strangled in the basement of her family's home in Boulder, Colorado. Killing of JonBenét Ramsey
  • 1946: American gangster Bugsy Siegel opened The Flamingo Hotel & Casino in Las Vegas, the oldest casino still in operation on the Las Vegas Strip. Bugsy Siegel
  • 1943: World War II: In the Battle of Cape Gloucester, American and Australian forces bombarded Japanese positions on New Britain in the Territory of New Guinea while U.S. Marines invaded from two sides of the island. World War II
  • 1919: American baseball player Babe Ruth (pictured) was sold by the Boston Red Sox to their rivals, the New York Yankees, beginning the 84-year-long "Curse of the Bambino". Babe Ruth
  • 1871: Thespis, the first comic opera by Gilbert and Sullivan, premiered at the Gaiety Theatre in London. Thespis (opera)
  • 1825: Imperial Russian Army officers led about 3,000 soldiers in protest against Nicholas I's assumption of the throne after his elder brother Konstantin removed himself from the line of succession. Imperial Russian Army
  • 1811: Seventy-two people died when a theater in Richmond, Virginia, was destroyed by fire, becoming the worst urban disaster in American history at the time. Richmond, Virginia
  • 1776: American Revolutionary War: George Washington led a Continental Army column across the Delaware River to launch a surprise attack against Hessian forces at the Battle of Trenton (painting shown). American Revolutionary War
  • 1709: The opera Agrippina, described as George Frideric Handel's first masterpiece, premiered in Venice. Agrippina (opera)

More events

  • 2025: Israel recognises Somaliland as an independent state, becoming the first country to do so. Israel
  • 2021: Three people are killed when a 14-year-old opens fire on civilians at a Texaco convenience store in Garland, Texas. 2021 Garland shooting
  • 2013: An Irkut-Avia Antonov An-12 crashes on approach to Irkutsk Northwest Airport, killing all nine people on board. Yakovlev Corporation
  • 2006: Two earthquakes in Hengchun, Taiwan measuring 7.0 and 6.9 on the moment magnitude scale kill two and disrupt telecommunications across Asia. 2006 Hengchun earthquakes
  • 2004: The 9.1–9.3 Mw Indian Ocean earthquake shakes northern Sumatra with a maximum Mercalli intensity of IX (Violent). One of the largest observed tsunamis, it affected coastal and partially mainland areas of Thailand, India, Sri Lanka, the Maldives, Malaysia, Myanmar, Bangladesh, and Indonesia; death toll is estimated at 227,898. 2004 Indian Ocean earthquake and tsunami
  • 2003: The 6.6 Mw Bam earthquake shakes southeastern Iran with a maximum Mercalli intensity of IX (Violent), leaving more than 26,000 dead and 30,000 injured. 2003 Bam earthquake
  • 1998: Iraq announces its intention to fire upon U.S. and British warplanes that patrol the northern and southern no-fly zones. Ba'athist Iraq
  • 1993: Kuban Airlines Flight 5719 crashes during a go-around at Gyumri Shirak International Airport, killing 35 of the 36 people on board. Kuban Airlines Flight 5719
  • 1991: The Supreme Soviet of the Soviet Union meets and formally dissolves the Soviet Union, ending the Cold War. Supreme Soviet of the Soviet Union
  • 1980: Witnesses report the first of several sightings of unexplained lights near RAF Woodbridge, in Rendlesham Forest, Suffolk, England, United Kingdom, an incident called "Britain's Roswell". Rendlesham Forest incident
  • 1975: Tu-144, the world's first commercial supersonic aircraft, surpassing Mach 2, goes into service. Tupolev Tu-144
  • 1968: The Communist Party of the Philippines is established by Jose Maria Sison, breaking away from the Partido Komunista ng Pilipinas-1930. Communist Party of the Philippines
  • 1968: Pan Am Flight 799 crashes during takeoff from Elmendorf Air Force Base, killing three. Pan Am Flight 799
  • 1963: The Beatles' "I Want to Hold Your Hand" and "I Saw Her Standing There" are released in the United States, marking the beginning of Beatlemania on an international level. The Beatles
  • 1948: The last Soviet troops withdraw from North Korea. Soviet Union
  • 1943: World War II: German warship Scharnhorst is sunk off Norway's North Cape after a battle against major Royal Navy forces. World War II
  • 1941: U.S. President Franklin D. Roosevelt signs a bill establishing the fourth Thursday in November as Thanksgiving Day in the United States. Franklin D. Roosevelt
  • 1926: World premiere of Sibelius's tone poem Tapiola. Jean Sibelius
  • 1898: Marie and Pierre Curie announce the isolation of radium. Marie Curie
  • 1871: Thespis, the first Gilbert and Sullivan collaboration, debuts. Thespis (opera)
  • 1862: Dakota War of 1862: The largest mass-hanging in U.S. history takes place in Mankato, Minnesota, where 38 Native American prisoners are hanged. Dakota War of 1862
  • 1860: First Rules derby is held between Sheffield F.C. and Hallam F.C., the oldest football fixture in the world. Rules derby
  • 1825: Advocates of liberalism in Russia rise up against Czar Nicholas I in the Decembrist revolt, but are later suppressed. Liberalism
  • 1811: A theater fire in Richmond, Virginia kills 72 people, including the Governor of Virginia George William Smith and the president of the First National Bank of Virginia Abraham B. Venable. Richmond Theatre fire
  • 1805: Austria and France sign the Treaty of Pressburg. Peace of Pressburg (1805)
  • 1793: Second Battle of Wissembourg: France defeats Austria. Second Battle of Wissembourg
  • 1776: American Revolutionary War: In the Battle of Trenton, the Continental Army under General George Washington executes a successful surprise attack and defeats a garrison of Hessian forces serving Great Britain. American Revolutionary War
  • 1723: Bach led the first performance of Darzu ist erschienen der Sohn Gottes, BWV 40, his first Christmas Cantata composed for Leipzig. Johann Sebastian Bach
  • 1704: Second Battle of Anandpur: In the Second Battle of Anandpur, Aurangzeb's two generals, Wazir Khan and Zaberdast Khan executed two children of Guru Gobind Singh, Zorawar Singh aged eight and Fateh Singh aged five, by burying them alive into a wall. Second siege of Anandpur
  • 887: Berengar I is elected as king of Italy by the lords of Lombardy. He is crowned with the Iron Crown of Lombardy at Pavia. Berengar I of Italy

About the date itself

December 26 is the 360th day of an ordinary year, with 5 days left after it (6 in a leap year, where it is the 361st day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Sunday, 2028: Tuesday, 2029: Wednesday, 2030: Thursday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 26 December 2027 in 2027 is 28 Rajab 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 51.
