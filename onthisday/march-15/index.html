March 15: what happened on this day

What happened on 15 March: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 1990: Iraqi authorities hanged Iranian freelance reporter Farzad Bazoft on charges of spying for Israel. Farzad Bazoft
  • 1974: Transvestism and Transsexualism in Modern Society, the UK's first trans-rights conference, opened with an evening reception in Leeds. Transvestism and Transsexualism in Modern Society
  • 1956: The musical My Fair Lady, based on George Bernard Shaw's play Pygmalion, debuted at the Mark Hellinger Theatre in New York City. My Fair Lady
  • 1943: World War II: German forces recaptured Kharkov after four days of house-to-house fighting against Soviet troops, ending the month-long Third Battle of Kharkov. World War II
  • 1931: Sing-Song Girl Red Peony (booklet pictured), one of the first sound films produced in China, was released in Shanghai. Sing-Song Girl Red Peony
  • 1921: Talaat Pasha, the main perpetrator of the Armenian genocide, was assassinated by Soghomon Tehlirian. Talaat Pasha
  • 1917: Russian Revolution: Tsar Nicholas II was forced to abdicate in the February Revolution, ending three centuries of Romanov rule. Russian Revolution
  • 1916: Six days after Pancho Villa and his cross-border raiders attacked Columbus, New Mexico, U.S. general John J. Pershing led a punitive expedition into Mexico to pursue him. Pancho Villa
  • 1875: John McCloskey, the Roman Catholic archbishop of New York, was created the first cardinal from the United States. John McCloskey
  • 1823: American sailor Benjamin Morrell erroneously reported the existence of New South Greenland, a phantom island, near Antarctica. Benjamin Morrell
  • 1311: In the Battle of Halmyros, the Catalan Company defeated the forces of Walter V, Count of Brienne, taking control of the Duchy of Athens, a Crusader state in present-day Greece. Battle of Halmyros
  • 856: Byzantine emperor Michael III overthrew the regency of his mother Theodora (pictured) to assume power for himself. Michael III

More events

  • 2022: The 2022 Sri Lankan protests begins amidst Sri Lanka's economic collapse. Aragalaya
  • 2019: Beginning of the 2019–20 Hong Kong protests. 2019–2020 Hong Kong protests
  • 2011: Beginning of the Syrian revolution. Syrian revolution

  • 1990: Mikhail Gorbachev is elected as the first and only President of the Soviet Union. Mikhail Gorbachev
  • 1978: Somalia and Ethiopia sign a truce to end the Ethio-Somali War. Somalia
  • 1974: Fifteen people are killed when Sterling Airways Flight 901, a Sud Aviation Caravelle, catches fire following a landing gear collapse at Mehrabad International Airport in Tehran, Iran. Sterling Airways Flight 901
  • 1961: At the 1961 Commonwealth Prime Ministers' Conference, South Africa announces that it will withdraw from the Commonwealth when the South African Constitution of 1961 comes into effect. 1961 Commonwealth Prime Ministers' Conference
  • 1943: World War II: Third Battle of Kharkiv: The Germans retake the city of Kharkiv from the Soviet armies. World War II
  • 1939: Carpatho-Ukraine declares itself an independent republic, but is annexed by Hungary the next day. Carpatho-Ukraine
  • 1927: The first Women's Boat Race between the University of Oxford and the University of Cambridge takes place on The Isis in Oxford. Women's Boat Race
  • 1921: Talaat Pasha, former Grand Vizir of the Ottoman Empire and chief architect of the Armenian genocide, is assassinated in Berlin by a 23-year-old Armenian, Soghomon Tehlirian. Talaat Pasha
  • 1919: The American Legion is founded. American Legion
  • 1918: Finnish Civil War: The battle of Tampere begins. Finnish Civil War
  • 1907: The first parliamentary elections of Finland (at the time the Grand Duchy of Finland) are held. 1907 Finnish parliamentary election
  • 1892: The Lobau bombing is one of the first attacks of the Ère des attentats (1892-1894). Lobau bombing
  • 1888: Start of the Anglo-Tibetan War of 1888. Sikkim expedition
  • 1875: Archbishop of New York John McCloskey is named the first cardinal in the United States. List of archbishops of New York
  • 1848: A revolution breaks out in Hungary, and the Habsburg rulers are compelled to meet the demands of the reform party. Hungarian Revolution of 1848
  • 1820: Maine is admitted as the twenty-third U.S. state. Maine
  • 1783: In an emotional speech in Newburgh, New York, George Washington asks his officers not to support the Newburgh Conspiracy. The plea is successful, and the threatened coup d'état never takes place. Newburgh, New York
  • 1672: King Charles II of England issues the Royal Declaration of Indulgence, granting limited religious freedom to all Christians. Declaration of Indulgence (1672)
  • 1564: Mughal Emperor Akbar abolishes the jizya tax on non-Muslim subjects. Akbar
  • 1412: Treaty of Lublowa: After the Peace of Thorn, Grand Master Heinrich von Plauen asks Sigismund of Hungary for economic aid. Sigismund agrees to mediate reduction to the third installment, demarcation of the Samogitian border, and other matters with a grand tournament. Hunts and lavish feasts were also organized. Sigismund invited, among others, polish king Wladyslaw Jagiello, Heinrich von Plauen and bosnian king Tvrtko II. There were people from 17 countries and languages - 40.000 nobles and 2000 knights were present from all over Europe, even England. Treaty of Lubowla
  • 1147: Afonso I of Portugal captures in a surprise attack the city of Santarem from the Almoravids. Afonso I of Portugal
  • 897: Al-Hadi ila'l-Haqq Yahya enters Sa'dah and founds the Zaydi Imamate of Yemen. Al-Hadi ila'l-Haqq Yahya
  • 856: Michael III, emperor of the Byzantine Empire, overthrows the regency of his mother, empress Theodora (wife of Theophilos) with support of the Byzantine nobility. Michael III
  • 351: Constantius Gallus is elevated as Caesar and then sent to Antioch to govern the Roman East. Constantius Gallus
  • -200: The Roman Republic under its new consuls Publius Sulpicius Galba and Gaius Aurelius Cotta declares war on Philip V of Macedon, starting the Second Macedonian War. Roman Republic

About the date itself

March 15 is the 74th day of an ordinary year, with 291 days left after it (292 in a leap year, where it is the 75th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Monday, 2028: Wednesday, 2029: Thursday, 2030: Friday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 15 March 2027 in 2027 is 7 Shawwal 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 11.
