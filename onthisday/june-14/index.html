June 14: what happened on this day

What happened on 14 June: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2026: A Bell 206 and AS350 collided in mid-air over Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, killing all 6 people aboard both aircraft including American singer Oliver Tree, Argentine YouTuber Gaspi, Argentine director and screenwriter Lucas A. Vignale, and Brazilian music producer Lucas Frota. 2026 Rio de Janeiro mid-air collision
  • 2017: A fire severely damaged Grenfell Tower in North Kensington, London, killing 72 people. Grenfell Tower fire
  • 1996: After an 81-day standoff sparked by their refusal to be evicted from their foreclosed property in Jordan, Montana, the Christian Patriot group Montana Freemen surrendered to the FBI. Foreclosure
  • 1982: Falklands War: British prime minister Margaret Thatcher announced that white flags were flying over Port Stanley, in advance of the formal surrender of Argentine forces. Falklands War
  • 1966: The Vatican formally abolished its 427-year-old list of prohibited books (title page pictured). Holy See
  • 1940: The Soviet Union issued an ultimatum to Lithuania demanding that the Red Army be allowed to enter the country and form a pro-Soviet government. Soviet ultimatum to Lithuania
  • 1934: The landmark Australian Eastern Mission concluded after a three-month diplomatic tour of East and South-East Asia. Australian Eastern Mission
  • 1900: The second of the German Naval Laws was passed, authorising the doubling in size of the Imperial German Navy. German Naval Laws
  • 1822: In a paper presented to the Royal Astronomical Society, English mathematician Charles Babbage proposed a difference engine (pictured), an automatic, mechanical calculator designed to tabulate polynomial functions. Royal Astronomical Society
  • 1777: The Second Continental Congress adopted the stars and stripes design for the flag of the United States. Second Continental Congress
  • 1740: War of Jenkins' Ear: Spanish troops stormed the British-held strategically crucial position of Fort Mose in Spanish Florida. War of Jenkins' Ear
  • 1644: First English Civil War: Prince Maurice abandoned his siege of Lyme Regis in Dorset after learning of the approach of a Parliamentarian relief force. First English Civil War

About the date itself

June 14 is the 165th day of an ordinary year, with 200 days left after it (201 in a leap year, where it is the 166th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Monday, 2028: Wednesday, 2029: Thursday, 2030: Friday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 14 June 2027 in 2027 is 9 Muharram 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 24.
