January 3: what happened on this day

What happened on 3 January: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 8 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2026: The United States launches a military operation in Venezuela, capturing president Nicolás Maduro (pictured) and his wife. United States
  • 2009: The cryptocurrency network of bitcoin was created when Satoshi Nakamoto mined the first block of the chain. Cryptocurrency
  • 2004: Flash Airlines Flight 604 crashed shortly after departure from Sharm El Sheikh International Airport which killed all 148 occupants on board. Flash Airlines Flight 604
  • 2002: Second Intifada: Israeli forces seized MV Karine A, which was carrying 50 tons of smuggled weapons on behalf of the Palestinian Authority. Second Intifada
  • 1973: CBS announced the sale of the New York Yankees professional baseball team to a group of investors headed by American businessman George Steinbrenner. CBS
  • 1961: All 25 people on board Aero Flight 311 died in Finland's worst civilian air accident when the aircraft crashed near Kvevlax. Aero Flight 311
  • 1941: Second World War: As part of Operation Compass, Australian and United Kingdom forces attacked Italian forces at the Battle of Bardia in Egypt. World War II
  • 1938: The American health charity March of Dimes was founded as the National Foundation for Infantile Paralysis to help raise money for polio research. March of Dimes
  • 1911: A gun battle in the East End of London left two dead and sparked a political row over the operational involvement of Winston Churchill, then Home Secretary. Siege of Sidney Street
  • 1777: American Revolutionary War: American forces commanded by George Washington defeated British troops at the Battle of Princeton (depicted). American Revolutionary War
  • 1749: The first issue of Berlingske, Denmark's oldest continually operating newspaper, was published. Berlingske
  • 1521: Pope Leo X issued the papal bull Decet Romanum Pontificem, excommunicating Martin Luther for refusing to retract 41 alleged errors found in his 95 Theses and other writings. Pope Leo X

More events

  • 2026: The United States conducts airstrikes across northern Venezuela, including the capital Caracas and captures Venezuelan president Nicolás Maduro and first lady Cilia Flores. United States
  • 2024: At least 91 people are killed in bombings in Kerman, Iran, during a ceremony commemorating the assassination of Iranian General Qasem Soleimani four years earlier. 2024 Kerman bombings

  • 1976: The International Covenant on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights, adopted by the United Nations General Assembly, comes into force. International Covenant on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights
  • 1961: Cold War: After a series of economic retaliations against one another, the United States severs diplomatic relations with Cuba. Cold War
  • 1961: A protest by agricultural workers in Baixa de Cassanje, Portuguese Angola, turns into a revolt, opening the Angolan War of Independence, the first of the Portuguese Colonial Wars. Baixa de Cassanje
  • 1961: Aero Flight 311 crashes into the forest in Kvevlax, Finland, killing 25 people. Aero Flight 311
  • 1958: The West Indies Federation is formed. West Indies Federation
  • 1956: A fire damages the top part of the Eiffel Tower. Eiffel Tower
  • 1949: The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas, the central bank of the Philippines, is established. Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas
  • 1946: Popular Canadian American jockey George Woolf suffers a concussion during a freak racing accident; he dies from the injury the following day. The annual George Woolf Memorial Jockey Award is created to honor him. Canadian Americans
  • 1933: Minnie D. Craig becomes the first woman elected as Speaker of the North Dakota House of Representatives, the first woman to hold a Speaker position anywhere in the United States. Minnie D. Craig
  • 1920: Over 640 are killed after a magnitude 6.4 earthquake strikes the Mexican states Puebla and Veracruz. 1920 Xalapa earthquake
  • 1913: First Balkan War: Greece completes its capture of the eastern Aegean island of Chios, as the last Ottoman forces on the island surrender. First Balkan War
  • 1911: A gun battle in the East End of London leaves two dead. It sparked a political row over the involvement of then-Home Secretary Winston Churchill. Siege of Sidney Street
  • 1871: In the Battle of Bapaume, an engagement in the Franco-Prussian War, General Louis Faidherbe's forces bring about a Prussian retreat. Battle of Bapaume (1871)
  • 1868: Meiji Restoration in Japan: The Tokugawa shogunate is abolished; agents of Satsuma and Chōshū seize power. Meiji Restoration
  • 1861: American Civil War: Georgia state militia troops seize Fort Pulaski from Federal forces weeks before the state secedes from the Union. Georgia in the American Civil War
  • 1848: Joseph Jenkins Roberts is sworn in as the first president of Liberia. Joseph Jenkins Roberts
  • 1815: Austria, the United Kingdom, and France form a secret defensive alliance against Prussia and Russia. Prussia
  • 1749: Benning Wentworth issues the first of the New Hampshire Grants, leading to the establishment of Vermont. Benning Wentworth
  • 1653: By the Coonan Cross Oath, the Eastern Church in India cuts itself off from colonial Portuguese tutelage. Coonan Cross Oath
  • 250: Emperor Decius orders everyone in the Roman Empire (except Jews) to make sacrifices to the Roman gods. Decius

About the date itself

January 3 is the 3rd day of an ordinary year, with 362 days left after it (363 in a leap year, where it is the 3rd day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Sunday, 2028: Monday, 2029: Wednesday, 2030: Thursday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 3 January 2027 in 2027 is 25 Rajab 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 53.
