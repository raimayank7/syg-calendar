April 19: what happened on this day

What happened on 19 April: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 9 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2020: A series of attacks in Nova Scotia, Canada, ended when the perpetrator was killed by police, leaving 22 victims dead. 2020 Nova Scotia attacks
  • 2015: Freddie Gray, a 25-year-old African American, died of injuries sustained while in the custody of the Baltimore Police Department. Killing of Freddie Gray
  • 2000: Air Philippines Flight 541 crashed in Samal, Davao del Norte, killing all 131 people on board. Air Philippines Flight 541
  • 1989: A gun turret exploded on board the United States Navy battleship Iowa, killing 47 sailors. Gun turret
  • 1984: "Advance Australia Fair", written by Scottish-born composer Peter Dodds McCormick, officially replaced "God Save the Queen" as Australia's national anthem. Advance Australia Fair
  • 1971: Salyut 1, the first space station, was launched from the Baikonur Cosmodrome near Tyuratam in the Soviet Union. Salyut 1
  • 1956: American actress Grace Kelly (pictured) became the princess consort of Monaco upon her marriage to Rainier III. Grace Kelly
  • 1927: American actress Mae West (pictured) was sentenced to ten days in jail for "corrupting the morals of youth" with her play Sex. Mae West
  • 1875: The Minute Man was unveiled as part of the centennial celebration of the Battles of Lexington and Concord. The Minute Man
  • 1809: War of the Fifth Coalition: French general Louis-Nicolas Davout defeated an Austrian force in Lower Bavaria, allowing him to rejoin the main French army. War of the Fifth Coalition
  • 1775: The American Revolutionary War began with the Battles of Lexington and Concord in the British colony of Massachusetts. American Revolutionary War
  • 1713: With no living male heirs, Charles VI, Holy Roman Emperor, issued the Pragmatic Sanction, allowing the Habsburg hereditary possessions to be inherited by a daughter. Charles VI, Holy Roman Emperor

  • 1042: Following the attempt of Byzantine Emperor Michael V Kalaphates to depose his wife and empress Zoe Porphyrogenita, a popular uprising in Constantinople breaks out with the intention to restore her. List of Byzantine emperors
  • 531: Battle of Callinicum: A Byzantine army under Belisarius is defeated by the Persians at Raqqa (northern Syria). Battle of Callinicum
