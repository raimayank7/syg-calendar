About the date itself

August 31 is the 243rd day of an ordinary year, with 122 days left after it (123 in a leap year, where it is the 244th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Tuesday, 2028: Thursday, 2029: Friday, 2030: Saturday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 31 August 2027 in 2027 is 29 Rabi' al-awwal 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 35.
