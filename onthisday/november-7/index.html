November 7: what happened on this day

What happened on 7 November: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2000: Hillary Clinton was elected as a US senator from New York, the first time a first lady had been elected to public office. Hillary Clinton
  • 1996: NASA launched Mars Global Surveyor from the Kennedy Space Center at Cape Canaveral, Florida. NASA
  • 1991: Magic Johnson announced his retirement from professional basketball due to HIV infection. Magic Johnson
  • 1987: Singapore's first Mass Rapid Transit line opened, with train services running between Yio Chu Kang and Toa Payoh. Mass Rapid Transit (Singapore)
  • 1972: A ship collision with the Sidney Lanier Bridge in the U.S. state of Georgia resulted in a bridge collapse (pictured), which killed ten people. Sidney Lanier Bridge
  • 1934: The first specimens of the tufted jay (pictured) to be scientifically described were collected in Mexico. Tufted jay
  • 1917: World War I: British forces captured Gaza following the retreat of the Ottoman garrison. World War I
  • 1916: Jeannette Rankin (pictured) became the first woman to be elected to the United States House of Representatives. Jeannette Rankin
  • 1861: American Civil War: Future U.S. president Ulysses S. Grant engaged in his first combat leadership role in the Battle of Belmont in Mississippi County, Missouri. American Civil War
  • 1837: American abolitionist Elijah Parish Lovejoy was murdered by a pro-slavery mob in Alton, Illinois, during an attack to destroy his printing press and abolitionist materials. Abolitionism in the United States
  • 1811: Tecumseh's War: American forces led by William Henry Harrison defeated the forces of Shawnee leader Tecumseh's growing confederation at the Battle of Tippecanoe near present-day Battle Ground, Indiana. Tecumseh's War
  • 1723: O Ewigkeit, du Donnerwort, BWV 60, a dialogue cantata by Johann Sebastian Bach for Leipzig, was first performed. O Ewigkeit, du Donnerwort, BWV 60

More events

  • 2026: A shooting took place in the Main Market Square in Kraków. At least 5 people were killed, and 15 were injured. 2026 Kraków shooting
  • 2020: Joe Biden is confirmed elected as the 46th president of the United States, defeating incumbent Donald Trump. Joe Biden
  • 2007: The Jokela school shooting in Jokela, Tuusula, Finland, takes place, resulting in the death of nine people. Jokela school shooting
  • 2000: The controversial US presidential election is later resolved in the Bush v. Gore Supreme Court case, electing George W. Bush as the 43rd President of the United States. 2000 United States presidential election
  • 1996: ADC Airlines Flight 086 crashes into the Lagos Lagoon in Epe, Lagos State, Nigeria, killing all 144 people on board. ADC Airlines Flight 086
  • 1991: Magic Johnson announces that he is HIV-positive and retires from the NBA. Magic Johnson
  • 1989: David Dinkins becomes the first African American to be elected Mayor of New York City. David Dinkins
  • 1987: In Tunisia, president Habib Bourguiba is overthrown and replaced by Prime Minister Zine El Abidine Ben Ali. Tunisia
  • 1983: Cold War: The command post exercise Able Archer 83 begins, eventually leading to the Soviet Union to place air units in East Germany and Poland on alert, for fear that NATO was preparing for war Cold War
  • 1975: In Bangladesh, a joint force of people and soldiers takes part in an uprising led by Colonel Abu Taher that ousts and kills Brigadier Khaled Mosharraf, freeing the then house-arrested army chief and future president Major General Ziaur Rahman. Bangladesh
  • 1967: Carl B. Stokes is elected as Mayor of Cleveland, Ohio, becoming the first African American mayor of a major American city. Carl Stokes
  • 1957: Cold War: The Gaither Report calls for more American missiles and fallout shelters. Cold War
  • 1949: The first oil was taken in Oil Rocks (Neft Daşları), the world's oldest offshore oil platform. Neft Daşları
  • 1944: Franklin D. Roosevelt is elected for a record fourth term as President of the United States. Franklin D. Roosevelt
  • 1936: Spanish Civil War: The Madrid Defense Council is formed to coordinate the Defense of Madrid against nationalist forces. Spanish Civil War
  • 1929: In New York City, the Museum of Modern Art opens to the public. Museum of Modern Art
  • 1919: The first Palmer Raid is conducted on the second anniversary of the Russian Revolution. Over 10,000 suspected communists and anarchists are arrested in 23 U.S. cities. Palmer raids
  • 1917: The October Revolution, which gets its name from the Julian calendar date of 25 October, occurs, according to the Gregorian calendar; on this date, the Bolsheviks storm the Winter Palace. October Revolution
  • 1916: Jeannette Rankin is the first woman elected to the United States Congress. Jeannette Rankin
  • 1914: The German colony of Kiaochow Bay and its centre at Tsingtao are captured by Japanese forces. This leaves Germany without a far east base. German Empire
  • 1912: The Deutsche Opernhaus (now Deutsche Oper Berlin) opens in the Berlin neighborhood of Charlottenburg, with a production of Beethoven's Fidelio. Deutsche Oper Berlin
  • 1900: Second Boer War: The Battle of Leliefontein takes place, during which the Royal Canadian Dragoons win three Victoria Crosses. Second Boer War
  • 1893: An anarchist throws two bombs in Barcelona's Liceu opera house, killing 20. Liceu bombing
  • 1874: A cartoon by Thomas Nast in Harper's Weekly, is considered the first important use of an elephant as a symbol for the United States Republican Party. Cartoon
  • 1861: The first Melbourne Cup horse race is held in Melbourne, Australia. Melbourne Cup
  • 1786: The oldest musical organization in the United States is founded as the Stoughton Musical Society. Stoughton Musical Society
  • 1723: O Ewigkeit, du Donnerwort, BWV 60, a dialogue cantata by Johann Sebastian Bach for Leipzig, was first performed. O Ewigkeit, du Donnerwort, BWV 60
  • 1619: Elizabeth Stuart is crowned Queen of Bohemia. Elizabeth Stuart, Queen of Bohemia
  • 1492: The Ensisheim meteorite, the oldest meteorite with a known date of impact, strikes the Earth in a wheat field outside the village of Ensisheim, Alsace, France. Ensisheim meteorite
  • 921: Treaty of Bonn: The Frankish kings Charles the Simple and Henry the Fowler sign a peace treaty or 'pact of friendship' (amicitia) to recognize their borders along the Rhine. Treaty of Bonn

About the date itself

It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Sunday, 2028: Tuesday, 2029: Wednesday, 2030: Thursday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 7 November 2027 in 2027 is 8 Jumada al-thani 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 44.
