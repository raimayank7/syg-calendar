March 29: what happened on this day

What happened on 29 March: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 11 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2017: British prime minister Theresa May invoked Article 50 of the Treaty on European Union, formally beginning the United Kingdom's withdrawal from the European Union. Theresa May
  • 2014: The first same-sex marriages in England and Wales took place following the passage of the Marriage (Same Sex Couples) Act 2013. Same-sex marriage in the United Kingdom
  • 1999: The Chamoli earthquake, one of the strongest to hit the foothills of the Himalayas in more than 90 years, killed at least 100 people. 1999 Chamoli earthquake
  • 1981: Dick Beardsley and Inge Simonsen jointly won the Men's Elite Race and Joyce Smith won the Women's Elite Race in the inaugural London Marathon. Dick Beardsley
  • 1974: NASA's Mariner 10 became the first space probe to make a flyby of Mercury. NASA
  • 1968: The funeral of Yuri Gagarin, the first man in space, began in Moscow with tens of thousands of people in attendance. Funeral of Yuri Gagarin and Vladimir Seryogin
  • 1941: The North American Regional Broadcasting Agreement to define technical standards for AM band radio stations came into effect. North American Regional Broadcasting Agreement
  • 1941: Second World War: British and Australian ships defeated Italian Regia Marina vessels at the Battle of Cape Matapan. World War II
  • 1865: American Civil War: The Appomattox campaign opened with the Battle of Lewis's Farm, in which the Confederate Army of Northern Virginia was forced into a series of retreats that would culminate in their surrender. American Civil War
  • 1823: Joaquín de Oreamuno led a coup d'état that overthrew Rafael Francisco Osejo, attempting to restore allegiance to the Mexican Empire. Joaquín de Oreamuno
  • 1800: William Matthews was ordained as the first Catholic priest born in British America. William Matthews (priest)
  • 1430: After an eight-year siege, the Ottoman Empire captured the city of Thessalonica from the Republic of Venice. Siege of Thessalonica (1422–1430)

More events

  • 1871: Royal Albert Hall is opened by Queen Victoria. Royal Albert Hall

  • 1847: Mexican–American War: United States forces led by General Winfield Scott take Veracruz after a siege. Mexican–American War
  • 1809: King Gustav IV Adolf of Sweden abdicates after a coup d'état. Gustav IV Adolf

  • 1632: Treaty of Saint-Germain is signed returning Quebec to French control after the English had seized it in 1629. Treaty of Saint-Germain-en-Laye (1632)
  • 1549: The city of Salvador, Bahia, the first capital of Brazil, is founded. Salvador, Bahia
  • 1430: The Ottoman Empire under Murad II captures Thessalonica from the Republic of Venice. Ottoman Empire

About the date itself

It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Monday, 2028: Wednesday, 2029: Thursday, 2030: Friday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 29 March 2027 in 2027 is 21 Shawwal 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table.
