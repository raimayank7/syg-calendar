July 8: what happened on this day

What happened on 8 July: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 11 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2022: Former Japanese prime minister Shinzo Abe (pictured) was assassinated with an improvised firearm. Shinzo Abe

  • 2003: Sudan Airways Flight 139 crashed near Port Sudan Airport during an emergency landing attempt, killing 116 of the 117 people on board. Sudan Airways Flight 139

  • 1927: The Spanish anarchist group Los Errantes are released from French detention, following a lengthy defense campaign against their extradition. Los Errantes
  • 1874: Members of the North-West Mounted Police at Fort Dufferin began their March West, their first journey to the Canadian Prairies. North-West Mounted Police
  • 1758: French and Indian War: French forces defeated the British at Fort Carillon in the British colony of New York. French and Indian War
  • 1709: Great Northern War: Swedish forces under Charles XII were defeated by Russian troops led by Peter the Great at the Battle of Poltava. Great Northern War

About the date itself
