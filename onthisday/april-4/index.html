April 4: what happened on this day

What happened on 4 April: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 13 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2013: A building collapsed on tribal land in Thane in Maharashtra, India, causing 74 deaths. 2013 Thane building collapse
  • 2011: Georgian Airways Flight 834 crashed at N'djili Airport in Kinshasa, killing 32 people and leaving one survivor. Georgian Airways Flight 834
  • 1988: Evan Mecham, Governor of Arizona, was removed from office after being convicted in his impeachment trial. Evan Mecham
  • 1975: Bill Gates and Paul Allen founded Microsoft in Albuquerque, New Mexico, to develop and sell BASIC interpreters for the Altair 8800. Bill Gates
  • 1973: A year after the completion of the second of the complex's twin towers, the World Trade Center in New York City was officially dedicated. Construction of the World Trade Center
  • 1973: A C-141, dubbed the Hanoi Taxi, flew the last mission of Operation Homecoming to return American prisoners of war from Vietnam. Lockheed C-141 Starlifter
  • 1949: Twelve nations signed the North Atlantic Treaty, establishing NATO, an international military alliance whereby its member states agree to mutual defense in response to an attack by any external party. North Atlantic Treaty
  • 1873: The Kennel Club, the world's oldest kennel club, was founded in the United Kingdom after Sewallis Shirley became frustrated by trying to organise dog shows without a consistent set of rules. Royal Kennel Club
  • 1859: Bryant's Minstrels premiered the minstrel song "Dixie" in New York City as part of their blackface show. Bryant's Minstrels
  • 1814: Napoleon abdicated as Emperor of the French and named his son Napoleon II to replace him. Napoleon
  • 1581: Queen Elizabeth I knighted Francis Drake for completing a circumnavigation of the world. Elizabeth I
  • 1081: The Komnenos dynasty came to full power with the coronation of Alexios I Komnenos (pictured) as Byzantine emperor. Komnenos

More events

  • 2023: Finland becomes a member of NATO after Turkey accepts its membership request. Finland

  • 1991: Senator John Heinz of Pennsylvania and six others are killed when a helicopter collides with their airplane over an elementary school in Merion, Pennsylvania. John Heinz
  • 1990: The current flag of Hong Kong is adopted for post-colonial Hong Kong during the Third Session of the Seventh National People's Congress. Flag of Hong Kong
  • 1987: Garuda Indonesia Flight 032 crashes at Medan Airport, killing 23. Garuda Indonesia Flight 035

  • 1977: Southern Airways Flight 242 crashes in New Hope, Paulding County, Georgia, killing 72. Southern Airways Flight 242

  • 1973: A Lockheed C-141 Starlifter, dubbed the Hanoi Taxi, makes the last flight of Operation Homecoming. Lockheed C-141 Starlifter
  • 1968: Martin Luther King Jr. is assassinated by James Earl Ray at a motel in Memphis, Tennessee. Assassination of Martin Luther King Jr.
  • 1967: Martin Luther King Jr. delivers his "Beyond Vietnam: A Time to Break Silence" speech in New York City's Riverside Church. Martin Luther King Jr.
  • 1960: France agrees to grant independence to the Mali Federation, a union of Senegal and French Sudan. France
  • 1949: Cold War: Twelve nations sign the North Atlantic Treaty creating the North Atlantic Treaty Organization. Cold War
  • 1945: World War II: United States Army troops liberate Ohrdruf forced labor camp in Germany. United States Army
  • 1945: World War II: Soviet Red Army troops liberate Hungary from German occupation. Red Army
  • 1933: U.S. Navy airship USS Akron is wrecked off the New Jersey coast due to severe weather. United States Navy
  • 1913: First Balkan War: Greek aviator Emmanouil Argyropoulos becomes the first pilot to die in the Hellenic Air Force when his plane crashes. First Balkan War
  • 1904: Two Ms ~7.1 earthquakes, among the largest in Europe, strike Bulgaria, killing over 200 people and causing destruction. 1904 Kresna earthquakes
  • 1887: Argonia, Kansas elects Susanna M. Salter as the first female mayor in the United States. Argonia, Kansas
  • 1865: American Civil War: A day after Union forces capture Richmond, Virginia, U.S. President Abraham Lincoln visits the Confederate capital. American Civil War
  • 1841: William Henry Harrison dies of pneumonia, becoming the first President of the United States to die in office, and setting the record for the briefest administration. Vice President John Tyler succeeds Harrison as President. William Henry Harrison
  • 1796: Georges Cuvier delivers the first paleontological lecture. Georges Cuvier
  • 1609: Moriscos are expelled from the Kingdom of Valencia. Morisco
  • 1423: Death of the Venetian Doge Tommaso Mocenigo, under whose rule victories were achieved against the Kingdom of Hungary and against the Ottoman Empire at the Battle of Gallipoli (1416). Doge (title)
  • 801: King Louis the Pious captures Barcelona from the Moors after a siege of several months. Louis the Pious
  • 611: Maya king Uneh Chan of Calakmul sacks rival city-state Palenque in southern Mexico. Scroll Serpent

About the date itself

April 4 is the 94th day of an ordinary year, with 271 days left after it (272 in a leap year, where it is the 95th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Sunday, 2028: Tuesday, 2029: Wednesday, 2030: Thursday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 4 April 2027 in 2027 is 27 Shawwal 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 13.
