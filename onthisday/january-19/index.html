January 19: what happened on this day

What happened on 19 January: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 13 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2012: The Hong Kong–based file-sharing website Megaupload was shut down by the FBI. File sharing
  • 2007: Turkish-Armenian journalist and human-rights activist Hrant Dink was assassinated by a Turkish nationalist in Istanbul. Hrant Dink
  • 2007: A four-man team, using only skis and kites, completed a 1,093-mile (1,759 km) trek to reach the Southern Pole of Inaccessibility, the first people to get there since 1967, and the first to do so on foot. Snowkiting
  • 2006: In the deadliest aviation accident in Slovak history, an Antonov An-24 operated by the Slovak Air Force crashed in northern Hungary, killing 42 of the 43 people on board. Antonov An-24
  • 1996: A tank barge and a tug grounded on a beach in Rhode Island, causing a spill of an estimated 828,000 U.S. gallons (3.13 million litres) of home heating oil. Barge
  • 1977: Iva Toguri (pictured), convicted of treason for broadcasting Japanese propaganda, was granted a full pardon by U.S. president Gerald Ford. Iva Toguri D'Aquino
  • 1972: The French newspaper L'Aurore revealed that the former Nazi SS officer Klaus Barbie, the "Butcher of Lyon", had been found to be living in Peru. L'Aurore (newspaper founded 1944)
  • 1920: The American Civil Liberties Union was founded by the directors of the National Civil Liberties Bureau. American Civil Liberties Union
  • 1915: World War I: The first major attack of the German bombing campaign against Britain took place when Zeppelins bombed several towns in Norfolk. World War I
  • 1909: A deed was recorded for David Hanbury to sell Island No. 2 in northern California to his brother John for $10 ($358.33 in .mw-parser-output .tooltip-dotted{border-bottom:1px dotted;cursor:help}2025). Deed
  • 1795: The Batavian Republic was established the day after William V fled the Dutch Republic as a result of the Batavian Revolution in Amsterdam. Batavian Republic
  • 1511: War of the League of Cambrai: Troops led by Pope Julius II captured Mirandola (depicted) after a brief siege. War of the League of Cambrai

About the date itself

January 19 is the 19th day of an ordinary year, with 346 days left after it (347 in a leap year, where it is the 19th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Tuesday, 2028: Wednesday, 2029: Friday, 2030: Saturday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 19 January 2027 in 2027 is 11 Sha'ban 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 3.
