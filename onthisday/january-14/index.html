January 14: what happened on this day

What happened on 14 January: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2018: In the "Minneapolis Miracle", American football player Stefon Diggs caught a 61-yard (56 m) touchdown pass that secured the Minnesota Vikings' victory in the National Football Conference divisional playoff game. Minneapolis Miracle
  • 2011: Arab Spring: Tunisian president Zine El Abidine Ben Ali fled the country after several violent protests. Arab Spring
  • 1978: Austrian logician Kurt Gödel, who suffered from an obsessive fear of being poisoned, died of starvation after his wife was hospitalized and unable to cook for him. Logic
  • 1973: Elvis Presley's (pictured) concert Aloha from Hawaii via Satellite was broadcast live to audiences in Asia and Oceania. Elvis Presley
  • 1970: The self-proclaimed Republic of Biafra in southeastern Nigeria surrendered to the federal government less than three years after declaring independence, ending the Nigerian Civil War. Biafra
  • 1969: A major fire and series of explosions aboard the U.S. Navy aircraft carrier USS Enterprise killed 28 sailors, injured 314 others, and destroyed 15 aircraft. 1969 USS Enterprise fire
  • 1939: Norway claimed Queen Maud Land, a 2.7-million km2 (1.0-million sq mi) region of Antarctica, as a dependent territory. Queen Maud Land
  • 1907: An earthquake registering 6.2 Mw struck Kingston, Jamaica, resulting in approximately 1,000 deaths. 1907 Kingston earthquake
  • 1900: Giacomo Puccini's opera Tosca (poster pictured), based on the play La Tosca by French dramatist Victorien Sardou, premiered at the Teatro Costanzi in Rome. Giacomo Puccini
  • 1814: Sweden and Denmark–Norway signed the Treaty of Kiel, whereby Frederick VI of Denmark ceded Norway to Sweden in return for the Swedish holdings in Pomerania. Denmark–Norway
  • 1724: Philip V (pictured), the first Bourbon king of Spain, abdicated in favour of his seventeen-year-old eldest son, who became Louis I. Philip V of Spain
  • 1641: Dutch–Portuguese War: The Dutch siege of Malacca against the Portuguese ended when Dutch troops seized the city's citadel (depicted). Dutch–Portuguese War

About the date itself

January 14 is the 14th day of an ordinary year, with 351 days left after it (352 in a leap year, where it is the 14th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Thursday, 2028: Friday, 2029: Sunday, 2030: Monday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 14 January 2027 in 2027 is 6 Sha'ban 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 2.
