June 1: what happened on this day

What happened on 1 June: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2018: For the first time since its transition to democracy, the Congress of Deputies in Spain passed a motion of no confidence against the government. Congress of Deputies
  • 2015: The river cruise ship Dongfang zhi Xing capsized in the Yangtze, resulting in 442 deaths in China's worst peacetime maritime disaster. Sinking of Dongfang zhi Xing
  • 2007: Cyclone Gonu develops from an area of convection in the Arabian Sea, later becoming the worst recorded natural disaster in Oman. Cyclone Gonu
  • 1999: On landing at Little Rock National Airport in the U.S. state of Arkansas, American Airlines Flight 1420 overran the runway and crashed (wreckage pictured), resulting in 11 deaths. Clinton National Airport
  • 1988: The Intermediate-Range Nuclear Forces Treaty was ratified, banning all American and Soviet land-based missiles with a range of 500 to 5,500 km (310 to 3,420 mi). Intermediate-Range Nuclear Forces Treaty
  • 1974: In an informal article in a medical journal, Henry Heimlich introduced the concept of abdominal thrusts, commonly known as the Heimlich maneuver, to treat victims of choking. Henry Heimlich
  • 1942: World War II: The crews of three Japanese submarines scuttled their vessels and committed suicide after entering Sydney Harbour and launching a failed attack. World War II
  • 1868: The Navajo and the U.S. government signed the Treaty of Bosque Redondo, which allowed those interned at Fort Sumner to return to their ancestral lands. Navajo
  • 1857: The Revolution of the Ganhadores, the first general strike in Brazil, began in Salvador, Bahia. Revolution of the Ganhadores
  • 1796: Tennessee was admitted as the 16th state of the United States. Tennessee
  • 1794: French Revolutionary Wars: A British fleet captured six French ships of the line in a naval battle off Ushant that came to be known as the Glorious First of June. French Revolutionary Wars
  • 1670: Charles II and Louis XIV signed a secret treaty, requiring England to aid France in its war against the Dutch Republic and the future conversion of Charles to Catholicism, in return for Louis making large payments to Charles and promising to aid him if there was a rebellion in England. Charles II of England

About the date itself

June 1 is the 152nd day of an ordinary year, with 213 days left after it (214 in a leap year, where it is the 153rd day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Tuesday, 2028: Thursday, 2029: Friday, 2030: Saturday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 1 June 2027 in 2027 is 26 Dhu al-Hijjah 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 22.
