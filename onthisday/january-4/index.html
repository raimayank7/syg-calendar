January 4: what happened on this day

What happened on 4 January: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 14 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2020: Sembawang Hot Spring Park in Singapore reopened after being redeveloped by the National Parks Board. Sembawang Hot Spring Park
  • 2019: A fire in an escape room in Koszalin, Poland, killed five teenagers. Koszalin escape room fire
  • 2010: The Burj Khalifa, the world's tallest structure, officially opened in Dubai. Burj Khalifa
  • 2004: Spirit (artist's impression depicted), the first of two rovers of NASA's Mars Exploration Rover mission, successfully landed on Mars. Spirit (rover)
  • 1989: Two American F-14 Tomcats shot down two Libyan MiG-23 Floggers that appeared to be attempting to engage them over the Gulf of Sidra. Grumman F-14 Tomcat
  • 1977: The English punk-rock band Sex Pistols' lewd and disruptive behaviour at Heathrow Airport prompted the record label EMI to end their contract. Punk rock
  • 1972: Rose Heilbron (pictured) became the first female judge to sit at the Central Criminal Court of England and Wales. Rose Heilbron
  • 1970: A magnitude-7.1 earthquake occurred in Tonghai County, China, killing at least 15,000 people. 1970 Tonghai earthquake
  • 1951: Korean War: Chinese and North Korean troops captured Seoul from United Nations forces. Korean War
  • 1909: British explorer Aeneas Mackintosh, a member of the Nimrod Expedition, escaped death by fleeing across ice floes. Aeneas Mackintosh
  • 1853: Solomon Northup (pictured) regained his freedom after having been sold into slavery in the American South; his memoir Twelve Years a Slave later became a bestseller. Solomon Northup
  • 1798: After his appointment as Prince of Wallachia, Constantine Hangerli arrived in Bucharest to assume the throne. List of princes of Wallachia

About the date itself

January 4 is the 4th day of an ordinary year, with 361 days left after it (362 in a leap year, where it is the 4th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Monday, 2028: Tuesday, 2029: Thursday, 2030: Friday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 4 January 2027 in 2027 is 26 Rajab 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 1.
