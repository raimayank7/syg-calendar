About these pages

Where the lists come from, what is left out and how to use them well.

Where the lists come from

English Wikipedia keeps a list of selected anniversaries and a page for every date with events, births, deaths and holidays. The Wikimedia foundation publishes them as a feed. We read that feed on 13 November 2026 for each of the 366 dates and keep a limited number of entries on each page: all the selected events, up to 30 other events, 24 births spread evenly across the centuries, 20 deaths spread the same way and up to 15 observances.

What the choices mean

The selection is Wikipedia's, not ours. The spread of births and deaths over the centuries avoids a list with only ancient names. It does not rank people by importance. If a person you expect is missing, open the Wikipedia page of the date by the link at the bottom.

Using it well

  • For a school project or article, treat these pages as a starting point and check with a book or a reliable site.
  • For birthday and anniversary posts, check dates of birth against the person's own biography page.
  • Dates before the Gregorian calendar was adopted (1582 in Catholic countries, later elsewhere) are given as the sources give them.
