April 2: what happened on this day

What happened on 2 April: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2015: Six elderly men burgled a safe-deposit facility in Hatton Garden, London, and stole items worth up to an estimated £14 million. Hatton Garden safe deposit burglary
  • 2015: Gunmen attacked Garissa University College in Kenya, killing 148 people and wounding 79 others. Garissa University College attack
  • 2012: A gunman shot at people inside Oikos University, a Korean Christian college in Oakland, California, leaving seven people dead and three injured. 2012 Oikos University shooting
  • 2002: Second Intifada: Palestinian militants sought refuge from advancing Israeli forces in the Church of the Nativity in Bethlehem, beginning a month-long siege. Second Intifada
  • 1992: Bosnian War: At least 48 civilians were massacred in the town of Bijeljina in Bosnia and Herzegovina. Bosnian War
  • 1992: John Gotti, the head of the Gambino crime family of New York City, was convicted of racketeering, murder, conspiracy to commit murder, loansharking, obstruction of justice, illegal gambling, and tax evasion. John Gotti
  • 1979: Spores of anthrax were accidentally released from a military research facility near the city of Sverdlovsk, causing at least 68 deaths. Spore
  • 1968: 2001: A Space Odyssey, the epic science fiction film produced and directed by Stanley Kubrick, premiered at the Uptown Theater in Washington, D.C. 2001: A Space Odyssey
  • 1911: The Australian Bureau of Statistics conducted the first national census of the country. Australian Bureau of Statistics
  • 1871: Creatures of Impulse, by W. S. Gilbert (pictured), premiered at the Court Theatre in London. Creatures of Impulse
  • 1865: American Civil War: On the third attempt, Union forces captured Petersburg, Virginia, although Confederate officials and most of their remaining troops were able to escape. American Civil War
  • 1860: The first modern Italian Parliament is opened in Turin, following the annexation of Central Italy into the Kingdom of Sardinia. Italian Parliament

About the date itself

April 2 is the 92nd day of an ordinary year, with 273 days left after it (274 in a leap year, where it is the 93rd day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Friday, 2028: Sunday, 2029: Monday, 2030: Tuesday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 2 April 2027 in 2027 is 25 Shawwal 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 13.
