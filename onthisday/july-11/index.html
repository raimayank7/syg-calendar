July 11: what happened on this day

What happened on 11 July: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2011: An explosion at the Evangelos Florakis Naval Base killed 13 people, including the head of the Cyprus Navy. Evangelos Florakis Naval Base explosion
  • 2010: The Islamist militia group Al-Shabaab carried out multiple suicide bombings in Kampala, Uganda, killing 74 people and injuring 85 others. Islamism
  • 1991: Shortly after taking off from Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, Nigeria Airways Flight 2120 caught fire and crashed, killing all 261 people on board. Jeddah
  • 1982: Italy defeated West Germany 3–1 to win their third FIFA World Cup title. Italy national football team
  • 1928: Archaeologist Ivan Borkovský discovered a medieval skeleton at Prague Castle; competing factions claimed the skeleton as Germanic or Slavic in origin. Ivan Borkovský
  • 1914: The US Navy launched the USS Nevada (BB-36) as its first Standard-type battleship. USS Nevada (BB-36)
  • 1864: A riot broke out in Leicester, England, at the failed launching of a gas balloon (pictured). Leicester balloon riot
  • 1846: British soldier Frederick John White died after a flogging, leading to a campaign to end the practice in the British Army. Death of Frederick John White
  • 1833: Yagan, a Noongar warrior, was killed for leading attacks on British colonists in Western Australia. Yagan
  • 1792: The Belfast Harp Festival, an early event in the Gaelic revival, began at the Assembly Rooms. Belfast Harp Festival
  • 1789: French Revolution: Jacques Necker was dismissed as Director-General of Finances of France, sparking public demonstrations in Paris that led to the storming of the Bastille. French Revolution
  • 1302: Franco-Flemish War: Flemish infantry defeated a large French army near Kortrijk at the Battle of the Golden Spurs. Franco-Flemish War

About the date itself

July 11 is the 192nd day of an ordinary year, with 173 days left after it (174 in a leap year, where it is the 193rd day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Sunday, 2028: Tuesday, 2029: Wednesday, 2030: Thursday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 11 July 2027 in 2027 is 7 Safar 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 27.
