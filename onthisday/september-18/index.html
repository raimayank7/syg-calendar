September 18: what happened on this day

What happened on 18 September: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 1981: While posing as an aristocrat, Belgian serial killer Nestor Pirotte murdered an antiques dealer in Brussels, for which crime he was sentenced to death. Nestor Pirotte
  • 1964: The wedding of King Constantine II of Greece and Princess Anne-Marie of Denmark took place in Athens. Wedding of Constantine II and Princess Anne-Marie
  • 1961: An aircraft crashed near Ndola in Northern Rhodesia, resulting in the deaths of 16 people, including United Nations secretary-general Dag Hammarskjöld. 1961 Ndola Transair Sweden Douglas DC-6 crash
  • 1950: Korean War: The Korean People's Army retreated from the Battle of the Pusan Perimeter after six weeks of fighting, marking the farthest that the KPA would advance in the war. Korean War
  • 1918: World War I: The Central Powers' defeat at the Battle of Dobro Pole played a role in the Bulgarian withdrawal from the war and led to the subsequent liberation of Vardar Macedonia. World War I
  • 1885: Five Chinese people were lynched outside of Pierce City in the Idaho Territory. 1885 Pierce City lynching
  • 1875: The Indianola hurricane dissipated over Mississippi after killing around eight hundred people in Texas. 1875 Indianola hurricane
  • 1873: Panic of 1873: The American bank Jay Cooke & Company declared bankruptcy, setting off a chain reaction of bank failures. Panic of 1873
  • 1851: The New York Times, the largest metropolitan newspaper in the United States, was founded. The New York Times
  • 1850: The United States Congress passed the Fugitive Slave Act, decreeing that all escaped slaves be brought back to their masters. United States Congress
  • 1048: Byzantine–Seljuk wars: Byzantine forces defeated their Seljuk opponents in the flanks of the nocturnal Battle of Kapetron, but learned of their Georgian allies' defeat in the centre the next morning. Byzantine–Seljuk wars
  • 324: Constantine the Great decisively defeated Licinius in the Battle of Chrysopolis, establishing Constantine's sole control over the Roman Empire and ending the Tetrarchy. Constantine the Great

  • 1964: The wedding of Constantine II of Greece and Princess Anne-Marie of Denmark takes place in Athens. Wedding of Constantine II and Princess Anne-Marie

  • 1947: The National Security Act reorganizes the United States government's military and intelligence services. National Security Act of 1947
  • 1944: World War II: The British submarine HMS Tradewind torpedoes Jun'yō Maru, killing 5,600, mostly slave labourers and POWs. HMS Tradewind

  • 1919: Fritz Pollard becomes the first African American to play professional football for a major team, the Akron Pros. Fritz Pollard
  • 1915: The Saturday Evening Post publishes the short story "Extricating Young Gussie" by P.G. Wodehouse, featuring the first appearance of Bertie Wooster and his valet Jeeves. The Saturday Evening Post

  • 1867: The fourth and current State Constitution of Maryland is ratified by voters. Constitution of Maryland
  • 1862: The Confederate States celebrate for the first and only time a Thanksgiving Day. Confederate States of America
  • 1860: Wars of Italian Unification: Battle of Castelfidardo: Royal Sardinian Army defeats forces of the Papal States, resulting in the conquest of Umbria and Marche by the Kingdom of Italy. Unification of Italy
  • 1837: Tiffany & Co. (first named Tiffany & Young) is founded by Charles Lewis Tiffany and Teddy Young in New York City. The store is called a "stationery and fancy goods emporium". Tiffany & Co.
  • 1809: The Royal Opera House in Covent Garden, London, opens. Royal Opera House
  • 1618: The twelfth baktun in the Mesoamerican Long Count calendar begins. Baktun
  • 1454: Thirteen Years' War: In the Battle of Chojnice, the Polish army is defeated by the Teutonic knights. Thirteen Years' War (1454–1466)
  • 1048: Battle of Kapetron between a combined Byzantine-Georgian army and a Seljuq army. Battle of Kapetron
