January 25: what happened on this day

What happened on 25 January: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 12 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2011: Arab Spring: The Egyptian revolution began with protests on the "Day of Anger", eventually leading to the removal of President Hosni Mubarak after nearly 30 years of rule. Arab Spring
  • 2010: Ethiopian Airlines Flight 409, en route to Addis Ababa, crashed into the Mediterranean Sea shortly after takeoff from Beirut, Lebanon, killing all 90 people on board. Ethiopian Airlines Flight 409
  • 2006: Three independent observing campaigns announced the discovery of OGLE-2005-BLG-390Lb, a super-Earth 21,500 ± 3,300 light-years away from Earth near the center of the Milky Way. OGLE-2005-BLG-390Lb
  • 1998: The Liberation Tigers of Tamil Eelam detonated a truck bomb at the sacred Buddhist Temple of the Tooth in Kandy, killing 17 people. Liberation Tigers of Tamil Eelam
  • 1993: Pakistani national Mir Aimal Kansi shot five people outside the Central Intelligence Agency headquarters in Langley, Virginia, killing two. Mir Aimal Kansi
  • 1990: Avianca Flight 52 ran out of fuel on approach to John F. Kennedy International Airport and crashed into the village of Cove Neck, New York, resulting in the deaths of 73 people. Avianca Flight 052
  • 1967: South Vietnamese junta leader Nguyễn Cao Kỳ fired his rival Nguyễn Hữu Có while the latter was overseas on a diplomatic visit. South Vietnam
  • 1932: The Alt Llobregat insurrection was suppressed in central Catalonia, and over 100 Catalan anarchist activists were arrested and deported. Alt Llobregat insurrection
  • 1890: American journalist Nellie Bly (pictured) completed a circumnavigation of the globe by land and sea in a then-record-breaking 72 days. Nellie Bly
  • 1792: Thomas Hardy founded the London Corresponding Society to seek a "radical reform of parliament", later influencing the reform movements of early-19th-century England. Thomas Hardy (political reformer)
  • 1725: Privateer Amaro Pargo was declared a hidalgo, a member of the Spanish nobility. Amaro Pargo
  • 1533: Anne Boleyn (depicted), already pregnant with the future Elizabeth I, secretly married Henry VIII of England in the second of his six marriages. Anne Boleyn

More events

  • 2019: A mining company's dam collapses in Brumadinho, Brazil, a south-eastern city, killing 270 people. Brumadinho dam disaster
  • 2015: A clash in Mamasapano, Maguindanao in the Philippines kills 44 members of Special Action Force (SAF), at least 18 from the Moro Islamic Liberation Front and five from the Bangsamoro Islamic Freedom Fighters. Mamasapano clash
  • 2011: The first wave of the Egyptian revolution begins throughout the country, marked by street demonstrations, rallies, acts of civil disobedience, riots, labour strikes, and violent clashes. Timeline of the Egyptian revolution of 2011
  • 2006: Mexican professional wrestler Juana Barraza is arrested in connection with the serial killing of at least ten elderly women. Lucha libre
  • 2003: Invasion of Iraq: A group of people leave London, England, for Baghdad, Iraq, to serve as human shields, intending to prevent the U.S.-led coalition troops from bombing certain locations. 2003 invasion of Iraq
  • 1998: A suicide attack by the Liberation Tigers of Tamil Eelam on Sri Lanka's Temple of the Tooth kills eight and injures 25 others. Liberation Tigers of Tamil Eelam
  • 1995: The Norwegian rocket incident: Russia almost launches a nuclear attack after it mistakes Black Brant XII, a Norwegian research rocket, for a US Trident missile. Norwegian rocket incident
  • 1993: Five people are shot outside the CIA Headquarters in Langley, Virginia. Two are killed and three wounded. 1993 CIA headquarters shooting
  • 1986: The National Resistance Movement topples the government of Tito Okello in Uganda. National Resistance Movement
  • 1979: Pope John Paul II starts his first official papal visits outside Italy to The Bahamas, Dominican Republic, and Mexico. Pope John Paul II
  • 1969: Brazilian Army captain Carlos Lamarca deserts in order to fight against the military dictatorship, taking with him ten machine guns and 63 rifles. Brazilian Army
  • 1964: Blue Ribbon Sports, which would later become Nike, is founded by University of Oregon track and field athletes. Nike, Inc.
  • 1961: Walt Disney Productions released the animated feature One Hundred and One Dalmatians, based on Dodie Smith's 1956 children's novel The Hundred and One Dalmatians. Walt Disney Pictures
  • 1949: The first Emmy Awards are presented in the United States; the venue is the Hollywood Athletic Club. Emmy Awards
  • 1946: The United Mine Workers rejoins the American Federation of Labor. United Mine Workers of America
  • 1942: World War II: Thailand declares war on the United States and United Kingdom. World War II
  • 1937: The Guiding Light debuts on NBC radio from Chicago. In 1952 it moves to CBS television, where it remains until September 18, 2009. Guiding Light
  • 1932: Alt Llobregat insurrection suppressed in Central Catalonia, Spain. Alt Llobregat insurrection
  • 1918: The Ukrainian People's Republic declares independence from Soviet Russia. Ukrainian People's Republic
  • 1917: Sinking of the SS Laurentic after hitting two German mines off the coast of northwest Ireland. SS Laurentic (1908)
  • 1890: Nellie Bly completes her round-the-world journey in 72 days. Nellie Bly
  • 1879: The Bulgarian National Bank is founded. Bulgarian National Bank
  • 1819: University of Virginia chartered by Commonwealth of Virginia, with Thomas Jefferson one of its founders. University of Virginia
  • 1791: The British Parliament passes the Constitutional Act of 1791 and splits the old Province of Quebec into Upper Canada and Lower Canada. Parliament of Great Britain
  • 1704: The Apalachee massacre: A combined British and Muscogee force from the Province of Carolina destroys the main fortified mission of Ayubale, breaking Spain's hold on Spanish Florida. Apalachee massacre
  • 1575: Luanda, the capital of Angola, is founded by the Portuguese navigator Paulo Dias de Novais. Luanda
  • 1554: São Paulo, Brazil, is founded by Jesuit priests. São Paulo
  • 1515: Coronation of Francis I of France takes place at Reims Cathedral, where the new monarch is anointed with the oil of Clovis and girt with the sword of Charlemagne. Coronation of the French monarch
  • 1479: The Treaty of Constantinople ends the 16-year-long First Ottoman–Venetian War. Treaty of Constantinople (1479)
  • 1327: Fourteen-year-old Edward III ascends the throne of England after his father the king is forced to abdicate by Queen Isabella and her lover, Roger Mortimer. Edward III

About the date itself

January 25 is the 25th day of an ordinary year, with 340 days left after it (341 in a leap year, where it is the 25th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Monday, 2028: Tuesday, 2029: Thursday, 2030: Friday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 25 January 2027 in 2027 is 17 Sha'ban 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 4.
