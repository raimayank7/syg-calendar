October 29: what happened on this day

What happened on 29 October: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 13 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2013: The first phase of the Marmaray project opened with an undersea rail tunnel (train pictured) across the Bosphorus strait. Marmaray
  • 2012: Hurricane Sandy, the largest Atlantic hurricane on record, made landfall in New Jersey and caused nearly $75 billion in damages, becoming the second-most destructive storm in U.S. history. Hurricane Sandy
  • 2007: Somali pirates hijacked a North Korean ship in the Indian Ocean northeast of Mogadishu. Dai Hong Dan incident
  • 1998: At 77 years old, former astronaut John Glenn (pictured) returned to space aboard the Space Shuttle Discovery on the STS-95 mission. John Glenn
  • 1991: Galileo became the first spacecraft to visit an asteroid when it made a flyby of Gaspra. Galileo (spacecraft)
  • 1960: A C-46 airliner carrying the Cal Poly Mustangs football team crashed during takeoff from Toledo Express Airport in Ohio, U.S., resulting in 22 deaths. Curtiss C-46 Commando
  • 1956: The Suez Crisis began with Israel invading the Sinai Peninsula and pushing Egyptian forces back toward the Suez Canal. Suez Crisis
  • 1956: Suez Crisis: The Israel Border Police massacred 48 Arab citizens in Kafr Qasim, among them women and children who were returning from work. Suez Crisis
  • 1948: Arab–Israeli War: The Israel Defense Forces massacred at least 52 villagers while capturing the Palestinian Arab village of Safsaf. 1948 Arab–Israeli War
  • 1883: The San Francisco Mint signed a contract to produce the Kalākaua coinage (coin pictured) for the Hawaiian Kingdom. San Francisco Mint
  • 1863: American Civil War: The Battle of Wauhatchie, one of the few night battles of the war, concluded with the Union Army opening a supply line to troops in Chattanooga, Tennessee. American Civil War
  • 1831: Rioting broke out in Bristol, England, after the Second Reform Bill failed during its passage through Parliament, causing up to 250 casualties and £300,000 of damage (pictured). 1831 Bristol riots

About the date itself
