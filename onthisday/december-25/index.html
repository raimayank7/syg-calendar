December 25: what happened on this day

What happened on 25 December: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 10 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2024: After the loss of aircraft systems, Azerbaijan Airlines Flight 8243 crashed near Aktau International Airport while attempting to perform an emergency landing after being hit by a Russian defense missile. Aircraft systems
  • 2016: A Tupolev Tu-154 of the Russian Defence Ministry crashed into the Black Sea shortly after taking off from Sochi International Airport, killing all 92 people on board. Tupolev Tu-154
  • 2009: A fire destroyed Longford's 19th-century St Mel's Cathedral, considered the "flagship cathedral" of the Irish midlands. Longford
  • 2000: Russian president Vladimir Putin signed into law a bill officially adopting a new national anthem, with music by Alexander Vasilyevich Alexandrov originally composed for the anthem of the Soviet Union. Vladimir Putin
  • 1990: British computer programmer Tim Berners-Lee introduced WorldWideWeb, the world's first web browser and WYSIWYG HTML editor. Tim Berners-Lee
  • 1968: In Tamil Nadu, India, families of striking Dalit workers were massacred by a gang, allegedly led by their landlords. Tamil Nadu
  • 1927: The Việt Nam Quốc Dân Đảng, a revolutionary socialist political party that sought Vietnamese independence from French colonial rule, was formed in Hanoi. Việt Nam Quốc Dân Đảng
  • 1914: The Royal Navy armed trawler Night Hawk sank after striking a mine, with the loss of six of her crew. HMT Night Hawk
  • 1809: American physician Ephraim McDowell performed the world's first removal of an ovarian tumor. Ephraim McDowell
  • 1725: J. S. Bach led the first performance of the Christmas cantata Unser Mund sei voll Lachens, BWV 110, making laughter audible in singing. Johann Sebastian Bach
  • 1100: Baldwin I was crowned the first king of Jerusalem (depicted) in the Church of the Nativity in Bethlehem. Baldwin I of Jerusalem
  • 1046: Henry III was crowned Holy Roman Emperor by Pope Clement II. Henry III, Holy Roman Emperor

  • 1868: Pardons for ex-Confederates: United States President Andrew Johnson grants an unconditional pardon to all Confederate veterans. Pardons for ex-Confederates
  • 1831: The Great Jamaican Slave Revolt begins; up to 20% of Jamaica's slaves mobilize in an ultimately unsuccessful fight for freedom. Baptist War
  • 1815: The Handel and Haydn Society, oldest continually performing arts organization in the United States, gives its first performance. Handel and Haydn Society
  • 1793: Northwest Indian War: General "Mad Anthony" Wayne and a 300 man detachment identify the site of St. Clair's 1791 defeat by the large number of unburied human remains at modern Fort Recovery, Ohio. Northwest Indian War
  • 1766: Mapuches in Chile launch a series of surprise attacks against the Spanish starting the Mapuche uprising of 1766. Mapuche

  • 1130: Count Roger II of Sicily is crowned the first king of Sicily. Roger II of Sicily
  • 1066: William the Conqueror, Duke of Normandy is crowned king of England, at Westminster Abbey, London. William the Conqueror
  • 1025: Coronation of Mieszko II Lambert as king of Poland. Mieszko II Lambert
  • 1000: The foundation of the Kingdom of Hungary: Hungary is established as a Christian kingdom by Stephen I of Hungary. AD 1000
  • 597: Augustine of Canterbury and his fellow-labourers baptise in Kent more than 10,000 Anglo-Saxons. Augustine of Canterbury
  • 350: Vetranio meets Constantius II at Naissus (Serbia) and is forced to abdicate his imperial title. Constantius allows him to live as a private citizen on a state pension. Vetranio
  • 333: Roman Emperor Constantine the Great elevates his youngest son Constans to the rank of Caesar. Constantine the Great

About the date itself

December 25 is the 359th day of an ordinary year, with 6 days left after it (7 in a leap year, where it is the 360th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Saturday, 2028: Monday, 2029: Tuesday, 2030: Wednesday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 25 December 2027 in 2027 is 27 Rajab 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 51.
