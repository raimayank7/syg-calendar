October 17: what happened on this day

What happened on 17 October: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2026: Juan Francisco Solórzano Foppa, a former Guatemalan tax chief, was assassinated. Juan Francisco Solórzano Foppa
  • 2001: Rehavam Ze'evi, the Israeli minister of tourism, was assassinated in revenge for the killing of PFLP leader Abu Ali Mustafa. Rehavam Ze'evi
  • 2000: A fatal rail crash at Hatfield, Hertfordshire, led to the introduction of widespread speed limit reductions throughout the British rail network and eventually caused the collapse of the railway management group Railtrack. Hatfield rail crash
  • 1973: Yom Kippur War: Egyptian forces retreated from the Battle of the Chinese Farm, allowing Israeli forces to build their first bridge across the Suez Canal. Yom Kippur War
  • 1964: Prime Minister Robert Menzies inaugurated the artificial Lake Burley Griffin (pictured) in the centre of the Australian capital Canberra. Robert Menzies
  • 1952: Indonesian Army elements surrounded the Merdeka Palace in Jakarta, demanding that President Sukarno disband the Provisional People's Representative Council. Indonesian Army
  • 1914: Off the coast of the Dutch island of Texel, a British naval squadron sank the German 7th Half Flotilla of torpedo boats in the Battle off Texel. Texel
  • 1860: The Open Championship, the oldest of the four major championships in men's golf, was first played at Prestwick Golf Club in Scotland. The Open Championship
  • 1814: A wooden beer-fermenting vat in London burst, destroying a second vat and causing a large flood of at least 128,000 imperial gallons (580,000 l; 154,000 US gal) of porter that killed eight people. London Beer Flood
  • 1771: Ascanio in Alba, an opera composed by Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart (pictured) at the age of 15, premiered in Milan. Ascanio in Alba
  • 1660: A series of executions of the commissioners who signed the death warrant of King Charles I of England concluded; six were hanged, drawn and quartered for treason. List of regicides of Charles I of England
  • 1604: German astronomer Johannes Kepler began observations of an exceptionally bright object, now known as Kepler's Supernova, that had appeared in the constellation Ophiuchus. Johannes Kepler

About the date itself

October 17 is the 290th day of an ordinary year, with 75 days left after it (76 in a leap year, where it is the 291st day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Sunday, 2028: Tuesday, 2029: Wednesday, 2030: Thursday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 17 October 2027 in 2027 is 17 Jumada al-awwal 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 41.
