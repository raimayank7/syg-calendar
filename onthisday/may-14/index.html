May 14: what happened on this day

What happened on 14 May: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 13 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2010: British politician Stephen Timms survived a murder attempt by an Islamic extremist during a constituency surgery. Stephen Timms
  • 2008: On the day of the UEFA Cup Final, violence erupted between football hooligan supporters of both teams and the Greater Manchester Police, resulting in 39 arrests and 39 injured officers. 2008 UEFA Cup final
  • 1955: Cold War: Eight Eastern Bloc countries signed a mutual defense treaty to establish the Warsaw Pact. Cold War
  • 1951: Trains ran on the Talyllyn Railway in Wales for the first time since its preservation, making it the first railway in the world to be operated by volunteers. Talyllyn Railway
  • 1940: World War II: The bulk of Dutch forces surrendered to the German Wehrmacht, ending the Battle of the Netherlands. World War II
  • 1939: In Lima, Peru, Lina Medina became the youngest confirmed mother in history, giving birth at the age of five years, seven months and twenty-one days. Lima
  • 1919: Sir Harry Hands, the mayor of Cape Town, performed the first public observance of a two-minute silence in remembrance of those killed in World War I. Harry Hands
  • 1878: The last witchcraft trial in the United States opened in Salem, Massachusetts. Salem witchcraft trial (1878)
  • 1863: American Civil War: Union troops captured Jackson, the capital of Mississippi. American Civil War
  • 1857: Mindon Min was crowned as King of Burma. Mindon Min
  • 1804: Led by Meriwether Lewis and William Clark, the Corps of Discovery left Camp Dubois near present-day Hartford, Illinois, to begin the first overland expedition to the West Coast of the United States and back. Meriwether Lewis
  • 1509: War of the League of Cambrai: French forces defeated the Venetians at the Battle of Agnadello in present-day Northern Italy. War of the League of Cambrai

More events

  • 2022: Ten people are killed in a mass shooting in Buffalo, New York. 2022 Buffalo shooting
  • 2021: China successfully lands Zhurong, the country's first Mars rover. Zhurong (rover)
  • 2010: Space Shuttle Atlantis launches on the STS-132 mission to deliver the first shuttle-launched Russian ISS component — Rassvet. This was originally slated to be the final launch of Atlantis, before Congress approved STS-135. Space Shuttle Atlantis
  • 2004: The Constitutional Court of South Korea overturns the impeachment of President Roh Moo-hyun. Constitutional Court of Korea
  • 2004: Crown Prince Frederik of Denmark and Mary Donaldson are married at Copenhagen Cathedral. Frederik X
  • 1988: Carrollton bus collision: A drunk driver traveling the wrong way on Interstate 71 near Carrollton, Kentucky, hits a converted school bus carrying a church youth group. Twenty-seven die in the crash and ensuing fire. Carrollton bus collision
  • 1980: Salvadoran Civil War: The Sumpul River massacre occurs in Chalatenango, El Salvador. Salvadoran Civil War
  • 1973: Skylab, the United States' first space station, is launched. Skylab
  • 1970: Andreas Baader is freed from custody by Ulrike Meinhof, Gudrun Ensslin and others, a pivotal moment in the formation of the Red Army Faction. Red Army Faction
  • 1955: Cold War: Eight Communist bloc countries, including the Soviet Union, sign a mutual defense treaty called the Warsaw Pact. Cold War
  • 1951: Trains run on the Talyllyn Railway in Wales for the first time since preservation, making it the first railway in the world to be operated by volunteers. Talyllyn Railway
  • 1943: World War II: A Japanese submarine sinks AHS Centaur off the coast of Queensland. AHS Centaur
  • 1940: World War II: Rotterdam, Netherlands, is bombed by the Luftwaffe of Nazi Germany despite a ceasefire, killing about 900 people and destroying the historic city center. World War II
  • 1935: The Constitution of the Philippines is ratified by a popular vote. Constitution of the Philippines
  • 1925: Mrs Dalloway, one of Virginia Woolf's earliest and best-known novels, is published. Mrs Dalloway
  • 1915: The May 14 Revolt takes place in Lisbon, Portugal. May 14 Revolt
  • 1913: Governor of New York William Sulzer approves the charter for the Rockefeller Foundation, which begins operations with a $100 million donation from John D. Rockefeller. William Sulzer
  • 1879: The first group of 463 Indian indentured laborers arrives in Fiji aboard the Leonidas. Indian indenture system
  • 1870: The first game of rugby in New Zealand is played in Nelson between Nelson College and the Nelson Rugby Football Club. Rugby football
  • 1868: Boshin War: The Battle of Utsunomiya Castle ends as former Tokugawa shogunate forces withdraw northward. Boshin War
  • 1857: Mindon Min is crowned as King of Burma in Mandalay, Burma. Mindon Min
  • 1836: The Treaties of Velasco are signed in Velasco, Texas. Treaties of Velasco
  • 1811: Paraguay: Pedro Juan Caballero, Fulgencio Yegros and José Gaspar Rodríguez de Francia start actions to depose the Spanish governor. Paraguay
  • 1804: William Clark and 42 men depart from Camp Dubois to join Meriwether Lewis at St Charles, Missouri, marking the beginning of the Lewis and Clark Expedition's historic journey up the Missouri River. William Clark
  • 1796: Edward Jenner administers the first smallpox inoculation. Edward Jenner
  • 1610: Henry IV of France is assassinated by Catholic zealot François Ravaillac, and Louis XIII ascends the throne. Henry IV of France
  • 1607: English colonists establish "James Fort", which would become Jamestown, Virginia, the earliest permanent English settlement in the Americas. Jamestown
  • 1509: Battle of Agnadello: In northern Italy, French forces defeat the Republic of Venice. Battle of Agnadello
  • 1450: A large Ottoman force begins the siege of Albanian rebel forces in Krujë, that ultimately will fail. Ottoman Empire
  • 1097: The Siege of Nicaea begins during the First Crusade. Siege of Nicaea

About the date itself

May 14 is the 134th day of an ordinary year, with 231 days left after it (232 in a leap year, where it is the 135th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Friday, 2028: Sunday, 2029: Monday, 2030: Tuesday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 14 May 2027 in 2027 is 8 Dhu al-Hijjah 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 19.
