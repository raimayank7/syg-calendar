April 7: what happened on this day

What happened on 7 April: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 14 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2017: A hijacker deliberately drove a truck into crowds along Drottninggatan in Stockholm, Sweden, killing five people. 2017 Stockholm truck attack
  • 2001: NASA's 2001 Mars Odyssey (artist's conception pictured), the longest-surviving continually active spacecraft in orbit around a planet other than Earth, launched from Cape Canaveral. NASA
  • 1995: First Chechen War: Russian paramilitary troops began a massacre of hundreds of civilians in Samashki, Chechnya. First Chechen War
  • 1994: A FedEx employee tried to hijack Federal Express Flight 705 in a failed suicide attempt. FedEx
  • 1979: Uganda–Tanzania War: Tanzanian forces defeated Ugandan troops and their Libyan allies at the Battle of Entebbe, opening the way for an advance on the Ugandan capital, Kampala. Uganda–Tanzania War
  • 1972: Communist forces overran the South Vietnamese town of Lộc Ninh. Viet Cong
  • 1949: The Rodgers and Hammerstein musical South Pacific, based on Tales of the South Pacific by James Michener, opened on Broadway. Rodgers and Hammerstein
  • 1948: The United Nations established the World Health Organization to act as a coordinating authority on international public health. United Nations
  • 1945: World War II: U.S. forces sank the Japanese battleship Yamato during Operation Kikusui I in the East China Sea. World War II
  • 1896: An Arctic expedition led by Norwegian explorer Fridtjof Nansen (pictured) reached 86°13.6′N, almost three degrees beyond the previous Farthest North latitude. Nansen's Fram expedition
  • 1862: American Civil War: Union forces defeated Confederate troops at the Battle of Shiloh, at the time the bloodiest battle in U.S. history, in Hardin County, Tennessee. American Civil War
  • 1831: Pedro II became emperor of the Empire of Brazil. Pedro II of Brazil

More events

  • 2022: Ketanji Brown Jackson is confirmed for the Supreme Court of the United States, becoming the first black female justice. Ketanji Brown Jackson
  • 2020: COVID-19 pandemic: China ends its lockdown in Wuhan. COVID-19 pandemic
  • 2018: Former Brazilian president, Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, is arrested for corruption by determination of Judge Sérgio Moro, from the "Car-Wash Operation". Lula stayed imprisoned for 580 days, before being released by the Brazilian Supreme Court. Brazilians
  • 2017: U.S. President Donald Trump orders the 2017 Shayrat missile strike against Syria in retaliation for the Khan Shaykhun chemical attack. Donald Trump
  • 2011: A gunman opens fire at an elementary school in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, killing twelve children and injuring 22 others before committing suicide. Rio de Janeiro school shooting
  • 2005: First release of Git distributed version control system. Git
  • 2003: Haitian president Jean-Bertrand Aristide demands reparations of $21 billion from France for the Haitian independence debt. Jean-Bertrand Aristide
  • 1995: First Chechen War: Russian paramilitary troops begin a massacre of civilians in Samashki, Chechnya. First Chechen War
  • 1994: Auburn Calloway attempts to destroy Federal Express Flight 705 in order to allow his family to benefit from his life insurance policy. Federal Express Flight 705
  • 1990: John Poindexter is convicted for his role in the Iran–Contra affair. In 1991 the convictions are reversed on appeal. John Poindexter
  • 1983: During STS-6, astronauts Story Musgrave and Don Peterson perform the first Space Shuttle spacewalk. STS-6
  • 1980: During the Iran hostage crisis, the United States severs relations with Iran. Iran hostage crisis
  • 1976: Member of Parliament and suspected spy John Stonehouse resigns from the Labour Party after being arrested for faking his own death. John Stonehouse
  • 1971: Vietnam War: President Richard Nixon announces his decision to quicken the pace of Vietnamization. Vietnam War
  • 1965: Representatives of the National Congress of American Indians testify before members of the US Senate in Washington, D.C., against the termination of the Colville tribe. National Congress of American Indians
  • 1956: Francoist Spain agrees to surrender its protectorate in Morocco. Francoist Spain
  • 1948: The World Health Organization is established by the United Nations. World Health Organization
  • 1944: In the Fragheto massacre, soldiers belonging to the German 356th Infantry Division kill 30 Italian civilians and 15 partisans near Casteldelci in central-northern Italy. Fragheto massacre
  • 1943: Ioannis Rallis becomes collaborationist Prime Minister of Greece during the Axis Occupation. Ioannis Rallis
  • 1939: Benito Mussolini declares an Italian protectorate over Albania and forces King Zog I into exile. Benito Mussolini
  • 1933: Prohibition in the United States is repealed for beer of no more than 3.2% alcohol by weight, eight months before the ratification of the Twenty-first Amendment to the United States Constitution. (Now celebrated as National Beer Day in the United States.) Prohibition in the United States
  • 1922: Teapot Dome scandal: United States Secretary of the Interior Albert B. Fall leases federal petroleum reserves to private oil companies on excessively generous terms. Teapot Dome scandal
  • 1906: The Algeciras Conference gives France and Spain control over Morocco. Algeciras Conference
  • 1862: American Civil War: The Union's Army of the Tennessee and the Army of the Ohio defeat the Confederate Army of Mississippi near Shiloh, Tennessee. American Civil War
  • 1805: Lewis and Clark Expedition: The Corps of Discovery breaks camp among the Mandan tribe and resumes its journey West along the Missouri River. Lewis and Clark Expedition
  • 1798: The Mississippi Territory is organized from disputed territory claimed by both the United States and the Spanish Empire. It is expanded in 1804 and again in 1812. Mississippi Territory
  • 1788: Settlers establish Marietta, Ohio, the first permanent settlement created by U.S. citizens in the recently organized Northwest Territory. List of early settlers of Marietta, Ohio
  • 1724: Premiere performance of Bach's St John Passion, BWV 245, at St. Nicholas Church, Leipzig. St John Passion structure
  • 1449: Felix V abdicates his claim to the papacy, ending the reign of the final Antipope. Amadeus VIII of Savoy
  • 1141: Empress Matilda becomes the first female ruler of England, adopting the title "Lady of the English". Empress Matilda

About the date itself

April 7 is the 97th day of an ordinary year, with 268 days left after it (269 in a leap year, where it is the 98th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Wednesday, 2028: Friday, 2029: Saturday, 2030: Sunday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 7 April 2027 in 2027 is 30 Shawwal 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 14.
