September 7: what happened on this day

What happened on 7 September: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2011: Yak-Service Flight 9633, carrying the players and coaching staff of the ice hockey team Lokomotiv Yaroslavl, crashed on take-off near Yaroslavl, Russia, resulting in the deaths of 44 of the 45 people on board. Lokomotiv Yaroslavl plane crash
  • 2010: A Chinese fishing trawler operating in disputed waters collided with Japan Coast Guard patrol boats near the Senkaku Islands, sparking a major diplomatic dispute between the two countries. Fishing trawler
  • 1999: Three weeks after an earthquake struck northwestern Turkey, a second earthquake struck Athens, causing Greece and Turkey to initiate "earthquake diplomacy". 1999 İzmit earthquake
  • 1996: American rapper Tupac Shakur was fatally shot in Paradise, Nevada, dying six days later. Tupac Shakur
  • 1984: An explosion on board a Maltese patrol boat that was disposing of illegal fireworks at sea off Gozo killed seven soldiers and policemen. C23 tragedy
  • 1940: Second World War: The Luftwaffe changed their strategy in the Battle of Britain and began bombing London and other cities and towns. World War II
  • 1916: World War I: The Supreme War Command was established to oversee the armed forces of all the Central Powers. World War I
  • 1901: With Beijing occupied by foreign troops from the Eight-Nation Alliance, Qing China was forced to sign the Boxer Protocol, an unequal treaty that ended the Boxer Rebellion. Eight-Nation Alliance
  • 1822: Pedro I declared the independence of Brazil from Portugal, establishing the Empire of Brazil (flag pictured). Pedro I of Brazil
  • 1778: Anglo-French War: Having established an alliance with the United States, France invaded the Caribbean island of Dominica and captured its British fort. Anglo-French War (1778–1783)
  • 1642: First English Civil War: Royalist and Parliamentarian forces clashed in the Battle of Babylon Hill, after which both sides claimed victory. First English Civil War
  • 1191: Third Crusade: Crusaders under Richard I of England defeated Ayyubid troops under Saladin at the Battle of Arsuf (depicted) in present-day Israel. Third Crusade

  • 1965: During an Indo-Pakistani War, China announces that it will reinforce its troops on the Indian border. India–Pakistan war of 1965

  • 1923: The International Criminal Police Organization (INTERPOL) is formed. Interpol
  • 1921: The Legion of Mary, the largest apostolic organization of lay people in the Catholic Church, is founded in Dublin, Ireland. Legion of Mary
  • 1911: French poet Guillaume Apollinaire is arrested and put in jail on suspicion of stealing the Mona Lisa from the Louvre museum. Guillaume Apollinaire
  • 1907: Cunard Line's RMS Lusitania sets sail on her maiden voyage from Liverpool, England, to New York City. Cunard Line

  • 1864: American Civil War: Atlanta is evacuated on orders of Union General William Tecumseh Sherman. Atlanta

  • 1822: Dom Pedro I declares Brazil independent from Portugal on the shores of the Ipiranga Brook in São Paulo. Pedro I of Brazil
  • 1812: French invasion of Russia: The Battle of Borodino, the bloodiest battle of the Napoleonic Wars, is fought near Moscow and results in a French victory. French invasion of Russia
  • 1764: Election of Stanisław August Poniatowski as the last ruler of the Polish–Lithuanian Commonwealth. Stanisław August Poniatowski
  • 1714: The treaty of Baden between the kingdom of France and the Holy Roman Empire is ratified. Treaty of Baden (1714)
  • 1652: Around 15,000 Han farmers and militia rebel against Dutch rule on Taiwan. Han Chinese
  • 1620: The town of Kokkola (Swedish: Karleby) is founded by King Gustavus Adolphus of Sweden. Kokkola
  • 1303: Guillaume de Nogaret takes Pope Boniface VIII prisoner on behalf of Philip IV of France. Guillaume de Nogaret
  • 1191: Third Crusade: Battle of Arsuf: Richard I of England defeats Saladin at Arsuf. Third Crusade

About the date itself

September 7 is the 250th day of an ordinary year, with 115 days left after it (116 in a leap year, where it is the 251st day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Tuesday, 2028: Thursday, 2029: Friday, 2030: Saturday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 7 September 2027 in 2027 is 6 Rabi' al-thani 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 36.
