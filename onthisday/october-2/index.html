October 2: what happened on this day

What happened on 2 October: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 14 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2018: The Washington Post journalist Jamal Khashoggi was assassinated in the Saudi consulate in Istanbul, Turkey. The Washington Post
  • 2006: A gunman killed five Amish girls before committing suicide in a one-room schoolhouse in Nickel Mines, Pennsylvania. West Nickel Mines School shooting
  • 2005: Typhoon Longwang made landfall in China as the deadliest tropical cyclone in that year to impact the country. Typhoon Longwang
  • 1990: A hijacked airliner collided with two other planes while attempting to land at Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport in China, killing 128 and injuring 71. Aircraft hijacking
  • 1971: Nguyễn Văn Thiệu was re-elected unopposed as President of South Vietnam. Nguyễn Văn Thiệu
  • 1967: Thurgood Marshall was sworn in as the first African-American justice of the Supreme Court of the United States. Thurgood Marshall
  • 1942: Second World War: HMS Curacoa (pictured) was accidentally rammed and sunk by RMS Queen Mary while escorting the liner to provide protection from submarine attacks. World War II
  • 1913: The Shubert Theatre (pictured) opened on Broadway with a production of Hamlet. Shubert Theatre (Broadway)
  • 1879: Qing China signed the Treaty of Livadia with the Russian Empire, but the terms were so unfavorable that the Chinese government refused to ratify the treaty. Qing dynasty
  • 1835: Mexican dragoons dispatched to disarm settlers at Gonzales in Mexican Texas encountered stiff resistance from a Texian militia at the Battle of Gonzales, the first armed engagement of the Texas Revolution. Dragoon
  • 1766: As part of wider food riots, citizens in Nottingham, England, looted large quantities of cheese; one man was killed during attempts to restore order. 1766 food riots
  • 1470: With King Edward IV of England forced to flee to the Burgundian Netherlands after a rebellion organised by Richard Neville, 16th Earl of Warwick, Henry VI was restored to the throne. Edward IV

  • 811: Michael I Rangabe is proclaimed as Byzantine Emperor, forcing the rival claimant Staurakios to abdicate his claim. Michael I Rangabe

About the date itself

October 2 is the 275th day of an ordinary year, with 90 days left after it (91 in a leap year, where it is the 276th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Saturday, 2028: Monday, 2029: Tuesday, 2030: Wednesday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 2 October 2027 in 2027 is 2 Jumada al-awwal 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 39.
