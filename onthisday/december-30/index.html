December 30: what happened on this day

What happened on 30 December: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 13 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2013: Supporters of Congolese religious leader Paul-Joseph Mukungubila carried out a series of attacks on television studios, the airport and a military base in Kinshasa. Paul-Joseph Mukungubila
  • 2009: Due to a rupture of the Lanzhou–Zhengzhou–Changsha pipeline in Shaanxi, China, caused approximately 150,000 l (40,000 US gal) of diesel oil flowed down the Wei River before finally reaching the Yellow River six days later. Lanzhou–Zhengzhou–Changsha pipeline
  • 2006: The Indonesian ferry MV Senopati Nusantara sank in the Java Sea during a storm, killing at least 400 people. MV Senopati Nusantara
  • 2006: Saddam Hussein, the former president of Iraq, was executed after being found guilty of crimes against humanity by the Iraqi High Tribunal. Saddam Hussein
  • 2005: Tropical Storm Zeta (pictured) became a tropical depression, making it the record-breaking 28th tropical cyclone of the 2005 Atlantic hurricane season, the most active in recorded history until 2020. Tropical Storm Zeta
  • 1958: The Guatemalan Air Force fired on Mexican fishing boats that had strayed into Guatemalan territory, triggering an eight-month conflict. Guatemalan Air Force
  • 1940: The Arroyo Seco Parkway, one of the first freeways built in the U.S., connecting downtown Los Angeles with Pasadena, California, was officially dedicated. Arroyo Seco Parkway
  • 1935: Second Italo-Ethiopian War: In retaliation for the execution of an Italian prisoner of war, the Italian Air Force destroyed a field hospital of the Swedish Red Cross in Dolo. Second Italo-Ethiopian War
  • 1903: In one of the deadliest single-building fires in United States history, the Iroquois Theatre fire in Chicago killed more than 600 people. Iroquois Theatre fire
  • 1845: The University of Galway, University College Cork and Queen's University Belfast were established as part of the Queen's University of Ireland. University of Galway
  • 1813: War of 1812: British forces captured Buffalo, New York, and burned down nearly all its buildings. War of 1812
  • 1460: Wars of the Roses: At the Battle of Wakefield, Lancastrian forces destroyed the Yorkist army and killed Richard of York at Sandal Magna in West Yorkshire, England. Wars of the Roses

About the date itself

December 30 is the 364th day of an ordinary year, with 1 days left after it (2 in a leap year, where it is the 365th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Thursday, 2028: Saturday, 2029: Sunday, 2030: Monday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 30 December 2027 in 2027 is 2 Sha'ban 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 52.
