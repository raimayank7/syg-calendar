August 11: what happened on this day

What happened on 11 August: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 14 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2012: At least 306 people were killed and 3,000 others injured in a pair of earthquakes near Tabriz, Iran. 2012 East Azerbaijan earthquakes
  • 1999: A tornado struck downtown Salt Lake City, damaging 120 homes and injuring over 100 people. 1999 Salt Lake City tornado
  • 1999: Ken Levine's System Shock 2 was released to mediocre sales, but later received critical acclaim and influenced subsequent first-person shooter game design. Ken Levine (game developer)
  • 1992: The Mall of America (pictured) in Bloomington, Minnesota, opened as the largest shopping mall in the United States. Mall of America
  • 1977: The U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration initiated an investigation into the alleged vulnerability of the Ford Pinto to fuel leakage and fire in a rear-end collision. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration
  • 1975: The Timorese Democratic Union launched a coup in Portuguese Timor, starting the East Timorese civil war. Timorese Democratic Union
  • 1973: At a party in New York City, Jamaican musician DJ Kool Herc (pictured) began rapping during an extended break, laying the foundation for hip hop music. DJ Kool Herc
  • 1952: King Talal of Jordan was forced to abdicate due to mental illness and was succeeded by his eldest son, Hussein. Talal of Jordan
  • 1929: The first Bud Billiken Parade and Picnic, the oldest and largest African-American parade in the United States, was held in Chicago. Bud Billiken Parade and Picnic
  • 1786: Francis Light founded George Town (city hall pictured), the first British settlement in Southeast Asia and the present-day capital of the Malaysian state of Penang. Francis Light
  • 1492: The first conclave to be held in the Sistine Chapel elected Roderic Borja as Pope Alexander VI to succeed Innocent VIII. 1492 conclave
  • 1309: Reconquista: Aragonese forces led by King James II landed on the coast of Almería, beginning an ultimately unsuccessful siege of the city, then held by the Emirate of Granada. Reconquista

More events

  • 2023: Luna 25 launches from the Vostochny Cosmodrome. Luna 25
  • 2017: At least 41 people are killed and another 179 injured after two passenger trains collide in Alexandria, Egypt. Alexandria train collision
  • 2006: The oil tanker MT Solar 1 sinks off the coast of Guimaras and Negros Islands in the Philippines, causing the country's worst oil spill. Oil tanker
  • 2003: Jemaah Islamiyah leader Riduan Isamuddin, better known as Hambali, is arrested in Bangkok, Thailand. Jemaah Islamiyah
  • 1996: The killing of Tassos Isaac by Turkish nationalists of the Grey Wolves in the United Nations Buffer Zone in Cyprus. Killing of Tassos Isaac
  • 1995: The Russell Hill subway accident in Toronto kills three and injures 30, due to a combination of human error and a safety system design flaw. 1995 Russell Hill subway accident
  • 1991: Nickelodeon's first line of "Nicktoons" (Doug, Rugrats and Ren & Stimpy) premiere on the channel. Nickelodeon
  • 1984: "We begin bombing in five minutes": United States President Ronald Reagan, while running for re-election, jokes while preparing to make his weekly Saturday address on National Public Radio. We begin bombing in five minutes
  • 1979: Two Aeroflot Tupolev Tu-134s collide over the Ukrainian city of Dniprodzerzhynsk and crash, killing all 178 aboard both airliners. Aeroflot
  • 1975: East Timor: Governor Mário Lemos Pires of Portuguese Timor abandons the capital Dili, following a coup by the Timorese Democratic Union (UDT) and the outbreak of civil war between UDT and Fretilin. Timor-Leste
  • 1972: Vietnam War: The last United States ground combat unit leaves South Vietnam. Vietnam War
  • 1965: Race riots (the Watts Riots) begin in the Watts area of Los Angeles, California. Watts riots
  • 1961: The former Portuguese territories in India of Dadra and Nagar Haveli are merged to create the Union Territory Dadra and Nagar Haveli. India
  • 1960: Chad declares independence from France. Chad
  • 1952: Hussein bin Talal is proclaimed King of Jordan. Hussein of Jordan
  • 1942: Actress Hedy Lamarr and composer George Antheil receive a patent for a Frequency-hopping spread spectrum communication system that later became the basis for modern technologies in wireless telephones, two-way radio communications, and Wi-Fi. Hedy Lamarr
  • 1929: Babe Ruth becomes the first baseball player to hit 500 home runs in his career with a home run at League Park in Cleveland, Ohio. Babe Ruth
  • 1920: The Latvian–Soviet Peace Treaty, which relinquished Russia's authority and pretenses to Latvia, is signed, ending the Latvian War of Independence. Latvian–Soviet Peace Treaty
  • 1919: Germany's Weimar Constitution is signed into law. Weimar Constitution
  • 1898: Spanish–American War: American troops enter the city of Mayagüez, Puerto Rico. Spanish–American War
  • 1858: The Eiger in the Bernese Alps is ascended for the first time by Charles Barrington accompanied by Christian Almer and Peter Bohren. Eiger
  • 1812: Peninsular War: French troops engage British-Portuguese forces in the Battle of Majadahonda. Peninsular War
  • 1804: Francis II assumes the title of first Emperor of Austria. Francis II, Holy Roman Emperor
  • 1685: Morean War: The 49-day Siege of Coron ends with the surrender and massacre of its garrison by the Venetians. Morean War
  • 1492: Rodrigo de Borja is elected as Head of the Catholic Church, taking the name Pope Alexander VI. Pope Alexander VI
  • 1332: Wars of Scottish Independence: Battle of Dupplin Moor: Scots under Domhnall II, Earl of Mar are routed by Edward Balliol. Wars of Scottish Independence
  • 1315: The Great Famine of Europe becomes so dire that even the king of England has difficulties buying bread for himself and his entourage. Great Famine of 1315–1317
  • 490: Battle of Adda: The Goths under Theodoric the Great and his ally Alaric II defeat the forces of Odoacer on the Adda River, near Milan. Goths
  • 117: Hadrian is proclaimed Roman emperor, two days after Trajan's death. AD 117
  • -2492: Traditional date of the defeat of Bel by Hayk, progenitor and founder of the Armenian nation. 25th century BC

About the date itself
