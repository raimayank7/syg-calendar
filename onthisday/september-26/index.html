September 26: what happened on this day

What happened on 26 September: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2016: The Ecnomiohyla rabborum became extinct when the last known surviving member died in captivity. Ecnomiohyla rabborum
  • 2014: Forty-three students of the Ayotzinapa Rural Teachers' College in Iguala, Mexico, were kidnapped and probably later killed. Ayotzinapa Rural Normal School
  • 2008: Swiss pilot and inventor Yves Rossy (pictured) flew a wingpack powered by jet engines across the English Channel. Yves Rossy
  • 1983: The racing yacht Australia II, captained by John Bertrand, won the America's Cup and ended the New York Yacht Club's 132-year defence of the trophy. Yacht racing
  • 1968: The Beatles completed the recording of John Lennon's song "Happiness Is a Warm Gun", regarded by all the band members as their favourite on the album The Beatles. The Beatles
  • 1959: Japan was struck by Typhoon Vera, the strongest and deadliest typhoon on record to make landfall on the country, causing damage in excess of US$261 million and more than 5,000 deaths. Typhoon Vera
  • 1933: As gangster Machine Gun Kelly surrendered to the FBI, he supposedly shouted "Don't shoot, G-Men!", which became a nickname for FBI agents. Machine Gun Kelly (gangster)
  • 1928: The Republic of China adopted Gwoyeu Romatzyh (designer pictured) as the official system for romanization of Mandarin Chinese. Nationalist government
  • 1917: World War I: The Battle of Polygon Wood, part of the Battle of Passchendaele, began near Ypres, Belgium. World War I
  • 1580: English explorer Francis Drake's galleon the Golden Hind (replica pictured) sailed into Plymouth, completing his circumnavigation of the globe. Francis Drake
  • 1493: Pope Alexander VI issued the papal bull Dudum siquidem, one of the Bulls of Donation, marking the beginning of the Spanish colonization of the Americas. Pope Alexander VI
  • 1087: William II, son of William the Conqueror, was crowned king of England. William II of England

More events

  • 2024: Hurricane Helene makes landfall in Perry, Florida as a Category 4 hurricane, killing over 250 people, causing US$78.7 billion in damage and becoming the deadliest hurricane in the mainland United States since Katrina. Hurricane Helene
  • 2022: A mass shooting occurs at a school in Izhevsk, Udmurtia, Russia, resulting in the deaths of 18 people, including 11 children. Izhevsk school shooting
  • 2010: The Philippine Bar exam bombing occurs near the De La Salle University in Taft Avenue, Manila injuring 47 people. 2010 Philippine Bar exam bombing
  • 2009: Typhoon Ketsana hits the Philippines, China, Vietnam, Cambodia, Laos and Thailand, causing 700 fatalities. Typhoon Ketsana
  • 2000: Anti-globalization protests in Prague (some 20,000 protesters) turn violent during the IMF and World Bank summits. 2000 Prague anti-globalization protests
  • 1997: A Garuda Indonesia Airbus A300 crashes near Medan airport, killing 234. Garuda Indonesia
  • 1997: An earthquake strikes the Italian regions of Umbria and the Marche, causing part of the Basilica of St. Francis at Assisi to collapse. 1997 Umbria–Marche earthquakes
  • 1992: A Nigerian Air Force Lockheed C-130 Hercules crashes in Ejigbo, Lagos, killing 159. Nigerian Air Force
  • 1983: Soviet Air Force officer Stanislav Petrov identifies a report of an incoming nuclear missile as a computer error and not an American first strike, thus preventing nuclear war. Stanislav Petrov
  • 1983: Soyuz 7K-ST No. 16L, intended to launch a crew to the Salyut 7 space station, explodes on the launch pad. The launch escape system is activated before the Soyuz-U rocket explodes, saving the crew. Soyuz 7K-ST No.16L
  • 1978: Air Caribbean Flight 309 crashes in Residencial Las Casas in San Juan, Puerto Rico, killing six. Air Caribbean Flight 309
  • 1960: In Chicago, the first televised debate takes place between presidential candidates Richard M. Nixon and John F. Kennedy. United States presidential debates
  • 1954: The Japanese rail ferry Tōya Maru sinks during a typhoon in the Tsugaru Strait, Japan, killing 1,172. Tōya Maru
  • 1950: Korean War: United Nations troops recapture Seoul from North Korean forces. Korean War
  • 1942: Holocaust: Senior SS official August Frank issues a memorandum detailing how Jews should be "evacuated". The Holocaust
  • 1933: As gangster Machine Gun Kelly surrenders to the FBI, he shouts out, "Don't shoot, G-Men!", which becomes a nickname for FBI agents. Machine Gun Kelly (gangster)
  • 1919: Ukrainian War of Independence: The Revolutionary Insurgent Army of Ukraine defeats the White Russian Volunteer Army at the Battle of Peregonovka. Ukrainian War of Independence
  • 1918: World War I: The Meuse-Argonne Offensive began which would last until the total surrender of German forces. Meuse–Argonne offensive
  • 1914: The United States Federal Trade Commission is established by the Federal Trade Commission Act. Federal Trade Commission
  • 1905: Albert Einstein publishes the third of his Annus Mirabilis papers, introducing the special theory of relativity. Albert Einstein
  • 1810: A new Act of Succession is adopted by the Riksdag of the Estates, and Jean Baptiste Bernadotte becomes heir to the Swedish throne. Swedish Act of Succession
  • 1789: George Washington appoints Thomas Jefferson the first United States Secretary of State. George Washington
  • 1777: American Revolutionary War: British troops capture and begin the occupation of Philadelphia, which had been serving as the American capital city, during the Philadelphia campaign. American Revolutionary War
  • 1687: Morean War: The Parthenon in Athens, used as a gunpowder depot by the Ottoman garrison, is partially destroyed after being bombarded during the Siege of the Acropolis by Venetian forces. Morean War
  • 1493: Pope Alexander VI issues the papal bull Dudum siquidem to the Spanish, extending the grant of new lands he made them in Inter caetera. Pope Alexander VI
  • 1423: Hundred Years' War: A French army defeats the English at the Battle of La Brossinière. Hundred Years' War
  • 1345: Friso-Hollandic Wars: Frisians defeat Holland in the Battle of Warns. Battle of Staveren
  • 1143: Election of pope Celestine II following the death of pope Innocent II. 1143 papal election
  • 1142: Stephen of Blois's army approaches Oxford, beginning the siege of Oxford. Stephen, King of England
  • 715: Ragenfrid defeats Theudoald at the Battle of Compiègne. Battle of Compiègne

About the date itself

September 26 is the 269th day of an ordinary year, with 96 days left after it (97 in a leap year, where it is the 270th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Sunday, 2028: Tuesday, 2029: Wednesday, 2030: Thursday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 26 September 2027 in 2027 is 25 Rabi' al-thani 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 38.
