April 11: what happened on this day

What happened on 11 April: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 13 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2023: A Myanmar Air Force airstrike killed at least 100 villagers in Pazigyi. Myanmar Air Force
  • 2011: A bomb exploded at Oktyabrskaya station on the Minsk Metro in Belarus, killing 15 people and injuring more than 200. 2011 Minsk Metro bombing
  • 2001: In a FIFA World Cup qualifying match, Australia defeated American Samoa 31–0, the largest margin of victory recorded in international football. 2002 FIFA World Cup qualification
  • 1993: Prisoners at the Southern Ohio Correctional Facility near Lucasville, Ohio, rioted and took over the prison for 11 days, resulting in the deaths of one officer and nine inmates. Southern Ohio Correctional Facility
  • 1968: Rudi Dutschke, the most prominent leader of the German student movement, survived an assassination attempt, which led to the largest protests to that date in Germany. Rudi Dutschke
  • 1963: Pope John XXIII issued Pacem in terris, the first papal encyclical addressed to "all men of good will" rather than only to Catholics. Pope John XXIII
  • 1951: The Stone of Scone, removed months earlier by Scottish students, was found on the altar of Arbroath Abbey. Stone of Scone
  • 1921: Emir Abdullah established the first centralised government in the recently created British protectorate of Transjordan. Abdullah I of Jordan
  • 1908: Blücher, the last armored cruiser built by the Imperial German Navy, was launched. SMS Blücher
  • 1885: Luton Town Football Club was founded in England. Luton Town F.C.
  • 1809: Napoleonic Wars: A hastily assembled Royal Navy fleet launched an assault against the main strength of the French Atlantic Fleet; an incomplete victory led to political turmoil in Britain. Napoleonic Wars
  • 1689: William III and Mary II (both pictured) were crowned joint sovereigns of England in a ceremony at Westminster Abbey. William III of England

Prison riot

About the date itself

April 11 is the 101st day of an ordinary year, with 264 days left after it (265 in a leap year, where it is the 102nd day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Sunday, 2028: Tuesday, 2029: Wednesday, 2030: Thursday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 11 April 2027 in 2027 is 4 Dhu al-Qa'dah 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 14.
