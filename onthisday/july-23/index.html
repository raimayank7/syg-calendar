July 23: what happened on this day

What happened on 23 July: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2010: The English-Irish boy band One Direction (pictured) were formed while auditioning for the 2010 series of the British singing competition The X Factor. One Direction
  • 2001: Megawati Sukarnoputri was sworn in (pictured) as the first female president of Indonesia following her predecessor's impeachment. Megawati Sukarnoputri
  • 1999: Forty-seven people were arrested in Tulia, Texas, for dealing cocaine; 35 of them were pardoned by the governor of Texas years later. 1999 Tulia drug arrests
  • 1995: Hale–Bopp, one of the most widely observed comets of the 20th century, was independently discovered by astronomers Alan Hale and Thomas Bopp. Comet Hale–Bopp
  • 1982: A helicopter crashed during the filming of Twilight Zone: The Movie in Valencia, California, killing actor Vic Morrow and two child actors. Twilight Zone accident
  • 1968: A shootout between police and a Black power group began in Cleveland, Ohio, sparking three days of rioting. Glenville shootout
  • 1942: The Holocaust: The gas chambers at Treblinka extermination camp began operation, killing 6,500 Jews who had been transported from the Warsaw Ghetto the day before. The Holocaust
  • 1942: Bulgarian poet and communist activist Nikola Vaptsarov was executed by firing squad. Nikola Vaptsarov
  • 1927: Wilfred Rhodes of England and Yorkshire became the only person to play in 1,000 first-class cricket matches. Wilfred Rhodes
  • 1921: The first National Congress of the Chinese Communist Party opened in a house in Shanghai. 1st National Congress of the Chinese Communist Party
  • 1891: During a visit of the French Navy to Kronstadt in Russia, the two nations reached a secret agreement on a joint response to any future war in Europe. Kronstadt–Toulon naval visits
  • 1860: The trial of the Eastbourne manslaughter, which later became an important legal precedent in the United Kingdom for discussions of corporal punishment in schools, began in Lewes. Eastbourne manslaughter

More events

  • 2018: A wildfire in East Attica kills at least 102 people. It is the deadliest wildfire in the history of Greece. 2018 Attica wildfires
  • 2014: TransAsia Airways Flight 222 crashes in Xixi village near Huxi, Penghu, during approach to Penghu Airport. Forty-eight of the 58 people on board are killed and five more people on the ground are injured. TransAsia Airways Flight 222
  • 2011: A high-speed train rear-ends another on a viaduct on the Yongtaiwen railway line in Wenzhou, Zhejiang province, China, resulting in 40 deaths. High-speed rail
  • 2005: Three bombs explode in the Naama Bay area of Sharm El Sheikh, Egypt, killing 88 people. 2005 Sharm El Sheikh bombings
  • 1999: ANA Flight 61 is hijacked in Tokyo, Japan by Yuji Nishizawa. All Nippon Airways Flight 61
  • 1997: Digital Equipment Corporation files antitrust charges against chipmaker Intel. Digital Equipment Corporation
  • 1993: China Northwest Airlines Flight 2119 crashes during takeoff from Yinchuan Xihuayuan Airport in Yinchuan, Ningxia, China, killing 55 people. China Northwest Airlines Flight 2119
  • 1992: Abkhazia declares independence from Georgia. Abkhazia
  • 1983: Thirteen Sri Lanka Army soldiers are killed after a deadly ambush by the militant Liberation Tigers of Tamil Eelam. Sri Lanka Army
  • 1982: Outside Santa Clarita, California, actor Vic Morrow and two children are killed when a helicopter crashes onto them while shooting a scene from Twilight Zone: The Movie. Santa Clarita, California
  • 1974: The Greek military junta collapses, and former Prime Minister Konstantinos Karamanlis is invited to lead the new government, beginning Greece's metapolitefsi era. Greek junta
  • 1970: Qaboos bin Said al Said becomes Sultan of Oman after overthrowing his father, Said bin Taimur initiating massive reforms, modernization programs and end to a decade long civil war. Qaboos bin Said
  • 1968: The only successful hijacking of an El Al aircraft takes place when a Boeing 707 carrying ten crew and 38 passengers is taken over by three members of the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine. The aircraft was en route from Rome, Italy to Lod, Israel. El Al
  • 1962: Telstar relays the first publicly transmitted, live trans-Atlantic television program, featuring Walter Cronkite. Telstar
  • 1962: Jackie Robinson becomes the first African American to be inducted into the National Baseball Hall of Fame. Jackie Robinson
  • 1945: The post-war legal processes against Philippe Pétain, the former president of Vichy France begin. Philippe Pétain
  • 1943: World War II: The British destroyers HMS Eclipse and HMS Laforey sink the Italian submarine Ascianghi in the Mediterranean after she torpedoes the cruiser HMS Newfoundland. HMS Eclipse (H08)
  • 1942: Bulgarian poet and communist activist Nikola Vaptsarov is executed by firing squad. Bulgaria
  • 1936: In Catalonia, Spain, the Unified Socialist Party of Catalonia is founded through the merger of Socialist and Communist parties. Catalonia
  • 1926: Fox Film buys the patents of the Movietone sound system for recording sound onto film. Fox Film
  • 1919: Prince Regent Aleksander Karađorđević signs the decree establishing the University of Ljubljana. Alexander I of Yugoslavia
  • 1908: The Second Constitution is accepted by the Ottomans. Second Constitutional Era
  • 1903: The Ford Motor Company sells its first car. Ford Motor Company
  • 1881: The Boundary Treaty of 1881 between Chile and Argentina is signed in Buenos Aires. Boundary Treaty of 1881 between Chile and Argentina
  • 1862: American Civil War: Henry Halleck becomes general-in-chief of the Union Army. American Civil War
  • 1840: The Province of Canada is created by the Act of Union. Province of Canada
  • 1821: While the Mora Rebellion continues, Greeks capture Monemvasia Castle. Turkish troops and citizens are transferred to Asia Minor's coasts. Greek War of Independence
  • 1793: Kingdom of Prussia re-conquers Mainz from France. Kingdom of Prussia
  • 1677: Scanian War: Denmark–Norway captures the harbor town of Marstrand from Sweden. Scanian War
  • 1319: A Knights Hospitaller fleet scores a crushing victory over an Aydinid fleet off Chios. Knights Hospitaller

About the date itself

July 23 is the 204th day of an ordinary year, with 161 days left after it (162 in a leap year, where it is the 205th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Friday, 2028: Sunday, 2029: Monday, 2030: Tuesday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 23 July 2027 in 2027 is 19 Safar 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 29.
