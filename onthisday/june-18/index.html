June 18: what happened on this day

What happened on 18 June: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 14 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2023: The Titan submersible imploded during a dive to the RMS Titanic wreck, killing all five people aboard. Titan (submersible)
  • 2012: Salman bin Abdulaziz Al Saud was appointed the crown prince of Saudi Arabia. Salman of Saudi Arabia
  • 1994: The Troubles: Ulster Volunteer Force members attacked a crowded bar in Loughinisland, Northern Ireland, with assault rifles, killing six people. The Troubles
  • 1983: Aboard Space Shuttle Challenger, astronaut Sally Ride became the first American woman and third one overall in space. Space Shuttle Challenger
  • 1972: British European Airways Flight 548 crashed near Staines-upon-Thames less than three minutes after departing from Heathrow Airport in London, killing all 118 people aboard in the worst air accident in the UK. British European Airways Flight 548
  • 1958: English composer Benjamin Britten's one-act opera Noye's Fludde was premiered at the Aldeburgh Festival. Benjamin Britten
  • 1953: A Douglas C-124 Globemaster II aircraft crashed just after takeoff from Tachikawa, Japan, killing all 129 people on board. Douglas C-124 Globemaster II
  • 1940: World War II: Charles de Gaulle gave his Appeal of 18 June speech, often considered to be the origin of the French Resistance. World War II
  • 1898: The Cadaver Tomb of René of Chalon (pictured) in Bar-le-Duc, France, was designated a monument historique. Cadaver Tomb of René of Chalon
  • 1858: Charles Darwin received a manuscript by fellow naturalist Alfred Russel Wallace on natural selection, which encouraged him to publish his own theory of evolution. Charles Darwin
  • 1757: Third Silesian War: The Austrian victory at the Battle of Kolín forced Prussian leader Frederick the Great to give up the Siege of Prague and retreat to Saxony. Third Silesian War
  • 618: Sui–Tang transition: Chinese governor Li Yuan (pictured) declared himself emperor, establishing the Tang dynasty. Transition from Sui to Tang

More events

  • 2023: Titan, a submersible operated by OceanGate Expeditions, implodes in the North Atlantic Ocean while attempting to view the wreck of the Titanic, killing all five people on board, including OceanGate co-founder and CEO Stockton Rush. Titan (submersible)
  • 2018: An earthquake of magnitude 6.1 strikes northern Osaka. 2018 Osaka earthquake
  • 2007: The Charleston Sofa Super Store fire kills nine firefighters in Charleston, South Carolina. Charleston Sofa Super Store fire
  • 1998: Propair Flight 420 crashes near Montréal–Mirabel International Airport in Quebec, Canada, killing 11. Propair Flight 420
  • 1984: A major clash between about 5,000 police and a similar number of striking miners takes place at Orgreave, South Yorkshire, during the 1984–85 UK miners' strike. Battle of Orgreave
  • 1983: Mona Mahmudnizhad, together with nine other women of the Baháʼí Faith, is sentenced to death and hanged in Shiraz, Iran, over her religious beliefs. Mona Mahmudnizhad
  • 1981: The Lockheed F-117 Nighthawk, the first operational aircraft initially designed around stealth technology, makes its first flight. Lockheed F-117 Nighthawk
  • 1972: Staines air disaster: One hundred eighteen people are killed when a BEA H.S. Trident crashes minutes after takeoff from London's Heathrow Airport. British European Airways Flight 548
  • 1965: Vietnam War: The United States Air Force uses B-52 bombers to attack guerrilla fighters in South Vietnam. Vietnam War
  • 1954: Carlos Castillo Armas leads an invasion force across the Guatemalan border, setting in motion the 1954 Guatemalan coup d'état. Carlos Castillo Armas
  • 1953: A United States Air Force C-124 crashes and burns near Tachikawa, Japan, killing 129. United States Air Force
  • 1948: Britain, France and the United States announce that on June 21, the Deutsche Mark will be introduced in western Germany and West Berlin. Over the next six days, Communists increasingly restrict access to Berlin.[citation needed] Deutsche Mark
  • 1945: William Joyce ("Lord Haw-Haw") is charged with treason for his pro-German propaganda broadcasting during World War II. William Joyce
  • 1940: World War II: The "Finest Hour" speech is delivered by Winston Churchill. This was their finest hour
  • 1928: Aviator Amelia Earhart becomes the first woman to fly in an aircraft across the Atlantic Ocean (she is a passenger; Wilmer Stultz is the pilot and Lou Gordon the mechanic). Aircraft pilot
  • 1908: Japanese immigration to Brazil begins when 781 people arrive in Santos aboard the ship Kasato-Maru. Japanese Brazilians
  • 1908: The University of the Philippines is established. University of the Philippines
  • 1906: Sultan Abdelaziz of Morocco ratifies the agreement reached in the Algeciras Conference in a personal decree. Abdelaziz of Morocco
  • 1873: Susan B. Anthony is fined $100 for attempting to vote in the 1872 presidential election. Susan B. Anthony
  • 1858: Charles Darwin receives a paper from Alfred Russel Wallace that includes nearly identical conclusions about evolution as Darwin's own, prompting Darwin to publish his theory. Charles Darwin
  • 1822: Konstantinos Kanaris blows up the Ottoman navy's flagship at Chios, killing the Kapudan Pasha Nasuhzade Ali Pasha. Konstantinos Kanaris
  • 1812: The United States declaration of war upon the United Kingdom is signed by President James Madison, beginning the War of 1812. United States declaration of war on the United Kingdom
  • 1799: Action of 18 June 1799: A frigate squadron under Rear-admiral Jean-Baptiste Perrée is captured by the British fleet under Lord Keith. Action of 18 June 1799
  • 1778: American Revolutionary War: The British Army abandons Philadelphia. American Revolutionary War
  • 1684: The charter of the Massachusetts Bay Colony is revoked via a scire facias writ issued by an English court. Massachusetts Bay Colony
  • 1429: Charles VII's army defeats an English army under John Talbot at the Battle of Patay during the Hundred Years' War. The English lost 2,200 men, over half their army, crippling their efforts during this segment of the war. Charles VII of France
  • 1265: A draft Byzantine–Venetian treaty is concluded between Venetian envoys and Emperor Michael VIII Palaiologos, but is not ratified by Doge Reniero Zeno. Byzantine–Venetian treaty of 1268
  • 1156: The treaty of Benevento between Pope Adrian IV and William I of Sicily is concluded. Treaty of Benevento
  • 1053: Battle of Civitate: Three thousand Norman horsemen of Count Humphrey rout the troops of Pope Leo IX. Battle of Civitate
  • 656: Ali becomes Caliph of the Rashidun Caliphate. Ali

About the date itself

It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Friday, 2028: Sunday, 2029: Monday, 2030: Tuesday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 18 June 2027 in 2027 is 13 Muharram 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table.
