October 9: what happened on this day

What happened on 9 October: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2019: Syrian civil war: Turkish forces began an offensive into north-eastern Syria following the withdrawal of U.S. troops from the region. Syrian civil war
  • 2012: Pakistani activist Malala Yousafzai (pictured) was severely injured by a Taliban gunman in a failed assassination attempt. Malala Yousafzai
  • 1962: Nick Holonyak, an engineer for General Electric, gave the first public demonstration of a light-emitting diode. Nick Holonyak
  • 1952: A footman shot and killed two colleagues and wounded the lady of the house at Knowsley Hall, England. Knowsley Hall shootings
  • 1921: The Mortimer War Memorial (pictured), designed by Herbert Maryon in memory of local servicemen who died in the First World War, was dedicated. Mortimer War Memorial
  • 1914: World War I: The civilian authorities of Antwerp surrendered and allowed the German army to capture the city. World War I
  • 1912: Following a reduction in pay, textile workers in Little Falls, New York, walked out of their mill, starting a three-month strike. Little Falls, New York
  • 1888: The Washington Monument in Washington, D.C., at the time the world's tallest building, officially opened to the general public. Washington Monument
  • 1813: Late in the Napoleonic Wars, Empress Marie Louise (pictured) issued decrees conscripting tens of thousands of French teenagers, who became known as Marie-Louises. Napoleonic Wars
  • 1793: French Revolution: After a month-long siege, the leaders of Lyon surrendered, ending their revolt against the National Convention. French Revolution
  • 1740: European soldiers and Javanese collaborators started massacring Chinese Indonesians in the port city of Batavia, modern-day Jakarta: at least 10,000 people were killed. 1740 Batavia massacre
  • 1708: Great Northern War: Russia defeated Sweden at the Battle of Lesnaya on the Russian–Polish border, in present-day Belarus. Great Northern War

More events

  • 2024: Hurricane Milton makes landfall in Siesta Key, Florida, as a Category 3 hurricane, causing US$34.3 billion in damage only two weeks after Hurricane Helene impacted the state. Hurricane Milton
  • 2016: The Arakan Rohingya Salvation Army launches its first attack on Myanmar security forces along the Bangladesh–Myanmar border. Arakan Rohingya Salvation Army
  • 2009: First lunar impact of NASA's Lunar Precursor Robotic Program. Lunar Precursor Robotic Program
  • 2006: North Korea conducts its first nuclear test. North Korea
  • 1992: The Peekskill meteorite, a 27.7 pounds (12.6 kg) meteorite crashed into a parked car in Peekskill, New York. Peekskill meteorite
  • 1986: Fox Broadcasting Company (FBC) launches as the fourth US television network. Fox Broadcasting Company
  • 1983: South Korean President Chun Doo-hwan survives an assassination attempt in Rangoon, Burma, but the blast kills 21 and injures 17 others. Chun Doo-hwan
  • 1980: Pope John Paul II greets the Dalai Lama during a private audience in Vatican City. Pope John Paul II
  • 1969: In Chicago, the National Guard is called in as demonstrations continue over the trial of the "Chicago Eight". Chicago
  • 1966: Vietnam War: the Republic of Korea Army commits the Binh Tai Massacre. Vietnam War
  • 1962: Uganda becomes an independent Commonwealth realm. Uganda
  • 1942: Australia's Statute of Westminster Adoption Act 1942 receives royal assent. Statute of Westminster Adoption Act 1942
  • 1937: Murder of 9 Catholic priests in Zhengding, China, who protected the local population from the advancing Japanese army. Zhengding Missionary Murder
  • 1934: A member of the Internal Macedonian Revolutionary Organization, Vlado Chernozemski, kills King Alexander I of Yugoslavia in an assassination organized by the Ustaše, while the French police accidentally kills Louis Barthou, Foreign Minister of France, in Marseille. Internal Macedonian Revolutionary Organization
  • 1918: The Finnish Parliament elects Prince Frederick Charles of Hesse as King of Finland, but he never accedes to the throne due to Germany's defeat in World War I. Parliament of Finland
  • 1913: The steamship SS Volturno catches fire in the mid-Atlantic. SS Volturno (1906)
  • 1900: The Cook Islands become a territory of the United Kingdom. Cook Islands
  • 1873: A meeting at the U.S. Naval Academy establishes the U.S. Naval Institute. United States Naval Institute
  • 1861: American Civil War: Union troops repel a Confederate attempt to capture Fort Pickens in the Battle of Santa Rosa Island. American Civil War
  • 1834: Opening of the Dublin and Kingstown Railway, the first public railway on the island of Ireland. Dublin and Kingstown Railway
  • 1825: Restauration arrives in New York Harbor from Norway, the first organized immigration from Norway to the United States. Restauration (ship)
  • 1812: War of 1812: In a naval engagement on Lake Erie, American forces capture two British ships: HMS Detroit and HMS Caledonia. War of 1812
  • 1804: Hobart, capital of Tasmania, is founded. Hobart
  • 1790: A severe earthquake in northern Algeria causes severe damage and a tsunami in the Mediterranean Sea and kills three thousand. 1790 Oran earthquake
  • 1760: Seven Years' War: Russian and Austrian troops briefly occupy Berlin. Seven Years' War
  • 1708: Peter the Great defeats the Swedes at the Battle of Lesnaya. Peter the Great
  • 1683: Allied Imperial and Polish-Lithuanian forces defeat the retreating Ottoman forces in the battle of Parkany. Holy Roman Empire
  • 1604: Kepler's Supernova is the most recent supernova to be observed within the Milky Way. Kepler's Supernova
  • 1531: Pressed by a food embargo, the five Catholic cantons of Central Switzerland declare war on the Protestant canton of Zurich, starting the Second War of Kappel. Central Switzerland
  • 1238: James I of Aragon founds the Kingdom of Valencia. James I of Aragon

About the date itself

October 9 is the 282nd day of an ordinary year, with 83 days left after it (84 in a leap year, where it is the 283rd day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Saturday, 2028: Monday, 2029: Tuesday, 2030: Wednesday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 9 October 2027 in 2027 is 9 Jumada al-awwal 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 40.
