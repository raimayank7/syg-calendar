January 10: what happened on this day

What happened on 10 January: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 12 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2007: A general strike began in Guinea as an attempt to force President Lansana Conté to resign, eventually resulting in the appointment of two new prime ministers. 2007 Guinean general strike
  • 1993: The Braer Storm, the strongest extratropical cyclone ever recorded in the North Atlantic, reached peak intensity. Braer Storm
  • 1946: The first session of the United Nations General Assembly convened at the Methodist Central Hall in London with representatives from 51 member states. United Nations General Assembly
  • 1946: The first session of the United Nations General Assembly convened in London with representatives from the original 51 member states. First session of the United Nations General Assembly
  • 1929: Tintin in the Land of the Soviets, the first volume of The Adventures of Tintin by the Belgian cartoonist Hergé, began serialisation. Tintin in the Land of the Soviets
  • 1927: The science fiction film Metropolis, directed by Fritz Lang, was released in Germany. Science fiction
  • 1917: Imperial Trans-Antarctic Expedition: Seven survivors of the Ross Sea party were rescued after being stranded for several months. Imperial Trans-Antarctic Expedition
  • 1901: The first great gusher of the Texas oil boom was discovered in the Spindletop oil field near Beaumont. Blowout (well drilling)
  • 1812: New Orleans (pictured), the first steamship on the Mississippi River, arrived at New Orleans to complete its maiden voyage. New Orleans (steamboat)
  • 1475: Moldavian–Ottoman Wars: Stephen the Great led Moldavian forces to defeat an Ottoman attack under Hadım Suleiman Pasha near Vaslui in present-day Romania. Moldavian–Ottoman Wars
  • 976: After the death of his guardian John I Tzimiskes, Basil II became the effective ruler and senior emperor of the Byzantine Empire. John I Tzimiskes
  • 236: Pope Fabian, said to have been chosen by the Holy Spirit when a dove landed on his head, began his papacy. Pope Fabian

About the date itself

January 10 is the 10th day of an ordinary year, with 355 days left after it (356 in a leap year, where it is the 10th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Sunday, 2028: Monday, 2029: Wednesday, 2030: Thursday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 10 January 2027 in 2027 is 2 Sha'ban 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 1.
