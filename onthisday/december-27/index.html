December 27: what happened on this day

What happened on 27 December: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 12 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2024: Acting president and prime minister of South Korea Han Duck-soo was impeached by the National Assembly. President of South Korea
  • 2009: During protests in Tehran against the recent presidential election, Iranian security forces opened fire on demonstrators. 2009 Iranian presidential election protests
  • 2007: Riots erupted in Mombasa, Kenya, after Mwai Kibaki was declared the winner of the presidential election—the first event in a political, economic, and humanitarian crisis. Mombasa
  • 2002: The human-cloning company Clonaid claimed to have performed the first reproductive cloning of a human, but provided no evidence for the claim. Human cloning
  • 1997: Loyalist Volunteer Force leader Billy Wright was assassinated in HM Prison Maze by members of the Irish National Liberation Army. Loyalist Volunteer Force
  • 1985: The body of murdered American primatologist Dian Fossey was discovered inside her cabin in Volcanoes National Park, Rwanda. Primatology
  • 1949: Indonesian National Revolution: Queen Juliana of the Netherlands signed papers that relinquished sovereignty of most of the Dutch East Indies, officially recognising the independence of Indonesia. Indonesian National Revolution
  • 1939: A magnitude 7.8 earthquake struck central Turkey, destroying 90 per cent of the buildings in the area, and causing over 32,000 deaths. 1939 Erzincan earthquake
  • 1932: New York City's Radio City Music Hall (pictured) opened with the world's largest auditorium at the time. Radio City Music Hall
  • 1831: HMS Beagle departed Plymouth, England, on a voyage to South America that established Charles Darwin (pictured) as a naturalist. HMS Beagle
  • 1657: Citizens of New Netherland presented the Flushing Remonstrance to Peter Stuyvesant, the director general of the colonial province, requesting an exemption to his ban on Quaker worship. New Netherland
  • 1521: A period of unrest in Wittenberg following the arrival of three men of the Radical Reformation, known as the Zwickau prophets, was quelled after Martin Luther's release from custody. Wittenberg

  • 1939: The 7.8 Mw Erzincan earthquake shakes eastern Turkey with a maximum Mercalli intensity of XI (Extreme). At least 32,700 people were killed. 1939 Erzincan earthquake

  • 1932: Radio City Music Hall, "Showplace of the Nation", opens in New York City. Radio City Music Hall

  • 1918: The Great Poland Uprising against the Germans begins. Greater Poland uprising (1918–1919)

  • 1845: Ether anesthetic is used for childbirth for the first time by Dr. Crawford Long in Jefferson, Georgia. Diethyl ether
  • 1845: Having coined the phrase "manifest destiny" the previous July, journalist John L. O'Sullivan argued in his newspaper New York Morning News that the United States had the right to claim the entire Oregon Country. Manifest destiny
  • 1831: Charles Darwin embarks on his journey aboard HMS Beagle, during which he will begin to formulate his theory of evolution. Charles Darwin
  • 1814: War of 1812: The destruction of the schooner USS Carolina brings to an end Commodore Daniel Patterson's makeshift fleet, which fought a series of delaying actions that contributed to Andrew Jackson's victory at the Battle of New Orleans. War of 1812
  • 1688: The Dutch Guards, commanded by Count Solms, enter London and occupy the Palace of Whitehall and St James's Palace, securing the capital for William of Orange during the 1688 invasion of England. Dutch Blue Guards
  • 1657: The Flushing Remonstrance articulates for the first time in North American history that freedom of religion is a fundamental right. Flushing Remonstrance
  • 1521: The Zwickau prophets arrive in Wittenberg, disturbing the peace and preaching the Apocalypse. Zwickau prophets
  • 1512: The Spanish Crown issues the Laws of Burgos, governing the conduct of settlers with regard to native Indians in the New World. Laws of Burgos

About the date itself

December 27 is the 361st day of an ordinary year, with 4 days left after it (5 in a leap year, where it is the 362nd day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Monday, 2028: Wednesday, 2029: Thursday, 2030: Friday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 27 December 2027 in 2027 is 29 Rajab 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 52.
