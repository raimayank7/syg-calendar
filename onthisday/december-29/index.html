December 29: what happened on this day

What happened on 29 December: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 8 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2024: Jeju Air Flight 2216 crashed at Muan International Airport while attempting to perform a belly-landing after losing engine thrust to a flock of Baikal teal. Jeju Air Flight 2216
  • 1994: Turkish Airlines Flight 278 crashed on approach to Van Ferit Melen Airport in Van, Turkey, killing 57 of the 76 people on board. Turkish Airlines Flight 278
  • 1975: Planted by unknown perpetrators, a bomb exploded (aftermath pictured) at LaGuardia Airport in New York City, killing 11 people and seriously injuring 74 others. 1975 LaGuardia Airport bombing
  • 1959: American physicist Richard Feynman gave a speech entitled "There's Plenty of Room at the Bottom" at Caltech, anticipating the field of nanotechnology. Richard Feynman
  • 1940: Second World War: The Luftwaffe began a major night bombing raid on the British capital as part of the Blitz, beginning what was later called the "Second Great Fire of London". World War II
  • 1928: The Northern Expedition, a military campaign by the National Revolutionary Army of the Kuomintang, ended with the complete control of the Republic of China. Northern Expedition
  • 1915: First World War: The French parliament passed a law granting the land occupied by British war graves as "the free gift of the French people". World War I
  • 1913: Cecil B. DeMille started filming Hollywood's first feature film, The Squaw Man (featured). Cecil B. DeMille
  • 1890: Sioux Wars: The United States Army killed 250 to 300 Lakota men, women and children at the Wounded Knee Massacre, beginning the Ghost Dance War. Sioux Wars
  • 1876: A railway bridge collapsed over the Ashtabula River in Ohio, killing 92 people and injuring 64 others on a Lake Shore and Michigan Southern Railway train. Ashtabula River railroad disaster
  • 1860: To counter the French Navy's Gloire, the world's first ironclad warship, the Royal Navy launched HMS Warrior, the world's first iron-hulled armoured warship. French Navy
  • 1845: The Republic of Texas was annexed by the United States, becoming the 28th state to be admitted to the Union. Republic of Texas

  • 1845: The United States annexes the Republic of Texas and admits it as the 28th state. Texas annexation

  • 1778: American Revolutionary War: British forces under the command of Lieutenant Colonel Archibald Campbell defeat American forces under Major General Robert Howe and capture the port city of Savannah, Georgia. American Revolutionary War
  • 1607: According to John Smith, Pocahontas, daughter of Powhatan leader Wahunsenacawh, successfully pleads for his life after tribal leaders attempt to execute him. John Smith (explorer)
  • 1503: The Battle of Garigliano was fought between a Spanish army under Gonzalo Fernández de Córdoba and a French army commanded by Ludovico II, Marquess of Saluzzo. Battle of Garigliano (1503)

About the date itself

December 29 is the 363rd day of an ordinary year, with 2 days left after it (3 in a leap year, where it is the 364th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Wednesday, 2028: Friday, 2029: Saturday, 2030: Sunday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 29 December 2027 in 2027 is 1 Sha'ban 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 52.
