March 20: what happened on this day

What happened on 20 March: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2014: Taliban militants killed nine civilians in a mass shooting at the Kabul Serena Hotel in Afghanistan. Taliban
  • 2010: Eyjafjallajökull (pictured) in Iceland began a series of volcanic events, later disrupting air travel across Europe. Eyjafjallajökull
  • 1993: The Troubles: Two children were killed by the second of two bomb attacks by the Provisional Irish Republican Army in Warrington, England. The Troubles
  • 1944: World War II: U.S. Marines made a landing on Emirau Island in the Bismarck Archipelago to develop an airbase as part of Operation Cartwheel. World War II
  • 1942: World War II: After being forced to flee the Philippines for Australia, US Army general Douglas MacArthur announced: "I came through and I shall return." World War II
  • 1926: Chiang Kai-shek initiated a purge of communist elements within the National Revolutionary Army in Guangzhou, China. Chiang Kai-shek
  • 1923: The Arts Club of Chicago hosted the opening of Pablo Picasso's first solo United States showing, entitled Original Drawings by Pablo Picasso. Arts Club of Chicago
  • 1922: The United States Navy commissioned its first aircraft carrier, USS Langley. United States Navy
  • 1861: An earthquake occurred in the Argentine province of Mendoza, causing at least 6,000 deaths and destroying most of the buildings in the province's capital city. 1861 Mendoza earthquake
  • 1854: The Republican Party was founded in Ripon, Wisconsin, by politicians opposing the Kansas–Nebraska Act and the expansion of slavery in the United States. Republican Party (United States)
  • 1760: A fire of unknown cause broke out in Boston, Massachusetts, destroying 349 buildings. Great Boston Fire of 1760
  • 1724: Following the death of Pope Innocent XIII, a papal conclave convened in Rome to elect a new pope. Pope Innocent XIII

  • 1600: The Linköping Bloodbath takes place on Maundy Thursday in Linköping, Sweden: five Swedish noblemen are publicly beheaded in the aftermath of the War against Sigismund (1598–1599). Linköping Bloodbath

About the date itself

March 20 is the 79th day of an ordinary year, with 286 days left after it (287 in a leap year, where it is the 80th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Saturday, 2028: Monday, 2029: Tuesday, 2030: Wednesday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 20 March 2027 in 2027 is 12 Shawwal 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 11.
