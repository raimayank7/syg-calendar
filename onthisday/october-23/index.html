October 23: what happened on this day

What happened on 23 October: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2022: Myanmar civil war: Burmese military forces launched airstrikes that killed at least 80 concertgoers in Kachin State. Myanmar civil war (2021–present)
  • 2022: Pakistani journalist Arshad Sharif is shot and killed by local police in Kajiado, Kenya. Arshad Sharif
  • 2015: Hurricane Patricia, the most intense tropical cyclone on record in the Western Hemisphere, peaked with maximum sustained winds of 215 mph (345 km/h) south of Mexico. Hurricane Patricia
  • 2011: During the Malaysian motorcycle Grand Prix, Marco Simoncelli (pictured) collided with two other riders, resulting in his death due to serious trauma to the head, neck, and chest. 2011 Malaysian motorcycle Grand Prix
  • 1995: The runway show for Alexander McQueen's collection The Hunger was staged at London's Natural History Museum. Alexander McQueen
  • 1972: Vietnam War: Operation Linebacker, a U.S. bombing campaign against North Vietnam in response to its Easter Offensive, ended after five months. Vietnam War
  • 1956: The Hungarian Revolution began as a peaceful student demonstration that attracted thousands while marching through central Budapest to the parliament building. Hungarian Revolution of 1956
  • 1955: The State of Vietnam voted in a referendum to become a republic. State of Vietnam
  • 1934: Jeannette Piccard (pictured) piloted a hot-air balloon flight that reached 57,579 feet (17,550 m), becoming the first woman to fly in the stratosphere. Jeannette Piccard
  • 1906: Alberto Santos-Dumont flew his biplane 14-bis for 50 metres (160 ft) at an altitude of about four metres (13 ft). Alberto Santos-Dumont
  • 1798: War of the Second Coalition: The Ottoman–Albanian forces of Ali Pasha of Janina defeated French troops and captured the town of Preveza at the Battle of Nicopolis. War of the Second Coalition
  • 1641: Irish Catholic gentry in Ulster tried to seize control of Dublin Castle, the seat of English rule in Ireland, to force concessions to Catholics. Ulster

More events

  • 2022: Xi Jinping is elected as General Secretary of the Chinese Communist Party by the Central Committee, beginning a third term of the paramount leader of China. Xi Jinping
  • 2020: Second Libyan Civil War: The Second Libyan Civil War comes to an end as all parties to the 5+5 Joint Libyan Military Commission agree to a ceasefire. Libyan civil war (2014–2020)
  • 2015: The lowest sea-level pressure in the Western Hemisphere, and the highest reliably-measured non-tornadic sustained winds, are recorded in Hurricane Patricia, which strikes Mexico hours later, killing at least 13 and causing over $280 million in damages. Atmospheric pressure
  • 2011: The Libyan National Transitional Council deems the Libyan Civil War over. National Transitional Council
  • 2007: A storm causes the Mexican Kab 101 oil platform to collide with a wellhead, leading to the death and drowning of 22 people during rescue operations after evacuation of the platform. Kab 101
  • 2004: A powerful earthquake and its aftershocks hit Niigata Prefecture in northern Japan, killing 35 people, injuring 2,200, and leaving 85,000 homeless or evacuated. 2004 Chūetsu earthquake
  • 1998: Israel and the Palestinian Authority sign the Wye River Memorandum. Israel
  • 1993: The Troubles: A Provisional IRA bomb prematurely detonates in Belfast, killing the bomber and nine civilians. The Troubles
  • 1989: The Hungarian Republic officially replaces the communist Hungarian People's Republic. End of communism in Hungary
  • 1989: An explosion at the Houston Chemical Complex in Pasadena, Texas, which registered a 3.5 on the Richter magnitude scale, kills 23 and injures 314. Phillips disaster of 1989
  • 1982: A gunfight breaks out between police officers and members of a religious cult in Arizona. The shootout leaves two cultists dead and dozens of cultists and police officers injured. Miracle Valley shootout
  • 1972: Vietnam War: Operation Linebacker, a US bombing campaign against North Vietnam in response to its Easter Offensive, ends after five months. Operation Linebacker
  • 1959: Aeroflot Flight 200 crashes while attempting to land at Vnukovo International Airport, killing 28. Aeroflot Flight 200
  • 1958: Belgian artist Peyo's comic characters, the Smurfs, make their debut in Spirou magazine. Belgium
  • 1955: Prime Minister Ngô Đình Diệm defeats former emperor Bảo Đại in a referendum and founds the Republic of Vietnam. Ngo Dinh Diem
  • 1945: Jackie Robinson of the Kansas City Monarchs signs a contract with the Brooklyn Dodgers' minor league team, the Montreal Royals, breaking Major League Baseball's color barrier. Jackie Robinson
  • 1942: World War II: Allied forces commence the Second Battle of El Alamein, which proves to be the key turning point in the North African campaign. World War II
  • 1942: World War II: The Battle for Henderson Field begins on Guadalcanal. Battle for Henderson Field
  • 1927: The Imatra Cinema is destroyed in a fire in Tampere, Finland, during showing the 1924 film Wages of Virtue; 21 people die in the fire and almost 30 are injured. Imatra (cinema)
  • 1923: German October: Due to a miscommunication with the party leadership, a militant section of the Communist Party of Germany launches an insurrection in Hamburg. German October
  • 1911: The Italo-Turkish War sees the first use of an airplane in combat when an Italian pilot makes a reconnaissance flight. Italo-Turkish War
  • 1882: Assommoir bombing: Anarchists such as Fanny Madignier commit the first deadly anarchist attack in France. Assommoir bombing
  • 1864: American Civil War: The Battle of Westport is the last significant engagement west of the Mississippi River, ending in a Union victory. American Civil War
  • 1850: The first National Women's Rights Convention begins in Worcester, Massachusetts. National Women's Rights Convention
  • 1732: The siege of Isfahan comes to an end when Safavid ruler Soltan Hoseyn capitulates to the besieging Afghan forces under Mahmud Hotak. Siege of Isfahan
  • 1666: The most intense tornado on record in English history, an F4 storm on the Fujita scale or T8 on the TORRO scale, strikes the county of Lincolnshire, with winds of more than 213 miles per hour (343 km/h). Fujita scale
  • 1641: Irish Catholic gentry from Ulster attempt to seize control of Dublin Castle, the seat of English rule in Ireland, so as to force concessions. Irish Catholics
  • 1295: The first treaty forming the Auld Alliance between Scotland and France against England is signed in Paris. Auld Alliance
  • 1086: Spanish Reconquista: At the Battle of Sagrajas, the Almoravids defeat the Castilians, but are unable to take advantage of their victory. Reconquista
  • 425: Valentinian III is elevated as Roman emperor at the age of six. Valentinian III

About the date itself

October 23 is the 296th day of an ordinary year, with 69 days left after it (70 in a leap year, where it is the 297th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Saturday, 2028: Monday, 2029: Tuesday, 2030: Wednesday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 23 October 2027 in 2027 is 23 Jumada al-awwal 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 42.
