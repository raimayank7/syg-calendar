October 14: what happened on this day

What happened on 14 October: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 13 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2021: Approximately 10,000 John Deere employees went on strike in one of the largest private-sector strikes in the United States. John Deere
  • 2012: Felix Baumgartner jumped from a helium balloon in the stratosphere to become the first person to break the sound barrier without vehicular power. Felix Baumgartner
  • 1980: The 6th Congress of the Workers' Party concluded, having anointed North Korean president Kim Il Sung's son Kim Jong Il as his successor. 6th Congress of the Workers' Party of Korea
  • 1964: Members of the Politburo voted to remove Nikita Khrushchev as First Secretary of the Communist Party of the Soviet Union and replace him with Leonid Brezhnev. Politburo of the Communist Party of the Soviet Union
  • 1956: The Queen Elizabeth Way, North America's first intercity divided highway, opens. Queen Elizabeth Way
  • 1953: Israeli military commander Ariel Sharon and his Unit 101 special forces attacked the village of Qibya on the West Bank, destroying 45 buildings, killing 42 villagers, and wounding 15 others. Ariel Sharon
  • 1943: World War II: During the second raid on Schweinfurt, the U.S. 8th Air Force suffered so many losses that it lost air supremacy over Germany for several months. World War II
  • 1943: The Second Philippine Republic, a Japanese puppet state, was established with Jose P. Laurel as its first president. Second Philippine Republic
  • 1939: Second World War: The German submarine U-47 torpedoed and sank the Royal Navy battleship HMS Royal Oak while the latter was anchored at Scapa Flow in Orkney, Scotland. World War II
  • 1913: The worst mining accident in the United Kingdom's history took place when an explosion resulted in 440 deaths at the Universal Colliery in Senghenydd, Wales. Mining accident
  • 1863: American Civil War: In the Battle of Bristoe Station, the Union II Corps surprised and repelled the Confederate attack on the Union rearguard, resulting in a Union victory. American Civil War
  • 1758: Third Silesian War: At the Battle of Hochkirch, an Austrian army under Leopold Joseph von Daun surprised the Prussians commanded by Frederick the Great, overwhelming them and forcing a general retreat. Third Silesian War

About the date itself

It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Thursday, 2028: Saturday, 2029: Sunday, 2030: Monday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 14 October 2027 in 2027 is 14 Jumada al-awwal 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 41.
