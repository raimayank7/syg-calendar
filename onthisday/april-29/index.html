April 29: what happened on this day

What happened on 29 April: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 7 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2015: The ringleaders of the Bali Nine were executed in Indonesia for attempting to smuggle 8.3 kg (18 lb) of heroin to Australia in 2005. Bali Nine
  • 2011: Watched by a worldwide television audience of tens of millions, Prince William and Catherine Middleton were married at Westminster Abbey in London. William, Prince of Wales
  • 1997: The Chemical Weapons Convention entered into force, outlawing the production, stockpiling and use of chemical weapons in the 87 countries that had ratified the convention. Chemical Weapons Convention
  • 1992: The acquittal of policemen who had beaten African-American motorist Rodney King sparked six days of civil unrest in Los Angeles (damage pictured), during which 63 people were killed. Rodney King
  • 1975: Vietnam War: North Vietnam concluded its East Sea Campaign by capturing all of the Spratly Islands held by South Vietnam. Vietnam War
  • 1970: Vietnam War: South Vietnamese forces began the Cambodian campaign, aiming to attack North Vietnamese jungle bases. Vietnam War
  • 1945: World War II: The U.S. Army liberated Dachau, the first Nazi concentration camp, and killed German prisoners of war. World War II
  • 1943: After suffering train derailments, defections, and outbreaks of dysentery and typhoid fever which killed two members, the Brazilian football club Santa Cruz returned from their Suicidal Tour (team pictured). Dysentery
  • 1910: Parliament passed the People's Budget, the first budget in British history with the express intent of redistributing wealth. Parliament of the United Kingdom
  • 1903: A rockslide buried part of the Canadian mining town of Frank under 110 million tonnes of rock, killing around 70 people. Frank Slide
  • 1826: In Parramatta, Australia, Scottish astronomer James Dunlop discovered Centaurus A (pictured), which was later recognised as one of the nearest radio galaxies to Earth. Parramatta
  • 1760: Seven Years' War: France began an unsuccessful attempt to retake Quebec City, which had been captured by Britain. Seven Years' War

More events

  • 2015: A baseball game between the Baltimore Orioles and the Chicago White Sox sets the all-time low attendance mark for Major League Baseball. Zero fans were in attendance for the game, as the stadium was officially closed to the public due to the 2015 Baltimore protests. Baltimore Orioles
  • 2013: A powerful explosion occurs in an office building in Prague, believed to have been caused by natural gas, and injures 43 people. 2013 Prague explosion
  • 2011: The wedding of Prince William and Catherine Middleton takes place at Westminster Abbey in London. Wedding of Prince William and Catherine Middleton
  • 2004: The final Oldsmobile is built in Lansing, Michigan, ending 107 years of vehicle production. Oldsmobile
  • 1992: Riots in Los Angeles begin, following the acquittal of police officers charged with excessive force in the beating of Rodney King. Over the next three days 63 people are killed and hundreds of buildings are destroyed. 1992 Los Angeles riots
  • 1991: A cyclone strikes the Chittagong district of southeastern Bangladesh with winds of around 155 miles per hour (249 km/h), killing at least 138,000 people and leaving as many as ten million homeless. 1991 Bangladesh cyclone
  • 1986: A fire at the Central library of the Los Angeles Public Library damages or destroys 400,000 books and other items. Los Angeles Public Library
  • 1986: The United States Navy aircraft carrier USS Enterprise becomes the first nuclear-powered aircraft carrier to transit the Suez Canal, navigating from the Red Sea to the Mediterranean Sea to relieve the USS Coral Sea. Aircraft carrier
  • 1985: Space Shuttle Challenger is launched on STS-51-B. Space Shuttle Challenger
  • 1975: Vietnam War: Operation Frequent Wind: The U.S. begins to evacuate U.S. citizens from Saigon before an expected North Vietnamese takeover. U.S. involvement in the war comes to an end. This happens after the Bombing of Tan Son Nhut Air Base. Operation Frequent Wind
  • 1974: Watergate scandal: United States President Richard Nixon announces the release of edited transcripts of White House tape recordings relating to the scandal. Watergate scandal
  • 1970: Vietnam War: United States and South Vietnamese forces invade Cambodia to interdict the Ho Chi Minh Trail in an attempt to cut off supplies to the Viet Cong and the North Vietnamese Army. Vietnam War
  • 1953: The first U.S. experimental 3D television broadcast shows an episode of Space Patrol on Los Angeles ABC affiliate KECA-TV. 3D television
  • 1952: Pan Am Flight 202 crashes into the Amazon basin near Carolina, Maranhão, Brazil, killing 50 people. Pan Am Flight 202
  • 1945: World War II: The Surrender of Caserta is signed by the commander of German forces in Italy. World War II
  • 1945: World War II: Allied airdrops of food begin over German-occupied regions of the Netherlands. Operations Manna, Chowhound, and Faust
  • 1945: Dachau concentration camp is liberated by United States troops. Dachau concentration camp
  • 1916: World War I: The UK's 6th Indian Division surrenders to Ottoman Forces at the Siege of Kut in one of the largest surrenders of British forces up to that point. World War I
  • 1911: Tsinghua University, one of mainland China's leading universities, is founded. Tsinghua University
  • 1910: The Parliament of the United Kingdom passes the People's Budget, the first budget in British history with the expressed intent of redistributing wealth among the British public. Parliament of the United Kingdom
  • 1864: Theta Xi fraternity is founded at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute, the only fraternity to be founded during the American Civil War. Theta Xi
  • 1862: American Civil War: The Capture of New Orleans by Union forces under David Farragut. American Civil War
  • 1861: Maryland in the American Civil War: Maryland's House of Delegates votes not to secede from the Union. Maryland in the American Civil War
  • 1826: The galaxy Centaurus A or NGC 5128 is discovered by James Dunlop. Centaurus A
  • 1770: James Cook arrives in Australia at Botany Bay, which he names. James Cook
  • 1760: French forces commence the siege of Quebec which is held by the British. Siege of Quebec (1760)
  • 1521: Swedish War of Liberation: Swedish troops defeat a Danish force in the Battle of Västerås. Swedish War of Liberation
  • 1492: The Crown's decision to expel the Jews is announced in Zaragoza, Aragon, to the kingdom's procurators. Expulsion of Jews from Spain
  • 1429: Joan of Arc arrives to relieve the Siege of Orléans. Joan of Arc
  • 1091: Battle of Levounion: The Pechenegs are defeated by Byzantine Emperor Alexios I Komnenos. Battle of Levounion
