September 24: what happened on this day

What happened on 24 September: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2019: The Supreme Court of the United Kingdom unanimously ruled that advice given by Prime Minister Boris Johnson to Queen Elizabeth II that Parliament should be prorogued was unlawful. Supreme Court of the United Kingdom
  • 1993: Norodom Sihanouk (pictured) became King of Cambodia with the restoration of the monarchy after a 23-year interregnum. Norodom Sihanouk
  • 1992: After his neighbor identified handwriting samples placed on local billboards by police, Oba Chandler was arrested three years after he committed a triple murder in the Tampa Bay area in Florida. Oba Chandler
  • 1964: The Warren Commission released its report to the U.S. president, concluding that Lee Harvey Oswald acted alone in the assassination of John F. Kennedy. Warren Commission
  • 1950: The "Great Smoke Pall", generated by the Chinchaga fire, the largest recorded fire in North American history, was first recorded in present-day Nunavut and may eventually have circled the entire globe. Chinchaga fire
  • 1941: Operation Barbarossa: A Wehrmacht training event known as the Mogilev Conference began, marking an increase in violence against Jews and other civilians in the areas under General Max von Schenckendorff's command. Operation Barbarossa
  • 1911: His Majesty's Airship No. 1, Britain's first rigid airship, was wrecked by strong winds before her maiden flight at Barrow-in-Furness (pictured). HMA No. 1
  • 1903: Alfred Deakin became the second Australian prime minister, succeeding Edmund Barton, who left office to become a founding justice of the High Court of Australia. Alfred Deakin
  • 1877: At the Battle of Shiroyama (depicted), the final engagement of the Satsuma Rebellion, the Imperial Japanese Army defeated rebel samurai of the Satsuma Domain led by Saigō Takamori. Battle of Shiroyama
  • 1869: Jay Gould, James Fisk, and other speculators plotted but failed to control the United States gold market, causing prices to plummet. Jay Gould
  • 1789: The Judiciary Act of 1789 was signed into law, establishing the U.S. federal judiciary and setting the number of Supreme Court justices at six. Judiciary Act of 1789
  • 1645: English Civil War: Royalists commanded by King Charles I suffered a significant defeat at the Battle of Rowton Heath. English Civil War

  • 1993: The Cambodian monarchy is restored, with Norodom Sihanouk as king. Monarchy of Cambodia

  • 1972: Japan Airlines Flight 472 lands at Juhu Aerodrome instead of Santacruz Airport in Bombay, India, overrunning the runway and resulting in 11 injuries. Japan Air Lines Flight 472 (1972)

  • 1957: President Eisenhower sends the 101st Airborne Division to Little Rock, Arkansas, to enforce desegregation. 101st Airborne Division
  • 1954: AEC Routemaster, the iconic London bus was introduced. AEC Routemaster
  • 1950: The eastern United States is covered by a thick haze from the Chinchaga fire in western Canada. Chinchaga fire

  • 1935: Earl and Weldon Bascom produce the first rodeo ever held outdoors under electric lights. Earl W. Bascom

  • 1911: His Majesty's Airship No. 1, Britain's first rigid airship, is wrecked by strong winds before her maiden flight at Barrow-in-Furness. HMA No. 1

  • 1846: Mexican–American War: General Zachary Taylor captures Monterrey. Mexican–American War
  • 1841: The Sultanate of Brunei cedes Sarawak to James Brooke. Sultanate of Brunei (1368–1888)

  • 1674: Second Tantrik Coronation of Shivaji. Shivaji
  • 1568: Spanish naval forces defeat an English fleet, under the command of John Hawkins, at the Battle of San Juan de Ulúa near Veracruz. Battle of San Juan de Ulúa (1568)

About the date itself

September 24 is the 267th day of an ordinary year, with 98 days left after it (99 in a leap year, where it is the 268th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Friday, 2028: Sunday, 2029: Monday, 2030: Tuesday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 24 September 2027 in 2027 is 23 Rabi' al-thani 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table.
