April 15: what happened on this day

What happened on 15 April: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 14 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2021: A mass shooting at a FedEx Ground facility in Indianapolis, Indiana, killed nine and injured seven. 2021 Indianapolis FedEx shooting
  • 2013: Dzhokhar and Tamerlan Tsarnaev set off two pressure cooker bombs during the running of the Boston Marathon, killing three people and injuring 264 others. Dzhokhar Tsarnaev
  • 1994: At a GATT ministerial meeting in Marrakesh, Morocco, representatives of 123 countries and the European Communities signed an agreement to establish the World Trade Organization. General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade
  • 1989: The death of former Chinese General Secretary Hu Yaobang triggered a series of events that led to the Tiananmen Square protests in Beijing. Death of Hu Yaobang
  • 1957: The Indiaman departed Victoria Coach Station, London, as part of the first UK–India bus service. Victoria Coach Station
  • 1952: The Boeing B-52 Stratofortress, a long-range, subsonic, jet-powered, strategic bomber operated by the U.S. Air Force for most of the aircraft's history, made its first flight. Boeing B-52 Stratofortress
  • 1936: The Great Arab Revolt in the British Mandate for Palestine began when unknown assailants attacked a convoy of trucks and killed two of the Jewish drivers. 1936–1939 Arab revolt in Palestine
  • 1923: Ten Japanese-American children were killed in a racially motivated arson attack on a school in Sacramento, California. Japanese mission school fire
  • 1919: Japanese soldiers killed more than 20 Korean civilians in Jeam-ri as retaliation for the March First Movement protests in the region. Jeam-ri massacre
  • 1802: English poet William Wordsworth and his sister Dorothy encountered a "long belt" of daffodils near Ullswater, inspiring him to pen his best-known work, "I Wandered Lonely as a Cloud". William Wordsworth
  • 1738: Serse, an opera by Baroque composer George Frideric Handel loosely based on Xerxes I of Persia, premiered in London. Serse
  • 1191: Henry VI, King of Germany (pictured), was crowned Holy Roman Emperor. Henry VI, Holy Roman Emperor

About the date itself

April 15 is the 105th day of an ordinary year, with 260 days left after it (261 in a leap year, where it is the 106th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Thursday, 2028: Saturday, 2029: Sunday, 2030: Monday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 15 April 2027 in 2027 is 8 Dhu al-Qa'dah 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 15.
