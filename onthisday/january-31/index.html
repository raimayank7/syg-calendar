January 31: what happened on this day

What happened on 31 January: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2013: A gas leak underneath the Pemex Executive Tower in Mexico City caused an explosion (aftermath pictured) that killed at least 37 people and injured another 121. Torre Ejecutiva Pemex
  • 2007: Emergency officials in Boston mistakenly identified LED placards depicting characters from Aqua Teen Hunger Force as IEDs, causing a panic. Aqua Teen Hunger Force
  • 2000: Alaska Airlines Flight 261, experiencing problems with its horizontal stabilizer system, crashed in the Pacific Ocean off Anacapa Island, California, killing all 88 people on board. Alaska Airlines Flight 261
  • 1997: Final Fantasy VII, the first video game in the Final Fantasy franchise to use 3-D computer graphics, was released. Final Fantasy VII
  • 1961: Aboard NASA's Mercury-Redstone 2, the chimpanzee Ham became the first hominid to be launched into outer space. NASA
  • 1957: A DC-7B operated by Douglas Aircraft collided in mid-air with a U.S. Air Force F-89 and crashed into a schoolyard in Pacoima, California. Douglas DC-7
  • 1919: Intense rioting over labour conditions broke out in Glasgow, Scotlan. Battle of George Square
  • 1900: Datu Muhammad Salleh, leader of a series of major disturbances in North Borneo, was shot dead in Tambunan, but his followers did not give up for five more years. Mat Salleh Rebellion
  • 1827: Mexican soldiers and Texan militiamen marched into Nacogdoches, Texas, to end the Fredonian Rebellion. Nacogdoches, Texas
  • 1747: The London Lock Hospital, the first voluntary hospital specialising in the treatment of venereal diseases, opened. London Lock Hospital
  • 1578: Eighty Years' War: Spanish forces won a crushing victory at the Battle of Gembloux, threatening the States General of the Netherlands and contributing to its move from Brussels to Antwerp. Eighty Years' War
  • 1208: Sverker II was defeated at the Battle of Lena by Erik Knutsson, who succeeded him as King of Sweden. Sverker the Younger

More events

  • 2025: Med Jets Flight 056 crashes near Roosevelt Mall in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, killing 8 people and injuring 23. Med Jets Flight 056
  • 2023: The last Boeing 747, the first wide-body airliner, is delivered to Atlas Air and operated for ApexLogistics. The aircraft was registered as N863GT and named "Empower". Boeing 747
  • 2020: The United Kingdom's membership within the European Union ceases in accordance with Article 50, after 47 years of being a member state. United Kingdom
  • 2009: At least 113 people are killed in Kenya and over 200 injured following an oil spillage ignition in Molo, days after a massive fire at a Nakumatt supermarket in Nairobi killed at least 25 people. Kenya
  • 2003: The Waterfall rail accident occurs near Waterfall, New South Wales, Australia. Waterfall rail accident
  • 2001: Two Japan Airlines planes nearly collide over Suruga Bay in Japan. Japan Airlines
  • 1996: An explosives-filled truck rams into the gates of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka in Colombo, killing at least 86 people and injuring 1,400. Colombo Central Bank bombing
  • 1978: The Crown of St. Stephen (also known as the Holy Crown of Hungary) goes on public display after being returned to Hungary from the United States, where it was held after World War II. Holy Crown of Hungary
  • 1971: The Winter Soldier Investigation, organized by the Vietnam Veterans Against the War to publicize alleged war crimes and atrocities by Americans and allies in Vietnam, begins in Detroit. Winter Soldier Investigation
  • 1966: The Soviet Union launches the unmanned Luna 9 spacecraft as part of the Luna program. Soviet Union
  • 1958: Cold War: Space Race: The Explorer 1, the first successful American satellite, detects the Van Allen radiation belt. Space Race
  • 1957: Eight people (five total crew from two aircraft and three on the ground) in Pacoima, California are killed following the mid-air collision between a Douglas DC-7 airliner and a Northrop F-89 Scorpion fighter jet. Pacoima, Los Angeles
  • 1951: United Nations Security Council Resolution 90 relating to the Korean War is adopted. United Nations Security Council Resolution 90
  • 1946: Cold War: Yugoslavia's new constitution, modeling that of the Soviet Union, establishes six constituent republics (Bosnia and Herzegovina, Croatia, Macedonia, Montenegro, Serbia and Slovenia). Cold War
  • 1945: US Army private Eddie Slovik is executed for desertion, the first such execution of an American soldier since the Civil War. Eddie Slovik
  • 1945: World War II: The end of fighting in the Battle of Hill 170 during the Burma Campaign, in which the British 3 Commando Brigade repulsed a Japanese counterattack on their positions and precipitated a general retirement from the Arakan Peninsula. Battle of Hill 170
  • 1944: World War II: During the Anzio campaign, the 1st Ranger Battalion (Darby's Rangers) is destroyed behind enemy lines in a heavily outnumbered encounter at Battle of Cisterna, Italy. Battle of Anzio
  • 1942: World War II: Allied forces are defeated by the Japanese at the Battle of Malaya and retreat to Singapore. World War II
  • 1919: The Battle of George Square takes place in Glasgow, Scotland, during a campaign for shorter working hours. Battle of George Square
  • 1918: Finnish Civil War: The Suinula massacre, which changes the nature of the war in a more hostile direction, takes place in Kangasala. Finnish Civil War
  • 1915: World War I: Germany is the first to make large-scale use of poison gas in warfare in the Battle of Bolimów against Russia. World War I
  • 1901: Anton Chekhov's Three Sisters premieres at Moscow Art Theatre in Russia. Anton Chekhov
  • 1891: History of Portugal: The first attempt at a Portuguese republican revolution breaks out in the northern city of Porto. History of Portugal (1834–1910)
  • 1865: American Civil War: Confederate General Robert E. Lee becomes general-in-chief of all Confederate armies. Confederate States of America
  • 1848: John C. Frémont is court-martialed for mutiny and disobeying orders. John C. Frémont
  • 1814: Gervasio Antonio de Posadas becomes Supreme Director of the United Provinces of the Río de la Plata (present-day Argentina). Gervasio Antonio de Posadas
  • 1703: Forty-seven rōnin, under the command of Ōishi Kuranosuke, avenged the death of their master, by killing Kira Yoshinaka. Forty-seven rōnin
  • 1606: Gunpowder Plot: Four of the conspirators, including Guy Fawkes, are executed for treason by hanging, drawing and quartering, for plotting against Parliament and King James. Gunpowder Plot
  • 1504: The Treaty of Lyon ends the Italian War, confirming French domination of northern Italy, while Spain receives the Kingdom of Naples. Italian Wars of 1499–1504
  • 1208: The Battle of Lena takes place between King Sverker the Younger of Sweden and his rival, Prince Erik Knutsson, whose victory puts him on the throne as King Eric X. Battle of Lena

About the date itself

January 31 is the 31st day of an ordinary year, with 334 days left after it (335 in a leap year, where it is the 31st day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Sunday, 2028: Monday, 2029: Wednesday, 2030: Thursday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 31 January 2027 in 2027 is 23 Sha'ban 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 4.
