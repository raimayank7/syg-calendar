May 22: what happened on this day

What happened on 22 May: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2026: A gas explosion at a coal mine in Changzhi, Shanxi, China, leaves at least 82 people dead. 2026 Liushenyu coal mine explosion
  • 2010: Upon landing in Mangalore, Air India Express Flight 812 overshot the runway and fell down a hillside, killing 158 of the 166 people on board. Mangaluru
  • 1998: In Public Prosecutor v Taw Cheng Kong, the Court of Appeal of Singapore overruled a High Court decision in the only time a statute in Singapore had been ruled unconstitutional. Public Prosecutor v Taw Cheng Kong
  • 1980: Location testing for Pac-Man, a Japanese arcade game that became an icon of 1980s popular culture, began in Shibuya, Tokyo. Pac-Man
  • 1960: The most powerful earthquake ever recorded, registering approximately 9.5 Mw, struck near Valdivia, Chile, generating tsunamis that reached Hawaii and Japan. 1960 Valdivia earthquake
  • 1939: Germany and Italy signed the Pact of Steel, a military and political alliance. Pact of Steel
  • 1911: The Uruguayan Regional Workers' Federation declared a general strike in solidarity with trolley workers in Montevideo. Uruguayan Regional Workers' Federation
  • 1897: The first Blackwall Tunnel under the River Thames was opened to improve commerce and trade in the East End of London. Blackwall Tunnel
  • 1856: Senator Charles Sumner (pictured) was assaulted by Representative Preston Brooks in the United States Senate chamber in retaliation for a speech in which Sumner fiercely criticized slaveholders. Charles Sumner
  • 1844: In Shiraz, Iran, the Báb declared himself to be a messenger of God to Mullá Husayn, leading to the foundation of Bábism, considered to be a precursor to the Baháʼí Faith. Shiraz
  • 1816: A riot broke out in Littleport, England, over high unemployment and rising grain costs, spreading to Ely the next day before being quelled by troops. Ely and Littleport riots of 1816
  • 1762: The Trevi Fountain (pictured) in Rome was officially inaugurated by Pope Clement XIII. Trevi Fountain

About the date itself
