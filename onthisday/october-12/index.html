October 12: what happened on this day

What happened on 12 October: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2013: Twelve people were killed in an apartment-building collapse (pictured) in Medellín, leading to new construction laws being passed in Colombia. Collapse of the Space Building
  • 1992: An earthquake with a magnitude of 5.8 or 5.9 struck south of Cairo, Egypt, killing 545 people. Seismic magnitude scales
  • 1979: Typhoon Tip, the largest and most intense tropical cyclone ever recorded, reached a worldwide record-low sea-level pressure of 870 mbar (25.69 inHg) in the western Pacific Ocean. Typhoon Tip
  • 1978: While in prison for murder, boxer James Scott defeated Eddie "The Flame" Gregory, a leading light heavyweight. James Scott (boxer)
  • 1960: Japan Socialist Party leader Inejirō Asanuma (pictured) was assassinated during a live television recording by a far-right ultra-nationalist using a short sword. Japan Socialist Party
  • 1946: Il Canto degli Italiani is adopted as national anthem by the newly formed Italian Republic. Canto degli italiani
  • 1928: The iron lung (example pictured), a type of medical ventilator, was used for the first time at the Boston Children's Hospital to treat an eight-year-old girl paralyzed by polio. Iron lung
  • 1917: First World War: New Zealand troops suffered more than 2,000 casualties, including more than 800 deaths, in the First Battle of Passchendaele, making it the nation's largest loss of life in one day. World War I
  • 1892: The Pledge of Allegiance of the United States was first used in public schools to coincide with the opening of the World's Columbian Exposition in Chicago. Pledge of Allegiance
  • 1890: The Uddevalla Suffrage Association was founded in Uddevalla, Sweden, with the purpose of bringing about universal suffrage. Uddevalla Suffrage Association
  • 1798: The Peasants' War began in Overmere, Southern Netherlands, with peasants taking up arms against the French occupiers. Peasants' War (1798)
  • 1492: Believing he had reached the East Indies, Christopher Columbus made landfall on an island in the Caribbean, sparking a series of events that led to the European colonization of the Americas. East Indies

More events

  • 2022: 2022 Bratislava shooting occurs outside a gay bar, killing 3 (including the perpetrator) and injuring one. 2022 Bratislava shooting
  • 2019: Eliud Kipchoge from Kenya becomes the first person to run a marathon in less than two hours with a time of 1:59:40 in Vienna. Eliud Kipchoge
  • 2017: The United States announces its decision to withdraw from UNESCO. Israel immediately follows. UNESCO
  • 2012: The European Union wins the 2012 Nobel Peace Prize. European Union
  • 2002: Terrorists detonate bombs in two nightclubs in Kuta, Bali, Indonesia, killing 202 and wounding over 200. 2002 Bali bombings

  • 1999: The former Autonomous Soviet Republic of Abkhazia declares its independence from Georgia. Abkhazia
  • 1996: New Zealand holds its first general election under the new mixed-member proportional representation system, which led to Jim Bolger's National Party forming a coalition government with Winston Peters's New Zealand First. New Zealand
  • 1992: A 5.8 earthquake occurred in Cairo, Egypt. At least 510 died. 1992 Cairo earthquake
  • 1984: The Provisional Irish Republican Army fail to assassinate Prime Minister Margaret Thatcher and her cabinet. The bomb kills five people and wounds at least 31 others. Provisional Irish Republican Army
  • 1979: Typhoon Tip becomes the largest and most intense tropical cyclone ever recorded. Typhoon Tip
  • 1976: Indian Airlines Flight 171 crashes at Santacruz Airport in Bombay, India, killing 95. Indian Airlines Flight 171
  • 1970: Vietnam War: Vietnamization continues as President Richard Nixon announces that the United States will withdraw 40,000 more troops before Christmas. Vietnamization
  • 1967: A bomb explodes on board Cyprus Airways Flight 284 while flying over the Mediterranean Sea, killing 66. Cyprus Airways Flight 284
  • 1963: After nearly 23 years of imprisonment, Reverend Walter Ciszek, a Jesuit missionary, was released from the Soviet Union. Walter Ciszek
  • 1959: At the national congress of the American Popular Revolutionary Alliance in Peru, a group of leftist radicals are expelled from the party who later form APRA Rebelde. APRA Rebelde
  • 1945: The Lao Issara took control of Laos' government and reaffirmed the country's independence. Lao Issara
  • 1933: The military Alcatraz Citadel becomes the civilian Alcatraz Federal Penitentiary. Fort Alcatraz
  • 1918: A massive forest fire kills 453 people in Minnesota. Cloquet Fire
  • 1909: Foundation of Coritiba Foot Ball Club. Coritiba Foot Ball Club
  • 1892: The Pledge of Allegiance is first recited by students in many US public schools. Pledge of Allegiance
  • 1871: The British in India enact the Criminal Tribes Act, naming many local communities "Criminal Tribes". Criminal Tribes Act
  • 1849: The city of Manizales, Colombia, is founded by 'The Expedition of the 20'. Manizales
  • 1799: Jeanne Geneviève Labrosse becomes the first woman to jump from a balloon with a parachute. Jeanne Geneviève Garnerin
  • 1793: The cornerstone of Old East, the oldest state university building in the United States, is laid at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. Old East
  • 1773: America's first insane asylum opens. Eastern State Hospital (Virginia)
  • 1692: The Salem witch trials are ended by a letter from Province of Massachusetts Bay Governor William Phips. Salem witch trials
  • 1428: The English and their Burgundian allies begin the Siege of Orleans, the most pivotal investiture of the Hundred Years' War. Battle of Orléans
  • 1398: In the Treaty of Salynas, Lithuania cedes Samogitia to the Teutonic Knights. Treaty of Salynas
  • 633: Battle of Hatfield Chase: King Edwin of Northumbria is defeated and killed by an alliance under Penda of Mercia and Cadwallon of Gwynedd. Battle of Hatfield Chase

About the date itself

October 12 is the 285th day of an ordinary year, with 80 days left after it (81 in a leap year, where it is the 286th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Tuesday, 2028: Thursday, 2029: Friday, 2030: Saturday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 12 October 2027 in 2027 is 12 Jumada al-awwal 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 41.
