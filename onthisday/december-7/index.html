December 7: what happened on this day

What happened on 7 December: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 12 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2015: The JAXA space probe Akatsuki entered into orbit around Venus to study the planet's atmosphere, five years after its first attempt failed. JAXA
  • 2014: The annual furry convention Midwest FurFest was targeted in an unsolved chlorine gas attack. Furry convention
  • 2011: The United States transferred its last base in the Al Anbar Governorate to the Iraqi government, ending the Anbar campaign. Al Anbar Governorate
  • 1995: The Galileo spacecraft arrived at Jupiter, a little more than six years after it was launched by Space Shuttle Atlantis during Mission STS-34. Galileo (spacecraft)
  • 1988: A 6.8 Ms earthquake struck the Spitak region of Armenia, killing at least 25,000 people (aftermath pictured). Surface-wave magnitude
  • 1975: The Indonesian military began a lengthy occupation of East Timor under the pretext of anti-colonialism. Indonesian occupation of East Timor
  • 1972: Construction workers found the remains of Martin Bormann near Lehrter Station in Berlin, ending a decades-long search after his conviction in absentia at the Nuremberg trials. Martin Bormann
  • 1942: Second World War: A small unit of Royal Marines launched Operation Frankton, in which they damaged six ships in the port of Bordeaux in German-occupied France. World War II
  • 1936: Australian cricketer Jack Fingleton (pictured) became the first player to score centuries in four consecutive Test innings. Cricket
  • 1862: American Civil War: The Battle of Prairie Grove ended a Confederate attempt to regain control of northwestern Arkansas. American Civil War
  • 1837: British troops swiftly defeated rebels led by William Lyon Mackenzie and Anthony Van Egmond at the Battle of Montgomery's Tavern, the only major confrontation of the Upper Canada Rebellion. William Lyon Mackenzie
  • 574: Suffering from mental illness, Eastern Roman emperor Justin II had his general Tiberius proclaimed Caesar, adopting him as his own son. Justin II

  • 1732: The Royal Opera House opens at Covent Garden, London, England. Royal Opera House
  • 1703: The Great Storm of 1703, the greatest windstorm ever recorded in the southern part of Great Britain, makes landfall. Winds gust up to 120 mph, and 9,000 people die. Great storm of 1703
  • 1688: Siege of Derry: 13 apprentice boys shut the gates of the town of Derry to a Jacobite army. Siege of Derry
  • 574: Byzantine Emperor Justin II, suffering recurring seizures of insanity, adopts his general Tiberius and proclaims him as Caesar. Justin II

About the date itself

December 7 is the 341st day of an ordinary year, with 24 days left after it (25 in a leap year, where it is the 342nd day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Tuesday, 2028: Thursday, 2029: Friday, 2030: Saturday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 7 December 2027 in 2027 is 9 Rajab 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 49.
