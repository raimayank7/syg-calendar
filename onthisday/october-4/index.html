October 4: what happened on this day

What happened on 4 October: 41 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 12 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 1943: World War II: Allied forces executed Operation Leader, an air raid against German shipping near Bodø, Norway. World War II
  • 1941: Willie Gillis, one of Norman Rockwell's trademark characters, debuted on the cover of The Saturday Evening Post. Willie Gillis
  • 1927: Gutzon Borglum and approximately 400 workers began sculpting Mount Rushmore. Gutzon Borglum
  • 1925: Great Syrian Revolt: Rebels led by Fawzi al-Qawuqji captured the city of Hama from the French Mandate of Syria. Great Syrian Revolt
  • 1918: First World War: The Japanese merhant ship Hirano Maru was sunk by a German submarine in the Celtic Sea with the loss of 291 lives. World War I
  • 1917: First World War: The Allies devastated the German defence at the Battle of Broodseinde, prompting a crisis among German commanders and causing a severe loss of morale in the 4th Army. World War I
  • 1876: Texas A&M University opened as the first public institution of higher education in the U.S. state. Texas A&M University
  • 1862: American Civil War: After a naval battle in Galveston Harbor, Texas, Confederate commanders negotiated the surrender of the city to Union forces. American Civil War
  • 1633: Smolensk War: Forces from the Polish–Lithuanian Commonwealth broke the Russian siege of Smolensk (depicted). Smolensk War
  • 1448: Skanderbeg and Gjergj Arianiti signed a peace treaty to end the Albanian–Venetian War. Skanderbeg
  • 1363: Red Turban Rebellions: The rebel leader Zhu Yuanzhang won the Battle of Lake Poyang by deploying ships intentionally set aflame when the emperor tried to escape. Red Turban Rebellions

More events

  • 2010: The Ajka plant accident in Hungary releases a million cubic metres of liquid alumina sludge, killing nine, injuring 122, and severely contaminating two major rivers. Ajka alumina plant accident

  • 1925: S2, a Finnish Sokol class torpedo boat, sinks during a fierce storm near the coast of Pori in the Gulf of Bothnia, taking with it the whole crew of 53. Finnish torpedo boat S2

  • 1917: World War I: The Battle of Broodseinde is fought between the British and German armies in Flanders. World War I
  • 1883: First meeting of the Boys' Brigade in Glasgow, Scotland. Boys' Brigade

  • 1824: Mexico adopts a new constitution and becomes a federal republic. 1824 Constitution of Mexico

  • 1693: Nine Years' War: Piedmontese troops are defeated by the French. Nine Years' War

  • 1582: The Gregorian Calendar is introduced by Pope Gregory XIII. Gregorian calendar
  • 1511: Formation of the Holy League of Aragon, the Papal States and Venice against France. War of the League of Cambrai
  • 1363: Battle of Lake Poyang: In one of the largest naval battles in history, Zhu Yuanzhang's rebels defeat rival Chen Youliang. Battle of Lake Poyang
  • 1209: Otto IV is crowned Emperor of the Holy Roman Empire by Pope Innocent III. Otto IV, Holy Roman Emperor

About the date itself

October 4 is the 277th day of an ordinary year, with 88 days left after it (89 in a leap year, where it is the 278th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Monday, 2028: Wednesday, 2029: Thursday, 2030: Friday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 4 October 2027 in 2027 is 4 Jumada al-awwal 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 40.
