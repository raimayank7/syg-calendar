March 11: what happened on this day

What happened on 11 March: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 8 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2020: The World Health Organization (WHO) officialy declared the COVID-19 outbreak as a pandemic. World Health Organization
  • 2020: The World Health Organization (WHO) declared the COVID-19 virus epidemic a pandemic. World Health Organization
  • 2012: U.S. Army soldier Robert Bales murdered sixteen civilians and wounded six others in Kandahar Province, Afghanistan. United States Army

About the date itself

March 11 is the 70th day of an ordinary year, with 295 days left after it (296 in a leap year, where it is the 71st day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Thursday, 2028: Saturday, 2029: Sunday, 2030: Monday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 11 March 2027 in 2027 is 3 Shawwal 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table.
