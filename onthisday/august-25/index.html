August 25: what happened on this day

What happened on 25 August: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2012: The NASA space probe Voyager 1 became the first man-made object to enter interstellar space. NASA
  • 2012: The skeleton of King Richard III of England was discovered under a car park in Leicester. Richard III of England
  • 2011: Mexican drug war: Fifty-two people were killed in an arson attack at a casino in Monterrey, Mexico. Mexican drug war
  • 1989: The NASA spacecraft Voyager 2 made its closest approach to Neptune and provided definitive proof of the existence of the planet's rings (pictured). NASA
  • 1975: Bruce Springsteen released his commercial breakthrough album, Born to Run. Bruce Springsteen
  • 1967: Founder of the American Nazi Party George Lincoln Rockwell was assassinated by John Patler, a former member of his group. American Nazi Party
  • 1914: World War I: During the sack of Louvain in Belgium, German troops burned the town's Catholic university, destroying hundreds of medieval manuscripts. World War I
  • 1875: Matthew Webb became the first person to swim across the English Channel, doing so in approximately 21 hours 40 minutes. Matthew Webb
  • 1835: The New York Sun perpetrated the Great Moon Hoax, publishing articles about the supposed discovery of life on the Moon (lithograph pictured). The Sun (New York City)
  • 1609: Italian astronomer Galileo Galilei (pictured) demonstrated his first telescope to Venetian lawmakers. Galileo Galilei
  • 1537: The Honourable Artillery Company, now the oldest surviving regiment in the British Army, was granted a royal charter by Henry VIII. Honourable Artillery Company
  • 1270: Philip III became King of France following the death of his father Louis IX during the Eighth Crusade. Philip III of France

More events

  • 2017: Hurricane Harvey makes landfall in Texas as a powerful Category 4 hurricane, the strongest hurricane to make landfall in the United States since 2004. Hurricane Harvey
  • 2012: Voyager 1 spacecraft enters interstellar space, becoming the first man-made object to do so. Voyager 1
  • 2010: A Filair Let L-410 Turbolet crashes on approach to Bandundu Airport, killing 20. Filair
  • 2005: Hurricane Katrina makes landfall in Florida. Hurricane Katrina
  • 2001: American singer Aaliyah and several members of her entourage are killed as their overloaded aircraft crashes shortly after takeoff from Marsh Harbour Airport, Bahamas. Aaliyah
  • 1991: Belarus gains its independence from the Soviet Union. Belarus
  • 1991: Linus Torvalds announces the first version of what will become Linux. Linus Torvalds
  • 1989: Pakistan International Airlines Flight 404, carrying 54 people, disappears over the Himalayas after takeoff from Gilgit Airport in Pakistan. The aircraft was never found. Pakistan International Airlines Flight 404
  • 1981: Voyager 2 spacecraft makes its closest approach to Saturn. Voyager 2
  • 1980: The last performance of the Jahrhundertring at the Bayreuth Festival receives ovations of 45 minutes. Jahrhundertring
  • 1960: The Games of the XVII Olympiad commence in Rome, Italy. 1960 Summer Olympics
  • 1950: To avert a threatened strike during the Korean War, President Truman orders Secretary of the Army Frank Pace to seize control of the nation's railroads. Frank Pace
  • 1945: Ten days after World War II ends with Japan announcing its surrender, armed supporters of the Chinese Communist Party kill U.S. intelligence officer John Birch, regarded by some of the American right as the first victim of the Cold War. Victory over Japan Day
  • 1944: World War II: Paris is liberated by the Allies. Liberation of Paris
  • 1942: World War II: Battle of Milne Bay: Japanese marines assault Allied airfields at Milne Bay, New Guinea, initiating the Battle of Milne Bay. Battle of Milne Bay
  • 1940: World War II: The first Bombing of Berlin by the British Royal Air Force. World War II
  • 1939: The United Kingdom and Poland form a military alliance in which the UK promises to defend Poland in case of invasion by a foreign power. Second Polish Republic
  • 1933: Nazi Germany and the Zionist Federation of Germany signed the Haavara Agreement. The agreement was a major factor in breaking the anti-Nazi boycott of 1933 and facilitated Jewish emigration from Germany and into British Mandate of Palestine. Nazi Germany
  • 1916: The United States National Park Service is created. National Park Service
  • 1914: World War I: The library of the Catholic University of Leuven is deliberately destroyed by the German Army. Hundreds of thousands of irreplaceable volumes and Gothic and Renaissance manuscripts are lost. Sack of Louvain
  • 1894: Kitasato Shibasaburō discovers the infectious agent of the bubonic plague and publishes his findings in The Lancet. Kitasato Shibasaburō
  • 1875: Captain Matthew Webb becomes the first person to swim across the English Channel, traveling from Dover, England, to Calais, France, in 21 hours and 45 minutes. Matthew Webb
  • 1830: The Belgian Revolution begins. Belgian Revolution
  • 1823: American fur trapper Hugh Glass is mauled by a grizzly bear while on an expedition in South Dakota. Hugh Glass
  • 1758: Seven Years' War: Frederick II of Prussia defeats the Russian army at the Battle of Zorndorf. Seven Years' War
  • 1630: Portuguese forces are defeated by the Kingdom of Kandy at the Battle of Randeniwela in Sri Lanka. Kingdom of Kandy
  • 1580: War of the Portuguese Succession: Spanish victory at the Battle of Alcântara brings about the Iberian Union. War of the Portuguese Succession
  • 1537: The Honourable Artillery Company, the oldest surviving regiment in the British Army, and the second most senior, is formed. Honourable Artillery Company
  • 1258: Regent George Mouzalon and his brothers are killed during a coup headed by the aristocratic faction under Michael VIII Palaiologos, paving the way for its leader to ultimately usurp the throne of the Empire of Nicaea. George Mouzalon
  • 766: Emperor Constantine V humiliates nineteen high-ranking officials, after discovering a plot against him. He executes the leaders, Constantine Podopagouros and his brother Strategios. Constantine V

About the date itself

August 25 is the 237th day of an ordinary year, with 128 days left after it (129 in a leap year, where it is the 238th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Wednesday, 2028: Friday, 2029: Saturday, 2030: Sunday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 25 August 2027 in 2027 is 23 Rabi' al-awwal 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 34.
