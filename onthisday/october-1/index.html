October 1: what happened on this day

What happened on 1 October: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2022: One hundred and thirty-five were killed in a human crush following a football match at Kanjuruhan Stadium in East Java, Indonesia. . Kanjuruhan Stadium disaster
  • 2017: A lone gunman fired more than 1,000 rounds of ammunition from his hotel suite on a crowd attending the Route 91 Harvest music festival on the Las Vegas Strip, resulting in 60 deaths and 867 injuries. Stephen Paddock
  • 2003: A levy was imposed on the hiring of foreign domestic helpers in Hong Kong, who numbered in the hundreds of thousands at the time. Foreign domestic helpers in Hong Kong
  • 1994: A tribunal was established to consider matters relating to the constitution of Singapore upon referral by the president. Constitution of the Republic of Singapore Tribunal
  • 1990: Fifty Rwandan Patriotic Front rebels deserted their Ugandan Army posts and crossed the border from Uganda into Rwanda, marking the start of the Rwandan Civil War. Rwandan Patriotic Front
  • 1978: Tuvalu adopted its national flag (pictured) on the day that the country gained its independence. Flag of Tuvalu
  • 1965: Seven Indonesian National Armed Forces officers, six of them generals, were murdered at dawn by a rebellious force in Jakarta; the Army then blamed the Communist Party, leading to a mass anti-communist purge that killed up to one million people. Indonesian National Armed Forces
  • 1949: Chinese Communist Party chairman Mao Zedong publicly proclaimed (pictured) the establishment of the People's Republic of China in Beijing's Tiananmen Square. Chinese Communist Party
  • 1918: First World War: British and Arab troops captured Damascus from the Ottoman Empire. World War I
  • 1891: Stanford University, founded by railroad magnate and politician Leland Stanford and his wife Jane in Palo Alto, California, admitted its first students. Stanford University
  • 1868: St Pancras railway station (pictured) in London, now the terminus of the Channel Tunnel Rail Link, opened to the public. St Pancras railway station
  • 1800: With the signing of the Third Treaty of San Ildefonso, Spain returned the colonial territory of Louisiana to France in return for territories in the Italian region of Tuscany. Third Treaty of San Ildefonso

About the date itself

October 1 is the 274th day of an ordinary year, with 91 days left after it (92 in a leap year, where it is the 275th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Friday, 2028: Sunday, 2029: Monday, 2030: Tuesday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 1 October 2027 in 2027 is 1 Jumada al-awwal 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 39.
