October 3: what happened on this day

What happened on 3 October: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2013: A boat carrying migrants from Libya to Italy sank off the Italian island of Lampedusa, resulting in more than 360 deaths. 2013 Lampedusa migrant shipwreck
  • 2008: The Emergency Economic Stabilization Act of 2008, establishing the Troubled Asset Relief Program, commonly referred to as a bailout of the U.S. financial system, was enacted. Emergency Economic Stabilization Act of 2008
  • 1992: Sinéad O'Connor tore up a photograph of Pope John Paul II on live television. Sinéad O'Connor
  • 1991: Nadine Gordimer became the first South African to win the Nobel Prize in Literature. Nadine Gordimer
  • 1981: A hunger strike by Irish republican prisoners at HM Prison Maze outside Belfast, Northern Ireland, ended after seven months and ten deaths. 1981 Irish hunger strike
  • 1963: Oswaldo López Arellano replaced Honduran president Ramón Villeda Morales in a violent coup, initiating two decades of military rule. Oswaldo López Arellano
  • 1953: Vancouver's Holy Rosary Cathedral was dedicated by Archbishop William Mark Duke, fifty-three years after it first opened. Holy Rosary Cathedral (Vancouver)
  • 1952: The United Kingdom successfully conducted its first nuclear test, becoming the world's third state with nuclear weapons. Operation Hurricane
  • 1951: The First Battle of Maryang-san, widely regarded as one of the Australian Army's greatest accomplishments during the Korean War, began. First Battle of Maryang-san
  • 1935: Italian forces under General Emilio De Bono invaded Abyssinia during the opening stages of the Second Italo-Abyssinian War. Emilio De Bono
  • 1792: Spanish forces departed Valdivia to suppress the indigenous Huilliche uprising in southern Chile. Valdivia
  • 1602: Anglo-Spanish War: An English fleet intercepted and attacked six Spanish ships at the Battle of the Narrow Seas (pictured). Anglo-Spanish War (1585–1604)

About the date itself

October 3 is the 276th day of an ordinary year, with 89 days left after it (90 in a leap year, where it is the 277th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Sunday, 2028: Tuesday, 2029: Wednesday, 2030: Thursday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 3 October 2027 in 2027 is 3 Jumada al-awwal 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 39.
