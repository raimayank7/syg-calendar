April 1: what happened on this day

What happened on 1 April: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2001: Same-sex marriage in the Netherlands was legalized, making it the first country to do so. Same-sex marriage in the Netherlands
  • 2001: An American Lockheed EP-3 and a Chinese Shenyang J-8 collided in mid-air off Hainan, resulting in an international dispute between the two countries. Lockheed EP-3
  • 1976: Steve Jobs and Steve Wozniak founded Apple Computer, Inc. (first logo pictured). Steve Jobs
  • 1970: U.S. president Richard Nixon signed the Public Health Cigarette Smoking Act into law, requiring that a prominent warning by the surgeon general be placed on cigarette packages. Richard Nixon
  • 1969: The Hawker Siddeley Harrier, the first operational fighter aircraft with V/STOL capabilities, entered service with the Royal Air Force. Hawker Siddeley Harrier
  • 1955: The British Army's Mobile Defence Corps, intended to assist with civil defence after a nuclear attack, was established by royal warrant. Mobile Defence Corps
  • 1941: Soviet border guards opened fire on civilians attempting to cross the border from the Soviet Union to Romania near Fântâna Albă, killing between 44 and 3,000 people. Fântâna Albă massacre
  • 1922: Under the South Seas Mandate, Japan set up a government in Koror, precipitating large-scale Japanese settlement in Palau. South Seas Mandate
  • 1879: The Inland Customs Line, established by the British for the collection of the salt tax in India, was abandoned. Inland Customs Line
  • 1865: American Civil War: The Union Army under Major General Philip Sheridan (pictured) inflicted more than 2,900 casualties on the Confederates at the Battle of Five Forks. American Civil War
  • 1346: Hundred Years' War: John, Duke of Normandy, laid siege to the town of Aiguillon. Hundred Years' War
  • 1293: Robert Winchelsey left England for Rome to be consecrated Archbishop of Canterbury, but a papal vacancy delayed the ceremony. Robert Winchelsey

More events

  • 2026: The Artemis II lunar flyby mission launches, marking the first crewed flight above low Earth orbit since Apollo 17 in 1972. Artemis II
  • 2016: The 2016 Nagorno-Karabakh conflict begins along the Nagorno-Karabakh Line of Contact. 2016 Nagorno-Karabakh conflict
  • 2006: Serious Organised Crime Agency (SOCA) of the Government of the United Kingdom is enforced, but later merged into National Crime Agency on 7 October 2013. Serious Organised Crime Agency
  • 2001: An EP-3E United States Navy surveillance aircraft collides with a Chinese People's Liberation Army Shenyang J-8 fighter jet. The Chinese pilot ejected but is subsequently lost. The Navy crew makes an emergency landing in Hainan, China and is detained. Lockheed EP-3
  • 2001: Same-sex marriage becomes legal in the Netherlands, the first contemporary country to allow it. Same-sex marriage in the Netherlands
  • 1997: Comet Hale–Bopp is seen passing at perihelion. Comet Hale–Bopp
  • 1989: Margaret Thatcher's new local government tax, the Community Charge (commonly known as the "poll tax"), is introduced in Scotland. Margaret Thatcher
  • 1984: Singer Marvin Gaye is shot to death by his father in his home in Arlington Heights, Los Angeles, California. Marvin Gaye
  • 1976: Steve Jobs and Steve Wozniak found Apple Computer, Inc. Steve Jobs
  • 1973: Project Tiger, a tiger conservation project, is launched in the Jim Corbett National Park, India. Project Tiger
  • 1970: President Richard Nixon signs the Public Health Cigarette Smoking Act into law. Richard Nixon
  • 1970: A Royal Air Maroc Sud Aviation Caravelle crashes near Berrechid, Morocco, killing 61. Royal Air Maroc
  • 1964: The British Admiralty, War Office and Air Ministry are replaced by a unified Defence Council of the United Kingdom. Admiralty (United Kingdom)
  • 1955: The EOKA rebellion against the British Empire begins in Cyprus, with the goal of unifying with Greece. EOKA
  • 1949: The Government of Canada repeals Japanese-Canadian internment after seven years. Government of Canada
  • 1948: Faroe Islands gain autonomy from Denmark. Faroe Islands
  • 1946: The 8.6 Mw Aleutian Islands earthquake shakes the Aleutian Islands with a maximum Mercalli intensity of VI (Strong). A destructive tsunami reaches the Hawaiian Islands resulting in dozens of deaths, mostly in Hilo, Hawaii. 1946 Aleutian Islands earthquake
  • 1945: World War II: The Tenth United States Army attacks the Thirty-Second Japanese Army on Okinawa. Tenth United States Army
  • 1941: Fântâna Albă massacre: Between two hundred and two thousand Romanian civilians are killed by Soviet Border Troops. Fântâna Albă massacre
  • 1939: Spanish Civil War: Generalísimo Francisco Franco of the Spanish State announces the end of the Spanish Civil War, when the last of the Republican forces surrender. Generalissimo
  • 1937: The Royal New Zealand Air Force is formed as an independent service. Royal New Zealand Air Force
  • 1935: India's central banking institution, the Reserve Bank of India, is formed. Reserve Bank of India
  • 1924: Adolf Hitler is sentenced to five years fortress confinement for his participation in the "Beer Hall Putsch" but spends only nine months in jail. Adolf Hitler
  • 1922: In newly formed Northern Ireland, six Catholics are murdered in the Arnon Street killings, one week after six others were killed in the McMahon killings. Northern Ireland
  • 1908: The Territorial Force (renamed Territorial Army in 1920) is formed as a volunteer reserve component of the British Army. Territorial Force
  • 1873: The White Star steamer SS Atlantic sinks off Nova Scotia, killing 547 in one of the worst marine disasters of the 19th century. White Star Line
  • 1865: American Civil War: Union troops led by Philip Sheridan decisively defeat Confederate troops led by George Pickett, cutting the Army of Northern Virginia's last supply line during the Siege of Petersburg. American Civil War
  • 1789: In New York City, the United States House of Representatives achieves its first quorum and elects Frederick Muhlenberg of Pennsylvania as its first Speaker. United States House of Representatives
  • 1572: In the Eighty Years' War, the Watergeuzen capture Brielle from the Seventeen Provinces, gaining the first foothold on land for what would become the Dutch Republic. Eighty Years' War
  • 527: Byzantine Emperor Justin I names his nephew Justinian I as co-ruler and successor to the throne. List of Byzantine emperors

About the date itself

April 1 is the 91st day of an ordinary year, with 274 days left after it (275 in a leap year, where it is the 92nd day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Thursday, 2028: Saturday, 2029: Sunday, 2030: Monday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 1 April 2027 in 2027 is 24 Shawwal 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 13.
