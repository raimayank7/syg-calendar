December 17: what happened on this day

What happened on 17 December: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 14 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2016: The first victims of a mass methanol poisoning which eventually killed over 70 inhabitants of Irkutsk, Russia, began arriving at hospitals. 2016 Irkutsk mass methanol poisoning
  • 2010: Arab Spring: Mohamed Bouazizi, a street vendor in Sidi Bouzid, set himself on fire in protest against police harassment, triggering the Tunisian revolution. Arab Spring
  • 1983: The Troubles: The Provisional Irish Republican Army detonated a car bomb outside Harrods in London, killing six people and injuring about 90 others. The Troubles
  • 1970: Polish soldiers fired at workers (memorial pictured) emerging from trains in Gdynia, beginning the government's crackdown on mass anti-communist protests across the country. December 1970 protests in Poland
  • 1951: The Civil Rights Congress presented a document to the United Nations Genocide Convention charging the United States government with genocide against African Americans. Civil Rights Congress
  • 1948: The Finnish Security Police was established to remove communist leadership from its predecessor, the State Police. Finnish Security and Intelligence Service
  • 1944: Nazi troops under Joachim Peiper killed unarmed prisoners of war, captured during the Battle of the Bulge, with machine guns near Malmedy, Belgium. Joachim Peiper
  • 1939: World War II: After sustaining moderate damage in the Battle of the River Plate two days earlier, the German cruiser Graf Spee was scuttled by its commander, Hans Langsdorff, to avoid its internment by Uruguay. World War II
  • 1918: About 1,000 demonstrators marched (pictured) on Government House in Darwin, Australia, where they burned an effigy of Administrator John Gilruth and demanded his resignation. Darwin rebellion
  • 1862: American Civil War: Union General Ulysses S. Grant issued General Order No. 11, expelling Jews from Tennessee, Mississippi, and Kentucky. American Civil War
  • 1583: Cologne War: Forces under Ernest of Bavaria defeated Gebhard Truchsess von Waldburg's troops at the siege of Godesberg. Cologne War
  • 942: William Longsword of Normandy was ambushed and assassinated by supporters of Arnulf I, Count of Flanders, while both were at a peace conference to settle their differences. William Longsword

More events

  • 2014: The United States and Cuba re-establish diplomatic relations after severing them in 1961. Cuba–United States relations
  • 2009: MV Danny F II sinks off the coast of Lebanon, resulting in the deaths of 44 people and over 28,000 animals. MV Danny F II
  • 2005: Jigme Singye Wangchuck abdicates the throne as King of Bhutan. Jigme Singye Wangchuck
  • 2003: SpaceShipOne, piloted by Brian Binnie, makes its first powered and first supersonic flight. SpaceShipOne
  • 2002: Second Congo War: The Congolese parties of the Inter Congolese Dialogue sign a peace accord which makes provision for transitional governance and legislative and presidential elections within two years. Second Congo War
  • 1997: Aerosvit Flight 241: A Yakovlev Yak-42 crashes into the Pierian Mountains near Thessaloniki Airport in Thessaloniki, Greece, killing all 70 people on board. Aerosvit Flight 241
  • 1989: Fernando Collor de Mello defeats Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva in the second round of the Brazilian presidential election, becoming the first democratically elected President in almost 30 years. Fernando Collor de Mello
  • 1983: Provisional IRA members detonate a car bomb at Harrods Department Store in London. Three police officers and three civilians are killed. Provisional Irish Republican Army
  • 1970: Polish protests: In Gdynia, soldiers fire at workers emerging from trains, killing dozens. December 1970 protests in Poland
  • 1967: Harold Holt, Prime Minister of Australia, disappears while swimming near Portsea, Victoria, and is presumed drowned. Harold Holt
  • 1960: Troops loyal to Emperor Haile Selassie in Ethiopia crush the coup that began December 13, returning power to their leader upon his return from Brazil. Haile Selassie absolves his son of any guilt. Haile Selassie
  • 1957: The United States successfully launches the first Atlas intercontinental ballistic missile at Cape Canaveral, Florida. SM-65 Atlas
  • 1950: The F-86 Sabre's first mission over Korea. North American F-86 Sabre
  • 1947: First flight of the Boeing B-47 Stratojet strategic bomber. Boeing B-47 Stratojet
  • 1944: World War II: Battle of the Bulge: Malmedy massacre: American 285th Field Artillery Observation Battalion POWs are shot by Waffen-SS Kampfgruppe Joachim Peiper. Battle of the Bulge
  • 1938: Otto Hahn discovers the nuclear fission of the heavy element uranium, the scientific and technological basis of nuclear energy. Otto Hahn
  • 1933: The first NFL Championship Game is played at Wrigley Field in Chicago between the New York Giants and Chicago Bears. The Bears win 23–21. 1933 NFL Championship Game
  • 1927: Indian revolutionary Rajendra Lahiri is hanged in Gonda jail, Uttar Pradesh, India, two days before the scheduled date. Rajendra Lahiri
  • 1918: Darwin Rebellion: Up to 1,000 demonstrators march on Government House in Darwin, Northern Territory, Australia. Darwin rebellion
  • 1903: The Wright brothers make the first controlled powered, heavier-than-air flight in the Wright Flyer at Kitty Hawk, North Carolina. Wright brothers
  • 1892: First issue of Vogue is published. Vogue (magazine)
  • 1862: American Civil War: General Ulysses S. Grant issues General Order No. 11, expelling Jews from parts of Tennessee, Mississippi, and Kentucky. American Civil War
  • 1835: The second Great Fire of New York destroys 53,000 square metres (13 acres) of New York City's Financial District. Great Fire of New York
  • 1807: Napoleonic Wars: France issues the Milan Decree, which confirms the Continental System. Napoleonic Wars
  • 1777: American Revolution: France formally recognizes the United States. American Revolution
  • 1665: The first account of a blood transfusion is published, in the form of a letter from physician Richard Lower to chemist Robert Boyle, in Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society Blood transfusion
  • 1583: Cologne War: Forces under Ernest of Bavaria defeat troops under Gebhard Truchsess von Waldburg at the Siege of Godesberg. Cologne War
  • 1398: Sultan Nasir-u Din Mehmud's armies in Delhi are defeated by Timur. Tughlaq dynasty
  • 1297: King Kyawswa of Pagan is overthrown by the three Myinsaing brothers, marking the de facto end of the Pagan Kingdom. Kyawswa of Pagan
  • 920: Romanos I Lekapenos is crowned co-emperor of the underage Constantine VII. Romanos I Lekapenos

About the date itself

December 17 is the 351st day of an ordinary year, with 14 days left after it (15 in a leap year, where it is the 352nd day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Friday, 2028: Sunday, 2029: Monday, 2030: Tuesday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 17 December 2027 in 2027 is 19 Rajab 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 50.
