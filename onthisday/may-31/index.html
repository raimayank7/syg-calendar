May 31: what happened on this day

What happened on 31 May: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 12 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2019: A shooting at a municipal building in Virginia Beach, United States, left 13 people dead, including the gunman, and injured four others. 2019 Virginia Beach shooting
  • 2017: A car bomb exploded near the German embassy in Kabul, Afghanistan, killing more than 90 people and injuring 463 others. May 2017 Kabul bombing
  • 2013: An extremely large, powerful, and erratic tornado struck Central Oklahoma, killing eight people and injuring more than 150 others. 2013 El Reno tornado
  • 2009: American physician George Tiller, one of the few doctors in the country who performed late-term abortions, was shot and killed by Scott Roeder, an anti-abortion activist. George Tiller
  • 1941: World War II: German forces dropped four bombs on neutral Ireland, killing 28 people and damaging Áras an Uachtaráin, the residence of the President of Ireland. World War II
  • 1935: A magnitude-7.7 earthquake struck Balochistan in British India, now part of Pakistan, killing between 30,000 and 60,000 people 1935 Quetta earthquake
  • 1902: The Second Boer War came to an end with the signing of the Treaty of Vereeniging in Pretoria, South Africa. Second Boer War
  • 1862: American Civil War: Confederate troops under Joseph E. Johnston and G. W. Smith engaged Union forces under George B. McClellan at the Battle of Seven Pines outside Richmond, Virginia. American Civil War
  • 1468: Cardinal Bessarion announced his donation of 746 Greek and Latin codices to the Republic of Venice, forming the Biblioteca Marciana. Bessarion
  • 1293: Majapahit forces under Raden Wijaya won a major victory in the Mongol invasion of Java, now considered to be the founding date of the Indonesian city of Surabaya. Majapahit
  • 1223: Mongol invasion of Kievan Rus': Mongol forces defeated a Kievan Rus' army at the Battle of the Kalka River in present-day Ukraine. Mongol invasion of Kievan Rus'
  • 1215: A Mongol army under Genghis Khan captured Zhongdu (modern Beijing), the capital of the Jin dynasty of China. Mongol Empire

More events

  • 2019: A shooting occurs inside a municipal building at Virginia Beach, Virginia, leaving 13 people dead, including the shooter, and four others injured. 2019 Virginia Beach shooting
  • 2016: Syrian civil war: The Syrian Democratic Forces (SDF) launch the Manbij offensive, in order to capture the city of Manbij from the Islamic State of Iraq and the Levant (ISIL). Syrian civil war
  • 2013: A record breaking 2.6 mile wide tornado strikes near El Reno, Oklahoma, United States, causing eight fatalities (including three storm chasers) and over 150 injuries. 2013 El Reno tornado
  • 2008: Usain Bolt breaks the world record in the 100m sprint, with a wind-legal (+1.7 m/s) 9.72 seconds. Usain Bolt
  • 2005: Vanity Fair reveals that Mark Felt was "Deep Throat". Vanity Fair (magazine)
  • 1997: The Confederation Bridge opens, linking Prince Edward Island with mainland New Brunswick. Confederation Bridge
  • 1986: The Ariane 2 rocket is launched on its maiden flight, carrying Intelsat VA F-14, but the mission ends in failure. Ariane 2
  • 1977: The Trans-Alaska Pipeline System is completed. Trans-Alaska Pipeline System
  • 1973: Indian Airlines Flight 440 crashes near Palam Airport in Delhi, killing 48. Indian Airlines Flight 440
  • 1970: The 7.9 Mw Ancash earthquake shakes Peru with a maximum Mercalli intensity of VIII (Severe) and a landslide buries the town of Yungay, Peru. Between 66,794 and 70,000 were killed and 50,000 were injured. 1970 Ancash earthquake
  • 1961: The South African Constitution of 1961 becomes effective, thus creating the Republic of South Africa, which remains outside the Commonwealth of Nations until 1 June 1994, when South Africa is returned to Commonwealth membership. South African Constitution of 1961
  • 1955: The U.S. Supreme Court expands on its Brown v. Board of Education decision by ordering district courts and school districts to enforce educational desegregation "at all deliberate speed." Supreme Court of the United States
  • 1947: Ferenc Nagy, the democratically elected Prime Minister of Hungary, resigns from office after blackmail from the Hungarian Communist Party accusing him of being part of a plot against the state. This grants the Communists effective control of the Hungarian government. Ferenc Nagy
  • 1941: Anglo-Iraqi War: The United Kingdom completes the re-occupation of Iraq and returns 'Abd al-Ilah to power as regent for Faisal II. Anglo-Iraqi War
  • 1924: Hope Development School fire kills 24 people, mostly disabled children. Hope Development School fire
  • 1921: The Tulsa race massacre kills at least 39, but other estimates of black fatalities vary from 55 to about 300. Tulsa race massacre
  • 1911: The RMS Titanic is launched in Belfast, Northern Ireland. Titanic
  • 1910: The South Africa Act comes into force, establishing the Union of South Africa. South Africa Act 1909
  • 1906: The attempted regicide of Spanish King Alfonso XIII and Queen Victoria Eugenie on their wedding day instead kills 24. Morral affair
  • 1889: Johnstown Flood: Over 2,200 people die after a dam fails and sends a 60-foot (18-meter) wall of water over the town of Johnstown, Pennsylvania. Johnstown Flood
  • 1879: Gilmore's Garden in New York City is renamed Madison Square Garden by William Henry Vanderbilt and is opened to the public at 26th Street and Madison Avenue. Madison Square Garden (1879)
  • 1862: American Civil War: Peninsula Campaign: Confederate forces under Joseph E. Johnston and G.W. Smith engage Union forces under George B. McClellan outside the Confederate capital of Richmond, Virginia. American Civil War
  • 1847: Qajar Iran and the Ottoman Empire determine their international boundary in the second treaty of Erzurum. Qajar Iran
  • 1805: French and Spanish forces begin the assault against British forces occupying Diamond Rock, Martinique. Battle of Diamond Rock
  • 1790: Manuel Quimper explores the Strait of Juan de Fuca. Manuel Quimper
  • 1775: American Revolution: The Mecklenburg Resolves are adopted in the Province of North Carolina. American Revolution
  • 1610: The pageant London's Love to Prince Henry on the River Thames celebrates the creation of Prince Henry as Prince of Wales. London's Love to Prince Henry
  • 1293: Mongols depart Java after the failed Mongol invasion against King Kertanegara of Singhasari. Mongols
  • 1215: Zhongdu (now Beijing), then under the control of the Jurchen ruler Emperor Xuanzong of Jin, is captured by the Mongols under Genghis Khan, ending the Battle of Zhongdu. Zhongdu

About the date itself

May 31 is the 151st day of an ordinary year, with 214 days left after it (215 in a leap year, where it is the 152nd day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Monday, 2028: Wednesday, 2029: Thursday, 2030: Friday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 31 May 2027 in 2027 is 25 Dhu al-Hijjah 1448 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 22.
