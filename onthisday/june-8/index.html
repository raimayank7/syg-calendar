June 8: what happened on this day

What happened on 8 June: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2009: Two American journalists, having been arrested for illegal entry into North Korea, were sentenced to twelve years hard labor before being pardoned two months later. 2009 imprisonment of American journalists by North Korea
  • 2008: Seven people were killed and 10 others were injured in a vehicle and knife attack in Tokyo, Japan. Akihabara massacre
  • 2007: A major storm caused the bulk carrier Pasha Bulker to run aground in New South Wales, Australia. 2007 New South Wales storms
  • 1982: Falklands War: The Argentine Air Force attacked British transport ships (damaged ship pictured) while unloading supplies off Bluff Cove in the Falkland Islands, killing 56 British servicemen and wounding 150 others. Falklands War
  • 1972: Vietnam War: Associated Press photographer Nick Ut took a Pulitzer Prize–winning photograph of a naked nine-year-old Phan Thi Kim Phuc fleeing after being burned by napalm. Vietnam War
  • 1953: An F5 tornado struck Flint and Beecher, Michigan, causing 116 fatalities, 844 injuries and $19 million in damage during a larger tornado outbreak sequence. 1953 Flint–Beecher tornado
  • 1950: Thomas Blamey became the only Australian to attain the rank of field marshal. Thomas Blamey
  • 1949: Nineteen Eighty-Four, a dystopian political novel by the English writer George Orwell about life under a fictional totalitarian government, was published. Nineteen Eighty-Four
  • 1929: Margaret Bondfield became the first female member of the Cabinet of the United Kingdom when she was named Minister of Labour by Ramsay MacDonald. Margaret Bondfield
  • 1862: American Civil War: The Confederate Army won a resounding victory at the Battle of Cross Keys, one of the two decisive battles in Jackson's Valley campaign. American Civil War
  • 1783: Laki, a volcanic fissure in Iceland, began an eight-month eruption, triggering a major famine and causing widespread fluoride poisoning. Laki
  • 1776: American Revolutionary War: British forces defeated the Continental Army at the Battle of Trois-Rivières, the last battle of the American invasion of Quebec. American Revolutionary War

About the date itself

It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Tuesday, 2028: Thursday, 2029: Friday, 2030: Saturday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 8 June 2027 in 2027 is 3 Muharram 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table.
