August 30: what happened on this day

What happened on 30 August: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 15 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2025: Ukrainian politician Andriy Parubiy (pictured) was assassinated in Lviv, Ukraine. Andriy Parubiy
  • 2007: A heavy bomber that had been unintentionally loaded with nuclear missiles flew them from North Dakota to Louisiana before they were recognized. Heavy bomber
  • 1984: Discovery, the third orbiter of NASA's Space Shuttle program, lifted off on its maiden voyage from Kennedy Space Center, Florida. Space Shuttle Discovery
  • 1981: Iranian president Mohammad-Ali Rajai and prime minister Mohammad-Javad Bahonar were assassinated in a bombing committed by the People's Mojahedin Organization of Iran. Mohammad-Ali Rajai
  • 1974: A bomb exploded at the Mitsubishi Heavy Industries headquarters in Marunouchi, Tokyo, killing eight people. 1974 Mitsubishi Heavy Industries bombing
  • 1967: Thurgood Marshall was confirmed as the first African-American Supreme Court justice. Thurgood Marshall
  • 1959: Writer and politician Abdul Muis became the first person to be awarded the posthumous title of National Hero of Indonesia. Abdul Muis
  • 1942: Second World War: German field marshal Erwin Rommel launched the last major Axis offensive of the Western Desert campaign, attacking British positions near El Alamein, Egypt. World War II
  • 1914: World War I: The Battle of Tannenberg resulted in the almost complete destruction of the Russian 2nd Army by the German 8th Army. World War I
  • 1896: Philippine Revolution: In the Battle of San Juan del Monte, the first real battle of the war, a Katipunan force temporarily captured a powder magazine before being beaten back by a Spanish garrison. Philippine Revolution
  • 1594: King James VI of Scotland held a masque at the baptism of Prince Henry, his first child. James VI and I
  • 1574: Guru Ram Das (pictured) became the fourth of the Sikh gurus, the spiritual masters of Sikhism. Guru Ram Das

More events

  • 2023: Gabonese coup d'état: After Ali Bongo Ondimba's reelection, a military coup ousts him, ending 56 years of Bongo family rule in Gabon. 2023 Gabonese coup d'état
  • 2021: The last remaining American troops leave Afghanistan, ending U.S. involvement in the war. 2020–2021 US troop withdrawal from Afghanistan
  • 2008: A Conviasa Boeing 737 crashes into Illiniza Volcano in Ecuador, killing all three people on board. Conviasa
  • 1998: Second Congo War: Armed forces of the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC) and their Angolan and Zimbabwean allies recapture Matadi and the Inga dams in the western DRC from RCD and Rwandan troops. Second Congo War
  • 1992: The 11-day Ruby Ridge standoff ends with Randy Weaver surrendering to federal authorities. Ruby Ridge standoff
  • 1984: STS-41-D: The Space Shuttle Discovery takes off on its maiden voyage. STS-41-D
  • 1983: STS-8: The Space Shuttle Challenger takes off on the first night launch of the shuttle program. Guion Bluford becomes the first African-American in space on this mission. STS-8
  • 1974: A Belgrade–Dortmund express train derails at the main train station in Zagreb killing 153 passengers. Belgrade
  • 1974: The Third World Population Conference ends in Bucharest, Romania. At the end of the ceremony, the UN-Romanian Demographic Centre is inaugurated. World Population Conference
  • 1963: The Moscow–Washington hotline between the leaders of the U.S. and the Soviet Union goes into operation. Moscow–Washington hotline
  • 1959: South Vietnamese opposition figure Phan Quang Dan is elected to the National Assembly despite soldiers being bussed in to vote for President Ngo Dinh Diem's candidate. Phan Quang Đán
  • 1945: The Japanese occupation of Hong Kong comes to an end. Japanese occupation of Hong Kong
  • 1945: The Allied Control Council, governing Germany after World War II, comes into being. Allied Control Council
  • 1941: The Tighina Agreement, a treaty regarding administration issues of the Transnistria Governorate, is signed between Germany and Romania. Tighina Agreement
  • 1936: The RMS Queen Mary wins the Blue Riband by setting the fastest transatlantic crossing. RMS Queen Mary
  • 1918: Fanni Kaplan shoots and seriously injures Bolshevik leader Vladimir Lenin, which, along with the assassination of Bolshevik senior official Moisei Uritsky days earlier, prompts the decree for Red Terror. Fanny Kaplan
  • 1916: Ernest Shackleton completes the rescue of all of his men stranded on Elephant Island in Antarctica. Ernest Shackleton
  • 1909: Burgess Shale fossils are discovered by Charles Doolittle Walcott. Burgess Shale
  • 1873: Austrian explorers Julius von Payer and Karl Weyprecht discover the archipelago of Franz Josef Land in the Arctic Sea. Julius von Payer
  • 1836: The city of Houston is founded by Augustus Chapman Allen and John Kirby Allen. Houston
  • 1813: First Battle of Kulm: French forces are defeated by an Austrian-Prussian-Russian alliance. Battle of Kulm
  • 1813: Creek War: Fort Mims massacre: Creek "Red Sticks" kill over 500 settlers (including over 250 armed militia) in Fort Mims, north of Mobile, Alabama. Creek War
  • 1800: Gabriel Prosser postpones a planned slave rebellion in Richmond, Virginia, but is arrested before he can make it happen. Gabriel's Rebellion
  • 1791: HMS Pandora sinks after having run aground on the outer Great Barrier Reef the previous day. HMS Pandora (1779)
  • 1727: Anne, eldest daughter of King George II of Great Britain, is given the title Princess Royal. Anne, Princess Royal and Princess of Orange
  • 1594: King James VI of Scotland holds a masque at the baptism of Prince Henry at Stirling Castle. James VI and I
  • 1574: Guru Ram Das becomes the Fourth Sikh Guru/Master. Guru Ram Das
  • 1464: Paul II succeeds Pius II as pope of the Catholic Church. Pope Paul II
  • 1282: Peter III of Aragon lands at Trapani to intervene in the War of the Sicilian Vespers. Peter III of Aragon
  • 1057: Elderly Byzantine Emperor Michael VI Bringas abdicates after just one year on the throne. List of Byzantine emperors

About the date itself

August 30 is the 242nd day of an ordinary year, with 123 days left after it (124 in a leap year, where it is the 243rd day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Monday, 2028: Wednesday, 2029: Thursday, 2030: Friday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 30 August 2027 in 2027 is 28 Rabi' al-awwal 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 35.
