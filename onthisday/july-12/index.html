July 12: what happened on this day

What happened on 12 July: 42 events, 24 births and 20 deaths from history and 14 observances, with the weekday and day of the year.

Selected events

  • 2008: An edit on the page of the coati nicknamed it the Brazilian aardvark, in which multiple news channels followed in pursuit. Coati
  • 2007: Two U.S. Army AH-64 Apache helicopters (pictured) conducted a series of air-to-ground attacks in Baghdad. Boeing AH-64 Apache
  • 1998: France defeated the defending champions Brazil 3–0 to win their first FIFA World Cup title. France national football team
  • 1997: Hayao Miyazaki's animated film Princess Mononoke is released, becoming the highest-grossing film in Japan for a few months. Hayao Miyazaki
  • 1963: Sixteen-year-old Pauline Reade, the first victim of serial killers Ian Brady and Myra Hindley, disappeared in Gorton, England. Moors murders
  • 1962: The English rock band the Rolling Stones (pictured) played their first concert at the Marquee Club in London. The Rolling Stones
  • 1943: World War II: German and Soviet forces engaged each other at the Battle of Prokhorovka (tanks pictured), one of the largest tank battles in military history. World War II
  • 1918: An explosion in the ammunition magazine of the Japanese battleship Kawachi resulted in the deaths of more than 600 officers and crewmen. Magazine (artillery)
  • 1916: First World War: Second Lieutenant Kléber Dupuy defeated a German attack on Fort Souville at a key moment of the Battle of Verdun. World War I
  • 1847: Up to ten people died in a riot between Catholics and members of the Orange Order in Woodstock, New Brunswick. 1847 Woodstock riot
  • 1801: French Revolutionary Wars: A squadron of British ships of the line defeated a larger squadron of Spanish and French vessels in the Strait of Gibraltar. French Revolutionary Wars
  • 1488: Choe Bu, an official of the Joseon dynasty, returned to Korea after months of shipwrecked travel in China. Choe Bu

  • 1801: British ships inflict heavy damage on Spanish and French ships in the Second Battle of Algeciras. Second Battle of Algeciras
  • 1799: Ranjit Singh conquers Lahore and becomes Maharaja of the Punjab (Sikh Empire). Ranjit Singh
  • 1789: In response to the dismissal of the French finance minister Jacques Necker, the radical journalist Camille Desmoulins gives a speech which results in the storming of the Bastille two days later. Jacques Necker
  • 1691: Battle of Aughrim (Julian calendar): The decisive victory of William III of England's forces in Ireland. Battle of Aughrim
  • 1576: Mughal Empire annexes Bengal after defeating the Bengal Sultanate at the Battle of Rajmahal. Mughal Empire

  • 1488: Joseon Dynasty official Choe Bu returns to Korea after months of shipwrecked travel in China. Joseon

  • 1335: Pope Benedict XII issues the papal bull Fulgens sicut stella matutina to reform the Cistercian Order. Pope Benedict XII
  • 927: King Constantine II of Scotland, King Hywel Dda of Deheubarth, Ealdred of Bamburgh and King Owain of the Cumbrians accept the overlordship of King Æthelstan of England, leading to seven years of peace in the north. Constantine II of Scotland
  • 526: Election of Pope Felix IV following the death of Pope John I earlier that year. Pope Felix IV

About the date itself

July 12 is the 193rd day of an ordinary year, with 172 days left after it (173 in a leap year, where it is the 194th day). It falls on these weekdays: 2027: Monday, 2028: Wednesday, 2029: Thursday, 2030: Friday. In the Hijri (Islamic) calendar, 12 July 2027 in 2027 is 8 Safar 1449 AH, by the Umm al-Qura table. ISO week number in 2027: 28.
