Thanksgiving in the US and Canada

American Thanksgiving falls on the fourth Thursday of November and Canadian Thanksgiving on the second Monday of October. Type a year to see both, with Black Friday, Cyber Monday and the number of days to Christmas. Each year page from 2020 to 2060 has the dates and some planning facts, such as the number of days left until Christmas.

Thanksgiving 2027

Thanksgiving in the United States in 2027 is on Thursday, 25 November 2027, and in Canada on Monday, 11 October 2027.
