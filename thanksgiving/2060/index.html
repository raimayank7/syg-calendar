Thanksgiving 2060 in the United States

American Thanksgiving in 2060 is on Thursday, 25 November 2060, the fourth Thursday of November. November 2060 has four Thursdays, the first on 4 November, so the fourth is the 25th. From Thanksgiving to Christmas Day there are 30 days, which is 4 full weeks and 2 days, and 29 days from Black Friday to Christmas Eve, counting both.

Thanksgiving 2060 in Canada

Canadian Thanksgiving in 2060 is on Monday, 11 October 2060, the second Monday of October, which is 45 days before American Thanksgiving. The holiday weekend runs from Saturday 9 October to Monday.

The shopping days

Black Friday, the day after American Thanksgiving, is Friday, 26 November 2060. Cyber Monday is the Monday after, Monday, 29 November 2060, and Giving Tuesday follows on Tuesday, 30 November 2060. These names are retail and charity conventions, not public holidays.
