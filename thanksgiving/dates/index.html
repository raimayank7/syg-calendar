How the dates move

American Thanksgiving is always between 22 and 28 November, and in 2020 to 2060 it falls on the 22nd 5 times, on the 28th 6 times. Canadian Thanksgiving is always between 8 and 14 October. Because both depend on the weekday, the date shifts forward by one day every year, or two in the year after a leap day, until it wraps back.

Early and late Thanksgiving

The earliest American Thanksgiving in this range is Thursday, 22 November 2029 and the latest is Thursday, 28 November 2024; the gap between Thanksgiving and Christmas varies by up to a week as a result, which changes the number of shopping days.
