Long weekends in 2027

Every stretch of three or more days off in 2027 for a five-day week, worked out from the official Central Government holiday list.

How this page is made. It is worked out by program from the official Central Government holiday list for 2027 (DoPT order dated 16 July 2026), for an office that is closed on Saturdays and Sundays. It is a planning aid, not an official list. Check your own office's holidays before you book anything.

What counts as a long weekend

A long weekend here is any unbroken run of three or more days off: a Saturday and Sunday joined to a gazetted holiday on the Friday or the Monday. The first table lists every such run in 2027 that needs no leave at all: there are 4.

The second table lists runs of four days or more that open up with one day of leave. The usual case is a holiday on a Tuesday or a Thursday: take the Monday or the Friday in between as leave, and the holiday joins the weekend. There are 11 such chances in 2027. Some overlap with each other or build on a long weekend in the first table; where a week has two holidays, more than one single day of leave can do the job, and each choice gets its own row.

Reading the tables

  • Days off gives the first and last day of the break, both included.
  • Days is the length of the break.
  • Take leave on is the one working day you would apply for.
  • Holidays that fall on a Saturday or Sunday add nothing for a five-day week and so do not appear, except where they sit inside a longer run.

If your office works six days

With only Sunday off, a long weekend needs a holiday on a Saturday or a Monday. Holidays on a Saturday, which a five-day office loses, become useful: in 2027 the gazetted list has Mahatma Gandhi's Birthday, Dussehra and Christmas Day on Saturdays, each making a two-day break with the Sunday, and holidays on a Monday do the same. Look at the 2027 year calendar, where every gazetted holiday is marked.

If you are not a Central Government employee

The list behind this page is for Central Government offices in Delhi and New Delhi. State governments, banks, schools and private companies keep different lists: a state may add its own festival days and drop some of these. The method is the same, though. Mark your own holidays on a printed year calendar, circle every one that falls on a Monday, Tuesday, Thursday or Friday, and the long weekends show themselves.

Planning tips

  • Apply early for the bridging day. Everyone in the office sees the same gap on the calendar.
  • Book travel early for the no-leave long weekends. Trains, flights and hotels fill first on the breaks that need no leave, and fares rise.
  • Watch the dates that can move. Id-ul-Fitr, Id-ul-Zuha, Muharram and Id-e-Milad depend on the sighting of the Moon and can shift by a day. Keep bookings around them flexible.
  • Use restricted holidays as bridges. A Central Government employee's two restricted holidays can be taken on a listed occasion that falls beside a weekend or a gazetted holiday. The full list is on the holiday page.
  • Count the working days you will miss with the working days calculator.
How many long weekends are there in 2027?

For a five-day week on the Central Government's Delhi list, 2027 has the long weekends shown in the first table without any leave, and more with one day of leave as shown in the second.

Which holidays in 2027 fall on a weekend?

Independence Day and Id-e-Milad (Sunday 15 August), Guru Nanak's Birthday (Sunday 14 November), and Mahatma Gandhi's Birthday, Dussehra and Christmas Day, which fall on Saturdays.

Is a "bridge" day a holiday?

No. It is an ordinary working day for which you apply for leave so that a holiday joins the weekend.
