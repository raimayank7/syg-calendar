Printable year calendar on one page

Type a year and print all twelve months on one sheet.

How to print a year

  1. Type the year. From October onward the box opens on next year, since that is the calendar most people want then.
  2. Choose whether weeks start on Sunday or Monday and whether holidays are marked.
  3. Tap Print or save as PDF. Only the twelve months are printed.

On A4 paper the months print three or four across. Portrait gives three columns and four rows; landscape gives four columns and three rows. If one month spills to a second sheet, choose "Fit to page" or lower the scale a little in the print window.

What a year-at-a-glance sheet is good for

A whole year on one page shows things a monthly page hides.

  • Leave planning: see at once where a holiday touches a weekend. Our list of long weekends in 2027 does this for the Central Government holidays.
  • School and college terms: mark term dates, exams and vacations with a highlighter.
  • Project and delivery dates: count weeks by eye, mark milestones.
  • Fees, premiums and renewals that come once or twice a year and are easy to forget: insurance, vehicle fitness and pollution certificates, domain names, subscriptions, annual maintenance contracts.
  • Birthdays and anniversaries: circle them once in January and the whole family can see what is coming.
  • Crop and garden timing: sowing and harvest windows across the seasons.
  • Counting days: for attendance, interest or rent, a year sheet lets you count days and weeks with a pencil.

Reading the sheet

Sundays are shaded. With the holiday box ticked, the gazetted holidays of 2027 are highlighted; hover over one, or see the holiday list, for its name. Today's date is outlined on screen when the current year is shown.

A quick way to check a year calendar is right: 4 April, 6 June, 8 August, 10 October and 12 December always share one weekday within a year. In 2027 they are all Sundays. The reason is explained on the day of the week page.

The same calendar in other years

Because a calendar depends only on the weekday of 1 January and on whether the year is a leap year, there are just fourteen different year calendars. The sheet you print for 2027 is also right, date for date, for 2038 and 2049. The same calendar years page lists the matches for any year. Holidays do not carry over, so untick the holiday box before printing a calendar to reuse.

Financial year and academic year

The sheet runs from January to December. For an April to March financial year or a school year that starts in the middle of the calendar year, print two sheets, or print the months you need one by one from the printable month calendar and pin them in a row.

Tips for a neat print

  • Untick "Headers and footers" in the print window.
  • Tick "Background graphics" to print the shading of Sundays and holidays, or leave it unticked to save ink.
  • Thicker paper, such as 100 gsm or more, hangs flat on a wall and takes ink from a highlighter without showing through.
  • For a desk mat, print in landscape and slip the sheet under a glass or clear cover.
Can I print a calendar for 2028 or 2030?

Yes. Type the year. All years from 1583 onward are available.

Does the calendar show Indian holidays?

For 2027, the 17 gazetted holidays of the Central Government are marked. Other years print without holidays until their official lists are published.

How do I save the calendar as a PDF?

Tap the print button and choose "Save as PDF" as the printer.
