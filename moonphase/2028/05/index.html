May 2028: what to expect

The full moon is on Monday, 8 May 2028, the traditional Flower Moon. The moon is darkest on Wednesday, 24 May 2028 (about 0% lit) and brightest on Monday, 8 May 2028 (about 100% lit), which is when the night sky is best for faint stars and when moonlight is strongest. The new moon, the darkest night, is on Wednesday, 24 May 2028.

The moon moves about 12 degrees against the stars each day and rises roughly 50 minutes later each day, so the moon you see at sunset moves east night by night. The percentage on this page is the lit part of the disc at midday UTC; at other hours it differs by about one to two points. Other months: April 2028 June 2028. The year 2028.
