How to read the result

The tool names the phase (new moon, waxing crescent, first quarter, waxing gibbous, full moon, waning gibbous, last quarter or waning crescent), the share of the disc that is lit, and the age of the moon in days since the last new moon. For a date you choose, the moon is evaluated at 12:00 UTC. The answer is the same all over the world apart from the hour of the day, and apart from how the crescent is tilted in the northern and southern hemispheres.

Your birthday moon

Type your date of birth to see which phase the moon was in. Many people like to know whether they were born under a full or a new moon. The table behind the tool covers 1900 to 2060 with the new, quarter and full moons from the same calculation as the year tables; against the library, the lit percentage is within about two points, and the phase name can differ near the boundary between two names when the moon is between phases.

For the days of the year's full moons use the year tables; for each month there is a page with the moon on every day. The numbers are astronomy, not astrology, and make no claims about luck or character.
