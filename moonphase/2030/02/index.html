February 2030: what to expect

The full moon is on Monday, 18 February 2030, the traditional Snow Moon. The moon is darkest on Saturday, 2 February 2030 (about 0% lit) and brightest on Monday, 18 February 2030 (about 100% lit), which is when the night sky is best for faint stars and when moonlight is strongest. The new moon, the darkest night, is on Saturday, 2 February 2030.

The moon moves about 12 degrees against the stars each day and rises roughly 50 minutes later each day, so the moon you see at sunset moves east night by night. The percentage on this page is the lit part of the disc at midday UTC; at other hours it differs by about one to two points. Other months: January 2030 March 2030. The year 2030.
