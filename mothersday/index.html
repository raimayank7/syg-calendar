About the dates

Mother's Day and Father's Day are not on the same date everywhere. Most countries keep Mother's Day on the second Sunday of May and Father's Day on the third Sunday of June; the United Kingdom keeps Mothering Sunday three weeks before Easter, Australia and New Zealand keep Father's Day in September, and some countries use a fixed date. Choose a year to see the main dates, or open the year page for the full list by country.

Mother's Day 2027 and Father's Day 2027

In 2027 Mother's Day is on Sunday, 9 May 2027 in the United States and India and Father's Day is on Sunday, 20 June 2027.
