Mother's Day 2029

In the United States, India, Canada and Australia, Mother's Day in 2029 is on Sunday, 13 May 2029, the second Sunday of May. In the United Kingdom and Ireland Mothering Sunday is on Sunday, 11 March 2029, because it depends on Easter (1 April) and falls three weeks before it. The two dates are 63 days apart this year.

Father's Day 2029

Father's Day in the United States, India, the United Kingdom and Canada is on Sunday, 17 June 2029, the third Sunday of June. Australia and New Zealand keep it on Sunday, 2 September 2029, and Brazil on Sunday, 12 August 2029. In Spain, Portugal and Italy it is on 19 March, and in Germany it is Ascension Day, Thursday, 10 May 2029.

Planning

Order gifts and cards at least a week before the date shown for your country: the earliest American Mother's Day in the 41 years from 2020 is 8 May and the latest is 14 May, so the days to wait vary from year to year. Mother's Day and Father's Day are 35 days apart in 2029.
