The rules

  • United States, India, United Kingdom, Canada, Pakistan, Japan, China: third Sunday of June.
  • Australia and New Zealand: first Sunday of September.
  • Brazil: second Sunday of August.
  • Spain, Portugal and Italy (Saint Joseph's Day): 19 March every year.
  • Germany (Vatertag, Ascension Day): Ascension Day, 39 days after Easter.
