The rules

  • United States, India, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, Brazil, Germany, Italy, South Africa, Pakistan: second Sunday of May.
  • United Kingdom and Ireland (Mothering Sunday): fourth Sunday of Lent, three weeks before Easter.
  • Spain and Portugal: first Sunday of May.
  • Mexico: 10 May every year.
