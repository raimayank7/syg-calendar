2029 at a glance

2029 begins on a Monday and ends on a Monday. It is not a leap year, so it has 365 days: 261 weekdays from Monday to Friday and 104 Saturdays and Sundays. By the international week count it has 52 numbered weeks, and week 1 starts on Monday, 1 January 2029.

The 13th falls on a Friday twice: in April and July. No month has five Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays together. Every date in 2029 shares its weekday with the same date in 2007, 2018, 2035 and 2046, so a calendar from one of those years can be reused; see years with the same calendar.

A quick way to place any date: in 2029 the dates 4 April, 6 June, 8 August, 10 October and 12 December, and the last day of February, all fall on a Wednesday. Count from the nearest of them to find the weekday of any other date. The method is explained on the day of the week page.

In the Indian national calendar the Saka year 1951 begins on Thursday, 22 March 2029. In the financial calendar, 1 April 2029 opens FY 2029-30.

Questions about 2029

Is 2029 a leap year?

No. 2029 has 365 days. The next leap year is 2032.

What day does 2029 start on?

1 January 2029 is a Monday, and 31 December 2029 is a Monday.

How many weeks are in 2029?

2029 has 365 days, which is 52 weeks and 1 day. In the international (ISO) week count it has 52 numbered weeks.
