October 2027 at a glance

October 2027 has 31 days. It begins on a Friday and ends on a Sunday. There are 21 weekdays (Monday to Friday) and 10 Saturdays and Sundays. After the 1 gazetted holiday that falls on a weekday, a five-day office works 20 days. Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays occur five times; the other weekdays occur four times.

The month covers ISO week 39 to week 43. Its first day is day 274 of the year and its last is day 304, leaving 61 days of 2027 after it. January 2027 starts on the same weekday, so the two months line up on the page.

In the Indian national calendar, 1 October is 9 Asvina 1949 and 31 October is 9 Kartika 1949; the month of Kartika begins on 23 October.

About October

October means "eighth month" in Latin. It has 31 days.

The rains end over most of India, though the north-east monsoon brings rain to Tamil Nadu and the south-east coast. The festive season is at its height in most years, with Navratri, Durga Puja, Dussehra and often Diwali. The third quarter of the financial year begins on the 1st. Fixed days: Gandhi Jayanti, also the birthday of Lal Bahadur Shastri, on the 2nd; Air Force Day on the 8th; World Post Day on the 9th; United Nations Day on the 24th; and National Unity Day on the 31st, the birthday of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel.

Festivals that follow the Moon change their date every year. For 2027 this page lists only the dates in the official lists above.

Questions

How many days are in October 2027?

October 2027 has 31 days: 21 weekdays and 10 Saturdays and Sundays.

What day does October 2027 start on?

1 October 2027 is a Friday. The month ends on Sunday, 31 October 2027.

Which holidays fall in October 2027?

The Central Government gazetted holidays are: Mahatma Gandhi's Birthday on 2 October, Dussehra (Vijaya Dashmi) on 9 October and Diwali (Deepavali) on 29 October.
