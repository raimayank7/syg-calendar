September 2027 at a glance

September 2027 has 30 days. It begins on a Wednesday and ends on a Thursday. There are 22 weekdays (Monday to Friday) and 8 Saturdays and Sundays. Wednesdays and Thursdays occur five times; the other weekdays occur four times.

The month covers ISO week 35 to week 39. Its first day is day 244 of the year and its last is day 273, leaving 92 days of 2027 after it. December 2027 starts on the same weekday, so the two months line up on the page.

In the Indian national calendar, 1 September is 10 Bhadra 1949 and 30 September is 8 Asvina 1949; the month of Asvina begins on 23 September.

There is no gazetted Central Government holiday in September 2027.

About September

September means "seventh month" in Latin; it was the seventh month when the Roman year began in March. It has 30 days.

The monsoon begins to withdraw from north-west India during the month. The autumn equinox falls around the 22nd or 23rd, and the first half of the financial year closes on the 30th. Ganesh Chaturthi and Onam fall in August or September. Fixed days: Teachers' Day on the 5th, the birthday of Dr Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan; Hindi Diwas on the 14th; Engineers' Day on the 15th, the birthday of Sir M. Visvesvaraya; and World Tourism Day on the 27th.

Festivals that follow the Moon change their date every year. For 2027 this page lists only the dates in the official lists above.

Questions

September 2027 has 30 days: 22 weekdays and 8 Saturdays and Sundays.

What day does September 2027 start on?

Which holidays fall in September 2027?

There is no gazetted Central Government holiday in September 2027. Restricted holidays: Ganesh Chaturthi / Vinayak Chaturthi on 4 September and Onam or Thiru Onam Day on 12 September.
