December 2027 at a glance

December 2027 has 31 days. It begins on a Wednesday and ends on a Friday. There are 23 weekdays (Monday to Friday) and 8 Saturdays and Sundays. Wednesdays, Thursdays and Fridays occur five times; the other weekdays occur four times.

The month covers ISO week 48 to week 52. Its first day is day 335 of the year and its last is day 365, leaving 0 days of 2027 after it. September 2027 starts on the same weekday, so the two months line up on the page.

In the Indian national calendar, 1 December is 10 Agrahayana 1949 and 31 December is 10 Pausha 1949; the month of Pausha begins on 22 December.

About December

December means "tenth month" in Latin. It has 31 days and closes the calendar year.

Winter settles over north India, with the shortest day of the year around the 21st or 22nd. Schools close for winter holidays in the colder states, and it is the main season for travel and weddings. The third quarter of the financial year closes on the 31st. Fixed days: World AIDS Day on the 1st, Navy Day on the 4th, Armed Forces Flag Day on the 7th, Human Rights Day on the 10th, Vijay Diwas on the 16th, National Farmers' Day on the 23rd and Christmas Day on the 25th.

Festivals that follow the Moon change their date every year. For 2027 this page lists only the dates in the official lists above.
