June 2027 at a glance

June 2027 has 30 days. It begins on a Tuesday and ends on a Wednesday. There are 22 weekdays (Monday to Friday) and 8 Saturdays and Sundays. After the 1 gazetted holiday that falls on a weekday, a five-day office works 21 days. Tuesdays and Wednesdays occur five times; the other weekdays occur four times.

The month covers ISO week 22 to week 26. Its first day is day 152 of the year and its last is day 181, leaving 184 days of 2027 after it. No other month of 2027 starts on the same weekday.

In the Indian national calendar, 1 June is 11 Jyaishtha 1949 and 30 June is 9 Ashadha 1949; the month of Ashadha begins on 22 June.

About June

June is probably named after the Roman goddess Juno. It has 30 days and holds the longest day of the year in the northern hemisphere, around the 21st.

The south-west monsoon normally reaches Kerala around the start of June and moves northward through the month, ending the heat. Schools reopen after the summer break in much of the country, and the first quarter of the financial year closes on the 30th. Fixed days: World Environment Day on the 5th and the International Day of Yoga on the 21st. Father's Day is kept on the third Sunday of June.

Festivals that follow the Moon change their date every year. For 2027 this page lists only the dates in the official lists above.
