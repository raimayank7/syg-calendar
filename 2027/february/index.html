February 2027 at a glance

February 2027 has 28 days. It begins on a Monday and ends on a Sunday. There are 20 weekdays (Monday to Friday) and 8 Saturdays and Sundays. Every weekday occurs exactly four times, because the month is exactly four weeks long.

The month covers ISO week 5 to week 8. Its first day is day 32 of the year and its last is day 59, leaving 306 days of 2027 after it. March and November 2027 starts on the same weekday, so the two months line up on the page.

In the Indian national calendar, 1 February is 12 Magha 1948 and 28 February is 9 Phalguna 1948; the month of Phalguna begins on 20 February.

Holidays in February 2027

There is no gazetted Central Government holiday in February 2027.

About February

February takes its name from Februa, a Roman festival of purification. It is the shortest month: 28 days, or 29 in a leap year.

Winter loosens its hold on the plains, mustard fields turn yellow and Basant Panchami welcomes spring. School board examinations begin for many students this month or the next. Fixed days: World Cancer Day on the 4th, International Mother Language Day on the 21st and National Science Day on the 28th, which marks the discovery of the Raman effect. The Union Budget is by recent practice presented on 1 February. Because the month is exactly four weeks long in ordinary years, every weekday occurs four times.

Festivals that follow the Moon change their date every year. For 2027 this page lists only the dates in the official lists above.

Questions

How many days are in February 2027?

February 2027 has 28 days: 20 weekdays and 8 Saturdays and Sundays.

What day does February 2027 start on?

1 February 2027 is a Monday. The month ends on Sunday, 28 February 2027.

Which holidays fall in February 2027?

There is no gazetted Central Government holiday in February 2027. Restricted holidays: Sri Panchami, Basant Panchami on 11 February, Shivaji Jayanti on 19 February and Guru Ravi Das's Birthday on 20 February.
