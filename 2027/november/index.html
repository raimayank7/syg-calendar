November 2027 at a glance

November 2027 has 30 days. It begins on a Monday and ends on a Tuesday. There are 22 weekdays (Monday to Friday) and 8 Saturdays and Sundays. Mondays and Tuesdays occur five times; the other weekdays occur four times.

The month covers ISO week 44 to week 48. Its first day is day 305 of the year and its last is day 334, leaving 31 days of 2027 after it. February and March 2027 starts on the same weekday, so the two months line up on the page.

In the Indian national calendar, 1 November is 10 Kartika 1949 and 30 November is 9 Agrahayana 1949; the month of Agrahayana begins on 22 November.

About November

November means "ninth month" in Latin. It has 30 days.

Early winter arrives in the north, the air turns crisp and the wedding season begins. Diwali, Guru Nanak's Birthday and Chhath fall in October or November. The kharif harvest comes in and wheat is sown. Fixed days: National Education Day on the 11th, the birthday of Maulana Abul Kalam Azad; Children's Day on the 14th, the birthday of Jawaharlal Nehru; and Constitution Day on the 26th, the day the Constitution was adopted in 1949.

Festivals that follow the Moon change their date every year. For 2027 this page lists only the dates in the official lists above.
