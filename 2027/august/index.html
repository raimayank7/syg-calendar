August 2027 at a glance

August 2027 has 31 days. It begins on a Sunday and ends on a Tuesday. There are 22 weekdays (Monday to Friday) and 9 Saturdays and Sundays. After the 1 gazetted holiday that falls on a weekday, a five-day office works 21 days. Sundays, Mondays and Tuesdays occur five times; the other weekdays occur four times.

The month covers ISO week 30 to week 35. Its first day is day 213 of the year and its last is day 243, leaving 122 days of 2027 after it. No other month of 2027 starts on the same weekday.

In the Indian national calendar, 1 August is 10 Sravana 1949 and 31 August is 9 Bhadra 1949; the month of Bhadra begins on 23 August.

About August

August is named after the Roman emperor Augustus. It has 31 days, the only month besides December that follows another 31-day month.

Rains continue across most of India. It is a month of festivals in most years, among them Raksha Bandhan and Janmashtami, whose dates follow the Moon. Fixed days: Quit India Day on the 9th, Independence Day on the 15th, and National Sports Day on the 29th, the birthday of the hockey player Major Dhyan Chand. Partition Horrors Remembrance Day is observed on the 14th.

Festivals that follow the Moon change their date every year. For 2027 this page lists only the dates in the official lists above.

Questions

How many days are in August 2027?

August 2027 has 31 days: 22 weekdays and 9 Saturdays and Sundays.

What day does August 2027 start on?

1 August 2027 is a Sunday. The month ends on Tuesday, 31 August 2027.

Which holidays fall in August 2027?

The Central Government gazetted holidays are: Independence Day on 15 August, Milad-un-Nabi or Id-e-Milad (Prophet Mohammad's Birthday) on 15 August and Janmashtami (Vaishnav) on 25 August.
