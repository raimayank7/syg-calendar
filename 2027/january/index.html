January 2027 at a glance

January 2027 has 31 days. It begins on a Friday and ends on a Sunday. There are 21 weekdays (Monday to Friday) and 10 Saturdays and Sundays. After the 1 gazetted holiday that falls on a weekday, a five-day office works 20 days. Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays occur five times; the other weekdays occur four times.

The month covers ISO week 53 of 2026 to week 4. Its first day is day 1 of the year and its last is day 31, leaving 334 days of 2027 after it. October 2027 starts on the same weekday, so the two months line up on the page.

In the Indian national calendar, 1 January is 11 Pausha 1948 and 31 January is 11 Magha 1948; the month of Magha begins on 21 January.

About January

January is named after Janus, the Roman god of doorways, who looks both back and forward. It has 31 days and opens the calendar year.

Across north India it is the coldest month, with fog on the plains and snow in the hills, while the south has its mildest weather of the year. The harvest festivals of mid-January, Lohri, Makar Sankranti, Pongal and Magh Bihu, mark the Sun's turn northward. Fixed days to remember: New Year's Day on the 1st, National Youth Day on the 12th, Army Day on the 15th, National Voters' Day on the 25th, Republic Day on the 26th and Martyrs' Day on the 30th. January is the tenth month of the financial year and the last quarter begins on the 1st.

Festivals that follow the Moon change their date every year. For 2027 this page lists only the dates in the official lists above.
