May 2027 at a glance

May 2027 has 31 days. It begins on a Saturday and ends on a Monday. There are 21 weekdays (Monday to Friday) and 10 Saturdays and Sundays. After the 2 gazetted holidays that fall on a weekday, a five-day office works 19 days. Saturdays, Sundays and Mondays occur five times; the other weekdays occur four times.

The month covers ISO week 17 to week 22. Its first day is day 121 of the year and its last is day 151, leaving 214 days of 2027 after it. No other month of 2027 starts on the same weekday.

In the Indian national calendar, 1 May is 11 Vaisakha 1949 and 31 May is 10 Jyaishtha 1949; the month of Jyaishtha begins on 22 May.

About May

May is probably named after Maia, a Roman goddess of growth. It has 31 days.

It is the peak of summer across most of India, with heat waves on the northern and central plains, school summer holidays and the season of mangoes. Hill stations are at their busiest. Fixed days: International Labour Day, also Maharashtra Day and Gujarat Day, on the 1st; National Technology Day on the 11th; and the International Day for Biological Diversity on the 22nd. Mother's Day is kept on the second Sunday of May.

Festivals that follow the Moon change their date every year. For 2027 this page lists only the dates in the official lists above.

Questions

How many days are in May 2027?

May 2027 has 31 days: 21 weekdays and 10 Saturdays and Sundays.

What day does May 2027 start on?

1 May 2027 is a Saturday. The month ends on Monday, 31 May 2027.

Which holidays fall in May 2027?

The Central Government gazetted holidays are: Id-ul-Zuha (Bakrid) on 17 May and Buddha Purnima on 20 May.
