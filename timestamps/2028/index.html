The year 2028 in seconds

2028 has 366 days, which is 31,622,400 seconds. It begins at Unix time 1,830,297,600, on Saturday 1 January 2028 00:00:00 UTC, and the next year begins at 1,861,920,000. Divide a timestamp by 86,400 and you get the number of days since 1 January 1970: 1 January 2028 is day 21,184 of that count.

Julian date

Astronomers count days in Julian dates. The Julian date of 1 January 2028 at 00:00 UTC is 2,461,771.5, because Unix time zero, 1 January 1970, is Julian date 2,440,587.5 and a day is 86,400 seconds.

Using these numbers

Databases, log files, APIs and programming languages often store a moment as a Unix timestamp, in seconds or in milliseconds (the milliseconds column is the seconds figure times 1,000). To check a value, paste it into the converter. A timestamp with 10 digits is in seconds and one with 13 digits is in milliseconds, for dates between 2001 and 2286.
