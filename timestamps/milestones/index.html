Reading the table

Unix time counts seconds since 1 January 1970. The billionth second came on 2001-09-09 01:46:40 UTC, and the counter reaches 2 billion on 2033-05-18 03:33:20 UTC. The largest value a signed 32-bit integer can hold, 2,147,483,647, falls on 2038-01-19 03:14:07 UTC, which is the date of the year 2038 problem. An unsigned 32-bit counter lasts until 2106-02-07 06:28:15 UTC.

Negative timestamps

Dates before 1970 have negative Unix times. The lowest value of a signed 32-bit integer, -2,147,483,648, is 1901-12-13 20:45:52 UTC.
