Financial year, quarters and half-years in India

In India the financial year, also called the fiscal year, runs from 1 April to 31 March of the next year.

The basics

A financial year (FY) is the twelve-month period for which governments and businesses draw up budgets and accounts. In India it runs from 1 April to 31 March. The Union Budget, state budgets, company accounts and most tax matters follow it.

Because it straddles two calendar years, it is written with both: the year from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027 is FY 2026-27, and the next is FY 2027-28. The home page of this site shows which financial year today falls in.

The four quarters

Results, advance payments and many returns are organised by quarter.

Q1April, May, June1 April30 June91
Q2July, August, September1 July30 September92
Q3October, November, December1 October31 December92
Q4January, February, March1 January31 March90 (91 in a leap year)

The two half-years are April to September (H1, 183 days) and October to March (H2, 182 days, or 183 when it includes a 29 February).

Take care with the word "quarter". Q1 of the Indian financial year is April to June, but Q1 of the calendar year is January to March. A foreign parent company, a software package or a spreadsheet function may mean the calendar quarter. When a date matters, write the months out.

Which financial year is a date in?

  • A date from April to December belongs to the financial year that starts in that calendar year: 10 October 2027 is in FY 2027-28.
  • A date from January to March belongs to the financial year that started the year before: 10 February 2027 is in FY 2026-27.

Year-end: why March is busy

Since books close on 31 March, the weeks before it are crowded with year-end work: stock-taking, closing of accounts, targets, renewals and last-date investments and payments. Many deadlines set by law or by departments are tied to the financial year and its quarters. Those dates are fixed and sometimes extended by official notification, so this page does not list them: check the website of the department concerned for the current year's dates.

Why April to March?

The April to March year was adopted in British India in 1867, bringing Indian accounts into step with the British government's own financial year, and it was kept after Independence. It also suits a farming economy: the main harvests are in by spring, and the monsoon, which decides so much of the year's fortunes, falls early in the financial year.

Changing to a January to December year has been studied more than once, but no change has been made.

Counting within a financial year

  • Days between two dates in the year: days between dates.
  • Working days in a quarter or a month: working days.
  • A sheet to pin up: print April to December of one year and January to March of the next from the printable month calendar.

A financial year has 365 days, or 366 when it includes a 29 February. FY 2027-28 contains 29 February 2028 and so has 366 days.

When does the financial year start in India?

On 1 April. It ends on 31 March of the following calendar year.

What is FY 2026-27?

The financial year from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027.

Is the financial year the same as the academic year?

No. Schools and colleges set their own sessions, commonly starting between April and July.
