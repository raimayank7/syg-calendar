Calendar

About Calendar

Calendar is a free site for the everyday questions people ask of a calendar: what is the date and week today, which day does a date fall on, how many days are there between two dates, when is the next holiday. It has calendars to print for any month and year, a set of date calculators, plain guides, and holiday lists.

Holidays: only from official lists

We print a holiday date only when we have read it in an official government list, and we name the list and the date we read it. Where no official list exists yet, the page says so and shows no date.

  • India, Central Government holidays 2027: Department of Personnel and Training, Office Memorandum F. No. 12/2/2023-JCA dated 16 July 2026, "Holidays to be observed in Central Government Offices during the year 2027", read on 12 November 2026 from the copy circulated by the Department of Posts on a government website. The list was typed by hand from the scanned order, and every row is checked by our build against the weekday and the Saka date printed in the order itself.
  • United States federal holidays: the Federal Holidays schedule of the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (opm.gov), fetched on 12 November 2026. Works of the US government are in the public domain.
  • United Kingdom bank holidays: the bank holidays list of GOV.UK (gov.uk/bank-holidays), fetched on 12 November 2026. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

We do not list state government, bank, stock exchange, court or school holidays, and we do not compute festival dates. Dates of festivals that follow the Moon can be changed by the government at short notice; the official announcement always prevails.

Calendars and calculators

The calendars and date tools use the Gregorian calendar rules and the published rules of the Indian national calendar (Saka era) and of ISO 8601 week numbers. They run in your own browser; nothing you enter is sent to us or stored. The arithmetic is tested against several thousand known dates, and the Saka converter against all 49 Saka dates printed in the 2027 holiday order.

Writing

All guides and explanations are our own writing. Historical facts about the calendar are long-settled and are stated from standard reference knowledge. The Hindi and Punjabi day and month names are written in their own scripts; they have not been reviewed by a language teacher, so please tell us if a spelling should be improved.

What this site is not

It is not a government website and is not connected with any department or organisation named on it. It is not a panchang: it gives no tithi, muhurat or astrological information.
