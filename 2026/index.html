2026 at a glance

2026 begins on a Thursday and ends on a Thursday. It is not a leap year, so it has 365 days: 261 weekdays from Monday to Friday and 104 Saturdays and Sundays. By the international week count it has 53 numbered weeks, and week 1 starts on Monday, 29 December 2025, which is in December 2025.

The 13th falls on a Friday three times: in February, March and November. May has five Fridays, five Saturdays and five Sundays. Every date in 2026 shares its weekday with the same date in 2009, 2015, 2037 and 2043, so a calendar from one of those years can be reused; see years with the same calendar.

A quick way to place any date: in 2026 the dates 4 April, 6 June, 8 August, 10 October and 12 December, and the last day of February, all fall on a Saturday. Count from the nearest of them to find the weekday of any other date. The method is explained on the day of the week page.

In the Indian national calendar the Saka year 1948 begins on Sunday, 22 March 2026. In the financial calendar, 1 April 2026 opens FY 2026-27.

Holidays in 2026

The Government of India publishes its holiday list for each year in the middle of the year before. The list for 2026 covered a year that is nearly over when this site was made, so no Indian holidays are marked on this calendar. We do not print dates from unofficial sources. Questions about 2026

Is 2026 a leap year?

No. 2026 has 365 days. The next leap year is 2028.

What day does 2026 start on?

1 January 2026 is a Thursday, and 31 December 2026 is a Thursday.

How many weeks are in 2026?

2026 has 365 days, which is 52 weeks and 1 day. In the international (ISO) week count it has 53 numbered weeks.
