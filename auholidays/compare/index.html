Reading the table

Each row is one date on which at least one state or territory has a public holiday. The last column shows how many of the eight have a holiday that day. Only 7 dates are public holidays in all eight states and territories: 1 Jan, 26 Jan, 26 Mar, 29 Mar, 25 Dec, 27 Dec, 28 Dec. The rest differ: for example the Monday 14 June King's Birthday is observed in all states except Queensland and Western Australia, which hold it on other dates (4 October and 27 September).

Easter

Good Friday (26 March) and Easter Monday (29 March) are holidays in every state and territory, and most also give the Saturday and Sunday of Easter: Easter Saturday is on the list in ACT, NSW, NT, QLD, SA and VIC, and Easter Sunday in all but Tasmania. Tasmania has Easter Tuesday for the public service.
