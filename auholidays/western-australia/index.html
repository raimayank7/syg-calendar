Western Australia in 2027

Western Australia lists 14 dated public holidays for 2027 on the Fair Work Ombudsman's page, of which 4 fall on a Saturday or Sunday: Easter Sunday (Sunday, 28 March 2027), Anzac Day (Sunday, 25 April 2027), Christmas Day (Saturday, 25 December 2027), Boxing Day (Sunday, 26 December 2027). The Fair Work page lists the holidays that apply across the state and some regional holidays; for local days such as regional shows check the state's own website.

Long weekends and leave

There are 7 runs of three or more days off in a row for people with a Saturday and Sunday weekend: 1 Jan to 3 Jan (3 days); 27 Feb to 1 Mar (3 days); 26 Mar to 29 Mar (4 days); 24 Apr to 26 Apr (3 days); 5 Jun to 7 Jun (3 days); 25 Sep to 27 Sep (3 days); 25 Dec to 28 Dec (4 days). One or two days of leave next to a holiday can give these breaks: 1 Jan to 4 Jan, 4 days off for 1 day of leave; 23 Jan to 26 Jan, 4 days off for 1 day of leave; 26 Feb to 1 Mar, 4 days off for 1 day of leave; 25 Mar to 29 Mar, 5 days off for 1 day of leave; 24 Dec to 28 Dec, 5 days off for 1 day of leave.

Christmas and Anzac Day

Anzac Day: Sunday, 25 April 2027; Additional public holiday for Anzac Day: Monday, 26 April 2027; Christmas Day: Saturday, 25 December 2027; Boxing Day: Sunday, 26 December 2027; Additional public holiday for Christmas Day: Monday, 27 December 2027; Additional public holiday for Boxing Day: Tuesday, 28 December 2027. These show how the state treats holidays that fall on a weekend: see the guide.
