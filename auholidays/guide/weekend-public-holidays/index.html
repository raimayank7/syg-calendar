Public holidays on a weekend in Australia

In 2027 Christmas Day, Boxing Day and Anzac Day fall at weekends, and the states treat that differently.

Each state and territory sets its own rules. The Fair Work Ombudsman's 2027 list shows the results: in 2027 Christmas Day is a Saturday and Boxing Day a Sunday, so the states that give extra days list Monday 27 December and Tuesday 28 December as additional public holidays (ACT, NSW, NT, QLD, SA, VIC, WA and Tasmania, which lists Monday 27 December as additional for Christmas Day and gives Boxing Day on Tuesday 28 December).

Anzac Day, Sunday 25 April

Anzac Day falls on a Sunday in 2027. The page shows New South Wales and Western Australia adding a Monday holiday on 26 April for it, the ACT moving the Anzac Day holiday to Monday 26 April and adding Sunday 25 April as an extra day, and Queensland and the Northern Territory listing only Monday 26 April. South Australia, Tasmania and Victoria list Sunday 25 April only.

Check with your employer

Whether you get a day off or penalty rates depends on your award or agreement as well as the public holiday list; the Fair Work Ombudsman gives more information on its public holidays pages. This page only shows what the official 2027 list says.
