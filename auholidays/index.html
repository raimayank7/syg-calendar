About these lists

The dates come from the Fair Work Ombudsman's 2027 list, which is based on each state and territory's own announcements. The table counts the dated holidays on that list, including the extra days many states give when a holiday falls on a weekend and partial-day holidays such as Christmas Eve in some states. The longest breaks usually come at Easter and Christmas. Dates of regional holidays and show days can differ in your area, so check your state website too.
