Hebrew year 5791

The Jewish year 5791 begins on Rosh Hashanah, 28 September 2030, and has 355 days. It is an ordinary year of twelve months: The year is "complete" (355 days). Rosh Hashanah is on a Saturday. The previous year 5790 began on 10 September 2029.

Ethiopian and Coptic new years

The Coptic year 1747 and the Ethiopian year 2023 both begin on 11 September 2030. The Ethiopian calendar has twelve months of 30 days and a short thirteenth month, Pagume, of 5 or 6 days, so its new year falls on 11 September, or on 12 September in the Gregorian year before a Gregorian leap year. The Ethiopian year number is 276 higher than the Coptic one, because the Ethiopian church counts the incarnation of Christ from a different year.

Indian national calendar and the Julian calendar

The Indian national calendar, adopted in 1957, begins its year, Saka 1952, on 22 March 2030, which is Chaitra 1. The Julian calendar, still used for the church year by several Orthodox churches, is 13 days behind the Gregorian calendar between 1900 and 2099, so its 1 January falls on 14 January 2030.
