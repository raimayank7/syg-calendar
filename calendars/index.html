What this converter does

Many countries and communities still use a calendar of their own next to the Gregorian one: Jewish festivals follow the Hebrew calendar, Ethiopia has thirteen months, India has a national Saka calendar, Thailand counts years from the Buddhist era and Japan names years after emperors. Choose a date and you see it in each calendar. The year pages show the new year of each calendar in a given Gregorian year, from 2020 to 2060.

Not included

The Islamic (Hijri) and Chinese calendars have their own sections on this site. The traditional Hindu festival calendar, the panchang, is not computed here, because the dates of festivals depend on local sunrise and on rules that differ between traditions.
