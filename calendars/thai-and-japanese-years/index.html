Thai, Japanese and Minguo Years

Three year counts that run alongside the Gregorian calendar: the Thai Buddhist era, the Japanese imperial eras and the Republic of China (Minguo) years.

Thai Buddhist era

Thailand counts years from the Buddhist era, which is the Gregorian year plus 543. Since 1941 the Thai year starts on 1 January, so 2027 is the year 2570 BE for the whole year. Before 1941 the new year started in April, so older dates need care.

Japanese eras

Japan names years after the reign of an emperor, starting a new count with each reign. The present era, Reiwa, began on 1 May 2019, so 2027 is Reiwa 9. Heisei ran from 8 January 1989 to 30 April 2019, Showa from 25 December 1926 to 7 January 1989 and Taisho from 30 July 1912 to 24 December 1926. The first year of an era runs only until the end of the Gregorian year, so Reiwa 1 was 2019.

Republic of China

Taiwan counts years from the founding of the Republic of China in 1912, which is year 1: the Minguo year is the Gregorian year minus 1911. So 2027 is year 116.
