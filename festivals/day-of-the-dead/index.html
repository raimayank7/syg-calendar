What it is

The Day of the Dead (Spanish: Día de (los) Muertos) is a holiday traditionally celebrated on November 1 and 2, though other days, such as October 31 or November 6, may be included depending on the locality. The multi-day holiday involves family and friends gathering to pay respects and remember friends and family members who have died. The celebrations can take a humorous tone, as celebrants remember amusing events and anecdotes about the departed. The festival is widely observed in Mexico and among people of Mexican heritage in other parts of the world. It falls during the Christian period of Allhallowtide. Some argue that there are Indigenous Mexican or ancient Aztec influences that account for the custom, though others see it as a local expression of the Allhallowtide season that was brought to the region by the Spanish; the Day of the Dead has become a way to remember those forebears of Mexican culture.

Traditions connected with the holiday include honoring the deceased using calaveras and marigold flowers known as cempazúchitl, building home altars called ofrendas with the favorite foods and beverages of the departed, and visiting graves with these items as gifts for the deceased. The celebration is not solely focused on the dead, as it is also common to give gifts to friends such as candy sugar skulls, to share traditional pan de muerto with family and friends, and to write light-hearted and often irreverent verses in the form of mock epitaphs dedicated to living friends and acquaintances, a literary form known as calaveras literarias.

How the date is decided

Day of the Dead is on the same date every year, 1 November, so only the weekday changes: it moves forward by one weekday each year and by two after a leap day. In 2027 it falls on Monday; the table above gives the weekday for each year.

That makes it easy to plan far ahead. A fixed date can also fall on a weekend, and in many countries a public holiday that does so is moved to a Monday or a Friday by rule, a matter that depends on the country and is not covered here.

Questions people ask

When is Day of the Dead in 2027?

Day of the Dead falls on Monday, 1 November 2027 in 2027.

Why does the date of Day of the Dead change every year?

It does not change: it is on the same date every year. Only the weekday changes.

Is the date the same in every country?

Mostly, but not always. Lunar festivals start at sunset or at moon sighting, which differ by place and time zone, and traditions in different regions can choose different days.
