What it is

Mahavira Janma Kalyanaka is one of the most important religious festivals in Jainism. It celebrates the birth of Mahavira, the twenty-fourth and last Tīrthaṅkara (supreme preacher) of present Avasarpiṇī. On the Gregorian calendar, the festivity occurs either in March or April.

How the date is decided

Mahavir Jayanti follows the Hindu lunisolar calendar (panchang): its date depends on the phase of the moon and the position of the sun, and is decided by astronomers and calendar-makers each year. For 2027 the date shown is the one in the official list of holidays of the Government of India (Department of Personnel and Training memorandum dated 16 July 2026). That list is for Central Government offices; the date for other years is published by the government a few months before, so no other years are given here.

The same festival can be observed on a different day in different regions of India, because tithis (lunar days) begin and end at different times of the day and traditions differ on which day counts. Check with your local temple, community or panchang before you plan.

Questions people ask

When is Mahavir Jayanti in 2027?

Mahavir Jayanti falls on Monday, 19 April 2027 in 2027.

Why does the date of Mahavir Jayanti change every year?

It follows the Hindu lunisolar calendar, so the Gregorian date changes every year.

Is the date the same in every country?

Mostly, but not always. Lunar festivals start at sunset or at moon sighting, which differ by place and time zone, and traditions in different regions can choose different days.
