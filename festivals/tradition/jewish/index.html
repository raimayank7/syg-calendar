About the jewish calendar

The Jewish calendar is lunisolar, and a festival begins at sunset on the evening before its date. The dates are worked out by arithmetic.

How their dates are decided

The festivals in this group are placed by: the Hebrew calendar. In 2027 the first falls on Tuesday, 23 March 2027 (Purim) and the last on Saturday, 25 December 2027 (Hanukkah). The longest gap between two of them is 113 days and the shortest 5.
