About the indian calendar

Indian festivals follow the Hindu lunisolar calendar. Their 2027 dates are from the official list of the Government of India; other years are not given because they are published later.

How their dates are decided

The festivals in this group are placed by: the Hindu lunisolar calendar (official 2027 list). In 2027 the first falls on Thursday, 14 January 2027 (Makar Sankranti) and the last on Sunday, 14 November 2027 (Guru Nanak Jayanti). The longest gap between two of them is 46 days and the shortest 1.
