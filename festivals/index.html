What this site is

A festival guide with dates that are computed or taken from an official list, not copied from other sites. Each festival has a page with its date for five years, an introduction from Wikipedia (credited), and a section on how its date is decided. The three guides explain Easter, lunar and solar calendars and why festivals fall on different days in different countries.

Dates are for planning. Jewish and Islamic days begin at sunset the evening before. Indian festival dates are given for 2027 only, from the official list of the Government of India. Checked November 2026.
