About the 2026 list

The list has 44 dates. The Indian festivals are not in the list for 2026: the official dates have not been read for this year, because they depend on the panchang and are published later.

Use it as a planning guide. For a day off work or school, follow the announcement of your own government or employer; for a religious observance, follow your community\'s announcement, which can differ by a day.

The shape of 2026

April is the busiest month, with 7 festivals, and July the quietest with 1. Western Easter is on Sunday, 5 April 2026 and Orthodox Easter on Sunday, 12 April 2026; Chinese New Year opens the lunar year of the Horse on Tuesday, 17 February 2026. The Jewish new year, Rosh Hashanah, starts the evening before Saturday, 12 September 2026, and Ramadan begins in February.
