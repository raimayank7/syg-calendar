What week number is it?

Find the week number of any date, or the dates of any numbered week, by the international (ISO 8601) rule.

The rule in one line

Under the international standard ISO 8601, weeks start on Monday, and week 1 of a year is the week that contains the year's first Thursday. Every other fact about week numbers follows from that sentence.

What follows from the rule

  • Week 1 always contains 4 January. That is the quickest way to find it.
  • A week belongs wholly to one year: the year that holds its Thursday, which means the year that holds most of its days.
  • The first days of January can belong to the last week of the year before. 1 January 2027 is a Friday, so it falls in week 53 of 2026, and week 1 of 2027 begins on Monday 4 January.
  • The last days of December can belong to week 1 of the next year.
  • Most years have 52 weeks. A year has 53 weeks when 1 January is a Thursday, or when it is a leap year and 1 January is a Wednesday. That happens five or six times in every 28 years: 2026 has 53 weeks and so will 2032 and 2037.

When the week's year differs from the calendar year of the date, the page says so. This matters in spreadsheets: label such a day with the week's own year, or week 53 and week 1 will be attached to the wrong year.

Who uses week numbers

Factories, shipping lines, schools and project teams plan by week number because "week 34" is shorter and clearer than a pair of dates, and it means the same Monday to Sunday for everyone using the standard. Many European calendars print the number beside each row. Payroll and retail reporting are also often weekly.

Other ways of numbering weeks

Not every system follows ISO.

  • In the United States, calendars usually start the week on Sunday, and week 1 is simply the week containing 1 January. Numbers can differ from ISO by one.
  • Spreadsheets offer both: in Excel and Google Sheets, WEEKNUM counts the American way by default, while ISOWEEKNUM gives the ISO week shown on this page.
  • A financial year may number its weeks from April or another month.

If a colleague's week number does not match yours, the two of you are almost certainly using different systems. Agree on one, or quote the dates.

Finding a week's dates by hand

Find 4 January of the year. Go back to the Monday on or before it: that is the first day of week 1. Add 7 days for every further week. The second box on this page does exactly this.

Does a week start on Sunday or Monday?

Under ISO 8601, on Monday. In everyday use it varies by country: calendars in India and the United States usually put Sunday first.

How many weeks are there in a year?

52 weeks and 1 day, or 52 weeks and 2 days in a leap year. In ISO numbering a year has 52 or 53 numbered weeks.
