About this eclipse

The total solar eclipse of Saturday, 22 July 2028 reaches its greatest at 02:55 UTC (08:25 IST). In a total solar eclipse the moon covers the whole sun for a few minutes along a narrow path; outside the path people see a partial eclipse. The longest duration of the total or annular phase on the central line is 05m10s.

Its magnitude is 1.056. For a solar eclipse that is the fraction of the sun's width covered at the greatest point on Earth, and for a lunar eclipse the fraction of the moon's width inside the Earth's dark shadow. It belongs to Saros series 146.

From India

Indian cities with a partial eclipse: Tirunelveli (24%), Madurai (20%), Teni (20%), Tiruchirappalli (18%), Coimbatore (16%), Kallakurichi (16%), Chennai (13%), Bengaluru (10%), Vijayawada (2%), Visakhapatnam (1%). Local sunrise, sunset and cloud decide what you actually see; times in the table are of the local maximum.

Safety

Never look at the sun without proper eclipse glasses (solar filters made to the ISO 12312-2 standard) or a solar filter on the front of a telescope or camera, even for a partial eclipse; sunglasses are not enough. Read the safety guide.
