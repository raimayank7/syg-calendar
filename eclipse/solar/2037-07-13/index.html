About this eclipse

The total solar eclipse of Monday, 13 July 2037 reaches its greatest at 02:39 UTC (08:09 IST). In a total solar eclipse the moon covers the whole sun for a few minutes along a narrow path; outside the path people see a partial eclipse. The longest duration of the total or annular phase on the central line is 03m58s.

Its magnitude is 1.041. For a solar eclipse that is the fraction of the sun's width covered at the greatest point on Earth, and for a lunar eclipse the fraction of the moon's width inside the Earth's dark shadow. It belongs to Saros series 127.

From India

None of the Indian cities we checked (all with more than a million people) can see this eclipse. Local sunrise, sunset and cloud decide what you actually see; times in the table are of the local maximum.

Safety

Never look at the sun without proper eclipse glasses (solar filters made to the ISO 12312-2 standard) or a solar filter on the front of a telescope or camera, even for a partial eclipse; sunglasses are not enough. Read the safety guide.
