About this eclipse

The total solar eclipse of Sunday, 2 September 2035 reaches its greatest at 01:55 UTC (07:25 IST). In a total solar eclipse the moon covers the whole sun for a few minutes along a narrow path; outside the path people see a partial eclipse. The longest duration of the total or annular phase on the central line is 02m54s.

Its magnitude is 1.032. For a solar eclipse that is the fraction of the sun's width covered at the greatest point on Earth, and for a lunar eclipse the fraction of the moon's width inside the Earth's dark shadow. It belongs to Saros series 145.

From India

Indian cities with a partial eclipse: Lucknow (62%), Patna (61%), Varanasi (60%), Prayagraj (59%), Kanpur (59%), Meerut (56%), Srinagar (56%), Dhanbad (55%), Ranchi (54%), Ghaziabad (53%). Local sunrise, sunset and cloud decide what you actually see; times in the table are of the local maximum.

Safety

Never look at the sun without proper eclipse glasses (solar filters made to the ISO 12312-2 standard) or a solar filter on the front of a telescope or camera, even for a partial eclipse; sunglasses are not enough. Read the safety guide.
