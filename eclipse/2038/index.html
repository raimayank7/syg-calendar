2038 at a glance

2038 has 7 eclipses in all: 3 solar and 4 lunar. The total ones are total solar eclipse on 26 December. Years have at least four and at most seven eclipses; every eclipse comes in a season, about every six months, when the moon's path crosses the sun's.
