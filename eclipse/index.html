Eclipses in 2026 to 2040

Between 2026 and 2040 there are 34 solar and 35 lunar eclipses. 10 of the solar ones are total, which is the kind that turns day into night for a few minutes; the next is on Monday, 2 August 2027. The next total lunar eclipse is on Sunday, 31 December 2028.

Each eclipse has its own page with the time of the greatest eclipse in UTC and in India, how large it is, how long it lasts, the regions NASA lists as seeing it, and a table of the cities where it can be seen. Solar eclipses are dangerous to look at without protection; read the safety guide first.
