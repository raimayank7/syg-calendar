2029 at a glance

2029 has 6 eclipses in all: 4 solar and 2 lunar. The total ones are total lunar eclipse on 26 June, total lunar eclipse on 20 December. Years have at least four and at most seven eclipses; every eclipse comes in a season, about every six months, when the moon's path crosses the sun's.
