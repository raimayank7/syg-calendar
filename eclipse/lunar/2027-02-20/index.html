About this eclipse

The penumbral lunar eclipse of Saturday, 20 February 2027 reaches its greatest at 23:12 UTC (04:42 IST). In a penumbral lunar eclipse the moon passes only through the faint outer shadow of the Earth. The dimming is slight and hard to see with the eye.

Its magnitude is -0.057. For a solar eclipse that is the fraction of the sun's width covered at the greatest point on Earth, and for a lunar eclipse the fraction of the moon's width inside the Earth's dark shadow. It belongs to Saros series 143.

From India

Indian cities where the moon is up at the greatest eclipse: Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Surat, Pune, Jaipur, Kanpur, Navi Mumbai. Local sunrise, sunset and cloud decide what you actually see; times in the table are of the local maximum.

Watching

A lunar eclipse is safe to look at with the naked eye, binoculars or a telescope. The moon turns darker and often coppery red in a total eclipse. Pick a dark place and give your eyes a few minutes to adjust.
