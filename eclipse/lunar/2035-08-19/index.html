About this eclipse

The partial lunar eclipse of Sunday, 19 August 2035 reaches its greatest at 01:10 UTC (06:40 IST). In a partial eclipse the moon covers only part of the sun, so nobody on Earth sees a complete eclipse. The partial phase lasts 01h17m.

Its magnitude is 0.104. For a solar eclipse that is the fraction of the sun's width covered at the greatest point on Earth, and for a lunar eclipse the fraction of the moon's width inside the Earth's dark shadow. It belongs to Saros series 119.

From India

In none of the Indian cities we checked (all with more than a million people) is the moon above the horizon at the greatest eclipse. Local sunrise, sunset and cloud decide what you actually see; times in the table are of the local maximum.

Watching

A lunar eclipse is safe to look at with the naked eye, binoculars or a telescope. The moon turns darker and often coppery red in a total eclipse. Pick a dark place and give your eyes a few minutes to adjust.
