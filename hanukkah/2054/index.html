Hanukkah 2054

Hanukkah in 2054 begins in the evening of Friday, 25 December 2054, when the first candle is lit, and the first full day is Saturday, 26 December 2054. The festival lasts eight days and ends on Saturday, 2 January 2055. It is the 25th of the Hebrew month of Kislev, in the Hebrew year 5815, and its date moves in the Gregorian calendar between late November and late December.

The rest of the Jewish year 2054

Passover begins in the evening of Wednesday, 22 April 2054 and its first day is 23 Apr; Rosh Hashanah, the new year 5815, begins in the evening of Friday, 2 October 2054, and Yom Kippur is on Monday, 12 October 2054. The spring holidays of 2054 belong to the Hebrew year 5814 and the autumn ones to 5815. 5814 is a leap year in the Hebrew calendar, so Purim falls in Adar II.
