Hanukkah 2021

Hanukkah in 2021 begins in the evening of Sunday, 28 November 2021, when the first candle is lit, and the first full day is Monday, 29 November 2021. The festival lasts eight days and ends on Monday, 6 December 2021. It is the 25th of the Hebrew month of Kislev, in the Hebrew year 5782, and its date moves in the Gregorian calendar between late November and late December.

The rest of the Jewish year 2021

Passover begins in the evening of Saturday, 27 March 2021 and its first day is 28 Mar; Rosh Hashanah, the new year 5782, begins in the evening of Monday, 6 September 2021, and Yom Kippur is on Thursday, 16 September 2021. The spring holidays of 2021 belong to the Hebrew year 5781 and the autumn ones to 5782.
