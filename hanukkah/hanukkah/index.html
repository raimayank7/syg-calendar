Why the date changes

Hanukkah always begins on the 25th of Kislev in the Hebrew calendar, but the Hebrew calendar follows the moon and the Gregorian calendar the sun, so the date in the Gregorian calendar changes from year to year. Between 2020 and 2060 the first candle is lit as early as 27 Nov and as late as 26 Dec.

The shortest and the longest wait

The Hebrew year adds a leap month seven times in 19 years, so in those years Hanukkah comes later in the Gregorian calendar relative to the previous year.
