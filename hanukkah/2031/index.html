Hanukkah 2031

Hanukkah in 2031 begins in the evening of Tuesday, 9 December 2031, when the first candle is lit, and the first full day is Wednesday, 10 December 2031. The festival lasts eight days and ends on Wednesday, 17 December 2031. It is the 25th of the Hebrew month of Kislev, in the Hebrew year 5792, and its date moves in the Gregorian calendar between late November and late December.

The rest of the Jewish year 2031

Passover begins in the evening of Monday, 7 April 2031 and its first day is 8 Apr; Rosh Hashanah, the new year 5792, begins in the evening of Wednesday, 17 September 2031, and Yom Kippur is on Saturday, 27 September 2031. The spring holidays of 2031 belong to the Hebrew year 5791 and the autumn ones to 5792.
