Hanukkah 2038

Hanukkah in 2038 begins in the evening of Tuesday, 21 December 2038, when the first candle is lit, and the first full day is Wednesday, 22 December 2038. The festival lasts eight days and ends on Wednesday, 29 December 2038. It is the 25th of the Hebrew month of Kislev, in the Hebrew year 5799, and its date moves in the Gregorian calendar between late November and late December.

The rest of the Jewish year 2038

Passover begins in the evening of Monday, 19 April 2038 and its first day is 20 Apr; Rosh Hashanah, the new year 5799, begins in the evening of Wednesday, 29 September 2038, and Yom Kippur is on Saturday, 9 October 2038. The spring holidays of 2038 belong to the Hebrew year 5798 and the autumn ones to 5799. 5798 is a leap year in the Hebrew calendar, so Purim falls in Adar II.
