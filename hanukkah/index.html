About the dates

The Jewish calendar follows both the moon and the sun, so the holidays move in the Gregorian calendar but always keep their place in the Hebrew one. Choose a year to see every holiday with the day it begins, remembering that each Jewish day begins at nightfall, so a holiday starts in the evening of the day before the date shown. Passover and Rosh Hashanah are also listed on each year page, together with the eight nights of Hanukkah.

Hanukkah in 2027

Hanukkah 2027 begins in the evening of Friday, 24 December 2027 and ends on Saturday, 1 January 2028.
