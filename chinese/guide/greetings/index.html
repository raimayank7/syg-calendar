Using the greetings

Xinnian kuaile is the plain "Happy New Year" and is safe for anyone, including people who do not celebrate Lunar New Year. Gongxi facai is a wish for prosperity said to friends and colleagues, especially in Cantonese-speaking places in the form "gung hei fat choy". Greetings in Cantonese, Vietnamese and Korean are different, and the written Chinese is the same. These phrases are standard greetings; for formal writing or business cards ask a speaker of the language.

Other things said at Lunar New Year

Red envelopes (hongbao) with money are given by married people to children and unmarried young adults. Families clean the house before the new year and many avoid sweeping on the first days. These are customs, which vary between regions and families, and are listed here as general knowledge, not rules.
