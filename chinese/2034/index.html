Lunar New Year 2034

Chinese New Year in 2034 falls on Sunday, 19 February 2034. It begins the year of the Tiger (虎 Hǔ), the third of the twelve animals; the year is named 甲寅 in the 60-year cycle, with the element Wood and the polarity Yang. The animal year runs until Wednesday, 7 February 2035, the day before the next new year, so it lasts 354 days.

Who belongs to the year of the Tiger

The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar year, not 1 January. People born from Sunday, 19 February 2034 to Wednesday, 7 February 2035 belong to the Tiger, and those born in January or February before the new year are of the animal of the year before, the Ox. Years of the Tiger are 1950, 1962, 1974, 1986, 1998, 2010, 2022, 2034.

The festival days

Spring Festival lasts until the Lantern Festival on Sunday, 5 March 2034, 15 days later. Qingming on Wednesday, 5 April 2034 is a day to sweep graves and remember ancestors. The Dragon Boat Festival is on Tuesday, 20 June 2034, the Mid-Autumn Festival on Wednesday, 27 September 2034 and the Double Ninth Festival on Friday, 20 October 2034. In mainland China, public holidays around these days are set each year by the State Council, which often moves working days to make a longer break; those arrangements are not on this site.
