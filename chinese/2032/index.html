Lunar New Year 2032

Chinese New Year in 2032 falls on Wednesday, 11 February 2032. It begins the year of the Rat (鼠 Shǔ), the first of the twelve animals; the year is named 壬子 in the 60-year cycle, with the element Water and the polarity Yang. The animal year runs until Sunday, 30 January 2033, the day before the next new year, so it lasts 355 days.

Who belongs to the year of the Rat

The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar year, not 1 January. People born from Wednesday, 11 February 2032 to Sunday, 30 January 2033 belong to the Rat, and those born in January or February before the new year are of the animal of the year before, the Pig. Years of the Rat are 1948, 1960, 1972, 1984, 1996, 2008, 2020, 2032.

The festival days

Spring Festival lasts until the Lantern Festival on Wednesday, 25 February 2032, 15 days later. Qingming on Sunday, 4 April 2032 is a day to sweep graves and remember ancestors. The Dragon Boat Festival is on Saturday, 12 June 2032, the Mid-Autumn Festival on Sunday, 19 September 2032 and the Double Ninth Festival on Tuesday, 12 October 2032. In mainland China, public holidays around these days are set each year by the State Council, which often moves working days to make a longer break; those arrangements are not on this site.
