How the zodiac works

Twelve animals take turns year after year, and the year changes on Lunar New Year, not on 1 January. Together with five elements and two polarities, they make a cycle of 60 years. The year 2027 is the Fire Goat year (丁未). The animal is sometimes called a Sheep or a Ram in English instead of Goat; the Chinese word covers all three.

Finding your animal

Look up your year of birth in the animal pages, then check whether your birthday is before the Lunar New Year of that year. If it is, you belong to the animal of the previous year. Every animal appears once in each block of twelve years, and the element changes every two years, so the same animal and element together return after 60 years.
