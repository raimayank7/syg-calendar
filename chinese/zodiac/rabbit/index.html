Year of the Rabbit

The Rabbit (兔, Tù) is the fourth of the twelve animals of the Chinese zodiac. The animals repeat every twelve years, and each combines with one of five elements (wood, fire, earth, metal, water) in a 60-year cycle, so the same animal-and-element year returns after 60 years. The most recent year of the Rabbit was 2023 and the next is 2035.

Who is a Rabbit

You belong to the year of the Rabbit if you were born between the Lunar New Year that starts the year and the day before the next one, for example from Thursday, 8 February 2035 to Sunday, 27 January 2036 for 2035. A person born in January or February before the new year belongs to the previous animal. The characteristics people attach to each animal are folk belief and fun; this site makes no claims about them.

All animals: Rat Ox Tiger Dragon Snake Horse Goat Monkey Rooster Dog Pig.
