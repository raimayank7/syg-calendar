What is Lunar New Year?

Lunar New Year, called the Spring Festival (春节) in China, is the first day of the Chinese lunar calendar and the most important festival of the year for Chinese families and for many communities across East and Southeast Asia. Families reunite, eat together on the eve and exchange greetings and red envelopes. It always falls between 21 January and 20 February. Dates on this site come from the Hong Kong Observatory tables and were checked with an astronomical calculation.

The year after the new year takes the next of twelve animals. Which animal you are depends on the date of your birth in relation to the new year, not on the calendar year.
