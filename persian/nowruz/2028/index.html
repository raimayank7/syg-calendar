Nowruz 2028: 20 March 2028

Persian New Year 2028 (1 Farvardin 1407) falls on Monday, 20 March 2028. The spring equinox is at 20 March 02:16 UTC.

When is Nowruz in 2028?

The spring equinox of 2028 is on Monday, 20 March 2028 at 02:16 UTC, which is 05:46 in Tehran. Because that is before noon in Tehran, the Persian year 1407 begins on Monday, 20 March 2028, a Monday. Many families gather at the moment of the equinox, called Sal Tahvil (the turning of the year), around the Haft-sin table.

The Persian year 1407 lasts 365 days, from 20 March to Monday, 19 March 2029, so it is an ordinary year with a 29-day Esfand.

The days of the festival

Nowruz is celebrated for about 13 days. The last day, Sizdah Bedar, is the 13th of Farvardin, on Saturday, 1 April 2028: families go out into nature. Chaharshanbe Suri, the fire-jumping festival, is held on the evening before the last Wednesday of the old year, and that Wednesday is Wednesday, 15 March 2028.

Nowruz is a holiday in Iran, Afghanistan, Tajikistan, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan and other countries, and is celebrated by Kurds, Parsis and other communities. Dates of national holidays are set by each government. Other years: 2026 2027 2029 2030. The Haft-sin table.
