About Khordad

Khordad is the month number 3 of the Persian year and has 31 days. The third month, the last of the spring. The month is named after Haurvatat, "wholeness" or perfection, according to the old Persian tradition.

Where it falls

Khordad starts between 21 May and 22 May in the years shown. The first six Persian months have 31 days, the next five have 30, and Esfand has 29, or 30 in a leap year. The calendar has no month that starts at a fixed Gregorian date, but the first of Farvardin is always on 20 or 21 March, so every month starts within a day or two of the same Gregorian date each year: for example, Khordad begins on 22 May in most years.
