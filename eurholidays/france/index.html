France in 2027

France has 11 public holidays in the official list for 2027, of which 4 fall on a Saturday or Sunday: Labour Day (Saturday, 1 May 2027), Victory in Europe Day (8 May 1945) (Saturday, 8 May 2027), Assumption (Sunday, 15 August 2027), Christmas Day (Saturday, 25 December 2027). The list is the "cas général" of Service-Public.gouv.fr, which applies to the mainland except Alsace-Moselle; the overseas departments have extra holidays.

Long weekends and leave

There are 4 runs of three or more days off in a row: 1 Jan to 3 Jan (3 days); 27 Mar to 29 Mar (3 days); 15 May to 17 May (3 days); 30 Oct to 1 Nov (3 days). One or two days of leave next to a holiday can give these breaks: 1 Jan to 4 Jan, 4 days off for 1 day of leave; 26 Mar to 29 Mar, 4 days off for 1 day of leave; 6 May to 9 May, 4 days off for 1 day of leave; 14 May to 17 May, 4 days off for 1 day of leave; 29 Oct to 1 Nov, 4 days off for 1 day of leave.

Alsace-Moselle

In Alsace and Moselle (the departments of Bas-Rhin, Haut-Rhin and Moselle) the official table adds Good Friday, in communes with a Protestant temple or a mixed church, on Friday, 26 March 2027, and Saint Stephen's Day, 26 December, on Sunday, 26 December 2027, so the region has 13 legal holidays.

The moveable days

Easter Monday (Monday, 29 March 2027), Ascension Day (Thursday, 6 May 2027) and Whit Monday (Monday, 17 May 2027) follow Easter, which is on Sunday, 28 March 2027; the other holidays are on fixed dates. Whit Monday is also the "day of solidarity" in many workplaces; check your own agreement.
