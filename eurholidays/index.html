About these lists

Spain's holidays differ by community, so the table counts only the days that every community has, while the community pages give each region's full calendar. France has one national list, plus extra days in Alsace-Moselle and overseas. Each page shows the date and weekday, the long weekends, the working days in every month, and the best breaks that one or two days of leave can make, assuming a Saturday and Sunday weekend.
