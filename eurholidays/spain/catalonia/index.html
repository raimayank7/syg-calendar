Catalonia in 2027

Catalonia has 12 public holidays in the official list for 2027, of which 3 fall on a Saturday or Sunday: Labour Day (Saturday, 1 May 2027), National Day of Catalonia (Saturday, 11 September 2027), Christmas Day (Saturday, 25 December 2027). In the Val d'Aran the holiday of 29 March (Easter Monday) is replaced by 17 June, a Thursday, the Fiesta de Arán.

Long weekends and leave

There are 4 runs of three or more days off in a row: 1 Jan to 3 Jan (3 days); 26 Mar to 29 Mar (4 days); 30 Oct to 1 Nov (3 days); 4 Dec to 6 Dec (3 days). One or two days of leave next to a holiday can give these breaks: 1 Jan to 6 Jan, 6 days off for 2 days of leave; 25 Mar to 29 Mar, 5 days off for 1 day of leave; 24 Jun to 27 Jun, 4 days off for 1 day of leave; 9 Oct to 12 Oct, 4 days off for 1 day of leave; 4 Dec to 8 Dec, 5 days off for 1 day of leave.

Regional days

The holidays set by Catalonia itself for 2027 are Easter Monday (Monday, 29 March 2027), Saint John's Day (Thursday, 24 June 2027), National Day of Catalonia (Saturday, 11 September 2027).

Days other communities have

The days that some communities keep but Catalonia does not have are: Saint Joseph's Day (19 Mar), Holy Thursday (25 Mar), Monday after the Assumption (16 Aug).

Other communities: Andalusia, Aragon, Asturias, Balearic Islands, Canary Islands, Cantabria, Castilla-La Mancha, Castile and León, Extremadura, Galicia, Community of Madrid, Region of Murcia, Navarre, Basque Country, La Rioja, Valencian Community, Ceuta, Melilla. All of Spain.
