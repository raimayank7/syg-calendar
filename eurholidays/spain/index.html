How the list is made

Spain's national list is not the same for everyone. Eight holidays are national and cannot be changed by a community: New Year's Day (1 Jan), Good Friday (26 Mar), Labour Day (1 May), National Day of Spain (12 Oct), All Saints' Day (1 Nov), Constitution Day (6 Dec), Immaculate Conception (8 Dec), Christmas Day (25 Dec). Epiphany, 6 January, is also kept by all 19 communities in 2027. The rest of each community's calendar comes from the national days that it keeps, such as Holy Thursday or Easter Monday, and its own regional day. Each community has 11 or 12 holidays in the official list for 2027, and each town then adds two local days of its own, which are not on this list.

In the Canary Islands the official notice adds one island holiday: 24 September in El Hierro, 17 September in Fuerteventura, 8 September in Gran Canaria, 4 October in La Gomera, 5 August in La Palma, 15 September in Lanzarote and La Graciosa, and 2 February in Tenerife. In the Val d'Aran the holiday of 29 March (Easter Monday) is replaced by 17 June, a Thursday, the Fiesta de Arán.
