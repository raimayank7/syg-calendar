What the 2027 calendar looks like in Kerala (India)

The list above has 21 national public holidays for 2027. 15 of them fall on a Monday to Friday, which are the ones that give a day off work; 6 fall on a Saturday or Sunday (Mannam Jayanthi, Maha Sivarathri, May Day, Ayyan Kali Jayanthi, Gandhi Jayanthi and Christmas), which mean no extra day off unless the country moves the holiday, as many do by rule or decree.

By weekday, 3 fall on a Monday, 1 on a Friday and 11 in the middle of the week. Mondays and Fridays make a long weekend of three days without any leave; holidays on Tuesday or Thursday leave one working day between the holiday and the weekend, which is the bridge day. The mid-week ones are Republic Day (Tuesday), Id-ul-Fitr (Ramzan) (Wednesday), Maundy Thursday (Thursday), Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Jayanthi (Wednesday), Vishu (Thursday), Muharram (Tuesday), Sreekrishna Jayanthi (Tuesday), Fourth Onam (Tuesday), Sree Narayana Guru Jayanthi (Wednesday), Sree Narayana Guru Samadhi (Wednesday) and Deepavali (Thursday).

The longest break without leave

The longest run of days off made by holidays and weekends alone is 11 to 15 September: 5 days, around Third Onam, Fourth Onam, Sree Narayana Guru Jayanthi.

The best use of leave days

The most efficient plan is to take 16 April (Friday) off: that gives 5 days off in a row from 14 to 18 April, a return of 5.0 days off for each day of leave. Plans are sorted by that return, and each uses only whole working days between weekends and holidays; they may overlap, so check before you combine them.

Source of the dates. The holiday dates are from the Government of Keralam, General Administration (Co-ordination) Department, G.O. (P) No. 22/2026/GAD dated 1 October 2026, published in the Kerala Gazette Extraordinary No. 2800 of 18 October 2026 (signed gazette; the copy was read from kppha.in on 13 November 2026 and the gazette can be checked at compose.kerala.gov.in). Only the 21 closed holidays that fall on working days are listed; the gazette also names seven more occasions that fall on Sundays or second Saturdays (Easter Sunday 28 March, Milad-i-Sherif 14 August, Independence Day 15 August, First Onam 11 September, Thiruvonam 12 September, Mahanavami 9 October, Vijayadasami 10 October) and 3 restricted holidays. Kerala government offices are closed on all Sundays and second Saturdays only, so the long weekends below, which assume a Saturday and Sunday weekend, fit schools and offices with a five-day week but not every Kerala office. Id-ul-Fitr, Bakrid and Muharram are marked subject to change depending on the appearance of the moon. Where we also compared them with Nager.Date (open source, date.nager.at), we used the official list wherever they differed. Holidays that are announced later (those based on the moon, or declared by a state or by decree) can change, and regional holidays are not included. Weekend taken as Saturday and Sunday. Confirm every date with the government notice or your employer. Checked November 2026.
