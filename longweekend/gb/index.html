About these dates

The holidays are the national public holidays listed by Nager.Date for United Kingdom. A holiday that falls on a Saturday or Sunday gives no extra day off in most plans here, although in some countries it is moved to the nearest Monday or Friday. The tables show the date as listed; check the government notice for any moved day.

A long weekend on this site is a run of at least three days off in a row made only by weekends and public holidays. A bridge day is a working day between a holiday and a weekend: take it as leave and the days off join into one break. The guide explains how to choose.
