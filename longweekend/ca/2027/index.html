What the 2027 calendar looks like in Canada

The list above has 8 national public holidays for 2027. 7 of them fall on a Monday to Friday, which are the ones that give a day off work; 1 fall on a Saturday or Sunday (Christmas Day), which mean no extra day off unless the country moves the holiday, as many do by rule or decree.

By weekday, 3 fall on a Monday, 2 on a Friday and 2 in the middle of the week. Mondays and Fridays make a long weekend of three days without any leave; holidays on Tuesday or Thursday leave one working day between the holiday and the weekend, which is the bridge day. The mid-week ones are Canada Day (Thursday) and National Day for Truth and Reconciliation (Thursday).

The longest break without leave

The longest run of days off made by holidays and weekends alone is 1 to 3 January: 3 days, around New Year's Day.

The best use of leave days

The most efficient plan is to take 2 July (Friday) off: that gives 4 days off in a row from 1 to 4 July, a return of 4.0 days off for each day of leave. Plans are sorted by that return, and each uses only whole working days between weekends and holidays; they may overlap, so check before you combine them.

2027 against 2028

In 2028 there are 5 long weekends from holidays alone and the best plan gives 9 days off for 4 days of leave; in 2027 there are 5 and 4 days off for 1 day. The changes come from the weekday on which each holiday falls: the same date moves forward by one weekday each year, two in a leap year. Open the 2028 page for Canada.
