How it works

  1. Choose your country and the year.
  2. Read the long weekends: runs of three or more days off made by a public holiday and a weekend.
  3. Look at the bridge plans: the leave days you take around a holiday and the number of days off in a row you get for them.

For example, a holiday on a Thursday makes a four-day break if you take the Friday off; two holidays a week apart can give a nine-day break for two or three leave days. The plan tables sort the best bargains first. The guide shows the method step by step.

Dates from Nager.Date, national holidays only, weekend = Saturday and Sunday; confirm with your government or employer. Checked November 2026.
