What the 2027 calendar looks like in Puducherry (India)

The list above has 17 national public holidays for 2027. 11 of them fall on a Monday to Friday, which are the ones that give a day off work; 6 fall on a Saturday or Sunday (Thiruvalluvar Day / Mattu Pongal, May Day, Independence Day / Milad-un-Nabi, Vinayagar Chathurthi, Gandhi Jayanthi and Christmas), which mean no extra day off unless the country moves the holiday, as many do by rule or decree.

By weekday, 3 fall on a Monday, 4 on a Friday and 4 in the middle of the week. Mondays and Fridays make a long weekend of three days without any leave; holidays on Tuesday or Thursday leave one working day between the holiday and the weekend, which is the bridge day. The mid-week ones are Republic Day (Tuesday), Ramzan (Id-ul-Fitr) (Wednesday), Tamil New Year / Dr. Ambedkar's Birthday (Wednesday) and Deepavali (Thursday).

The longest break without leave

The longest run of days off made by holidays and weekends alone is 1 to 3 January: 3 days, around New Year.

The best use of leave days

The most efficient plan is to take 29 October (Friday) off: that gives 5 days off in a row from 28 October to 1 November, a return of 5.0 days off for each day of leave. Plans are sorted by that return, and each uses only whole working days between weekends and holidays; they may overlap, so check before you combine them.

Source of the dates. The holiday dates are from the Government of Puducherry, Home Department, G.O. The Mahe and Yanam regions have their own shorter lists and are not included. Long weekends below assume a Saturday and Sunday weekend; whether an office works on Saturdays depends on the department. Id-ul-Fitr, Bakrid and Milad-un-Nabi are marked "subject to change depending on the appearance of the moon" in the notification. Where we also compared them with Nager.Date (open source, date.nager.at), we used the official list wherever they differed. Holidays that are announced later (those based on the moon, or declared by a state or by decree) can change, and regional holidays are not included. Weekend taken as Saturday and Sunday.
