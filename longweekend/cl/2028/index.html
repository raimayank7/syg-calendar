What the 2028 calendar looks like in Chile

The list above has 16 national public holidays for 2028. 12 of them fall on a Monday to Friday, which are the ones that give a day off work; 4 fall on a Saturday or Sunday (New Year's Day, Holy Saturday, Navy Day and Our Lady of Mount Carmel), which mean no extra day off unless the country moves the holiday, as many do by rule or decree.

By weekday, 5 fall on a Monday, 3 on a Friday and 4 in the middle of the week. Mondays and Fridays make a long weekend of three days without any leave; holidays on Tuesday or Thursday leave one working day between the holiday and the weekend, which is the bridge day. The mid-week ones are National Day of Indigenous Peoples (Tuesday), Assumption of Mary (Tuesday), Army Day (Tuesday) and All Saints Day (Wednesday).

The longest break without leave

The longest run of days off made by holidays and weekends alone is 16 to 19 September: 4 days, around National holiday, Army Day.

The best use of leave days

The most efficient plan is to take 19 June (Monday) off: that gives 4 days off in a row from 17 to 20 June, a return of 4.0 days off for each day of leave. Plans are sorted by that return, and each uses only whole working days between weekends and holidays; they may overlap, so check before you combine them.

2028 against 2027

In 2027 there are 8 long weekends from holidays alone and the best plan gives 10 days off for 4 days of leave; in 2028 there are 8 and 4 days off for 1 day. The changes come from the weekday on which each holiday falls: the same date moves forward by one weekday each year, two in a leap year. Open the 2027 page for Chile.

Source of the dates. The holiday dates are from Nager.Date (date.nager.at), an open-source, community-maintained data set. Holidays that are announced later (those based on the moon, or declared by a state or by decree) can change, and regional holidays are not included. Weekend taken as Saturday and Sunday.
