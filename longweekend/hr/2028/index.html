What the 2028 calendar looks like in Croatia

The list above has 14 national public holidays for 2028. 10 of them fall on a Monday to Friday, which are the ones that give a day off work; 4 fall on a Saturday or Sunday (New Year's Day, Easter Sunday, Victory and Homeland Thanksgiving Day and the Day of Croatian defenders and Remembrance Day), which mean no extra day off unless the country moves the holiday, as many do by rule or decree.

By weekday, 3 fall on a Monday, 0 on a Friday and 7 in the middle of the week. Mondays and Fridays make a long weekend of three days without any leave; holidays on Tuesday or Thursday leave one working day between the holiday and the weekend, which is the bridge day. The mid-week ones are Epiphany (Thursday), National Day (Tuesday), Corpus Christi (Thursday), Anti-Fascist Struggle Day (Thursday), Assumption Day (Tuesday), All Saints' Day (Wednesday) and St. Stephen's Day (Tuesday).

The longest break without leave

The longest run of days off made by holidays and weekends alone is 23 to 26 December: 4 days, around Christmas Day, St. Stephen's Day.

The best use of leave days

The most efficient plan is to take 7 January (Friday) off: that gives 4 days off in a row from 6 to 9 January, a return of 4.0 days off for each day of leave. Plans are sorted by that return, and each uses only whole working days between weekends and holidays; they may overlap, so check before you combine them.

2028 against 2027

In 2027 there are 3 long weekends from holidays alone and the best plan gives 4 days off for 1 day of leave; in 2028 there are 3 and 4 days off for 1 day. The changes come from the weekday on which each holiday falls: the same date moves forward by one weekday each year, two in a leap year. Open the 2027 page for Croatia.
