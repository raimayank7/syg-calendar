Why only some countries

The planner works with a Saturday and Sunday weekend, because the weekend days of a country are not part of a holiday list and are not taken from any data source on this site. Countries with a Friday and Saturday weekend, or a mixed week, are left out rather than shown wrongly. Holidays are national ones only: regional holidays of states, provinces, cantons and territories are not included, even where they matter more than the national list.
