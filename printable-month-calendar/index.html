Printable month calendar for any month and year

Choose a month and year, set the options and print. The page prints as one clean sheet with large boxes to write in.

How to print a month

  1. Choose the month and type the year. Any year from 1583 onward works.
  2. Set the options: week starting on Sunday or Monday, holidays marked or not, and lines for notes.
  3. Tap Print or save as PDF. In the print window choose Landscape for the widest boxes. Only the calendar sheet is printed; the menus, buttons and this text are left out.

To save a PDF, choose "Save as PDF" or "Microsoft Print to PDF" as the printer. On a phone, use the browser's Share or Print option and choose "Save as PDF".

Getting a good print

  • Paper: A4, landscape. The table stretches to the width of the sheet.
  • Margins: "Default" or "Minimum". If the last row falls on a second sheet, set the scale to "Fit to page" or 90%.
  • Headers and footers: untick "Headers and footers" in the print window to remove the page address and date that browsers add.
  • Background graphics: tick it if you want the light shading of Sundays and holidays to print. Without it the calendar prints in plain black lines, which saves ink.

Sunday first or Monday first?

Wall calendars in India and North America usually put Sunday in the first column. Calendars in most of Europe, and the international standard ISO 8601, start the week on Monday, which keeps Saturday and Sunday together at the end. Choose whichever your household or office is used to. A weekly planner that numbers its weeks should use Monday; see week numbers.

About the holiday markings

When the box is ticked, the 17 gazetted holidays of the Central Government for 2027 are printed in their date boxes by name. They come from the official list for Central Government offices in Delhi and New Delhi; state, bank and school holidays differ. We mark only years for which we hold an official list, so other years print without holidays. The full list with the restricted holidays is on the 2027 holiday page. Untick the box for a plain calendar, for example for use outside India.

Ideas for a printed month

  • Family planner on the fridge: school events, fee dates, doctor's visits, who is travelling when.
  • Bill and EMI tracker: write each due date in its box and tick it when paid.
  • Study timetable: one subject or chapter per day up to the exam.
  • Duty roster or shift chart for a small shop or team.
  • Habit tracker: a tick for each day of walking, reading or practice. An unbroken row is its own reward.
  • Milk, newspaper and maid account: mark the days of no delivery or leave and settle up at the month's end.
  • Fasting and festival days: note the family's own observances, which no printed calendar lists exactly.

Need the whole year?

The printable year calendar puts twelve small months on one sheet. For the year 2027 this site also has a ready page for every month with its holidays and notes, starting from January 2027.

Is the printable calendar free?

Yes. There is nothing to download, no login and no watermark.

Can I print a calendar for last year or a year far ahead?

Yes, any year from 1583 onward. Past years are useful for records and attendance sheets.

Why are no holidays marked for 2028?

The government had not published the 2028 list when this site was last updated. We mark only officially announced holidays.
