Countdown: days until any date

Tap an occasion or choose your own date to see how many days are left, or how many have passed.

How to use it

  1. Tap one of the occasion buttons, or choose any date in the box: an exam, a wedding, a journey, a birthday, the last day of a job.
  2. Read the days to go. The page also gives the wait in weeks and in months, and the number of weekdays in between.
  3. Tap Send on WhatsApp to share the count with family or a group.

If the date has already passed, the page shows how many days ago it was. That is handy for "days since" counts: days since you stopped a habit, since a child was born, since a project began.

About the occasion buttons

The buttons always point to the next occurrence, so on 2 January the New Year button already counts to the following 1 January.

  • New Year is 1 January.
  • Republic Day is 26 January, the day the Constitution of India came into force in 1950.
  • Independence Day is 15 August.
  • Gandhi Jayanti is 2 October.
  • Christmas is 25 December.
  • Diwali appears only for a year whose date we have from the official Central Government holiday list. For 2027 that list gives Friday, 29 October. The date of Diwali follows the lunar calendar and changes every year, so we do not guess it for years we have no official list for.

Other festivals that move with the moon are on the holiday list, with the dates the government has announced.

How the count is made

The count is the number of midnights between today and the chosen date. Today is not counted and the day itself is: if the exam is tomorrow, the answer is 1 day to go. "Nights of sleep" is the same figure, which children find easier to picture.

Weekdays in between counts Mondays to Fridays from tomorrow up to and including the date. It is a rough guide to working time left. For a count that also removes public holidays, use the working days calculator.

The count uses the date set on your device. If your device's date is wrong, so is the countdown.

Ideas for using a countdown

  • Exam preparation: divide the syllabus by the weekdays left to see how much each day must cover. Keep the last week free for revision.
  • Saving for something: divide the amount by the weeks left.
  • Weddings and functions: bookings, invitations and tailoring each need to be done a certain number of weeks ahead, so count backwards from the day.
  • Travel: passports, visas and tickets have lead times. Check them against the days left.
  • Children: a paper chain with one link for each night of sleep makes the wait for a birthday or a festival something they can see.

Related tools

To find the date a number of days from now, use add days to a date. For the gap between two dates that are both not today, use days between dates.

How many days are left in this year?

Tap New Year: the count to 1 January is one more than the days left in the year, because it includes the last day itself.

Does the countdown include today?

No. Today is not counted; the chosen day is.

Why is there no button for Holi or Eid?

Their dates change every year. We show a festival button only where we hold the official date, and the full list of announced dates is on the holidays page.
