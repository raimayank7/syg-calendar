What you can do here

  • See today at a glance: the date, the day number in the year, the ISO week number, the financial year and the date in the Indian national calendar are shown at the top of this page. Step the small calendar backward and forward to look at other months.
  • Print a calendar: a ready page for every year and for each month of 2027, or make your own with the printable month calendar and the printable year calendar, with a Sunday or a Monday start.
  • Work out a date: days between two dates, add or subtract days, exact age, the weekday of any date, working days and a countdown.
  • Check a holiday: the Central Government holidays of 2027, the long weekends they make, and the federal and bank holidays of the United States and the United Kingdom.

Holidays only from official lists

Many calendar sites print festival dates years ahead from their own guesses, and they often disagree with each other and with the government. This site takes a different line. A holiday appears here only when we have read it in an official list, and each page names that list and the date we read it. For 2027 the Central Government's order gives 17 gazetted holidays for its offices in Delhi and New Delhi; they are marked on the calendars and listed month by month. For later years no Indian holiday is shown until the government publishes its list, which it normally does in the middle of the year before.

State, bank, court and school holidays differ from this list and from each other. For those, look at the notification of your own state or institution.

The Indian national calendar

Alongside the common calendar, India has an official civil calendar that counts years in the Saka era. Government orders and the Gazette print both dates. Today's Saka date is shown above, the Saka date converter turns any date either way, and the guide explains its twelve months.

Everything runs on your device

The calculators need no login and send nothing anywhere. A date of birth typed into the age calculator stays in your browser and is forgotten when you close the page. "Today" is taken from the clock of your own phone or computer, so if that is set wrongly the page will be wrong in the same way.

What is the date today?

It is shown at the top of this page in full, with the weekday, as set on your device.

Which holidays are marked on the calendars?

For 2027, the 17 gazetted holidays of the Central Government of India for its Delhi offices, from the official order. Other years are unmarked until their official lists are published.

Can I print the calendars?

Yes. Every year and month page has a print button, and the page prints as a clean sheet without menus or advertisements.

Does the site show tithi or muhurat?

No. It is a civil calendar site and gives no panchang or astrological information.
