Holiday lists from official sources

Every date on these pages comes from a government list that we name and date. Nothing is guessed.

  • Central Government holidays 2027 (India) DoPT order
  • Long weekends in 2027 worked out from the list
  • US federal holidays 2026 to 2030 OPM schedule
  • UK bank holidays 2026 to 2028 GOV.UK list

How we handle holidays

A holiday list is only useful if it is right, and the only body that can say what a public holiday is, is the government that declares it. So every date on these pages is copied from an official list, with the name and date of the list given on the page. We do not work out festival dates ourselves and we do not copy from other calendar sites.

This has a cost: a year appears here only after its official list is published. The Government of India issues the Central Government holiday list for a year in the middle of the year before; the order for 2027 is dated 16 July 2026. Until the next order appears, later years are shown without Indian holidays.

What each list covers

  • India: the Central Government holidays of 2027 are the holidays of Central Government administrative offices in Delhi and New Delhi: gazetted holidays, when offices close, and restricted holidays, from which each employee may choose two. The page explains how offices in other states arrive at their own list from the same order. From it we work out the long weekends of 2027.
  • United States: federal holidays are set by law for federal employees. States and private employers decide for themselves, though most follow the main ones.
  • United Kingdom: bank holidays differ between England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

What these lists do not cover

India has no single national holiday list for everyone. Apart from the three national holidays, each state government declares its own public holidays; banks follow the holidays their state notifies under the Negotiable Instruments Act; courts, stock exchanges, schools and universities each publish their own calendars; and a private employer's list is a matter of its own policy and the state's rules. A date that is a holiday for a Central Government office in Delhi may be a working day for a bank in Chennai.

So use these pages for planning, and confirm with your own office, bank or school before you rely on a date.

Dates that can move

The holidays of Id-ul-Fitr, Id-ul-Zuha, Muharram and Id-e-Milad depend on the sighting of the Moon. The official order itself says their dates may be changed, and that a change may be announced at very short notice through the press and broadcast media. If the government announces a change, the announcement is right and this page is out of date until we correct it.

Why are there no Indian holidays for 2028 yet?

The official list for 2028 had not been published when these pages were last updated. We add a year only from the government's own order.

Are these bank holidays?

For India, no. Bank holidays are notified state by state. The UK page does list bank holidays, which is the British name for public holidays.

Where is the official Indian list published?

The Department of Personnel and Training issues it as an Office Memorandum on its website, dopt.gov.in, and ministries circulate copies.
