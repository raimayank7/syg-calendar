The moon in Nanhai in 2027

In Nanhai (latitude 23.02, longitude 113.14) the moon rises on average 50 minutes later each day in 2027. Because of that delay 12 days have no moonrise and 12 days have no moonset, and these show a dash in the table. On the days when the moon is nearly full it rises between 17:14 and 19:46 local time. At midday the moon is fully lit on 14 days of the year and almost dark on 14 days around the new moon.

Using the table

A full moon rises close to sunset and sets close to sunrise; a new moon rises and sets with the sun and cannot be seen. A waxing crescent is a thin moon in the evening sky after sunset, a waning crescent a thin moon before sunrise. The lit part in the last column is the share of the moon's disc that is lit at midday, which is the same all over the world within a point or two. The dates of the phases themselves are in the moon phase section of this site.

The time zone used is Asia/Shanghai, with daylight saving time where the zone has it; the time shown is the local clock time on the day shown.
