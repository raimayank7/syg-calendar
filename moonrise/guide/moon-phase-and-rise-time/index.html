Moon phase and rise time

The phase of the moon tells you when it rises, crosses the sky and sets.

The time the moon rises follows from how far it is from the sun in the sky. A new moon is near the sun and rises with it at sunrise and sets at sunset. A first quarter moon rises around midday and sets around midnight, so it is high in the sky at sunset. A full moon is opposite the sun: it rises at sunset, is highest at midnight and sets at sunrise. A last quarter moon rises around midnight and sets around midday, so you can see it in the morning.

Crescents

A waxing crescent, a few days after new moon, rises a little after the sun and sets a little after sunset, so it is seen low in the west in the evening. A waning crescent rises just before dawn.
