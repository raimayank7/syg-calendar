About these times

The moon rises in the east and sets in the west, but about 50 minutes later each day, so the time of moonrise changes all the time. Each city page lists moonrise and moonset for every day of 2027, with the part of the moon that is lit at midday, month tables that show the earliest and latest moonrise and the days with no moonrise. Times are in local clock time including daylight saving.
