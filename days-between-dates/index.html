Days between two dates

Choose two dates. The number of days between them appears at once, with weeks, months and weekdays.

How to use it

  1. Choose the first date and the second date. On a phone the date picker opens; on a computer you can also type the date.
  2. Read the answer. It changes as soon as either date changes.
  3. Tick Count both the first and the last day if your count should include both ends.

If the first date is later than the second, the page swaps them and tells you so.

Counting one end or both ends

This small choice causes most disagreements about "how many days".

  • Not counting the first day answers "how many days from then until now?" From Monday to Friday is 4 days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday. Ages, due dates and interest periods are normally counted this way.
  • Counting both days answers "how many days does it cover?" A hotel conference from Monday to Friday covers 5 days. Leave applications, event durations and "days of the tournament" are counted this way.

When a form or a rule matters, read how it defines the period. Legal and banking periods often say "excluding the first day" or "both days inclusive" in so many words.

What each line means

To leave out public holidays as well, use the working days calculator.

Why months are tricky

"One month from 31 January" has no exact answer, because February has no 31st. This page follows the common rule: it counts the most whole months that fit without passing the second date, staying on the last day of a shorter month when it must, and then counts the remaining days. From 31 January to 1 March in a common year is therefore 1 month and 1 day. For any purpose that depends on exact days (notice periods, interest, visas), use the day count on the first line; it has only one possible answer.

Things people work out here

  • Days left until an exam, a wedding or a journey, and days since an event.
  • The length of a notice period, a warranty or a course.
  • The number of days of a rental, a hotel stay (nights are days without the first day counted) or a project.
  • How many weeks pregnant, counted from a date a doctor gave. The page only counts days; it gives no medical guidance.
  • The gap between two bills or two salary dates.

How the counting is done

Each date is turned into a count of days from a fixed starting point, and one count is subtracted from the other. Leap years are handled by the calendar rule itself: every fourth year has 366 days, except century years that cannot be divided by 400. The arithmetic ignores clock time and time zones, so a change to or from daylight saving time cannot add or lose a day.

How many days are there between two dates a year apart?

365, or 366 if a 29 February falls between them.

Does the calculator include the end date?

Yes, the last day is always counted. The first day is counted only if you tick the box.

Can I use dates from past centuries?

Yes, from year 1 onward, but the page applies today's calendar to every year. Dates before the calendar change of 1582 (1752 in Britain) were recorded in the older Julian calendar.
