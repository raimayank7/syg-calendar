November 2027

The IMO working list shows 5 showers peaking in November. The Southern Taurids peak on 6 November with a moon that is first quarter. The Northern Taurids peak on 13 November with a moon that is full moon. The Leonids peak on 18 November with a moon that is waning gibbous. The Alpha Monocerotids peak on 22 November with a moon that is waning crescent. The November Orionids peak on 28 November with a moon that is new moon.

Observing is best in the dark of the new moon: in November the new moon is on 28 November and the full moon on 14 November, so the darkest nights are in the days around 28 November. 3 more showers are active during the month without peaking in it; they add a few meteors an hour to the sky.

All peaks are for 2027 only: the date of a shower's peak moves by a day or so from year to year, and the moon is different every year. Other months: January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, December.
