About this list

This list follows the Working List of Visual Meteor Showers of the International Meteor Organization for 2027. The big three are the Quadrantids in January, the Perseids in August and the Geminids in December, and the Eta Aquariids in May and the Orionids in October are well known. For each shower you can see the peak date, the ZHR, the radiant position and how much of the moon is lit on the peak night, with our own note on whether the sky will be dark.

2027 highlights

The strongest shower is the Geminids with up to 150 meteors an hour, peaking on 14 December with the moon full moon. The Perseids peak on 13 August with the moon waxing gibbous.
