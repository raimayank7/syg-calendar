Reading the table

In the 41 years of the table both Easters fall on the same day in 12 of them: 2025, 2028, 2031, 2034, 2037, 2038, 2041, 2045, 2048, 2052, 2055, 2058. In the other years Orthodox Easter is later, by 7, 28, 35 days, never earlier.

Earliest and latest

Between 2020 and 2060 the earliest Western Easter is Sunday, 25 March 2035 and the latest is Sunday, 25 April 2038. Western Easter can fall anywhere from 22 March to 25 April; Orthodox Easter, converted to the Gregorian calendar, falls from 4 April to 8 May in the years 1900 to 2099.
