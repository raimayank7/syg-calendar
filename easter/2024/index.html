Easter in 2024

Western Easter in 2024 is on Sunday, 31 March 2024, which is 30 days after the start of March, in the early part of the range from 22 March to 25 April. Orthodox Easter is on Sunday, 5 May 2024. Orthodox Easter is 35 days after Western Easter this year; the next year when they fall together is 2025.

Days around Easter

Lent before Western Easter begins on Ash Wednesday, Wednesday, 14 February 2024, 46 days before Easter, which makes 40 fasting days if Sundays are not counted. Good Friday is Friday, 29 March 2024. Pentecost, seven weeks after Easter, is Sunday, 19 May 2024. In the Orthodox calendar Great Lent starts on Clean Monday, Monday, 18 March 2024, 48 days before Easter, and Good Friday is Friday, 3 May 2024.

Planning notes

Many countries give a holiday on Good Friday (29 March) and on Easter Monday (1 April). Schools and businesses in those countries often close for the days between. Because Easter moves, so do these holidays, and Western Easter shifts by up to five weeks from one year to the next, so check the year before booking travel or exams.
