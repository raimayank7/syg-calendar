About Easter dates

Easter is a moveable feast. It always falls on a Sunday between 22 March and 25 April in the Western churches, and the dates of Lent, Good Friday and Pentecost move with it. The Orthodox churches use the Julian calendar to find Easter, so their date is often later. This site gives both, for every year from 1900 to 2099 in the tool and with a page for each year from 2020 to 2060.

What you get

For each year you see Easter Sunday by both reckonings and the days that depend on it: Ash Wednesday (46 days before Western Easter), Good Friday (two days before), Pentecost (49 days after) and Clean Monday (48 days before Orthodox Easter). The guides explain the rule, the computus and why two dates exist.
