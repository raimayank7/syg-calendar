Reading the table

The two dates in the table are close to the longest and the shortest day of the year in the northern hemisphere; in the southern hemisphere it is the other way round. They are spread over 2 time zones: Asia/Shanghai, Asia/Urumqi.

On 21 June, the longest day among these cities is in Daqing (15 h 50 min) and the shortest in Haikou (13 h 21 min), a spread of 150 minutes. On 21 December the longest is in Haikou (10 h 55 min) and the shortest in Daqing (8 h 34 min), a spread of 141 minutes. The spread comes from latitude: places further from the equator have a bigger change in daylight during the year.

The northernmost of these cities is Daqing at 46.6° and the southernmost Haikou at 20.0°, a difference of 26.5 degrees of latitude (about 2947 km). Each degree of latitude changes the length of the longest day by a few minutes in temperate regions, which is why the two ends of a long country do not share the same daylight.
