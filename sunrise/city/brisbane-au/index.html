Sunrise and sunset in Brisbane through 2027

Brisbane lies at 27.47°S, 153.03°E, in the time zone Australia/Brisbane (UTC+10 in January). Using GeoNames, it has about 2,780,063 people. All times on this page are local clock times.

The longest and shortest day

The longest day of 2027 in Brisbane is 22 December, with 13 h 53 min of daylight (sunrise 04:50, sunset 18:43). The shortest is 22 June, with 10 h 24 min (sunrise 06:38, sunset 17:02). The difference between the two is 3 h 29 min, which is a moderate swing over a year.

Earliest and latest sunrise and sunset

The earliest sunrise is at 04:45 on 2 December and the latest at 06:39 on 3 July. The earliest sunset is at 17:00 on 10 June and the latest at 18:48 on 11 January. These dates are not the solstices. Because the sun runs slightly ahead of and behind the clock during the year (the equation of time), the earliest sunset comes some days before the shortest day, and the latest sunrise some days after it, in the same way in both hemispheres.

Daylight changes fastest around the equinoxes: at most about 99 seconds from one day to the next in Brisbane, around 14 March. Around the solstices it hardly changes at all.

No daylight saving time

The time zone Australia/Brisbane has no clock changes in 2027, so sunrise and sunset glide through the year without a sudden jump. If you compare sunrise with a place that does use daylight saving time, remember the hour is theirs, not the sun's.

Light before sunrise and after sunset

Civil twilight, when the sun is up to six degrees below the horizon and it is still light enough to walk and read outside, begins at 06:12 on 21 June and at 04:23 on 21 December, and ends at 17:27 and 19:09. On 21 June twilight adds 0 h 26 min of light before sunrise and 0 h 26 min after sunset; on 21 December 0 h 27 min and 0 h 27 min. Solar noon, when the sun is highest, is at 11:50 on 21 June and at 11:46 on 21 December, not exactly at 12:00 because Brisbane lies east of the centre of its time zone.

Compared with Canberra

On 21 June daylight in Brisbane lasts 10 h 24 min, which is 38 minutes longer than the 9 h 47 min in Canberra; on 21 December it is 13 h 53 min against 14 h 33 min. Sunrise on 21 June is at 06:37 here and 07:12 there, because the two places differ in longitude and in latitude (-27.5° against -35.3°).
