Sunrise and sunset in Longyearbyen through 2027

Longyearbyen lies at 78.22°N, 15.65°E, in the time zone Arctic/Longyearbyen (UTC+1 in January and UTC+2 in July). Using GeoNames, it has about 2,368 people. All times on this page are local clock times.

This is within reach of the polar regions: the sun stays above the horizon all day on 129 days and stays below it all day on 112 days in 2027. On the other days the longest day lasts 22 h 46 min on 25 August.

The longest and shortest day

The longest day of 2027 in Longyearbyen is 25 August, with 22 h 46 min of daylight (sunrise 01:16, sunset 00:02). The shortest is 26 October, with 1 h 19 min (sunrise 12:01, sunset 13:20). The difference between the two is 21 h 27 min, which is a large swing over a year.

Earliest and latest sunrise and sunset

The earliest sunrise is at 01:16 on 25 August and the latest at 12:01 on 26 October. The earliest sunset is at 00:02 on 25 August and the latest at 23:39 on 16 April. These dates are not the solstices. Because the sun runs slightly ahead of and behind the clock during the year (the equation of time), the earliest sunset comes some days before the shortest day, and the latest sunrise some days after it, in the same way in both hemispheres.

Daylight changes fastest around the equinoxes: at most about 3968 seconds from one day to the next in Longyearbyen, around 25 August. Around the solstices it hardly changes at all.

Daylight saving time

In 2027 the clocks change in this time zone on 28 March (clocks forward 60 minutes) and 31 October (clocks back 60 minutes). The table below uses the clock time that applies on each day, so you will see sunrise and sunset jump by the change that day. Sunrise and sunset are not different for the sun; only the clock changes.
