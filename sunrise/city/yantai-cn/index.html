Sunrise and sunset in Yantai through 2027

Yantai lies at 37.48°N, 121.44°E, in the time zone Asia/Shanghai (UTC+8 in January). Using GeoNames, it has about 7,102,100 people. All times on this page are local clock times.

The longest and shortest day

The longest day of 2027 in Yantai is 21 June, with 14 h 45 min of daylight (sunrise 04:33, sunset 19:19). The shortest is 22 December, with 9 h 35 min (sunrise 07:05, sunset 16:40). The difference between the two is 5 h 11 min, which is a moderate swing over a year.

Earliest and latest sunrise and sunset

The earliest sunrise is at 04:33 on 14 June and the latest at 07:09 on 6 January. The earliest sunset is at 16:36 on 7 December and the latest at 19:19 on 29 June. These dates are not the solstices. Because the sun runs slightly ahead of and behind the clock during the year (the equation of time), the earliest sunset comes some days before the shortest day, and the latest sunrise some days after it, in the same way in both hemispheres.

Daylight changes fastest around the equinoxes: at most about 145 seconds from one day to the next in Yantai, around 18 March. Around the solstices it hardly changes at all.

No daylight saving time

The time zone Asia/Shanghai has no clock changes in 2027, so sunrise and sunset glide through the year without a sudden jump. If you compare sunrise with a place that does use daylight saving time, remember the hour is theirs, not the sun's.

Light before sunrise and after sunset

Civil twilight, when the sun is up to six degrees below the horizon and it is still light enough to walk and read outside, begins at 04:02 on 21 June and at 06:35 on 21 December, and ends at 19:50 and 17:09. On 21 June twilight adds 0 h 31 min of light before sunrise and 0 h 31 min after sunset; on 21 December 0 h 29 min and 0 h 29 min. Solar noon, when the sun is highest, is at 11:56 on 21 June and at 11:52 on 21 December, not exactly at 12:00 because Yantai lies east of the centre of its time zone.

Compared with Beijing

On 21 June daylight in Yantai lasts 14 h 45 min, which is 15 minutes shorter than the 15 h 00 min in Beijing; on 21 December it is 9 h 35 min against 9 h 20 min. Sunrise on 21 June is at 04:33 here and 04:46 there, because the two places differ in longitude and in latitude (37.5° against 39.9°).
