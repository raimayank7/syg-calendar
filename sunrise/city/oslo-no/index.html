Sunrise and sunset in Oslo through 2027

Oslo lies at 59.91°N, 10.75°E, in the time zone Europe/Oslo (UTC+1 in January and UTC+2 in July). Using GeoNames, it has about 1,082,575 people. All times on this page are local clock times.

The longest and shortest day

The longest day of 2027 in Oslo is 21 June, with 18 h 50 min of daylight (sunrise 03:54, sunset 22:44). The shortest is 22 December, with 5 h 54 min (sunrise 09:18, sunset 15:12). The difference between the two is 12 h 56 min, which is a large swing over a year.

Earliest and latest sunrise and sunset

The earliest sunrise is at 03:54 on 19 June and the latest at 09:20 on 27 December. The earliest sunset is at 15:11 on 16 December and the latest at 22:44 on 23 June. These dates are not the solstices. Because the sun runs slightly ahead of and behind the clock during the year (the equation of time), the earliest sunset comes some days before the shortest day, and the latest sunrise some days after it, in the same way in both hemispheres.

Daylight changes fastest around the equinoxes: at most about 327 seconds from one day to the next in Oslo, around 16 March. Around the solstices it hardly changes at all.

Daylight saving time

In 2027 the clocks change in this time zone on 28 March (clocks forward 60 minutes) and 31 October (clocks back 60 minutes). The table below uses the clock time that applies on each day, so you will see sunrise and sunset jump by the change that day. Sunrise and sunset are not different for the sun; only the clock changes.

Light before sunrise and after sunset

Civil twilight, when the sun is up to six degrees below the horizon and it is still light enough to walk and read outside, begins at 02:10 on 21 June and at 08:20 on 21 December, and ends at 00:28 and 16:09. On 21 June twilight adds 1 h 44 min of light before sunrise and 1 h 44 min after sunset; on 21 December 0 h 58 min and 0 h 58 min. Solar noon, when the sun is highest, is at 13:19 on 21 June and at 12:15 on 21 December, not exactly at 12:00 because Oslo lies west of the centre of its time zone.
