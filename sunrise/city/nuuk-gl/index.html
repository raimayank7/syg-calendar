Sunrise and sunset in Nuuk through 2027

Nuuk lies at 64.18°N, 51.72°W, in the time zone America/Nuuk (UTC-2 in January and UTC-1 in July). Using GeoNames, it has about 14,798 people. All times on this page are local clock times.

The longest and shortest day

The longest day of 2027 in Nuuk is 21 June, with 21 h 11 min of daylight (sunrise 03:53, sunset 01:04). The shortest is 21 December, with 4 h 06 min (sunrise 11:22, sunset 15:28). The difference between the two is 17 h 05 min, which is a large swing over a year.

Earliest and latest sunrise and sunset

The earliest sunrise is at 03:53 on 20 June and the latest at 11:23 on 25 December. The earliest sunset is at 00:01 on 23 July and the latest at 23:59 on 22 May. These dates are not the solstices. Because the sun runs slightly ahead of and behind the clock during the year (the equation of time), the earliest sunset comes some days before the shortest day, and the latest sunrise some days after it, in the same way in both hemispheres.

Daylight changes fastest around the equinoxes: at most about 401 seconds from one day to the next in Nuuk, around 30 April. Around the solstices it hardly changes at all.

Daylight saving time

In 2027 the clocks change in this time zone on 28 March (clocks forward 60 minutes) and 31 October (clocks back 60 minutes). The table below uses the clock time that applies on each day, so you will see sunrise and sunset jump by the change that day. Sunrise and sunset are not different for the sun; only the clock changes.

Solar noon is at 14:29 on 21 June and at 13:25 on 21 December.
