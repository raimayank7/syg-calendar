Sunrise and sunset in Lisbon through 2027

Lisbon lies at 38.73°N, 9.15°W, in the time zone Europe/Lisbon (UTC+0 in January and UTC+1 in July). Using GeoNames, it has about 517,802 people. All times on this page are local clock times.

The longest and shortest day

The longest day of 2027 in Lisbon is 21 June, with 14 h 53 min of daylight (sunrise 06:12, sunset 21:05). The shortest is 22 December, with 9 h 27 min (sunrise 07:51, sunset 17:19). The difference between the two is 5 h 25 min, which is a moderate swing over a year.

Earliest and latest sunrise and sunset

The earliest sunrise is at 06:11 on 14 June and the latest at 08:01 on 30 October. The earliest sunset is at 17:15 on 7 December and the latest at 21:06 on 28 June. These dates are not the solstices. Because the sun runs slightly ahead of and behind the clock during the year (the equation of time), the earliest sunset comes some days before the shortest day, and the latest sunrise some days after it, in the same way in both hemispheres.

Daylight changes fastest around the equinoxes: at most about 152 seconds from one day to the next in Lisbon, around 18 March. Around the solstices it hardly changes at all.

Daylight saving time

In 2027 the clocks change in this time zone on 28 March (clocks forward 60 minutes) and 31 October (clocks back 60 minutes). The table below uses the clock time that applies on each day, so you will see sunrise and sunset jump by the change that day. Sunrise and sunset are not different for the sun; only the clock changes.

Light before sunrise and after sunset

Civil twilight, when the sun is up to six degrees below the horizon and it is still light enough to walk and read outside, begins at 05:40 on 21 June and at 07:21 on 21 December, and ends at 21:37 and 17:48. On 21 June twilight adds 0 h 32 min of light before sunrise and 0 h 32 min after sunset; on 21 December 0 h 30 min and 0 h 30 min. Solar noon, when the sun is highest, is at 13:38 on 21 June and at 12:35 on 21 December, not exactly at 12:00 because Lisbon lies west of the centre of its time zone.
