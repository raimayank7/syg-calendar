Sunrise and sunset in Reykjavik through 2027

Reykjavik lies at 64.14°N, 21.90°W, in the time zone Atlantic/Reykjavik (UTC+0 in January). Using GeoNames, it has about 118,918 people. All times on this page are local clock times.

The longest and shortest day

The longest day of 2027 in Reykjavik is 21 June, with 21 h 08 min of daylight (sunrise 02:55, sunset 00:04). The shortest is 22 December, with 4 h 07 min (sunrise 11:22, sunset 15:30). The difference between the two is 17 h 01 min, which is a large swing over a year.

Earliest and latest sunrise and sunset

The earliest sunrise is at 02:55 on 20 June and the latest at 11:23 on 25 December. The earliest sunset is at 00:00 on 28 June and the latest at 23:59 on 15 June. These dates are not the solstices. Because the sun runs slightly ahead of and behind the clock during the year (the equation of time), the earliest sunset comes some days before the shortest day, and the latest sunrise some days after it, in the same way in both hemispheres.

Daylight changes fastest around the equinoxes: at most about 400 seconds from one day to the next in Reykjavik, around 29 April. Around the solstices it hardly changes at all.

No daylight saving time

The time zone Atlantic/Reykjavik has no clock changes in 2027, so sunrise and sunset glide through the year without a sudden jump. If you compare sunrise with a place that does use daylight saving time, remember the hour is theirs, not the sun's.

Solar noon is at 13:29 on 21 June and at 13:26 on 21 December.
