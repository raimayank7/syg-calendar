Sunrise and sunset in Saint Petersburg through 2027

Saint Petersburg lies at 59.94°N, 30.31°E, in the time zone Europe/Moscow (UTC+3 in January). Using GeoNames, it has about 5,351,935 people. All times on this page are local clock times.

The longest and shortest day

The longest day of 2027 in Saint Petersburg is 21 June, with 18 h 51 min of daylight (sunrise 03:35, sunset 22:26). The shortest is 22 December, with 5 h 53 min (sunrise 10:00, sunset 15:54). The difference between the two is 12 h 57 min, which is a large swing over a year.

Earliest and latest sunrise and sunset

The earliest sunrise is at 03:35 on 19 June and the latest at 10:02 on 27 December. The earliest sunset is at 15:52 on 16 December and the latest at 22:26 on 23 June. These dates are not the solstices. Because the sun runs slightly ahead of and behind the clock during the year (the equation of time), the earliest sunset comes some days before the shortest day, and the latest sunrise some days after it, in the same way in both hemispheres.

Daylight changes fastest around the equinoxes: at most about 328 seconds from one day to the next in Saint Petersburg, around 16 March. Around the solstices it hardly changes at all.

No daylight saving time

The time zone Europe/Moscow has no clock changes in 2027, so sunrise and sunset glide through the year without a sudden jump. If you compare sunrise with a place that does use daylight saving time, remember the hour is theirs, not the sun's.

Light before sunrise and after sunset

Civil twilight, when the sun is up to six degrees below the horizon and it is still light enough to walk and read outside, begins at 01:50 on 21 June and at 09:02 on 21 December, and ends at 00:11 and 16:51. On 21 June twilight adds 1 h 45 min of light before sunrise and 1 h 45 min after sunset; on 21 December 0 h 58 min and 0 h 58 min. Solar noon, when the sun is highest, is at 13:00 on 21 June and at 12:57 on 21 December, not exactly at 12:00 because Saint Petersburg lies west of the centre of its time zone.

Compared with Moscow

On 21 June daylight in Saint Petersburg lasts 18 h 51 min, which is 77 minutes longer than the 17 h 34 min in Moscow; on 21 December it is 5 h 53 min against 7 h 00 min. Sunrise on 21 June is at 03:35 here and 03:45 there, because the two places differ in longitude and in latitude (59.9° against 55.8°).
