What the times mean

  • Sunrise and sunset: the moment the top edge of the sun is at the horizon, with the usual allowance for the bending of light.
  • First light and last light: civil twilight, the sun 6° below the horizon; light enough to see outdoors.
  • Solar noon: when the sun is highest, which is rarely exactly 12:00 on the clock.
  • Day length: sunset minus sunrise.

Questions people ask

How do I find sunrise and sunset for my city?

Type the city in the box on the home page, choose it, and the times for today appear. Choose another date with the date box, or use your location.

How accurate are the times?

Within about a minute of the official data of the U.S. Naval Observatory in our checks, for places below 72° latitude. The horizon at your place and the weather can add minutes.

Why is the earliest sunset before the shortest day?

Because the sun runs ahead of the clock in December, a difference called the equation of time. See the guide on this site.

Does it include daylight saving time?

Yes. The tables use the clock time in force on each day, so they jump when clocks change.

Computed, not measured: hills, buildings and weather change what you see. Not a source of prayer times or religious rulings. Checked November 2026.
