2021 in weeks

2021 has 52 ISO weeks. Week 1 starts on Monday, 4 January 2021 and week 52 ends on Sunday, 2 January 2022. 1 January 2021 is a Friday and falls in ISO week 53 of 2020; 31 December is a Friday and falls in ISO week 52 of 2021. A year has 53 weeks only when 1 January is a Thursday, or in a leap year when 1 January is a Wednesday; 2021 is not, so it has 52 weeks. The next 53-week year is 2026.

Weeks that cross the new year

The first days of January belong to week 53 of 2020 when the week has fewer than four days in the new year, and the last days of December belong to week 1 of 2022 when the week has at least four days in it. In 2021, January 1 is in week 53 of 2020 (the last week of the year before) and December 31 is in week 52 of 2021.

ISO and US weeks

The right-hand column shows the US-style number. In 2021 the first week by the US rule is the week of 27 Dec, and by the ISO rule week 1 is the week of 4 Jan, so the two numberings differ by up to one week during the year and are sometimes the same. The guide explains the difference.
