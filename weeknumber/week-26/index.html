When is week 26?

ISO week 26 falls in June in most years: its Monday is as early as 21 Jun and as late as 28 Jun in the years of the table, depending on how the weekdays line up with the dates. Week 26 exists in every year.

Day of the year and quarter

The Monday of week 26 is between day 173 and day 179 of the year, which puts it in quarter 2. Week 26 ends on a Sunday between 27 Jun and 4 Jul. The last column shows the US-style week number (Sunday start, week 1 holds 1 January) of the Thursday of the same week; it is 26 or 27, so a team that numbers weeks the US way says "week 26 or 27" for the days that ISO calls week 26.

Using week 26 in planning

When someone asks for a delivery, a leave or a payroll run "in week 26", write the dates as well as the number, because the ISO number and the fiscal week of a company can differ, and because the week with this number sits on different dates every year, from 21 Jun to 28 Jun in the table. Check the year first, since week numbers repeat every year.

Every ISO week contains a Thursday of its own year, which is why the rule gives the week to the year that holds that Thursday. Open a year to see all its weeks: 2026 2027 2028. This week and week finder.
