2034 in weeks

2034 has 52 ISO weeks. Week 1 starts on Monday, 2 January 2034 and week 52 ends on Sunday, 31 December 2034. 1 January 2034 is a Sunday and falls in ISO week 52 of 2033; 31 December is a Sunday and falls in ISO week 52 of 2034. A year has 53 weeks only when 1 January is a Thursday, or in a leap year when 1 January is a Wednesday; 2034 is not, so it has 52 weeks. The next 53-week year is 2037.

Weeks that cross the new year

The first days of January belong to week 52 of 2033 when the week has fewer than four days in the new year, and the last days of December belong to week 1 of 2035 when the week has at least four days in it. In 2034, January 1 is in week 52 of 2033 (the last week of the year before) and December 31 is in week 52 of 2034.

ISO and US weeks

The right-hand column shows the US-style number. In 2034 the first week by the US rule is the week of 1 Jan, and by the ISO rule week 1 is the week of 2 Jan, so the two numberings differ by up to one week during the year and are sometimes the same. The guide explains the difference.
