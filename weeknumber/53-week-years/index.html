The rule

A year has 53 ISO weeks if 1 January is a Thursday, or if it is a leap year in which 1 January is a Wednesday. Otherwise it has 52. In the 111 years from 1990 to 2100 there are 20 such years; they come every 5 or 6 years (the gaps in this range are 5, 6 years).

Why it matters

Payroll, retail and factory calendars that count in weeks need to know when a 53rd week arrives: it adds a week of work and sales to the year, and week-on-week comparisons with the previous year shift by a week. Companies that plan in 4-4-5 style fiscal calendars must decide where the extra week goes, and the rules differ from company to company.
