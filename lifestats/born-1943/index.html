Born in 1943

Someone born in 1943 turns 84 in 2027 on their birthday and is 83 until then. A person born in the middle of 1943, on 1 July, has lived about 30,500 days by 1 January 2027, or about 4,357 weeks. In those years 21 leap years have passed, each adding a day, and 1 January 1943 was a Friday.

Chinese zodiac for 1943

The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not 1 January. Lunar New Year in 1943 was on Friday, 5 February 1943, so people born from that date to Monday, 24 January 1944 are Goat, while those born earlier in the year belong to the year of the Horse. The twelve animals repeat every twelve years: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig.
