How to use the converter

  1. For a Gregorian date, choose it in the left box. The Hijri date appears under it with the weekday, the month name and its Arabic spelling.
  2. For a Hijri date, type the day, choose the month and type the year in AH. The Gregorian date appears under it. A day 30 in a month with only 29 days is refused with a message.
  3. For a range of dates or a year, use the holiday list or a year page.

What the Hijri calendar is

The Hijri calendar counts months by the moon, from the Hijra of the Prophet Muhammad in 622 CE. Its twelve months are used by Muslims to find the times of fasting, festivals and the pilgrimage. Read how it works and why dates can differ by a day.

Questions people ask

What is today's Hijri date?

Open this page: the box at the top shows today\'s Hijri date from the Umm al-Qura table, worked out in your browser.

When does Ramadan start in 2027?

By the Umm al-Qura calendar, Ramadan 1448 AH begins on Monday, 8 February 2027. Countries that sight the moon may begin a day later.

Why do Ramadan and Eid start on different days in different countries?

Many countries start a month when the new crescent is sighted locally, while Saudi Arabia uses a calculated calendar. The crescent is visible earlier in the west, so a country in the east can start a day later.

How accurate is the converter?

It follows the Umm al-Qura table for 1356 to 1500 AH. It is exact for that table, but it cannot say what a moon-sighting authority will announce.
