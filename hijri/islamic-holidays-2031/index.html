Hijri Calendar › Holidays

Dates can differ by a day or more. Islamic months begin with the sighting of the new moon, and countries and religious authorities decide differently, so Ramadan, Eid and other dates may be observed one day (sometimes two) later or earlier than shown. The dates on this site are calculated by the Umm al-Qura method, the official civil calendar of Saudi Arabia (King Abdulaziz City for Science and Technology, ummulqura.org.sa). They are an estimate for planning, not an announcement: wait for your own authority's announcement.

Umm al-Qura calendar. The Islamic day begins at sunset, so nights such as Laylat al-Qadr and Shab-e-Barat begin on the evening before the date shown. Moon sighting can move the date in your country by a day.

Which Hijri years 2031 covers

2031 runs from 7 Ramadan 1452 AH to 16 Ramadan 1453 AH. A Hijri year is 354 or 355 days, so the Gregorian year contains one complete Hijri year and parts of two. That is why Muharram, Safar and a few other months fall twice in some Gregorian years, and why an observance sometimes occurs twice in the same year, once near its beginning and once near its end.

In 2031 the fast runs from 16 December to 23 January, which is -326 days before Eid. The month has 29 days in the Umm al-Qura table.

How to use this list

  • For work and school, the government of your country announces which days are holidays, usually a few days before; this list is for planning only.
  • If a date is within a day of the weekend, expect that a one-day change after the moon sighting may lead to a long weekend or none.
  • For travel to Muslim countries during Ramadan and Eid, prices and opening hours change; check ahead.

Ramadan and the two Eids in 1453 AH

Ramadan begins on Tuesday, 16 December 2031, a Tuesday, and lasts 29 days, so Eid al-Fitr is on Wednesday, 14 January 2032. That is -355 days after the start of Ramadan of the previous year (Thursday, 26 December 2030), the usual shift of ten or eleven days. It falls in December in the northern hemisphere, so the fast runs through the hours of shorter daylight there; in the southern hemisphere the season is the opposite.

Eid al-Adha comes 68 days after Eid al-Fitr, on Monday, 22 March 2032, with the Day of Arafat the day before, on Sunday, 21 March 2032. The weekday of an Eid decides whether the holiday joins a weekend: Eid al-Fitr is on a Wednesday and Eid al-Adha on a Monday this year.

The rest of the year

The Islamic year opens on Wednesday, 23 April 2031. Ashura follows on Friday, 2 May 2031, nine days later. Mawlid is on Wednesday, 2 July 2031, 70 days after the new year; Isra and Mi’raj is on Thursday, 13 November 2031 and the middle of Sha’ban on Sunday, 30 November 2031. From Isra and Mi’raj to the first day of Ramadan is 33 days, and from the first day of Ramadan to the Day of Arafat 96 days.

Ashura fall on a Friday in 1453 AH. Friday is the day of the weekly congregational prayer, so a festival on Friday joins two gatherings.
