Hijri Calendar › Months

Dates can differ by a day or more. Islamic months begin with the sighting of the new moon, and countries and religious authorities decide differently, so Ramadan, Eid and other dates may be observed one day (sometimes two) later or earlier than shown. The dates on this site are calculated by the Umm al-Qura method, the official civil calendar of Saudi Arabia (King Abdulaziz City for Science and Technology, ummulqura.org.sa). They are an estimate for planning, not an announcement: wait for your own authority's announcement.

About the year

1457 AH has 355 days: 5 months of 29 days and 7 of 30. A Hijri year is 354 days in a common year and 355 in a leap year, and the leap day is added to Dhu al-Hijjah in the tabular calendar; the Umm al-Qura table instead sets each month from the moon. The year begins on a Sunday and ends on a Thursday, and it touches the Gregorian years 2035 and 2036.

Ramadan of 1457 AH falls from 1 November to 30 November. To convert a single day, use the converter; for holidays by Gregorian year, see the holiday list.

Moon sighting may shift each date by a day in countries that do not follow the Umm al-Qura calendar.

Ramadan and the two Eids in 1457 AH

Ramadan begins on Thursday, 1 November 2035, a Thursday, and lasts 30 days, so Eid al-Fitr is on Saturday, 1 December 2035. That is -354 days after the start of Ramadan of the previous year (Sunday, 12 November 2034), the usual shift of ten or eleven days. It falls in November in the northern hemisphere, so the fast runs through the hours of shortening daylight there; in the southern hemisphere the season is the opposite.

Eid al-Adha comes 69 days after Eid al-Fitr, on Friday, 8 February 2036, with the Day of Arafat the day before, on Thursday, 7 February 2036. A Friday Eid brings two sermons close together and is often a long weekend.

The rest of the year

The Islamic year opens on Sunday, 11 March 2035. Ashura follows on Tuesday, 20 March 2035, nine days later. Mawlid is on Sunday, 20 May 2035, 70 days after the new year; Isra and Mi’raj is on Saturday, 29 September 2035 and the middle of Sha’ban on Wednesday, 17 October 2035. From Isra and Mi’raj to the first day of Ramadan is 33 days, and from the first day of Ramadan to the Day of Arafat 98 days.

Eid al-Adha fall on a Friday in 1457 AH. Friday is the day of the weekly congregational prayer, so a festival on Friday joins two gatherings.
