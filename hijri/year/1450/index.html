Hijri Calendar › Months

Dates can differ by a day or more. Islamic months begin with the sighting of the new moon, and countries and religious authorities decide differently, so Ramadan, Eid and other dates may be observed one day (sometimes two) later or earlier than shown. The dates on this site are calculated by the Umm al-Qura method, the official civil calendar of Saudi Arabia (King Abdulaziz City for Science and Technology, ummulqura.org.sa). They are an estimate for planning, not an announcement: wait for your own authority's announcement.

About the year

1450 AH has 354 days: 6 months of 29 days and 6 of 30. A Hijri year is 354 days in a common year and 355 in a leap year, and the leap day is added to Dhu al-Hijjah in the tabular calendar; the Umm al-Qura table instead sets each month from the moon. The year begins on a Thursday and ends on a Sunday, and it touches the Gregorian years 2028 and 2029.

Ramadan of 1450 AH falls from 16 January to 13 February. To convert a single day, use the converter; for holidays by Gregorian year, see the holiday list.

Moon sighting may shift each date by a day in countries that do not follow the Umm al-Qura calendar.

Ramadan and the two Eids in 1450 AH

Ramadan begins on Tuesday, 16 January 2029, a Tuesday, and lasts 29 days, so Eid al-Fitr is on Wednesday, 14 February 2029. That is -354 days after the start of Ramadan of the previous year (Friday, 28 January 2028), the usual shift of ten or eleven days. It falls in January in the northern hemisphere, so the fast runs through the hours of shorter daylight there; in the southern hemisphere the season is the opposite.

Eid al-Adha comes 69 days after Eid al-Fitr, on Tuesday, 24 April 2029, with the Day of Arafat the day before, on Monday, 23 April 2029. The weekday of an Eid decides whether the holiday joins a weekend: Eid al-Fitr is on a Wednesday and Eid al-Adha on a Tuesday this year.

The rest of the year

The Islamic year opens on Thursday, 25 May 2028. Ashura follows on Saturday, 3 June 2028, nine days later. Mawlid is on Thursday, 3 August 2028, 70 days after the new year; Isra and Mi’raj is on Thursday, 14 December 2028 and the middle of Sha’ban on Sunday, 31 December 2028. From Isra and Mi’raj to the first day of Ramadan is 33 days, and from the first day of Ramadan to the Day of Arafat 97 days.

None of the ten main observances falls on a Friday in 1450 AH.
