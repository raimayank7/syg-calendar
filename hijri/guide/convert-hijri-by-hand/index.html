Hijri Calendar

Dates can differ by a day or more. Islamic months begin with the sighting of the new moon, and countries and religious authorities decide differently, so Ramadan, Eid and other dates may be observed one day (sometimes two) later or earlier than shown. The dates on this site are calculated by the Umm al-Qura method, the official civil calendar of Saudi Arabia (King Abdulaziz City for Science and Technology, ummulqura.org.sa). They are an estimate for planning, not an announcement: wait for your own authority's announcement.

Convert a Hijri Date by Hand

A formula for converting between Gregorian and Hijri dates with pencil and paper, how accurate it is, and why it can be a day out.

The tabular method

For a quick answer without a table, you can use the arithmetic (tabular) Islamic calendar. It treats the months as alternately 30 and 29 days and adds a leap day to the last month in 11 of every 30 years. A Hijri date is turned into a day count, and that count is turned into a Gregorian date. The same method is the one most programs use when no table is available.

Day number of a Hijri date

Count the days since the start of year 1: 354 days for each past year, plus 11 leap days in each 30-year cycle (the years numbered 2, 5, 7, 10, 13, 16, 18, 21, 24, 26 and 29 in the cycle), plus the days in the months already complete this year (30 for odd months, 29 for even months), plus the day of the month. Add the day number of the start of the calendar, the Gregorian date of 16 July 622 in the Julian calendar, to get a Gregorian date.

How good is it?

The tabular calendar is within one day of the Umm al-Qura calendar most of the time but not always, and it can be out by two days against observed months. We use the Umm al-Qura table on this site instead, because it matches the dates used in Saudi Arabia, and we say so on every page. Use the tabular method only for a rough check.

A worked check

Open the converter and enter 1 Ramadan 1448 to see the Umm al-Qura date, then work the same date out by hand. If your answer is within a day, you have done it right.
