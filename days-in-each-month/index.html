How many days are in each month?

Seven months have 31 days, four have 30, and February has 28, or 29 in a leap year.

In a leap year, add 1 to every start number from March onward. The total is 365 days, or 366 in a leap year.

The knuckle trick

Make a fist and look at the back of your hand. The four knuckles stick up and the three gaps between them dip down.

  1. Start on the first knuckle (the one of the index finger) and say "January". A knuckle means 31 days.
  2. Move into the dip beside it and say "February". A dip means a short month: 30 days, or 28 or 29 for February.
  3. Carry on: knuckle March (31), dip April (30), knuckle May (31), dip June (30), knuckle July (31).
  4. You have reached the last knuckle. Start again on the first knuckle for August (31), then dip September (30), knuckle October (31), dip November (30), knuckle December (31).

July and August both land on knuckles, and both have 31 days. That is the only place where two long months sit side by side, apart from December and January across the new year.

The rhyme

An old English rhyme does the same job:

*Thirty days have September, April, June and November. All the rest have thirty-one, except February alone, which has twenty-eight days clear, and twenty-nine in each leap year.*

In short: the four 30-day months are April, June, September and November. Everything else except February is 31.

Why February is short

The answer goes back to early Rome. The oldest Roman calendar had ten months beginning in March and simply did not count the dead of winter. Around 700 BCE, tradition says, the months of January and February were added to cover that gap, and February, the last month of the year at that time, was left with the fewest days. When Julius Caesar reformed the calendar in 46 BCE he lengthened several months to bring the year to 365 days, but February kept 28, with a 29th day every fourth year.

A popular story says the emperor Augustus stole a day from February so that his month, August, would be as long as Julius Caesar's July. Historians do not accept it: the month lengths we use were already fixed in Caesar's reform, before the month was renamed after Augustus.

The second half of the year is always longer than the first. The middle moment of an ordinary year is noon on 2 July.

For the Indian financial year, April to March: the first quarter (April to June) has 91 days, the second and third 92 each, and the fourth (January to March) 90, or 91 when it contains a 29 February.

Useful to know

  • Weeks in a month: every month has 4 full weeks. February in an ordinary year has exactly 4 weeks; all other months have 2 or 3 days more.
  • Five of a weekday: a 31-day month has three weekdays that occur five times, a 30-day month has two, and a 29-day February has one. That is why some months have five Sundays, or five salary Fridays.
  • Monthly rent or salary by the day: dividing by 30 is a common shortcut, but offices differ. Some divide by the actual days in the month, some by 30, some by 26 working days. Follow your contract.
Which months have 30 days?

April, June, September and November.

Which month has 28 days?

All of them have at least 28. Only February stops there, in ordinary years.

How many months have 31 days?

Seven: January, March, May, July, August, October and December.
