Posting about women, girls and families

Avoid cliches such as "behind every great man" and avoid tokenism: if you post a day for women, show that you work for women on the other days too. Check helpline numbers before posting.

Ideas that work on any of these days

  • Celebrate one woman or girl you know and say exactly what she did: a skill, a decision, a risk she took. Ask her first.
  • Share three facts about the day from a named source (a UN agency, a census or your ministry) with the year.
  • Offer something useful: a free session, a mentoring slot, a list of scholarships or helplines. A gesture is stronger than a greeting.
  • Show the women in your team or community at work, not only on the day: a photo, a role and one achievement each.
  • Start a "she taught me" thread: ask followers to name the one lesson they got from a woman in their life.
