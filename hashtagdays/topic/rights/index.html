Posting about human rights, law and justice

These days concern people who have been harmed. Use respectful, factual words; avoid graphic images; do not name victims without permission; and be ready to moderate comments. Do not give legal advice, but point to an official or legal-aid source.

Ideas that work on any of these days

  • Share the plain-language text of one right named by the day, from the United Nations or your national constitution, with a link.
  • Tell a short story of a person or group helped by this work, with their agreement, instead of a general slogan.
  • List where to get help locally: a helpline, legal aid centre or commission. Check each number is current before you post it.
  • Thank a person who works on the day: a lawyer, a social worker, a journalist. Name them only with consent.
  • Ask a question that invites listening: what would make your neighbourhood fairer? Moderate the replies and remove abuse.
