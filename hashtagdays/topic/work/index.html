Posting about work, business and economy

Keep promotions honest and clear. Do not claim savings or returns you cannot prove, and do not give financial, tax or legal advice in a caption: link to the official source.

Ideas that work on any of these days

  • Thank your team in a specific way: name three people and one thing each did this month.
  • Share one lesson from your own work that relates to the day, in the first person and in under 100 words.
  • Post a behind-the-scenes photo or a short video of how your product or service is made.
  • Offer something free and useful for the day: a template, a checklist, a short consultation.
  • Ask customers for one story of how you helped them, and share it with their permission.
