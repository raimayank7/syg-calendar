Posting about children, youth and education

Children's privacy matters most: no faces without consent, no names of schools with children's photos, no location tags. Do not exaggerate numbers and link to the source of every figure.

Ideas that work on any of these days

  • Post a photo or drawing by a child, with a parent's permission and no full name or school.
  • Share a simple activity families can do in ten minutes for the day: read aloud, a counting game, a walk, a drawing.
  • Say thanks to a teacher or mentor and name what they did, in one concrete sentence.
  • Post a "did you know" about the day with a figure from a named source, such as UNICEF, UNESCO or the ministry of education.
  • Start a reading or learning challenge with the hashtag and give a simple goal: one page, one chapter, one new word.
