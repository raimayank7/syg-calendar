Posting about general awareness

Check every fact before you post and link to the official page of the body that leads the day.

Ideas that work on any of these days

  • Explain the day in two or three plain sentences and link to the body that leads it.
  • Share a photo from your own place that fits the day and say why you chose it.
  • Ask followers one open question about the day, and answer a few replies.
  • Thank someone who works on this topic in your area, with permission.
  • Post one useful link or number for the day, checked this week.
