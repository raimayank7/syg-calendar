Posting about peace, security and cooperation

These topics are tense. Use calm, factual words; avoid taking sides in a live conflict unless you are an organisation that does so on principle; do not share graphic images; and check every claim before posting.

Ideas that work on any of these days

  • Share one act of kindness between neighbours or communities from your own town, with consent and a photo.
  • Post a short quote from a peace worker or a survivor, with credit and the date it was said.
  • Explain what the day means in plain words, and why the United Nations or its agency set it aside.
  • Ask followers to name a person who brings people together in your area and why.
  • Share a reading or film list on the day, five items with one line each.
