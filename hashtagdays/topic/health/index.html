Posting about health and medicine

Health posts need care. Do not give diagnoses, doses or cures; do not share a claim you cannot trace to a health authority; and link to the official page. Some days concern illnesses that people live with, so use respectful words and avoid pity, blame or fear.

Ideas that work on any of these days

  • Post one fact about the day from the website of the health body that leads it (the World Health Organization or your national health ministry), with a link, rather than a fact you cannot source.
  • Share a simple habit that fits the day and costs nothing: a walk, a check-up, hand washing, a glass of water. Keep it practical and kind.
  • Ask your followers to tell the check-up or screening they keep putting off; reply to a few comments with the next step and where to get it.
  • Thank the people who work on the day: nurses, community health workers, pharmacists, volunteers. A short photo or quote works well.
  • Make a "myth or fact" carousel for the day: three common beliefs and the official answer, each with the source in small print.
