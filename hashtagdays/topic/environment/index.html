Posting about environment and nature

Environmental claims must be accurate. Use numbers from a named source with a year, avoid "saving the planet" claims for a small product, and do not post photos that show people or animals in distress without a good reason.

Ideas that work on any of these days

  • Post one photo of nature near you with a sentence about the day. A real local picture does better than stock art.
  • Share one change anyone can make this week for the day: switch off a light, carry a bag, plant a seed, fix a leaking tap. Name the exact action.
  • Run a small challenge for the day: count the number of single-use items you use and post the total. Ask others to join with the hashtag.
  • Tag a local group that works on the day and ask what help they need. Volunteers and donations are better than slogans.
  • Make a before-and-after post of a clean-up, planting or saving drive, with numbers: bags, kilos, trees, hours.
