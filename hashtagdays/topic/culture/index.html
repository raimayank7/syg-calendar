Posting about culture, language and arts

When you share a poem, song or picture by someone else, get permission or use a work in the public domain. Be careful with religious and cultural symbols: describe, do not mock.

Ideas that work on any of these days

  • Share a line, a rhyme, a recipe or a song in your own language and give a translation in the caption.
  • Post a photo of a place or object of cultural value near you and say what it is and who looks after it.
  • Ask your followers for a word from their language or dialect that has no good translation. Collect the answers in a post.
  • Interview an elder, artist or teacher in two or three questions and post a quote with a photo, with their permission.
  • For a library, shop or school: set up a small display or reading corner for the day and post a photo.
