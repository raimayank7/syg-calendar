Posting about food, farming and nutrition

Check food and health claims. Do not say a food cures or prevents a disease, mention allergens where relevant, and credit the cook or the farmer in photos.

Ideas that work on any of these days

  • Post a photo of a dish or a crop connected to the day, with a few words on where it was grown or who made it.
  • Share a simple recipe that fits the day with the cost of the ingredients and the time it takes.
  • Thank a farmer, a cook or a shopkeeper who feeds your neighbourhood, and name one thing they did.
  • Tell followers one way to waste less food this week, such as a use for leftovers, with a clear step.
  • Ask followers for their family recipe and gather the replies into a post.
