Posting about science, technology and knowledge

State what is known and what is not. Do not present a prediction or a single study as settled fact, and name your source with its year.

Ideas that work on any of these days

  • Explain the day in three short lines a ten-year-old would follow, and add one picture or simple diagram.
  • Show a small experiment or demonstration people can do at home with materials they already have.
  • Introduce one scientist or engineer connected to the day with what she or he found and when. Use a reliable source.
  • Share a surprising number from the body that leads the day, with the year and the link.
  • Ask a question: what would you like science to solve in your town? Answer a few in follow-up posts.
