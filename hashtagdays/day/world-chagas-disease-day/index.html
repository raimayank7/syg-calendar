World Chagas Disease Day: date, background and post ideas

World Chagas Disease Day is on 14 April every year. It is led by WHO; below are the background, hashtags and 5 post ideas.

What it is

World Chagas Disease Day is observed on April 14 to raise awareness around Chagas disease, a neglected tropical disease present mostly in Latin America. It was first celebrated on April 14, 2020, and was named after Carlos Ribeiro Justiniano Chagas, the Brazilian doctor who diagnosed the first case on April 14, 1909. World Chagas Disease Day was approved for creation on May 24, 2019, at the 72nd session of the World Health Assembly, and officially created at the WHA plenary on May 28, 2019.

The proposal for a World Chagas Disease Day was instituted by the International Federation of Associations of People Affected by Chagas Disease, and was supported by several health institutions, universities, research centres, organizations and foundations.

"An annual day celebrated at global level is bound to attract international attention," said Dr Pedro Albajar Viñas, WHO Medical Officer (Chagas disease). "These days can help to provide visibility and commit countries to enhance control interventions for a disease that has remained largely neglected, but still present in many countries." Celebrating World Chargas Disease Day provides a unique opportunity to express a voice in favor of this disease.

World Chagas Disease Day is one of 11 official global public health campaigns marked by the World Health Organization (WHO), along with World Tuberculosis Day, World Health Day, World Malaria Day, World Immunization Week, World No Tobacco Day, World Blood Donor Day, World Hepatitis Day, World Patient Safety Day, World Antimicrobial Awareness Week and World AIDS Day.

Who set it up

The United Nations list names no resolution as the basis and the agency WHO as the body that leads it.

Hashtag suggestions

  • #WorldChagasDiseaseDay
  • #ChagasDiseaseDay
  • #Disease

Mix one tag for the day with one or two for your own topic or town. Keep to three to five tags; long strings of tags look like spam.

5 ways to post about it

  1. Post one fact about World Chagas Disease Day from the website of the health body that leads it (the World Health Organization or your national health ministry), with a link, rather than a fact you cannot source.
  2. Share a simple habit that fits World Chagas Disease Day and costs nothing: a walk, a check-up, hand washing, a glass of water. Keep it practical and kind.
  3. Ask your followers to tell the check-up or screening they keep putting off; reply to a few comments with the next step and where to get it.
  4. Thank the people who work on World Chagas Disease Day: nurses, community health workers, pharmacists, volunteers. A short photo or quote works well.
  5. Make a "myth or fact" carousel for World Chagas Disease Day: three common beliefs and the official answer, each with the source in small print.

These ideas are for the topic group "Health and medicine".

Take care

Health posts need care. Do not give diagnoses, doses or cures; do not share a claim you cannot trace to a health authority; and link to the official page. Some days concern illnesses that people live with, so use respectful words and avoid pity, blame or fear.

Timing

In 2027 the day falls on a Wednesday. Schedule your post for the morning of the day itself, and make a short teaser three days before and a thank-you the day after. If the day falls on a weekend, prepare and schedule the posts in advance.
