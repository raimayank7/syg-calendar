World Cotton Day: date, background and post ideas

World Cotton Day is on 7 October every year. It is on the United Nations list; below are the background, hashtags and 5 post ideas.

What it is

Cotton is a soft, fluffy staple fiber that grows in a boll, or protective case, around the seeds of the cotton plants of the genus Gossypium in the mallow family Malvaceae. The fiber is almost pure cellulose and may contain minor percentages of waxes, fats, pectins, and water. Under natural conditions, cotton bolls facilitate the dispersal of the seeds.

The plant is a shrub native to tropical and subtropical regions around the world, including the Americas, Africa (including Egypt) and India. The greatest diversity of wild cotton species is found in Mexico, followed by Australia and Africa. Cotton was independently domesticated in the Old and New Worlds.

The fiber is most often spun into yarn or thread and used to make a soft, breathable, and durable textile. The use of cotton for fabric is known to date to prehistoric times; the presence of Gossypium barbadense has been identified at a site in Nanchoc District, Peru, and dated to the 7th–6th millennia BC, while indigo dyed textile fragments, dated to the 4th–3rd millennia BC, having been found at Huaca Prieta, in Peru. Fragments of a cotton thread, used to connect a string of eight copper beads and dated to the sixth millennium BC, have been found at Mehrgarh, Kachi, Pakistan.

Although cultivated since antiquity, it was the invention of the cotton gin that lowered the cost of production and led to its widespread use, and it is the most widely used natural fiber cloth in clothing today.

Current estimates for world production are about 25 million tonnes or 110 million bales annually, accounting for 2.5% of the world's arable land. India is the world's largest producer of cotton. The United States has been the largest exporter for many years.

Who set it up

It was established by resolution A/RES/75/318 of the United Nations General Assembly, adopted in its 75th session (September 2020 to September 2021).

Hashtag suggestions

  • #WorldCottonDay
  • #CottonDay
  • #Cotton

Mix one tag for the day with one or two for your own topic or town. Keep to three to five tags; long strings of tags look like spam.

5 ways to post about it

  1. Explain World Cotton Day in two or three plain sentences and link to the body that leads it.
  2. Share a photo from your own place that fits World Cotton Day and say why you chose it.
  3. Ask followers one open question about World Cotton Day, and answer a few replies.
  4. Thank someone who works on this topic in your area, with permission.
  5. Post one useful link or number for World Cotton Day, checked this week.

These ideas are for the topic group "General awareness".

Take care

Check every fact before you post and link to the official page of the body that leads the day.

Timing

In 2027 the day falls on a Thursday. Schedule your post for the morning of the day itself, and make a short teaser three days before and a thank-you the day after. If the day falls on a weekend, prepare and schedule the posts in advance.
