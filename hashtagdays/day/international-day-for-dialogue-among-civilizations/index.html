International Day for Dialogue among Civilizations: date, background and post ideas

International Day for Dialogue among Civilizations is on 10 June every year. It is on the United Nations list; below are the background, hashtags and 5 post ideas.

YearDate
2026Wednesday, 10 June 2026
2027Thursday, 10 June 2027
2028Saturday, 10 June 2028
2029Sunday, 10 June 2029

What it is

International Day for Dialogue among Civilizations is on the United Nations list of international days and weeks. We did not find a matching Wikipedia article, so the background is not summarised here; the United Nations page for the day has the official text.

Who set it up

It was established by resolution A/RES/78/286 of the United Nations General Assembly, adopted in its 78th session (September 2023 to September 2024).

Hashtag suggestions

  • #InternationalDayDialogueAmongCivilizations
  • #DialogueAmongCivilizationsDay
  • #Civilizations

Mix one tag for the day with one or two for your own topic or town. Keep to three to five tags; long strings of tags look like spam.

5 ways to post about it

  1. Share a line, a rhyme, a recipe or a song in your own language and give a translation in the caption.
  2. Post a photo of a place or object of cultural value near you and say what it is and who looks after it.
  3. Ask your followers for a word from their language or dialect that has no good translation. Collect the answers in a post.
  4. Interview an elder, artist or teacher in two or three questions and post a quote with a photo, with their permission.
  5. For a library, shop or school: set up a small display or reading corner for International Day for Dialogue among Civilizations and post a photo.

These ideas are for the topic group "Culture, language and arts".

Take care

When you share a poem, song or picture by someone else, get permission or use a work in the public domain. Be careful with religious and cultural symbols: describe, do not mock.

Timing

In 2027 the day falls on a Thursday. Schedule your post for the morning of the day itself, and make a short teaser three days before and a thank-you the day after. If the day falls on a weekend, prepare and schedule the posts in advance.
