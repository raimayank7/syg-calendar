World Autism Awareness Day: date, background and post ideas

World Autism Awareness Day is on 2 April every year. It is on the United Nations list; below are the background, hashtags and 5 post ideas.

YearDate

What it is

World Autism Awareness Day or World Autism Acceptance Day is an internationally recognized day (observed annually on 2 April) that encourages member states of the United Nations to take measures to raise global awareness of autism, promote the acceptance, appreciation and inclusion of autistic individuals and recognize such individuals' local and global contributions. The date was chosen to mark the beginning of World Autism Awareness Month, an observance which serves a similar purpose.

Alternatively, Autistic Pride Day, a pride celebration organized by and for autistic people, is held annually on 18 June.

Who set it up

It was established by resolution A/RES/62/139 of the United Nations General Assembly, adopted in its 62nd session (September 2007 to September 2008).

Hashtag suggestions

  • #WorldAutismAwarenessDay
  • #AutismDay
  • #Autism

Mix one tag for the day with one or two for your own topic or town. Keep to three to five tags; long strings of tags look like spam.

5 ways to post about it

  1. Post one fact about World Autism Awareness Day from the website of the health body that leads it (the World Health Organization or your national health ministry), with a link, rather than a fact you cannot source.
  2. Share a simple habit that fits World Autism Awareness Day and costs nothing: a walk, a check-up, hand washing, a glass of water. Keep it practical and kind.
  3. Ask your followers to tell the check-up or screening they keep putting off; reply to a few comments with the next step and where to get it.
  4. Thank the people who work on World Autism Awareness Day: nurses, community health workers, pharmacists, volunteers. A short photo or quote works well.
  5. Make a "myth or fact" carousel for World Autism Awareness Day: three common beliefs and the official answer, each with the source in small print.

These ideas are for the topic group "Health and medicine".

Take care

Health posts need care. Do not give diagnoses, doses or cures; do not share a claim you cannot trace to a health authority; and link to the official page. Some days concern illnesses that people live with, so use respectful words and avoid pity, blame or fear.

Timing

In 2027 the day falls on a Friday. Schedule your post for the morning of the day itself, and make a short teaser three days before and a thank-you the day after. If the day falls on a weekend, prepare and schedule the posts in advance.
