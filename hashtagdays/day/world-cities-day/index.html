World Cities Day: date, background and post ideas

World Cities Day is on 31 October every year. It is on the United Nations list; below are the background, hashtags and 5 post ideas.

YearDate
2026Saturday, 31 October 2026

What it is

World Cities Day is an annual United Nations observance day held on 31 October. The global observance, first held in 2014, is organized by the United Nations Human Settlements Programme (UN-Habitat) in coordination with each year's selected host city.

The dates come from the United Nations list of international days and weeks; the rest of the page is ours.

Who set it up

It was established by resolution A/RES/68/239 of the United Nations General Assembly, adopted in its 68th session (September 2013 to September 2014).

Hashtag suggestions

  • #WorldCitiesDay
  • #CitiesDay
  • #Cities

Mix one tag for the day with one or two for your own topic or town. Keep to three to five tags; long strings of tags look like spam.

5 ways to post about it

  1. Thank your team in a specific way: name three people and one thing each did this month.
  2. Share one lesson from your own work that relates to World Cities Day, in the first person and in under 100 words.
  3. Post a behind-the-scenes photo or a short video of how your product or service is made.
  4. Offer something free and useful for the day: a template, a checklist, a short consultation.
  5. Ask customers for one story of how you helped them, and share it with their permission.

These ideas are for the topic group "Work, business and economy".

Take care

Keep promotions honest and clear. Do not claim savings or returns you cannot prove, and do not give financial, tax or legal advice in a caption: link to the official source.

Timing

In 2027 the day falls on a Sunday, so a weekend: schedule it. Schedule your post for the morning of the day itself, and make a short teaser three days before and a thank-you the day after. If the day falls on a weekend, prepare and schedule the posts in advance.
