World Children's Day: date, background and post ideas

World Children's Day is on 20 November every year. It is on the United Nations list; below are the background, hashtags and 5 post ideas.

What it is

Children's Day is a commemorative date celebrated annually in honour of children, whose date of observance varies by country.

In 1925, International Children's Day was first proclaimed in Geneva during the World Conference on Child Welfare. Since 1950, it is celebrated on 1 June in many countries that were part of the Eastern Bloc and Non-Aligned Movement, which follow the suggestion from Women's International Democratic Federation. World Children's Day is celebrated on 20 November to commemorate the issuance of the Declaration of the Rights of the Child by the UN General Assembly on 20 November 1959, along with the adoption of the Convention on the Rights of the Child on that date in 1989. In some countries, it is Children's Week and not Children's Day.

Who set it up

The United Nations list names A/RES/836(IX as the basis.

Hashtag suggestions

  • #WorldChildrensDay
  • #ChildrensDay
  • #Childrens

Mix one tag for the day with one or two for your own topic or town. Keep to three to five tags; long strings of tags look like spam.

5 ways to post about it

  1. Post a photo or drawing by a child, with a parent's permission and no full name or school.
  2. Share a simple activity families can do in ten minutes for World Children's Day: read aloud, a counting game, a walk, a drawing.
  3. Say thanks to a teacher or mentor and name what they did, in one concrete sentence.
  4. Post a "did you know" about World Children's Day with a figure from a named source, such as UNICEF, UNESCO or the ministry of education.
  5. Start a reading or learning challenge with #WorldChildrensDay and give a simple goal: one page, one chapter, one new word.

These ideas are for the topic group "Children, youth and education".

Take care

Children's privacy matters most: no faces without consent, no names of schools with children's photos, no location tags. Do not exaggerate numbers and link to the source of every figure.

Timing

In 2027 the day falls on a Saturday, so a weekend: schedule it. Schedule your post for the morning of the day itself, and make a short teaser three days before and a thank-you the day after. If the day falls on a weekend, prepare and schedule the posts in advance.
