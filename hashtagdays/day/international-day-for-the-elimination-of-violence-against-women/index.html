International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women: date, background and post ideas

International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women is on 25 November every year. It is on the United Nations list; below are the background, hashtags and 5 post ideas.

What it is

The United Nations General Assembly has designated November 25 as the International Day for the Elimination of Violence Against Women (Resolution 54/134). The premise of the day is to raise awareness around the world that women are subjected to rape, domestic violence and other forms of violence; furthermore, one of the aims of the day is to highlight that the scale and true nature of the issue is often hidden.

For 2014, the official theme framed by the UN Secretary-General's campaign UNiTE to End Violence against Women, was Orange your Neighbourhood. For 2018, the official theme was "Orange the World:#HearMeToo", for 2019 it was "Orange the World: Generation Equality Stands Against Rape", for 2020 it was "Orange the World: Fund, Respond, Prevent, Collect!", for 2021 it was "Orange the World: End Violence against Women Now!", for 2022 the theme was "UNiTE! Activism to end violence against women and girls", for 2023 it was "UNiTE! Invest to Prevent Violence Against Women & Girls! #No Excuse", for 2024 it was "Every 10 minutes, a woman is killed. #NoExcuse UNiTE to End Violence against Women" and for 2025 it was "Digital violence is real violence. There is #NoExcuse for online abuse".

The day got an additional designation in 2011 as the Roses Revolution Day by the namesake movement against obstetric violence, supported by the German National Committee of UN Women.

Who set it up

It was established by resolution A/RES/54/134 of the United Nations General Assembly, adopted in its 54th session (September 1999 to September 2000).

Hashtag suggestions

  • #InternationalDayEliminationViolenceWomen
  • #EliminationViolenceWomenDay
  • #Women

Mix one tag for the day with one or two for your own topic or town. Keep to three to five tags; long strings of tags look like spam.

5 ways to post about it

  1. Celebrate one woman or girl you know and say exactly what she did: a skill, a decision, a risk she took. Ask her first.
  2. Share three facts about International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women from a named source (a UN agency, a census or your ministry) with the year.
  3. Offer something useful: a free session, a mentoring slot, a list of scholarships or helplines. A gesture is stronger than a greeting.
  4. Show the women in your team or community at work, not only on the day: a photo, a role and one achievement each.
  5. Start a "she taught me" thread: ask followers to name the one lesson they got from a woman in their life.

These ideas are for the topic group "Women, girls and families".

Take care

Avoid cliches such as "behind every great man" and avoid tokenism: if you post a day for women, show that you work for women on the other days too. Check helpline numbers before posting.

Timing

In 2027 the day falls on a Thursday. Schedule your post for the morning of the day itself, and make a short teaser three days before and a thank-you the day after. If the day falls on a weekend, prepare and schedule the posts in advance.
