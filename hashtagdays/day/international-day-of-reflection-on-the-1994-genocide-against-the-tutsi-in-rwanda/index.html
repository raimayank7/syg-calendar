International Day of Reflection on the 1994 Genocide against the Tutsi in Rwanda: date, background and post ideas

International Day of Reflection on the 1994 Genocide against the Tutsi in Rwanda is on 7 April every year. It is on the United Nations list; below are the background, hashtags and 5 post ideas.

What it is

Gisozi is one of 15 sectors in the district of Gasabo in the province of Kigali in Rwanda.

The introduction is from the English Wikipedia article Gisozi, available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence (read 13 November 2026). The introduction is the plain-text lead of the article, unchanged except that it is shown without images, and it stays under that licence. The dates come from the United Nations list of international days and weeks; the rest of the page is ours.

Who set it up

It was established by resolution A/RES/58/234 of the United Nations General Assembly, adopted in its 58th session (September 2003 to September 2004).

Hashtag suggestions

  • #InternationalDayReflection1994GenocideTutsiRwanda
  • #Reflection1994GenocideDay
  • #Rwanda

Mix one tag for the day with one or two for your own topic or town. Keep to three to five tags; long strings of tags look like spam.

5 ways to post about it

  1. Share the plain-language text of one right named by International Day of Reflection on the 1994 Genocide against the Tutsi in Rwanda, from the United Nations or your national constitution, with a link.
  2. Tell a short story of a person or group helped by this work, with their agreement, instead of a general slogan.
  3. List where to get help locally: a helpline, legal aid centre or commission. Check each number is current before you post it.
  4. Thank a person who works on International Day of Reflection on the 1994 Genocide against the Tutsi in Rwanda: a lawyer, a social worker, a journalist. Name them only with consent.
  5. Ask a question that invites listening: what would make your neighbourhood fairer? Moderate the replies and remove abuse.

These ideas are for the topic group "Human rights, law and justice".

Take care

These days concern people who have been harmed. Use respectful, factual words; avoid graphic images; do not name victims without permission; and be ready to moderate comments. Do not give legal advice, but point to an official or legal-aid source.

Timing

In 2027 the day falls on a Wednesday. Schedule your post for the morning of the day itself, and make a short teaser three days before and a thank-you the day after. If the day falls on a weekend, prepare and schedule the posts in advance.
