International Day of Clean Energy: date, background and post ideas

International Day of Clean Energy is on 26 January every year. It is on the United Nations list; below are the background, hashtags and 5 post ideas.

YearDate

What it is

The International Renewable Energy Agency (IRENA) is an intergovernmental organization mandated to facilitate cooperation, advance knowledge, and promote the adoption and sustainable use of renewable energy. It is the first international organization to focus exclusively on renewable energy, addressing needs in both industrialized and developing countries. It was founded in 2009, and its statute entered into force on 8 July 2010. The agency is headquartered in Masdar City, Abu Dhabi. The Director-General of IRENA is Francesco La Camera, a national of Italy. IRENA is an official United Nations observer.

Who set it up

It was established by resolution A/RES/77/327 of the United Nations General Assembly, adopted in its 77th session (September 2022 to September 2023).

Hashtag suggestions

  • #InternationalDayCleanEnergy
  • #CleanEnergyDay
  • #Energy

Mix one tag for the day with one or two for your own topic or town. Keep to three to five tags; long strings of tags look like spam.

5 ways to post about it

  1. Post one photo of nature near you with a sentence about International Day of Clean Energy. A real local picture does better than stock art.
  2. Share one change anyone can make this week for International Day of Clean Energy: switch off a light, carry a bag, plant a seed, fix a leaking tap. Name the exact action.
  3. Run a small challenge for the day: count the number of single-use items you use and post the total. Ask others to join with #InternationalDayCleanEnergy.
  4. Tag a local group that works on International Day of Clean Energy and ask what help they need. Volunteers and donations are better than slogans.
  5. Make a before-and-after post of a clean-up, planting or saving drive, with numbers: bags, kilos, trees, hours.

These ideas are for the topic group "Environment and nature".

Take care

Environmental claims must be accurate. Use numbers from a named source with a year, avoid "saving the planet" claims for a small product, and do not post photos that show people or animals in distress without a good reason.

Timing

In 2027 the day falls on a Tuesday. Schedule your post for the morning of the day itself, and make a short teaser three days before and a thank-you the day after. If the day falls on a weekend, prepare and schedule the posts in advance.
