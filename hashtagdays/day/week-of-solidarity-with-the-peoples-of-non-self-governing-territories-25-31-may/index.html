Week of Solidarity with the Peoples of Non-Self-Governing Territories, 25-31 May: date, background and post ideas

Week of Solidarity with the Peoples of Non-Self-Governing Territories, 25-31 May is on 25 May every year. It is on the United Nations list; below are the background, hashtags and 5 post ideas.

YearDate
2026Monday, 25 May 2026
2027Tuesday, 25 May 2027
2028Thursday, 25 May 2028
2029Friday, 25 May 2029

What it is

Week of Solidarity with the Peoples of Non-Self-Governing Territories, 25-31 May is on the United Nations list of international days and weeks. We did not find a matching Wikipedia article, so the background is not summarised here; the United Nations page for the day has the official text.

Who set it up

It was established by resolution A/RES/54/91 of the United Nations General Assembly, adopted in its 54th session (September 1999 to September 2000).

Hashtag suggestions

  • #WeekSolidarityWithPeoplesNonSelfGoverningTerritories2531May
  • #SolidarityWithPeoplesDay
  • #May

Mix one tag for the day with one or two for your own topic or town. Keep to three to five tags; long strings of tags look like spam.

5 ways to post about it

  1. Share the plain-language text of one right named by Week of Solidarity with the Peoples of Non-Self-Governing Territories, 25-31 May, from the United Nations or your national constitution, with a link.
  2. Tell a short story of a person or group helped by this work, with their agreement, instead of a general slogan.
  3. List where to get help locally: a helpline, legal aid centre or commission. Check each number is current before you post it.
  4. Thank a person who works on Week of Solidarity with the Peoples of Non-Self-Governing Territories, 25-31 May: a lawyer, a social worker, a journalist. Name them only with consent.
  5. Ask a question that invites listening: what would make your neighbourhood fairer? Moderate the replies and remove abuse.

These ideas are for the topic group "Human rights, law and justice".

Take care

These days concern people who have been harmed. Use respectful, factual words; avoid graphic images; do not name victims without permission; and be ready to moderate comments. Do not give legal advice, but point to an official or legal-aid source.

Timing

In 2027 the day falls on a Tuesday. Schedule your post for the morning of the day itself, and make a short teaser three days before and a thank-you the day after. If the day falls on a weekend, prepare and schedule the posts in advance.
