International Day against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking: date, background and post ideas

International Day against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking is on 26 June every year. It is on the United Nations list; below are the background, hashtags and 5 post ideas.

What it is

The International Day Against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking is a United Nations International Day against drug abuse and the illegal drug trade. It is observed annually since 26 June 1989. The date 26 June is to commemorate Lin Zexu's dismantling of the opium trade in Humen, Guangdong, ending on 25 June 1839, just before the First Opium War in China. The observance was instituted by UN General Assembly Resolution 42/112 of 7 December 1987.

On 26 June 1987, two important texts (Comprehensive Multidisciplinary Outline of Future Activities in Drug Abuse Control & Declaration of the International Conference on Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking) were adopted at the International Conference on Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking, which was held in Vienna during 17–26 June 1987. The Conference recommended that an annual day should be observed to mark the importance of the fight against drug abuse and illicit trafficking. Both the dates 17 June and 26 June were suggested, and in the later meetings 26 June was chosen and written into the draft and final resolution. It is often referred to by anti-drug campaigners as 6/26, a play on the "4/20" day that celebrates marijuana.

The UN's 2007 World Drug Report

puts the value of the illegal drug trade at US$322 billion a year.

Campaigns, rallies, poster designing and many other programs are conducted. People of different countries celebrate the day together.

‘Health for Justice. Justice for Health’, the theme for International Day against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking 2019, highlights that “justice and health are two sides of the same coin when it comes to addressing drug problems.”

Who set it up

It was established by resolution A/RES/42/112 of the United Nations General Assembly, adopted in its 42nd session (September 1987 to September 1988).

Hashtag suggestions

  • #InternationalDayDrugAbuseIllicitTrafficking
  • #DrugAbuseIllicitDay
  • #Trafficking

Mix one tag for the day with one or two for your own topic or town. Keep to three to five tags; long strings of tags look like spam.

5 ways to post about it

  1. Post one fact about International Day against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking from the website of the health body that leads it (the World Health Organization or your national health ministry), with a link, rather than a fact you cannot source.
  2. Share a simple habit that fits International Day against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking and costs nothing: a walk, a check-up, hand washing, a glass of water. Keep it practical and kind.
  3. Ask your followers to tell the check-up or screening they keep putting off; reply to a few comments with the next step and where to get it.
  4. Thank the people who work on International Day against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking: nurses, community health workers, pharmacists, volunteers. A short photo or quote works well.
  5. Make a "myth or fact" carousel for International Day against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking: three common beliefs and the official answer, each with the source in small print.

These ideas are for the topic group "Health and medicine".

Take care

Health posts need care. Do not give diagnoses, doses or cures; do not share a claim you cannot trace to a health authority; and link to the official page. Some days concern illnesses that people live with, so use respectful words and avoid pity, blame or fear.

Timing

In 2027 the day falls on a Saturday, so a weekend: schedule it. Schedule your post for the morning of the day itself, and make a short teaser three days before and a thank-you the day after. If the day falls on a weekend, prepare and schedule the posts in advance.
