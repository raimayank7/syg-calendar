World Telecommunication and Information Society Day: date, background and post ideas

World Telecommunication and Information Society Day is on 17 May every year. It is on the United Nations list; below are the background, hashtags and 5 post ideas.

YearDate
2026Sunday, 17 May 2026

What it is

World Telecommunication and Information Society Day is an international day proclaimed in November 2006 by the International Telecommunication Union Plenipotentiary Conference in Antalya, Turkey, to be celebrated annually on 17 May.

The introduction is from the English Wikipedia article World Telecommunication and Information Society Day, available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence (read 13 November 2026). The introduction is the plain-text lead of the article, unchanged except that it is shown without images, and it stays under that licence. The dates come from the United Nations list of international days and weeks; the rest of the page is ours.

Who set it up

It was established by resolution A/RES/60/252 of the United Nations General Assembly, adopted in its 60th session (September 2005 to September 2006).

Hashtag suggestions

  • #WorldTelecommunicationInformationSocietyDay
  • #TelecommunicationInformationSocietyDay
  • #Society

Mix one tag for the day with one or two for your own topic or town. Keep to three to five tags; long strings of tags look like spam.

5 ways to post about it

  1. Share the plain-language text of one right named by World Telecommunication and Information Society Day, from the United Nations or your national constitution, with a link.
  2. Tell a short story of a person or group helped by this work, with their agreement, instead of a general slogan.
  3. List where to get help locally: a helpline, legal aid centre or commission. Check each number is current before you post it.
  4. Thank a person who works on World Telecommunication and Information Society Day: a lawyer, a social worker, a journalist. Name them only with consent.
  5. Ask a question that invites listening: what would make your neighbourhood fairer? Moderate the replies and remove abuse.

These ideas are for the topic group "Human rights, law and justice".

Take care

These days concern people who have been harmed. Use respectful, factual words; avoid graphic images; do not name victims without permission; and be ready to moderate comments. Do not give legal advice, but point to an official or legal-aid source.

Timing

In 2027 the day falls on a Monday. Schedule your post for the morning of the day itself, and make a short teaser three days before and a thank-you the day after. If the day falls on a weekend, prepare and schedule the posts in advance.
