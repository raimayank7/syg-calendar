World Youth Skills Day: date, background and post ideas

World Youth Skills Day is on 15 July every year. It is on the United Nations list; below are the background, hashtags and 5 post ideas.

What it is

Jayathma Wickramanayake (born 22 November 1990) is a Sri Lankan born international civil servant who currently serves a Senior Policy Advisor at UN Women. She previously served as the United Nations Secretary-General's Envoy on Youth. Appointed by the United Nations Secretary-General António Guterres in June 2017, she succeeded Ahmad Alhendawi of Jordan, who served as the first Envoy on Youth from 2013 to 2017.

Prior to taking her role as the Envoy, Wickramanayake led youth development efforts both on an international and national level in her home country, Sri Lanka. In this regard, she co-founded the youth organization Hashtag Generation, aimed at increasing the civic and political engagement of Sri Lankan young people, particularly young women. Wickramanayake also served as the country's first-ever Youth Delegate and actively participated in the establishment of World Youth Skills Day.

Who set it up

It was established by resolution A/RES/69/145 of the United Nations General Assembly, adopted in its 69th session (September 2014 to September 2015).

Hashtag suggestions

  • #WorldYouthSkillsDay
  • #YouthSkillsDay
  • #Skills

Mix one tag for the day with one or two for your own topic or town. Keep to three to five tags; long strings of tags look like spam.

5 ways to post about it

  1. Post a photo or drawing by a child, with a parent's permission and no full name or school.
  2. Share a simple activity families can do in ten minutes for World Youth Skills Day: read aloud, a counting game, a walk, a drawing.
  3. Say thanks to a teacher or mentor and name what they did, in one concrete sentence.
  4. Post a "did you know" about World Youth Skills Day with a figure from a named source, such as UNICEF, UNESCO or the ministry of education.
  5. Start a reading or learning challenge with #WorldYouthSkillsDay and give a simple goal: one page, one chapter, one new word.

These ideas are for the topic group "Children, youth and education".

Take care

Children's privacy matters most: no faces without consent, no names of schools with children's photos, no location tags. Do not exaggerate numbers and link to the source of every figure.

Timing

In 2027 the day falls on a Thursday. Schedule your post for the morning of the day itself, and make a short teaser three days before and a thank-you the day after. If the day falls on a weekend, prepare and schedule the posts in advance.
