How to use this site

  1. Open the month you are planning.
  2. Choose the two or three days that fit your account.
  3. Open each day: read the background, copy a hashtag, adapt a post idea.

The guide to planning a year of posts shows how to make a calendar; hashtag rules and mistakes to avoid keep you out of trouble.

Questions people ask

Where does the list of days come from?

From the United Nations List of International Days and Weeks, read on 13 November 2026. Each page names the resolution that set up the day.

Are the hashtags official?

No. They are suggestions made from the name of the day. Check the page of the organisation that leads the day for an official tag.

Can I use the post ideas as they are?

They are our own ideas for you to adapt. Add your own words, photos and facts, and check any figure you use against its source.
