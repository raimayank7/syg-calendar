Plan a Year of Awareness Day Posts

How to choose which awareness days to mark, build a simple content calendar and write posts that help instead of just joining in.

Pick fewer days

The United Nations list has about two hundred days and weeks. No account should mark them all. Choose the days that match what you really do: a clinic marks health days, a school marks education and children's days, a shop marks the days of its trade. Aim for one or two a month.

Build a calendar

  1. Go through the months in this site and tick the days you will mark.
  2. Put them in a calendar app with a reminder two weeks before.
  3. Write the post a week before. Decide who approves it.
  4. Schedule it for the morning of the day. Prepare a thank-you or follow-up for the next day.

Make the post useful

The posts that people save and share give something: a fact with a source, a how-to, a link to help, a story told by the person it is about. A picture with the name of the day and nothing else adds nothing. Each day page here has ideas for its topic.

Mind the tone

Some days celebrate and some remember suffering. Do not use a cheerful template for a sad day. If you are not sure, read what the organisations that lead the day post.

Look back

At the end of the year, count which posts brought replies, saves or visits, and drop the days that brought nothing.
