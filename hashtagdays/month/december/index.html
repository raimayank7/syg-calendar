Planning December

December has 24 observances on the United Nations list, of which 24 have a fixed date. Most common topic groups: Human rights, law and justice (7), General awareness (4), Work, business and economy (3). Pick the two or three that fit your account and skip the rest: a feed that marks every day looks like noise.

Weeks are listed with the date the United Nations gives for their first day. Some observances have a date that changes each year (for example a weekday of the month), so check the date for the year before you plan. Where a day has a page on Wikipedia, its introduction is shown on the day page with credit, so you can read the background before you write.

A simple plan: put the dates in a calendar two weeks ahead, write the post a week ahead, schedule it the day before, and be ready to answer comments on the day.
