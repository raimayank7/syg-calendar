Bank holidays in the UK

In the United Kingdom public holidays are called bank holidays. There is no single list: England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland each have their own. The tables below combine the three official lists for 2026 to 2028. The last column shows where each holiday applies; "All of the UK" means all three.

GOV.UK publishes the dates a few years ahead. Extra bank holidays for royal and national occasions are announced separately when they are declared, so check the official list before fixing plans far in advance.

How the three lists differ

  • Easter Monday is a bank holiday in England, Wales and Northern Ireland but not in Scotland.
  • Scotland has 2 January and St Andrew's Day (30 November), and its summer bank holiday is the first Monday of August. In the rest of the UK the summer bank holiday is the last Monday of August.
  • Northern Ireland has St Patrick's Day (17 March) and the Battle of the Boyne (Orangemen's Day) on 12 July.
  • New Year's Day, Good Friday, the early May bank holiday, the spring bank holiday, Christmas Day and Boxing Day are common to all three.

In Scotland many local holidays are fixed by local councils and businesses, so the bank holiday list is only part of the picture there.

Substitute days

When a bank holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, a substitute weekday becomes the bank holiday, normally the following Monday. When Christmas Day and Boxing Day both fall on a weekend, the Monday and Tuesday after are given. The official list marks these as "Substitute day", and the tables above keep that note. In 2027, for example, Christmas Day is a Saturday and Boxing Day a Sunday, so the bank holidays are Monday 27 and Tuesday 28 December.

Good Friday and Easter Monday

These two move every year with Easter, which is the Sunday after the first full moon of spring by the church's reckoning. The other movable holidays are Mondays fixed by rule: the first Monday of May, the last Monday of May and the last (or in Scotland the first) Monday of August.

Does everyone get the day off?

Not automatically. GOV.UK explains that an employer does not have to give paid leave on bank holidays; it depends on the employment contract. Banks and most offices close, many shops stay open with shorter hours, and public transport often runs a Sunday timetable.

Working with people in the UK

  • Dates are written day first, as in India: 27/12/2027 is 27 December. See date formats.
  • The tax year runs from 6 April to 5 April, and the government's financial year from 1 April to 31 March. See financial years.
  • The UK numbers its weeks by the ISO rule, with weeks starting on Monday. See week numbers.
  • The days between Christmas and New Year are very quiet in most offices.
How many bank holidays are there in England?

Eight in a normal year in England and Wales. Scotland has nine and Northern Ireland ten.

What is a substitute day?

A weekday that becomes the bank holiday when the holiday's own date falls on a Saturday or Sunday.

Is Easter Monday a holiday in Scotland?

It is not on Scotland's bank holiday list, though some employers and councils there give it.
