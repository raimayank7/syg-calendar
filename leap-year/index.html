Is it a leap year?

Type a year to see whether it is a leap year, and which part of the rule decides it.

The rule

A year is a leap year, with 366 days and a 29 February, if:

  1. it can be divided by 4, and
  2. it is not a century year, unless that century year can be divided by 400.

So 2024, 2028 and 2032 are leap years. 1900 was not, because it is a century year that 400 does not divide. 2000 was, because 400 divides it. 2100, 2200 and 2300 will not be leap years; 2400 will.

Why leap years exist

The Earth takes about 365 days, 5 hours, 48 minutes and 46 seconds to go once round the Sun, measured from one spring equinox to the next. A calendar of 365 days is therefore nearly a quarter of a day too short. Left alone, the calendar would slip against the seasons by about 24 days every century, and festivals fixed by date would drift from winter into autumn.

Adding one day every fourth year makes the average year 365.25 days. That is a little too long, by about 11 minutes a year. The mistake is small but it builds up to a day in about 128 years.

Why century years are special

Julius Caesar's calendar, used from 45 BCE, had the simple every-fourth-year rule. By the 1500s the extra 11 minutes a year had moved the calendar ten days away from the seasons. In 1582 Pope Gregory XIII ordered two corrections: ten dates were skipped that October, and three leap days were to be dropped in every 400 years, by making century years ordinary unless they divide by 400.

This gives 97 leap years in every 400 years and an average year of 365.2425 days. It is still about 27 seconds too long, which adds up to one day in roughly 3,000 years: good enough that no further correction has been agreed.

Leap years near now

2020, 2024, 2028, 2032, 2036, 2040, 2044, 2048. In this stretch every fourth year is one; the next exception is 2100.

What changes in a leap year

  • February has 29 days and the year has 366.
  • Dates from March onward fall two weekdays later than the year before, not one.
  • People born on 29 February have a true birthday. See the note on the age calculator.
  • Salaried staff paid by the month get the same monthly pay for one more day. Interest counted by the day is spread over 366 days where the rule says "actual days".
  • In the Indian national (Saka) calendar the first month, Chaitra, has 31 days in place of 30, and the year begins on 21 March in place of 22 March. See the Saka date page.

A quick mental check

Look at the last two digits of the year. If they form a number that 4 divides (04, 08, 12 ... 96), the year is a leap year. If the year ends in 00, look at the first two digits and test those for division by 4: 20 divides, so 2000 was a leap year; 21 does not, so 2100 is not.

Is 2027 a leap year?

No. 2027 divided by 4 leaves a remainder of 3. The next leap year is 2028.

Why is the extra day put in February?

In the early Roman calendar the year began in March, so February was the last month, and corrections were made at the year's end. The habit outlived the change of New Year to January.

How many leap years are there in a century?

Usually 24. A century that includes a year divisible by 400, such as 2000, has 25.
