The leap-year rule

A year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4, except for years divisible by 100, which are leap years only if they are also divisible by 400. So 2024 and 2028 are leap years, 1900 and 2100 are not, and 2000 and 2400 are. A leap year has 366 days, with 29 February added to February. The rule is the one of the Gregorian calendar, used by most of the world.
