How often

Between 2020 and 2060 there are 69 Friday the 13ths in 41 years, an average of 1.68 a year. A year has at least one and at most three. Over the 400-year cycle of the Gregorian calendar the 13th falls on a Friday 688 times, a Wednesday 687 times, a Sunday 687 times, a Monday 685 times, a Tuesday 685 times, a Thursday 684 times and a Saturday 684 times (4,800 months in all, counted by the build), so Friday is the most common day.

Why

The 400-year Gregorian cycle is exactly 20,871 weeks, so every date repeats its weekday after 400 years, but the months have different lengths, so the 13th does not fall evenly on the seven weekdays. That Friday is the most common is a quirk of the calendar, not a sign of anything.
