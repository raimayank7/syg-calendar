Reading the list

From 1900 to 2200 there are 73 leap years. 1900, 2100 and 2200 are century years not divisible by 400, so they are not leap years, while 2000 is. The gap between leap years is four years, except around those century years, where it is eight. The list includes the years of the 2024 Paris and 2028 Los Angeles Olympics, which fall in leap years.

Weekday of 29 February

29 February falls on every weekday in turn, and the pattern repeats every 28 years inside a century. The next 29 February in the list falls on a Tuesday in 2028.
