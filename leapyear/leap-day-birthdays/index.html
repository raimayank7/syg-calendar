Leap day birthdays

People born on 29 February are called leaplings. Here is how rare it is and how their age is counted.

How rare is it?

In the 400-year Gregorian cycle there are 97 leap days among 146,097 days. If births were spread evenly over all days, about 1 person in 1506 would be born on 29 February. Calendars of different countries treat the day in different ways; many people in the world have never met a person born on 29 February.

When the birthday comes

A leapling has a real birthday only in a leap year, once in four years, with the exception of 2100, 2200 and 2300, which are not leap years. Someone born on 29 February 2000 has real birthdays in 2004, 2008, 2012, 2016, 2020, 2024, 2028 and so on, and passes 2100 without one. Most people celebrate on 28 February or 1 March in the other years.

Age

A leapling born on 29 February 2000 is 26 on 28 February or 1 March 2026. Age in completed years does not change by the choice of 28 February or 1 March; only the day on which the year is counted changes. Legal rules for the exact day vary from country to country and from purpose to purpose (voting, driving, retirement), so check the rule of the authority that asks.

Next leap days

The next 29 February dates are 2028 (Tuesday), 2032 (Sunday), 2036 (Friday), 2040 (Wednesday), 2044 (Monday), 2048 (Saturday).
